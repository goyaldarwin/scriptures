Privacy

शास्त्र · Scriptures — website and apps. Last updated 18 October 2026.

In short

The app collects nothing about you. It has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your reading place, bookmarks, notes, reading-plan progress and settings are saved only in the app's storage on your own device. They are never sent to us. If you choose “Save my file” in Settings, a file is saved where you choose; what you do with it is up to you.

What the app downloads

The texts, meanings and verse timings are downloaded from our website (goyaldarwin.github.io), which is hosted by GitHub. Like any web server, GitHub may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Audio

Recitations are played with YouTube's own embedded player. When you play audio, YouTube (Google) receives the request as it does for any embedded video, under Google's privacy policy. Nothing is sent to YouTube unless you press play.

Helpers' timing files

If you help with audio timings and choose “Save as a file”, the file contains the times you marked and the name you type. It is shared only if you send it yourself.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions: github.com/goyaldarwin/scriptures/issues.

गोपनीयता

संक्षेपमें

यह ऐप आपके बारेमें कुछ भी एकत्र नहीं करता। इसमें न खाता है, न साइन-इन, न विज्ञापन, न विश्लेषण, न ट्रैकिंग।

आपके फ़ोनमें क्या रहता है

आप कहाँ तक पढ़े, बुकमार्क, टिप्पणियाँ, पाठ-योजनाकी प्रगति और सेटिंग्स केवल आपके अपने फ़ोनमें सेव होती हैं। ये हमें कभी नहीं भेजी जातीं।

ऐप क्या डाउनलोड करता है

मूल पाठ, अर्थ और पदोंके समय हमारी वेबसाइट (goyaldarwin.github.io, GitHub पर) से आते हैं। GitHub सुरक्षाके लिये सामान्य तकनीकी लॉग रख सकता है।

ऑडियो

पाठ YouTube के अपने प्लेयरसे चलता है। ऑडियो चलानेपर YouTube (Google) को वैसा ही अनुरोध मिलता है जैसा किसी भी एम्बेड किये वीडियोके लिये — Google की गोपनीयता नीतिके अनुसार। चलाये बिना YouTube को कुछ नहीं भेजा जाता।
