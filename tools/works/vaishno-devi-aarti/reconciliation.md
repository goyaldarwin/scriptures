# Reconciliation: जय वैष्णवी माता

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** Aarti Mala (booklet `aarti-mala`), one printed page, "श्री वैष्णो जी की आरती"; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-353), read on the page image. The page is a printout of indif.com (its watermark). Sixteen printed lines, no stanza breaks, each line's halves joined by a comma; the lines end । and ।। in turn.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/vaishno-devi/vaishno-devi-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/vaishno-mata-aarti.html`): the same āratī in the same order, eight groups of four half-lines (the refrain and seven stanzas); a typed copy with its own spellings.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (the book's comma between the halves of a line becomes a line break, a half per line); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| the stanzas | sixteen lines, no breaks | the refrain and seven stanzas of two printed lines (four half-lines) each | Every second line ends ।। and the rhyme; Drik Panchang divides the same way. |
| every line | the halves joined by a comma | two lines | The standard; the comma marks the half. |
| stanza numbers | none (।।) | ॥१॥ … ॥७॥ | The standard. |
| refrain, line 4 | आरती मै गाता | आरती मैं गाता | A dropped anusvāra: the pronoun "I" is मैं (Drik Panchang मैं). |
| stanza 2, line 1 | ब्रह्मावेद पढे नित द्वारे | ब्रह्मा वेद पढ़े नित द्वारे | A missing space (Brahmā reads the Veda) and a dropped nukta (पढ़े); Drik Panchang has both. |
| stanza 2, line 3 | सेवत चंवर डुलावत | सेवक चंवर डुलावत | A one-letter slip: each clause of the stanza names who serves (ब्रह्मा, शंकर, नारद), and सेवत leaves this one without its subject; Drik Panchang सेवक ("the servant waves the fly-whisk"). |
| stanza 5, line 1 | पाव सुपारी ध्वाजा नारियल | पान सुपारी ध्वजा नारियल | Two misprints: the offering is पान (betel leaf) beside सुपारी, and ध्वाजा is not a word (ध्वजा, a flag); Drik Panchang पान … ध्वजा. |
| stanza 5, line 3 | दास खड़े चरणो में | दास खड़े चरणों में | A dropped anusvāra (the oblique plural चरणों); Drik Panchang चरणों. |
| stanza 6, lines 3–4 | (missing) | उसकी इच्छा पूरण । माता हो जावे | The page drops the second line of the last two stanzas, joining the first halves of stanzas 6 and 7 as one couplet (…आवे । …गावे ।।): the sentence "जो जन निश्चय करके द्वार तेरे आवे" is left without its main clause. Taken from Drik Panchang. |
| stanza 7, lines 3–4 | (missing) | कहते सेवक ध्यानू । सुख सम्पत्ति पावे | The same omission; taken from Drik Panchang. |

Kept as the book has them, where Drik Panchang differs: शीश पर (Drik पे), बिराजे (Drik विराजे), ज्योती (Drik ज्योति), देखने को (Drik देखन को), झूले (Drik झूलें), घन्टा (Drik घंटा), ऊंचा (Drik ऊँचा), निशदिन (Drik निश-दिन).
