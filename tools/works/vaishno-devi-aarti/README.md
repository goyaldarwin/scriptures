# जय वैष्णवी माता (Jai Vaishnavi Mata)

The āratī of Vaiṣṇo Devī, in Hindi (`hi`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): Aarti Mala (page image i-353), checked against Drik Panchang; two lines the page leaves out are taken from Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/shemaroo-bhakti/`: Shemaroo Bhakti (YouTube `OMt-BtXI_HA`, "Vaishno Mata Aarti | Jai Vaishnavi Mata | वैष्णो देवी की आरती | Navratri | Vaishno Devi Ki Aarti"), its track 0:00–5:12 of the video (after 5:12: music). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list.

The closing stanza names its singer: कहते सेवक ध्यानू ("says the servant Dhyanu").

Verse types: टेक (refrain, `tek`, shown once at the start although it is sung after every stanza) and पद (stanza, `pad`). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (Jai Vaishnavi Mata) › one section (`aarti`) › verses: the refrain (`aarti.tek`, type `tek`) and 7 stanzas.
- **Numbering:** each stanza's `n` is its number (॥१॥ …), and it is cited by it ("Stanza 3"); the refrain has none.
- **Topics:** one, the āratī's title. **Reading plans:** none.
