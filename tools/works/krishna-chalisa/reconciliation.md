# Reconciliation: श्री कृष्ण चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 33–36; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-032 … i-035), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/krishna/krishna-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shree-krishna-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of a related text. Its opening dohās differ: it has बंशी शोभित … / अरुण अधर जनु बिम्बा फल पिताम्बर शुभ साज, then जय मनमोहन … / करहु कृपा हे रवि तनय राखहु जन की लाज, where the book has the two dohās बंशी शोभित … नयन कमल अभिराम and पूर्ण इन्द्र अरविन्द मुख … कृष्ण चन्द्र महाराज. The book's are kept. Its 40 chaupāīs are the book's, in the same order.
- **Chalisa Mala** (the indif.com printout, page images i-377 … i-379): a printout of the same web text as Drik Panchang; not a separate witness.
- **For a doubtful reading only:** Shubh Panchang (shubhpanchang.com, Krishna Chalisa), and Aaj Tak (aajtak.in, Shri Krishna Chalisa), which prints Drik Panchang's text. Sites that copy one another count as one witness.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 11 | अलक घुँघँराले | अलक घुँघराले | A second candrabindu printed by mistake on the second syllable; the word has one nasal (Drik Panchang घुंघराले). |
| chaupāī 12 | अका बका कागा सुर मारयो | अका बका कागासुर मारयो | The demon's name (Kāgāsura) is one word, broken in print; Drik Panchang कागासुर. |
| chaupāī 29 | मारथ के पारथ रथ हांके | भारथ के पारथ रथ हांके | मारथ is not a word: म for भ. भारथ (the Bhārata war) is the reading of Shubh Panchang, and rhymes with पारथ; Drik Panchang has भारत. |
| chaupāī 39 | क्षमहुबेगि | क्षमहु बेगि | A missing space (Drik Panchang क्षमहु बेगि). |
| closing dohā | अष्ट सिद्धि नवनिद्धि फल | अष्ट सिद्धि नवनिधि फल | नवनिद्धि is a misprint for नवनिधि, the nine treasures (Drik Panchang नवनिधि). |
| closing dohā | लहै पदारथ चरि | लहै पदारथ चारि | चरि is a misprint: the four goals (पदारथ चारि), rhyming with धारि; Drik Panchang चारि. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: the two opening dohās (above); बिम्ब फल (Drik बिम्बा फल); कृष्ण चन्द्र; नटनागर, नथइया, चरइया (Drik नट-नागर, नथैया, चरैया); बंशी मधुर अधर धरि टेरी, होवे पूर्ण विनय यह मेरी (Drik वंशी … धरी तेरी, होवे पूर्ण मनोरथ मेरो); बैजन्ती; सोहै / मोहै; बांसुरी; पूतनहिं; अगिन, भये शीतल (Drik अग्नि, भै शीतल); मूसर धार (Drik मसूर धार); मुख मँह (Drik महं); दे निर्भय कीन्हें (Drik दै … किन्हें); पूरण करि अभिलाषा; संहारियो (chaupāī 20); कहँ; षट दस सहस कुमारी; दें भीमहिं तृणचीर संहारा (Drik दै भिन्हीं तृण चीर सहारा; Shubh Panchang has the book's दें भीमहिं … संहारा: Bhīma is shown the split straw, and Jarāsandha is killed); मूठि; माँगे; लखी प्रेमकी; नहिं बल थांके (Drik ताके); बजा कर; राणा, साँप; उरते; तव शत निन्दा (Drik तब); द्रोपदी; तुरतहि, भये अरि मुँह काला; अस अनाथ के नाथ (Drik अस नाथ के नाथ); नइया; सुन्दरदास; की जय (Drik की जै); पाठ करे.
