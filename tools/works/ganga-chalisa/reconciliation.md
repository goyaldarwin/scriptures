# Reconciliation: श्री गंगा चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 256–259; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-255 … i-258), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/ganga/ganga-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/ganga-mata-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, verse for verse, but badly garbled in typing (जहानु, हनानी, डालिनी, जदिता रत्ना, सीधी तरनी अमिता पितु पड़ पिरही, उद्दिहिन, भुत नभ्दिशी and many more); it is used only where it shows a slip of the book.
- **For a doubtful reading only:** web search for chaupāī 3 found Aaj Tak (aajtak.in/religion/chalisa/shri-ganga-chalisa-2450469) and InstaAstro (instaastro.com/blog/maa-ganga-ji-ki-chalisa), both with Drik Panchang's garbled words (जहानु, सहस्त्र, भुत नभ्दिशी): copies of it, counted with it.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 3 | जय जय जय हनु सुता अघ हननी | जय जय जहनु सुता अघ हननी | हनु सुता makes no sense, and the half has 18 mātrās for 16: the ज of जहनु (Jahnu, whose daughter the Gaṅgā is: जाह्नवी, chaupāī 20) has been set as a third जय. Drik Panchang has जय जय जहानु सुता (a typing slip for जहनु). The book's letters ह-नु are kept, with the ज restored. |
| chaupāī 7 | तरल तरंग तंग मन भावनि | तरल तरंग तुंग मन भावनि | तंग ("narrow") makes no sense of waves; तुंग ("lofty") is the opening dohā's own word (अनुपम तुंग तरंग), and Drik Panchang has तुंग. |
| chaupāī 10 | साठि सहत्र सगर सुत तारयो | साठि सहस्र सगर सुत तारयो | सहत्र is not a word: the sixty thousand (सहस्र) sons of Sagara; Drik Panchang has सहस्र. |
| chaupāī 35 | महाँ अधिन अधमन कहँ तारें | महाँ अघिन अधमन कहँ तारें | अधिन is a ध for घ: अघिन, "sinners", beside अधमन, "the lowly"; Drik Panchang has अघिन. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang (beyond its typing slips): मम तनु साजे (Drik सजे), मुनि भागीरथ in chaupāī 17 (Drik पुनि भागीरथी; neither is clearly wrong), धरयौ, तइ जगि, सकल सिद्ध in chaupāī 36 (Drik सिद्धि), नित नव (Drik नए), धरैं गंग का ध्यान, नैत्र (Drik नेत्र; rhymes with चैत्र), सम्वत् भुज नभ दिशि (Drik संवत भुत नभ्दिशी).

## Meanings

## Readings settled
- chalisa.cau4, मम तनु: मम ("my body"), as printed; the book, Drik Panchang, Aaj Tak (aajtak.in, Shri Ganga Chalisa) and Shubh Panchang (shubhpanchang.com, Ganga Chalisa) all print मम, and no source has सम, so the plain reading stands.
- chalisa.cau40, वागीसा: the reader "becomes a master of speech", as the meaning has it; Monier-Williams gives vāg-īśa first as "a master of language, eloquent, an orator", and the reading needs no giver the line does not name.
- chalisa.do3, सम्वत् भुज नभ दिशि … नैत्र: the year kept in its number-words (arm, sky, direction), as the meaning has it; read right to left they give no likely year, and दिशि may be 4, 8 or 10, so no figure is given; नैत्र is "eye" (Monier-Williams also lists netra as the numeral 2, but the year is complete in the first line and नैत्र is the rhyme of चैत्र), as printed.
