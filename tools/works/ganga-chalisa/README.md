# श्री गंगा चालीसा (Shri Ganga Chalisa)

The chālīsā of the Gaṅgā that opens "जय जय जय जग पावनी जयति देवसरि गंग", in Awadhi (`awa`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 256–259), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** none yet.
- **Poet:** chaupāī 39 names him, Sundaradāsa of Kakarā village (by Durvāsā's hermitage); the last dohā gives the date of writing as a chronogram, saṁvat "भुज नभ दिशि", on Rāma's birthday in Caitra.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohā (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohās (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 40 chaupāīs are numbered 1–40 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the three dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Hanumān Chālīsā's in the Sundarakāṇḍa (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** the form of the Gaṅgā, the Gaṅgā at the holy places, the descent of the Gaṅgā, the fruit of her water and her name, the poet's signature. **Reading plans:** none.
