# जय सिद्धिदात्री माँ तू सिद्धि की दाता (Jai Siddhidatri Maa Tu Siddhi Ki Data)

The āratī of Devī Siddhidātrī, one of the nine forms of Durgā (Navadurgā), in Hindi (`hi`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): Drik Panchang, "Siddhidatri Mata Aarti" (the book has no copy of it). Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/madhuram-devotional/`: Madhuram Devotional Music (YouTube `cG3MFWP0EXw`, "माँ सिद्धिदात्री की आरती 🪔 जय सिद्धिदात्री माँ तू सिद्धि की दाता | Navratri Navami Aarti Lyrics"), its track 0:13–3:00 of the video (before 0:13: a sung mantra 'ॐ देवी सिद्धिदात्र्यै नमः' twice; after 3:00: 'जय माँ सिद्धिदात्री' twice and the jaikāra 'बोलो साँचे दरबार की जय'). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list.

Verse types: पद (stanza, `pad`); the source prints no refrain, so there is no टेक (`tek` is declared, as in every āratī, but unused). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (Jai Siddhidatri Maa Tu Siddhi Ki Data) › one section (`aarti`) › verses: 5 stanzas of four lines.
- **Numbering:** each stanza's `n` is its number (॥१॥ …), and it is cited by it ("Stanza 3").
- **Topics:** one, the āratī's opening words. **Reading plans:** none.
