# Reconciliation: जय सिद्धिदात्री माँ तू सिद्धि की दाता

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** "Siddhidatri Mata Aarti" (`hinduism/aarti/durga/siddhidatri-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/devi-siddhidatri-aarti.html`), headed आरती देवी सिद्धिदात्री जी की. Five groups of four printed lines, no refrain marked.
- The book (५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित) and the other booklets have no copy of this āratī; Drik Panchang is the only witness.

The rule: keep the base; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; stanzas numbered (॥१॥). The page marks no refrain, so none is made: the text is five stanzas.

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| line ends | । and ॥ alternating | । and, at a stanza's end, ॥N॥ | The standard. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥५॥ | The standard. |

Kept as the base has them: the page's line breaks and words throughout, the short line कठिन काम सिद्ध करती हो तुम, जभी, दाती, सर्वसिद्धि, सुमिरन, मूर्ति, काज, मैया, सवाली, महा नन्दा मन्दिर, वास तेरा (twice, in stanza 5), भक्ति है सवाली तू जिसकी दाता.
