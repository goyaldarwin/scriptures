# ॐ जै यक्ष कुबेर हरे (Om Jai Yaksha Kuber Hare)

The āratī of Kubera, the lord of wealth and of the yakṣas, in Hindi (`hi`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): Drik Panchang, "Shri Kubera Aarti" (drikpanchang.com/lyrics/aarti/lord-kubera/lord-kubera-aarti.html); the āratī is not in the book. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/choovill/`: choovill (YouTube `zJRIWAo3ZWg`, "Kubera Aarti for Abundance & Financial Well-Being | Om Jai Yaksha Kubera Hare  ॐ जय यक्ष कुबेर हरे."). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list.
- **Poet:** the last stanza names प्रेमपाल (Prempal).

Verse types: टेक (refrain, `tek`, shown once at the start although it is sung after every stanza) and पद (stanza, `pad`). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (Om Jai Yaksha Kuber Hare) › one section (`aarti`) › verses: the refrain (`aarti.tek`, type `tek`) and 7 stanzas.
- **Numbering:** each stanza's `n` is its number (॥१॥ …), and it is cited by it ("Stanza 3"); the refrain has none.
- **Topics:** one, the āratī's title. **Reading plans:** none.
