# Reconciliation: ॐ जै यक्ष कुबेर हरे

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** "Shri Kubera Aarti", under the heading "आरती श्री कुबेर जी की" (`hinduism/aarti/kubera/kuber-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/lord-kubera-aarti.html`): the refrain and seven stanzas, each in four printed lines (a line of the form "…, स्वामी …" holds two half-lines), with the cue "ॐ जै यक्ष कुबेर हरे...॥" after the refrain and after every stanza.
- **The book:** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan) and the other booklets in scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` do not print this āratī, so Drik Panchang is the only witness.

The rule: keep the base; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (a printed line holding two half-lines divided by a comma is split, a half per line); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Base | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| stanzas 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, line 2 | "सिर पर छत्र फिरे, स्वामी सिर पर छत्र फिरे" and the like: one printed line | two lines (…फिरे । स्वामी … फिरे ।) | The standard; the comma marks the half, and the "स्वामी …" repeat is its own line as in the refrain and stanzas 1 and 4. |
| every line | ends in a comma, । or nothing | ends । (the last of a stanza ॥N॥) | The standard. Stanza 5, line 2 (हम तेरी शरण पड़े) has no mark at all in the base, a dropped daṇḍa. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥७॥ | The standard. |
| after the refrain and every stanza | ॐ जै यक्ष कुबेर हरे...॥ | (not shown) | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item (टेक). |

Kept as the base has them: जै, स्वामी जै यक्ष जै यक्ष कुबेर हरे, भगतों, भण्डार, कई-कई (with its hyphen), गावैं, करैं and करें, भाँति भाँति, लगावैं, उड़द, सारे काम संवारे (which does not rhyme with पड़े, as the other stanzas rhyme; no other witness to correct it by), मणी, मोतियन, जोत, प्रेमपाल, मनवांछित.

## Readings settled
The readings the words alone leave open, each decided (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §2).

- **aarti.pad7** — कहत प्रेमपाल स्वामी, स्वामी part of the name or an address: part of the poet's signature, "Premapāla Svāmī says", as कहत शिवानन्द स्वामी in the āratīs this one is modelled on (ॐ जय जगदीश हरे, जय अम्बे गौरी); the address स्वामी of this āratī stands at the head of its repeat lines (स्वामी जो कोई नर गावे), not at a line's end. Meaning unchanged.
