# श्री गीता चालीसा (Shri Gita Chalisa)

The chālīsā of the Bhagavad Gītā that opens "प्रथमहिं गुरुको शीश नवाऊँ", in Awadhi (`awa`): Arjuna asks who is the better devotee, the one who worships the Lord with form or the one who meditates on the formless, and Kṛṣṇa answers (the subject of the Gītā's twelfth chapter).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): Drik Panchang's Gita Chalisa page (the book, ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित, has no copy). Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** none yet.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › one section, the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`) › verses. The work has no opening or closing dohā.
- **Numbering:** the 40 chaupāīs are numbered 1–40 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12").
- **Ids:** as the Gaṇeśa Chālīsā's (`chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** the invocation, Arjuna's question, the devotee of the Lord with form, meditation on the formless, the easy way. **Reading plans:** none.
