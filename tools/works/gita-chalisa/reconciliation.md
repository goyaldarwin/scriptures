# Reconciliation: श्री गीता चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base)** (`hinduism/chalisa/gita/gita-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/gita-chalisa.html`): the only copy the owner's sources have; the book (५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित) does not print this chālīsā.
- **For a doubtful reading only:** a web search for the line; it found only Bhakti Bharat, which prints Drik Panchang's text (one witness with it).

The rule: keep Drik Panchang's text; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end.

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 14 | उनकी श्रद्घा अटल अपार | उनकी श्रद्धा अटल अपार | A typing slip: श्रद्घा (द + घ) is not a word; श्रद्धा (द + ध), "faith". |
| chaupāī 39 | फिर होवेगा मोहिं सामान | फिर होवेगा मोहिं समान | सामान ("goods") makes no sense; the line says the devotee becomes "like me" (मोहिं समान), as chaupāīs 23, 27 and 33 use समान. The metre needs it too: with समान the half has 16 mātrās, with सामान 17. |

Kept as Drik Panchang has them: कुण (chaupāī 9, the Rajasthani "which"), भगवान् (chaupāī 10), भय रु (chaupāī 17, रु for अरु), बुद्धी (chaupāī 20; the metre needs the long ī), ब्यापक, सगुन, the halves shorter than 16 mātrās (chaupāīs 2, 7, 13, 22 and others), as printed.
