# श्री तुलसी चालीसा (Shri Tulsi Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Tulasī that opens "जय जय तुलसी भगवती सत्यवती सुखदानी", in Awadhi (`awa`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): Drik Panchang (lyrics/chalisa/tulasi-mata/tulasi-mata-chalisa); the book (५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित) has no copy. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`; open doubts: `docs/reviews/tulsi-chalisa-doubts.jsonl`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** none yet.
- **Poet:** chaupāīs 38–39 name him, the poet Sundara, living near Durvāsā's hermitage east of Prayāga (the place of the Gaṅgā and Śītalā Chālīsās' poets).

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohās (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohās (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 40 chaupāīs are numbered 1–40 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the six dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Hanumān Chālīsā's in the Sundarakāṇḍa (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** Tulasī's penance and Lakṣmī's curse, Rādhā's curse and Śaṅkhacūḍa, Vṛndā and Jalandhara, the glory of Tulasī. **Reading plans:** none.
