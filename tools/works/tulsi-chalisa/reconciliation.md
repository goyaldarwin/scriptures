# Reconciliation: श्री तुलसी चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base)** (`hinduism/chalisa/tulsi/tulsi-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/tulasi-mata-chalisa.html`): two opening dohās, 40 chaupāīs, four closing dohās. Its typing is poor in many places (कध, व्हौ रौ पाती, धग्व, halves with a word missing); there is no printed copy to check it against: the book (५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित) has no Tulasī Chālīsā.
- **For a doubtful reading only:** web search found Navbharat Live (navbharatlive.com/religion/recite-tulsi-chalisa-on-thursday-1438362.html), InstaAstro and Aaj Tak; Navbharat Live, checked word by word at the doubtful places, has Drik Panchang's very slips (बिरजिनी, संग्राम, प्रयास, धग्व, कध, सुनी उर शोक उपारा): a copy of it, counted with it, like Bhakti Bharat. No independent witness was found.

The rule: keep Drik Panchang's text; correct it only where it is clearly wrong by the rhyme, the metre, the grammar or the sense, and say why. Where nothing settles a doubtful reading, it stays as printed (chaupāīs 3, 5, 6, 9, 22, 27, 30 and the last dohā: "Readings settled" below). The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the page's commas are not kept.

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| opening dohā 2 | श्री हरि शीश बिरजिनी | श्री हरि शीश बिराजिनी | बिरजिनी is not a word, and the half has 12 mātrās for a dohā's 13: बिराजिनी, "seated (on Hari's head)". |
| chaupāī 17 | लीन्हा शंकर से संग्राम | लीन्हा शंकर से संग्रामा | The half rhymes with बलधामा and needs its 16 mātrās; the final ा is dropped. |
| chaupāī 39 | जो प्रयास ते पूर्व ललामा | जो प्रयाग ते पूर्व ललामा | प्रयास ("effort") makes no sense; the poet places Durvāsā's hermitage east of Prayāga, as the same poets' signatures do in this library's Gaṇeśa Chālīsā (जग प्रयाग ककरा दुर्वासा) and the book's Śītalā Chālīsā (तट प्रयाग से पूरब पासा). |

Kept as Drik Panchang has them: every other reading, with its spellings (सुनी, जानी, हरीहीँ, होहू, मह, नाही, हरिही, तुहु, शङ्ख चुड, व्यन्जन, बसही, हरीराम). The closing dohās are irregular in metre and are kept as printed.

## Readings settled
Every copy found on the web (Navbharat Live, InstaAstro, Bhakti Bharat) is Drik Panchang's text with its slips (InstaAstro checked again at each place below: कध, व्हौ रौ पाती, धग्व, सुनी उर शोक उपारा, दियो श्राप बिना विचारे, विनय उस, उस अयोग्य, लड़हि … अत्र, all as Drik); Aaj Tak prints a different Tulasī Chālīsā. No independent witness, so the text stays as printed everywhere; the meaning gives the plain reading.
- **chalisa.cau3, cau6** (उस for अस?): उस, as printed: the oblique of वह, "(a prayer) to him" (cau3), "that (unfitting boon)" (cau6); both read as Hindi, so nothing shows अस. Meanings unchanged.
- **chalisa.cau5** (कध, not a word): read as कंध, "shoulder" (Platts kandh), the printed letters with a dropped anusvāra, which also gives the half its 16 mātrās; क्रोध would need two letters added. Meaning changed: Lakṣmī "laid a curse upon her shoulders" (was "growing angry"). Text kept as printed (no witness gives कंध).
- **chalisa.cau9** (व्हौ रौ पाती): read as "I shall be (व्हौ, ह्वैहौं) your husband (पाती, पति)", the only sense the letters and the context (Hari's promise, cau8) allow; रौ adds nothing. Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.cau22** (सुनी उर शोक उपारा, 13 mātrās): as printed; उपारा is उपाड़ना, "to uproot" (Platts upāṛ), not अपार: "hearing it, grief uprooted her heart". Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.cau27** (दियो श्राप बिना विचारे, 14 mātrās): as printed, "you cursed without thinking"; a missing word is not supplied without a witness. Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.cau30** (धग्व, not a word): read as धरब, Awadhi "I shall take (the form)" (र mistyped as ग्, ब as व), the sense the line and cau29–31 need; दग्ध ("burnt", Platts dagdha) does not fit Hari's promise. Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.do6** (सुखराम / तुलसीदास, no rhyme): as printed: "Sukharāma, son (सूत, सुत) of Tulasī, Tulasīdāsa, composed this Mānasa Chālīsā"; nothing shows another reading. Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.cau38** (कवि सुन्दर इक): the poet's signature, "Sundara, a poet (इक, 'one, a')", as in the Gaṅgā and Śītalā Chālīsās; cau39 goes on with where he lives (near Durvāsā's hermitage), so the subject is the poet, not anyone who sings. Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.do4** (हार, लड़हि, अत्र): हरि हार ह्वै, "Hari becomes the remover" (हार, "taking away", Platts hār, rt. hṛ); लड़हि read as लहहि, "obtain" (ड़ for ह; "fight" makes no sense); अत्र kept as the word it is, "here" (Platts atra), not अन्न. Meaning unchanged.
