# Barah Maha Majh — reconciliation

The text is Sri Guru Granth Sahib's, taken unchanged (`text.txt` → `[take guru-granth-sahib 133.1 .. 136.2]`). Its
witnesses and every choice are in `tools/works/guru-granth-sahib/reconciliation.md`; a correction is made there and

## Where it begins and ends

Checked against the Granth's data (`library/sikhism/gurbani/guru-granth-sahib/work.json`, made from `text.txt`), not
written from memory:

- **First verse** `133.1` (Ang 133), its first lines: ਬਾਰਹ ਮਾਹਾ ਮਾਂਝ ਮਹਲਾ ੫ ਘਰੁ ੪ ॥ / ੴ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ ॥ / ਕਿਰਤਿ ਕਰਮ ਕੇ ਵੀਛੁੜੇ ਕਰਿ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਮੇਲਹੁ ਰਾਮ ॥
- **Last verse** `136.2` (Ang 136), its last line: ਨਾਨਕੁ ਮੰਗੈ ਦਰਸ ਦਾਨੁ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਕਰਹੁ ਹਰੇ ॥੧੪॥੧॥
- The verse before it in the Granth ends ਨਾਨਕ ਰਤਾ ਸਚਿ ਨਾਇ ਹਰਿ ਵੇਖੈ ਸਦਾ ਹਜੂਰੇ ॥੮॥੫॥੩੯॥੧॥੩੨॥੧॥੫॥੩੯॥ and the verse after it begins
  ਮਾਝ ਮਹਲਾ ੫ ਦਿਨ ਰੈਣਿ ॥: neither is part of the bani.
- 14 stanzas; Ang 133–136, as the Granth's Ang view gives the pages of its first and last lines.
