# Anand Sahib — reconciliation

The text is Sri Guru Granth Sahib's, taken unchanged (`text.txt` → `[take guru-granth-sahib 917.1 .. 922.6]`). Its
witnesses and every choice are in `tools/works/guru-granth-sahib/reconciliation.md`; a correction is made there and
reaches this work at the next export.

## Where it begins and ends

Checked against the Granth's data (`library/sikhism/gurbani/guru-granth-sahib/work.json`, made from `text.txt`), not
written from memory:

- **First verse** `917.1` (Ang 917), its first lines: ਰਾਮਕਲੀ ਮਹਲਾ ੩ ਅਨੰਦੁ ॥ / ੴ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ ॥ / ਅਨੰਦੁ ਭਇਆ ਮੇਰੀ ਮਾਏ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੂ ਮੈ ਪਾਇਆ ॥
- **Last verse** `922.6` (Ang 922), its last line: ਬਿਨਵੰਤਿ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਗੁਰ ਚਰਣ ਲਾਗੇ ਵਾਜੇ ਅਨਹਦ ਤੂਰੇ ॥੪੦॥੧॥
- The verse before it in the Granth ends ਨਾਮ ਦਾਨੁ ਨਾਨਕ ਵਡਿਆਈ ਤੇਰਿਆ ਸੰਤ ਜਨਾ ਰੇਣਾਰੀ ॥੮॥੩॥੮॥੨੨॥ and the verse after it begins
  ਰਾਮਕਲੀ ਸਦੁ ॥: neither is part of the bani.
- 40 pauris; Ang 917–922, as the Granth's Ang view gives the pages of its first and last lines.
