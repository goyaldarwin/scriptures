# श्री महाकाली चालीसा (Shri Mahakali Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Mahākālī that opens "जय जय सीताराम के मध्यवासिनी अम्ब", in Awadhi (`awa`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 208–212), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** none yet.

The poet names himself in the second opening dohā: "काली चालीसा रचत एक सिद्धि कवि हिन्द" (a poet who signs as सिद्धि कवि; also in chaupāīs 12 and 35).

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohās (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohā (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 42 chaupāīs are numbered 1–42 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the four dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Hanumān Chālīsā's in the Sundarakāṇḍa (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** praise of Mahākālī, her form and her might, her many forms and shrines, the prayer and its fruit. **Reading plans:** none.
