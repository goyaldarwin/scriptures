# Reconciliation: श्री महाकाली चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 208–212; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-207 … i-211), read on the page images. The āratī printed after it on p. 212 (आरती श्री महाकाली जी की) is its own work.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/kali/mahakali-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/mahakali-mata-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text.
- **Chalisa Mala** (a printout of indif.com; the same book folder, page images i-420, i-421): another web copy. It ends otherwise: after "कितनी स्तुति करूँ अखण्डे" it has three chaupāīs of its own ("यह चालीसा जो नर गावे", "माला औ फल फूल चढ़ावे", "सब की तुम समान महतारी") and its own closing dohā ("सब जीवों के जीव में"), in place of the book's last five chaupāīs and closing dohā; and it has "कलित गले कोमल रच्चाये" for the book's "कलित कण्ठ शोभित नरमुण्डा". The book's version is the text.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 20 | तारागण तू ब्योंम विताने | तारागण तू ब्योम विताने | ब्योंम is not a word: a stray anusvāra on ब्योम ("sky"), as Drik Panchang has it (Chalisa Mala has the same slip). |
| chaupāī 22 | धूप्र विलोचनि (as the page's letter reads) | धूम्र विलोचनि | The demon is Dhūmralocana ("smoke-eyed"); Drik Panchang has धूम्र. The page's म्र is printed so that it reads as प्र. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: दुःख दारिद्रता (opening dohā 3; Drik दरिद्रता; Chalisa Mala as the book), ह्रीं काली श्रीं महाकराली (Drik श्री महाकाली; श्रीं continues the bīja syllables ह्रीं … क्रीं, and महाकराली fills the half-line's 16 mātrās; Chalisa Mala has महा कराली), नलिनबिलोचनि (Drik नलिनविलोचनि), जगकी (Drik जग की), सुमति हे (Drik सुमति है; हे rhymes with प्रकृति हे), विन्ध्याचल वासिनि (Drik वासिनी), तुम्हरी (Drik तुम्हरि). The book's जननकी, मद्य मतंगे, स्वछन्द रद मारद, झननन तच्छु मरिरिन, शक्तिजितचण्डे and the poet's सिद्धि कवि are the book's, Drik Panchang the same.
