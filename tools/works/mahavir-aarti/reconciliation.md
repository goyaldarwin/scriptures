# Reconciliation: जय महावीर प्रभो

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), p. 145; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-144), read on the page image. Its order: the refrain, कुण्डलपुर में, दीनानाथ दयानिधि, पापाचार मिटाया, अर्जुनमाली गौतम, पावन नाम, and the closing stanza with the poet's name (करुणासागर … ज्ञानमुनि गुण गावे).
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/mahavir/mahavir-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-mahavir-aarti.html`): the same āratī, in the same order and the same words, each printed line broken in two; its refrain cue is ॐ जय महावीर प्रभु.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (the book's comma between the halves of a line becomes a line break, a half per line); a line the book prints without a comma stays one line; the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| every stanza | two printed lines, halved by commas | a line per half, ending । and ॥N॥ | The standard; the comma marks the half. The refrain's first line and stanza 6's first line have no comma and stay whole (Drik Panchang breaks them after प्रभो and तेरी). |
| refrain, line 1; stanza 6, line 1 | प्रभो! … प्रभो!; करुणासागर! | प्रभो … प्रभो; करुणासागर | Exclamation marks are the printer's punctuation, not part of the verse, like its commas; no verse of the library carries one. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥६॥ | The standard. |
| after the refrain and every stanza | ॐ॥ | (not shown) | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item (टेक). |

Kept as the book has them: every word; Drik Panchang does not differ from the book in any word (जन्में, हर्षाए, परउपकारी, निसिदिन in both).
