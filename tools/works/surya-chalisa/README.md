# श्री सूर्य चालीसा (Shri Surya Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Bhagavān Sūrya that opens "कनक बदन कुण्डल मकर", in Awadhi (`awa`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 74–77), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/anant-bhakti-geet/`: Anant Bhakti Geet (YouTube `GNBcwOjQS0A`, "श्री सूर्य चालीसा | Surya Chalisa Lyrics In Hindi | Powerful Surya Dev Mantra | ANANT BHAKTI GEET"). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list. Not sung, so untimed: `chalisa.cau36`.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohā (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohā (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 40 chaupāīs are numbered 1–40 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the two dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Hanumān Chālīsā's in the Sundarakāṇḍa (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** the Sun's names and chariot, the twelve names, the armour of protection, the glory of the Sun god, the Sun of the twelve months. **Reading plans:** none.
