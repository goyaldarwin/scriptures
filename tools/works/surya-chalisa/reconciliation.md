# Reconciliation: श्री सूर्य चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 74–77; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-073 … i-076), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/surya/surya-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/surya-deva-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text (one opening dohā, 40 chaupāīs, one closing dohā), in the same order. It drops हँस from chaupāī 25 (कटिमंह, रहत मन मुदभर).

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas and exclamation marks are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāīs 1–4, 8 | सहस्त्रांशु! सप्ताश्व …, भानु! पतंग! मरीची! भास्कर! सविता! …, विवस्वान! आदित्य! …, अम्बरमणि! खग! …, जोते! | without the exclamation marks | Punctuation of the printer, not part of the verse (as the commas). |

| chaupāī 23 | त्वष्टा-वरुण रहम सुउष्णकर | त्वष्टा-वरुण रहत सुउष्णकर | रहम makes no sense here; रहत ("dwell"), as in the verses around it (रहैं, बसत); Drik Panchang रहत. |

| chaupāī 33 | मन्द सदृश सुतजग में जाके | मन्द सदृश सुत जग में जाके | A missing space: "whose son in the world is Manda (Śani)"; Drik Panchang सुत जग. |
| chaupāī 34 | धन्य-२ तुम दिनमनि देवा | धन्य-धन्य तुम दिनमनि देवा | The २ is the printer's sign for a repeated word; Drik Panchang धन्य-धन्य. |
| chaupāī 37 | मध वेदांगनाम रवि उदयन | मधु वेदांगनाम रवि उदयन | मध has lost its u: मधु, the month (Caitra) in the list of months; Drik Panchang मधु. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: ध्याइये; सहस्रांशुप्रद्योतन कहि कहि; हाँकत; पुरंदर; नवावै; चार पदारथ सो जन पावै (Drik जन सो); पुञ्ज; कौ (chaupāīs 13, 20); प्रबलमोह, फँद; तिग्मतेजसः; कटि मंह हँस रहत मन मुदभर (Drik lacks हँस); करहुँ; दरिद्र कुष्ट (Drik दद्रु कुष्ठ: both are readings that make sense, "poverty and leprosy" or "ringworm and leprosy"); कबहुँ, मनमहं; मन्द, बाँके; नियमसों, भ्रमसों; परम धन्य सो नर (Drik सों); वेदांगनाम; वैसाख; गावहि, लहै विविध होंहि (closing dohā).

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
The readings the words alone leave open, each decided (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §2).

- **chalisa.cau6** — अरुण सदृश सारथी, when Aruṇa is himself the Sun's charioteer: kept as printed and read as the words say, "a charioteer lovely as Aruṇa". The same poet two chaupāīs later writes उच्चैःश्रवा सदृश हय, "horses like Uccaiḥśravā", so सदृश after a name is "like" in this text (our glossary: समान); MW's other sense "fit, worthy" would need another word order. Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.cau16** — रिपु सों जमलहते सोतेहि छन: divided रिपु सों जम लहते, सो तेहि छन ("their enemies obtain Yama, that very moment"), लहना "to obtain" (Platts) as in मोदलहि (cau5) and लहै (closing dohā). The book (i-074) and Drik Panchang print the same; no witness divides it otherwise. Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.cau23** — सुउष्णकर: read as an epithet of Tvaṣṭā and Varuṇa ("of fair warm rays"), the plain reading: उष्णकर "hot-rayed" (MW) is an adjective before it is a name of the Sun, and the half has one verb (रहत) for the pair. Drik Panchang and Aaj Tak print the same words. Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.cau24** — भानुमान उरसर्म सुउदरचन: kept as printed (the book, Drik Panchang, Aaj Tak agree) and read with उरसर्म as उरः + शर्म "guard of the chest" and सु-उदर "the belly well (guarded)"; no source of the armour could be read to restore other names, so the plain reading stands. Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.cau40** — अगहन भिन्न: kept as printed. मित्र (the Sun of Mārgaśīrṣa in some lists) is a likely original, but every witness (the book i-076, Drik Panchang, Aaj Tak) prints भिन्न and no source of this chālīsā with मित्र was found, so nothing shows the print is a corruption; भिन्न read as the words say, "another (name)". Meaning unchanged.
