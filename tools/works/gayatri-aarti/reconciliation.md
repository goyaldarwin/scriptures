# Reconciliation: जयति जय गायत्री माता

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), p. 201, under the heading "आरती श्री गायत्री जी की"; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-200), read on the page image. The refrain line (centred), ten printed lines, one stanza each (its two halves joined by a comma, with commas between the names inside the halves), and the refrain line again.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/gayatri/gayatri-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/gayatri-mata-aarti.html`): the same ten stanzas in the same order, each in four lines, with the cue "जयति जय गायत्री माता..." after each; its refrain has a second line, सत् मारग पर हमें चलाओ, जो है सुखदाता (the second half of the book's last stanza). The page then prints a second, different āratī under the same heading, "आरती श्री गायत्रीजी की" (ज्ञानदीप और श्रद्धा की बाती …, six couplets); it is not in the book and is not part of this work.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (the book's comma between the halves of a line becomes a line break, a half per line); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| every stanza | one printed line, its halves and the names inside them joined by commas | two lines, split at the half (after कर्त्री, अम्बे, राशी …); the other commas are spaces | The standard; each half ends on the stanza's rhyme. |
| the last line | जयति जय गायत्री माता, जयति जय गायत्री माता। | (not written) | The closing repeat of the refrain; the refrain is shown once. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥१०॥ | The standard. |
| stanza 1, line 2 | दारिद्रय | दारिद्र्य | A typing slip (a dropped halant): दारिद्रय is not a word; दारिद्र्य is "poverty", beside दैन्य. Drik Panchang has the same slip. |
| stanza 4, line 1 | जग गङ्गा गीता | जय गङ्गा गीता | A one-letter slip: the line lists the Mother's names (कामधेनु, सत् चित् आनन्दा, गङ्गा, गीता), hailed with जय as in stanza 6 (जय सतरूपा); जग ("world") stands in the list with no sense. Drik Panchang has जय गंगा गीता, and so does InstaAstro (<https://instaastro.com/blog/gayatri-mata-aarti/>), an independent web copy. |
| stanza 5, line 1 | प्राणयिनी | प्रणयिनी | प्राणयिनी is not a word; प्रणयिनी ("beloved") is. Drik Panchang प्रणयिनी; InstaAstro (above) प्रणयनी. |
| stanza 5, line 2 | सहस्त्रार | सहस्रार | सहस्त्र is a common misspelling of सहस्र ("thousand"); the thousand-petalled cakra is सहस्रार, as Drik Panchang has it. |
| stanza 10, line 2 | चलाओं | चलाओ | A stray anusvāra: the imperative is चलाओ, as Drik Panchang has it. |

Kept as the book has them, where Drik Panchang differs: the refrain as one line (Drik adds सत् मारग पर हमें चलाओ, जो है सुखदाता), निरंजन (Drik निरञ्जन), ब्रह्मरूपिणी (Drik ब्रह्म रुपिणी), पालनी (Drik पालिनी), जगद्धातृ (Drik जगतधातृ), भव भय हारी, जन हितकारी (Drik joined), भय हारिणि, भव तारिणि (Drik joined), गङ्गा (Drik गंगा), ब्रह्माणी (Drik ब्रहाणी), सतरूपा (Drik सतरुपा), हम हैं दीन-हीन दुःख-दारिद्र (Drik है दीन हीन दुःख दरिद्र), यद्यपि (Drik यदपि), बालक हैं (Drik है), स्नेह सनी करुणामयी (Drik स्नेहसनी करुणामयि), तुष्टि-पुष्टि, सत्यमार्ग (Drik सत् मार्ग). Both have अघहरी; it stays.
