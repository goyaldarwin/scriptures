# श्री भैरव चालीसा (Shri Bhairav Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Bhairava that opens "श्री भैरव संकट हरन", in Awadhi (`awa`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 64–67), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** none yet.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohā (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohā (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 37 chaupāīs are numbered 1–37 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the two dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Gaṇeśa Chālīsā's (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** hail to Bhairava, his form, one with Śiva in three colours, his names and greatness, the prayer. **Reading plans:** none.
