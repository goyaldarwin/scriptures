# Reconciliation: श्री भैरव चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 64–67; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-063 … i-066), read on the page images. The āratī printed after the closing dohā (आरती श्री भैरव जी की) is its own work.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/bhairava/bhairav-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-bhairava-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, with its own slips (जि दास for निज दास, जयत for जपत, तम्हरो).
- **For a doubtful reading only:** Shubh Panchang (shubhpanchang.com, Bhairava Chālīsā), found by web search, read for chaupāī 5 (सफल). Bhakti Bharat prints Drik Panchang's text and counts with it as one witness.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept (also those inside a half: श्याम वर्ण, वपु; काशी कोतवाल, संकटहर; संकटहरण, हरहु). The book's half-r in टार्‍यो / सार्‍यो is written as the same letters, र्य.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 33 | दुष्टन कहँ प्रभु अहछु भयंकर | दुष्टन कहँ प्रभु अहहु भयंकर | अहछु is not a word, a damaged letter: the book has अहहु ("you are") in chaupāīs 13 and 25, and Drik Panchang has अहहु here. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: निज दास (Drik जि दास), सफल एक ते एक (Drik सकल; Shubh Panchang the same as the book), घुँघुरू बाजैं / भाजैं (Drik घुँघरू बाजै / भाजै), लिए, अविनासी (Drik अविनाशी), कहुँ होइ (Drik कहुं होई), जपत (Drik जयत), अहहु अतीता (Drik अहहू), कालमयी (Drik कालजयी), सार्यो (Drik सारयो), तब चरणन (Drik तव), श्रीक्रोधेश, बूढ़त, तुम्हरो (Drik तम्हरो), परे (Drik परै), सुखकन्द, देहु (Drik देहुं).
