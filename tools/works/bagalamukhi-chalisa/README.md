# श्री बगलामुखी चालीसा (Shri Baglamukhi Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Bagalāmukhī that opens "सिर नवाइ बगलामुखी लिखूँ चालीसा आज", in Awadhi (`awa`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 251–254), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** none yet.
- **Poet:** the closing dohās name him, Kulapati Miśra, son of Santaśaraṇa, of Haripur village in Haridwar district, and the date of writing, Śrāvaṇa śukla of 1995.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohā (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohās (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 40 chaupāīs are numbered 1–40 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the three dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Hanumān Chālīsā's in the Sundarakāṇḍa (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** the Mother's form, the way of worship, the fruit of the fire offering, the fruit of the japa, the poet's signature. **Reading plans:** none.
