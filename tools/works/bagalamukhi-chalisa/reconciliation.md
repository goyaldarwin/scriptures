# Reconciliation: श्री बगलामुखी चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 251–254; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-250 … i-253), read on the page images. The āratī printed after it (p. 255, i-254) is its own work.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/bagalamukhi/bagalamukhi-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/bagalamukhi-mata-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, word for word the book's but for the three readings listed below.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept; its hyphens are.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 26 | गग्गुल तिल सँग होम करावै | गुग्गुल तिल सँग होम करावै | गग्गुल is a misprint: the book itself has गुग्गुल (guggul resin) in chaupāī 25, and Drik Panchang has गुग्गुल in both. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: महरानी (Drik महारानी; महरानी keeps the half at 16 mātrās), सुख-सम्पति (Drik सुख-सम्पत्ति; the book has सुख-संपति in chaupāī 22 too), यामे (Drik जामे).

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
- chalisa.cau16 — मातु भगति तब: kept, Mother, devotion to you (तब as तव, your). The book writes तब for तव in chaupāī 2 too (बगला सम तब आनन, read on i-250), and भगति needs the genitive; "then" does not fit an address. The text stays as printed (page image i-251: तब; Drik Panchang the same).
- chalisa.cau37 — पुत्रादिक फल चारी: kept, the four fruits, a son and the rest. The plain words; चारी as "moving" makes no sense with फल.
- chalisa.do3 — सन् उन्नीस सौ पिचानबे: kept, 1995 of the Common Era: सन् marks the Common Era (a Vikrama year is संवत्); the book is of 2000.
