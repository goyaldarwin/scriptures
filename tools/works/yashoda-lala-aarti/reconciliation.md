# Reconciliation: आरति करत यसोदा प्रमुदित

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** "Shri Yashodalala Aarti" (`hinduism/aarti/krishna/yashoda-lala-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-yashodalala-aarti.html`), headed श्री यशोदालाल आरती. Four groups of four printed lines, no refrain marked; the last names the poet (कृष्णदास-प्रभु).
- The book (५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित) and the other booklets have no copy of this āratī; Drik Panchang is the only witness.
- **For a doubtful reading only:** a web search for stanza 3 found no independent printing of the pada.

The rule: keep the base; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; stanzas numbered (॥१॥). The page marks no refrain, so none is made: the text is four stanzas.

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| stanza 4, lines 1–2 | बरसत कुसुम निकर-सुर-नर- / मुनि व्रजजुवती मुसकात। | बरसत कुसुम निकर-सुर-नर-मुनि । / व्रजजुवती मुसकात । | The page breaks the compound सुर-नर-मुनि across its lines, leaving a line ending in a hyphen; the word is put whole on the first line. |
| line ends | , । ॥ or none | । and, at a stanza's end, ॥N॥ | The standard. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥४॥ | The standard. |

Kept as the base has them: the page's line breaks elsewhere, यसोदा, अङ्ग, बल-बल, सुबरन-थार, घृत-भीनी बात, अच्छत, भाँत, बिबिध, कुम्कुमा, नगदात (these two are open doubts: `docs/reviews/yashoda-lala-aarti-doubts.jsonl`), व्रजजुवती, कृष्णदास-प्रभु, गिरधर, निरख, ससि-काँत.
