# जय पुरुषोत्तम देवा (Jai Purushottam Deva)

The āratī of Puruṣottama (Viṣṇu as the lord of the Puruṣottama or Adhika month), in Hindi (`hi`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): Drik Panchang, Purushottam Dev Ki Aarti; none of the printed booklets has it. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/devpriya-bhakti-ka-sagar/`: DevPriya Bhakti ka Sagar (YouTube `UFdSv6yJ5As`, "॥ श्री पुरुषोत्तम देव की आरती ॥Adhik Maas Special Aarti of Purushottam Dev #bhakti"), its track 0:00–3:10 of the video (after 3:10: music). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list.
- **Poet:** the last stanza names Prabhudās (प्रभुदास).

Verse types: टेक (refrain, `tek`, shown once at the start although it is sung after every stanza) and पद (stanza, `pad`). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (Jai Purushottam Deva) › one section (`aarti`) › verses: the refrain (`aarti.tek`, type `tek`) and 7 stanzas.
- **Numbering:** each stanza's `n` is its number (॥१॥ …), and it is cited by it ("Stanza 3"); the refrain has none.
- **Topics:** one, the āratī's title. **Reading plans:** none.
