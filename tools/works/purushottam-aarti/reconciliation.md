# Reconciliation: जय पुरुषोत्तम देवा

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** Purushottam Dev Ki Aarti (`hinduism/aarti/vishnu/purushottam-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/lord-purushottam-aarti.html`): the refrain and seven stanzas, each half-line printed on its own line, with the refrain cue "जय पुरुषोत्तम देवा" after every stanza.
- **The book:** none. The āratī is not in ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (`hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/`) or the other booklets; Drik Panchang is the only witness.

The rule: keep Drik Panchang; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; each printed line is a line, ending । and the stanza's last ॥N॥; the refrain (टेक) is shown once.

| Place | Base | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| after every stanza | जय पुरुषोत्तम देवा॥ (refrain cue) | (not shown) | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item (टेक). |
| every line | the printed commas | (none) | The standard: no commas in an āratī's verse. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥७॥ | The standard. |

No reading of the base is changed. Kept as the base has them: सुक्ख, छार, द्रोपदि, सन्मानी, दृढ़धन्वा, व्रतविधि, मणीग्रिव (the name Maṇigrīva in the metre's form: मणीग्रिव gives the line its 12 mātrās, as मेधावी मुनि कन्या), सहजही, the hyphens (सुर-मुनि, व्रत-पूजा).

## Readings settled
- aarti.pad7 — प्रभुदास: the poet's name, "says Prabhudāsa", stays; an āratī's last stanza carries its poet's signature (README), and वे is already the subject, so प्रभुदास as "the Lord's servants" would repeat it.
