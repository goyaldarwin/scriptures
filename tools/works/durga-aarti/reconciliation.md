# Reconciliation: अम्बे तू है जगदम्बे काली

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), p. 207; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-206), read on the page image, under the heading "आरती श्री काली जी की". The refrain (two printed lines) and three stanzas of four printed lines each; the last printed line of every stanza ends with the refrain's last line, "…, ओ मैया हम सब उतारें तेरी आरती।". The āratī ends on this page (p. 208 begins the Mahākālī Chālīsā).
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/durga/durga-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/durga-mata-aarti.html`, "Durga Mata Ki Aarti"): another version, four stanzas: the book's three (तेरे भक्त जनो पर माता, माँ-बेटे का है इस जग में, नहीं मांगते धन और दौलत) and a fourth, चरण शरण में खड़े तुम्हारी, which the book does not have. Its first stanza ends दुष्टों को तू ही ललकारती (the book: दुखियों के दुःख को निवारती), and its third has हम तो मांगें तेरे चरणों में छोटा सा कोना (the book: हम तो मांगते तेरे मन का एक छोटा सा कोना). Each stanza in seven lines, with the cue "ओ मैया हम सब उतारे तेरी आरती॥" after each.
- **Aarti Mala** (page image i-339, "श्री दुर्गा जी की आरती", a printout carrying the indif.com watermark): a longer version, ten stanzas, with other wordings: the book's three stanzas (the first ending दुष्टो को तू ही संहारती, the second and third with other last lines), then चौदस के दिन तेरे भवन मे, हम पापी माँ अधम अधम अनाड़ी, मन मंदिर मे गूंज रहा है, बीच भँवर मे आन पड़ी नैया, तुम हो मेरी इष्टाध्यायी, यह छोटा सा परिवार हमारा.

The recording: Anuradha Paudwal, "Ambe Tu Hai Jagdambe" (YouTube `OQ5K_GILwzQ`), the owner's choice: the refrain, then the book's three stanzas in its order, each followed by the refrain, and the refrain to the end. In six lines she sings another witness's wording, and the text takes it, each word as a witness writes it (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3: the text follows the recording).

The book's version is the text (three stanzas), with the lines she sings otherwise. The rule: keep the book, except where the recording sings another witness's wording; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (a printed line is split at its half, one half per line); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang (she sings these as the book has them, or the transcript cannot tell them apart): जगदम्बे काली जय दुर्गे (Drik adds commas only), गुन गायें (Drik गुण गावें), उतारें (Drik उतारे), सौ सौ सिंहों से बलशाली (Drik सौ-सौ सिहों से बलशाली; Aarti Mala सौ सौ सिंहो सी तू बलशाली; the transcripts may hear से भी बलशाली, which no witness writes: for the owner's ear), पूत कपूत सुने हैं, सब पर करुणा दरसाने वाली (Drik सब पे करूणा दर्शाने वाली), ना चांदी ना सोना (Drik न चांदी न सोना), एक छोटा सा कोना (Drik छोटा सा कोना). Drik Panchang's fourth stanza (चरण शरण में खड़े तुम्हारी) and Aarti Mala's further stanzas are not in the book's version, she does not sing them, and they are not part of the text.

## Readings settled
- **aarti.tek** (तेरे ही गुन गायें भारती): भारती is Bhāratī, Sarasvatī, the goddess of speech (MW bhāratī, "speech; Sarasvatī"; the Hindī Śabdasāgara the same): the goddess of speech herself sings the Mother's praise. The people of Bhārata would be भारत or भारतवासी, not भारती. Meaning as given.
