# Reconciliation: ॐ जय हनुमत वीरा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), p. 116; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-115), read on the page image. Nine printed lines (the refrain and eight stanzas), each two halves joined by a comma and ending with the refrain cue ॥ॐ॥. Two letters are lost to damage on the page (stanza 3 स्·ीव, stanza 4 ल·क).
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/hanuman/balaji-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-balaji-aarti.html`): the same text, in the same order, a quarter-line per line, the refrain marked after each stanza.
- **Aarti Mala** (the same scriptures-data folder, page image i-320; a printout of indif.com): another version of the same āratī, in the same order, differing in several halves (below).

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (the book's comma between the halves of a line becomes a line break, a half per line); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| every line | one printed line, its halves joined by a comma | two lines, ending । and ॥ / ॥N॥ | The standard; the comma marks the half. |
| every line's end | ॥ॐ॥ | not written | The refrain cue; the refrain is shown once. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥८॥ | The standard. |
| stanza 3, line 1 | स्·ीव (damaged) | सुग्रीव | The page is damaged there; Drik Panchang and Aarti Mala have सुग्रीव. |
| stanza 4, line 1 | जारि ल·क (damaged) | जारि लंक | The same; Drik Panchang and Aarti Mala have लंक. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: पवन-पुत्र अंजनी-सुत (Drik पवन-पुत्र-अंजनी-सुत), मेंहदीपुर (Drik मेहंदीपुर). The rest is word for word as Drik Panchang, including जारि लंक को ले सिय की सुधि.

Aarti Mala's version differs in: दुःख दारिद्र्य मिटाओ संकट छय हारी (stanza 1), तुरतहिं छोड़ दियो (stanza 2), अभिमानी बलि मेट्यो कीर्ति रही छाई (stanza 3, second half), जारि लंक सिय-सुधि ले आए (stanza 4), लक्ष्मण को (stanza 5), रामहिं ले अहिरावण जब पाताल गयो (stanza 6), राजत मेहंदीपुर में दर्शन सुखकारी (stanza 7), आरति, वंछित (stanza 8). The book's readings are kept.
