# Reconciliation: ॐ जय श्रीश्याम हरे

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), p. 156, "आरती श्री श्याम जी की"; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-155), read on the page image. The refrain (ॐ जय श्रीश्याम हरे, प्रभु जय श्रीश्याम हरे। / निज भक्तन के तुमने पूरण काम करे॥) and six stanzas of two printed lines, each stanza's first line opening with the cue "हरि ॐ जय श्रीश्याम हरे,". Its order: गल पुष्पों, रत्नसिंहासन, मोदक खीर, झांझ नागारा, जो ध्यावे, श्रीश्याम बिहारीजी की आरती (with the poet's name, दासमनोहर).
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/khatu-shyam/khatu-shyam-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shyam-baba-aarti.html`): another version (below).

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The book prints another version than Drik Panchang, so the book's version is the text. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (the book's comma between the halves of a line becomes a line break, a half per line; a comma inside a half becomes a space); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| the start of every stanza | हरि ॐ जय श्रीश्याम हरे, | (not shown) | The refrain cue, printed before each stanza; the refrain is the first item (टेक). |
| every printed line | the halves joined by a comma | two lines | The standard; the comma marks the half. The refrain's second printed line has no comma; it is broken at its half (निज भक्तन के तुमने / पूरण काम करे) where Drik Panchang breaks the same line, so that the refrain has four half-lines like every stanza. |
| stanza 4, line 1 | झांझ, नागारा | झांझ नागारा | The standard: a pause inside a line is a space. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥६॥ | The standard. |
| stanza 6, line 4 | मन वान्छित | मन वांछित | न् before छ is not a Hindi spelling; the nasal is written ं. |

**Drik Panchang's version differs:** its refrain is ॐ जय श्री श्याम हरे, बाबा जय श्री श्याम हरे। खाटू धाम विराजत, अनुपम रूप धरे; its stanzas are रतन जड़ित सिंहासन (with सिर पर चंवर ढुरे and तन केसरिया बागो, कुण्डल श्रवण पड़े), गल पुष्पों की माला (with खेवत धूप अग्नि पर, दीपक ज्योति जले, and no पीत बसन line), मोदक खीर चूरमा (with सेवा नित्य करे), झांझ कटोरा और घड़ियावल (धुरे), जो ध्यावे फल पावे (सेवक जन), 'श्री श्याम बिहारीजी' की आरती (with कहत भक्त जन in place of the poet's name), and two stanzas the book lacks: तन मन धन सब कुछ है तेरा, and जय श्री श्याम हरे, बाबा जी श्री श्याम हरे। निज भक्तों के तुमने, पूरण काज करे (the book's refrain line 3 as a closing stanza). The book's रत्नसिंहासन राजत, सेवक भक्त खड़े and पीत बसन पीताम्बर are not in it.

Kept as the book has them, where Drik Panchang differs: श्रीश्याम joined, प्रभु (Drik बाबा), भक्तन, काम (Drik काज), मुकट (Drik मुकुट), कर्ण (Drik श्रवण), अगिन (Drik अग्नि), जरे (Drik जले), सुवर्ण (Drik सुवरण), नागारा (Drik कटोरा), घुरे (Drik धुरे; घुरना, to resound), गावें, जय जयकार (Drik जय-जयकार), सेवक जब (Drik सेवक जन), श्रीश्याम श्याम (Drik श्री श्याम-श्याम), दासमनोहर written joined.
