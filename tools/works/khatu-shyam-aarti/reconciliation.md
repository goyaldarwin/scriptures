## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), p. 156, "आरती श्री श्याम जी की"; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-155), read on the page image. The refrain (ॐ जय श्रीश्याम हरे, प्रभु जय श्रीश्याम हरे। / निज भक्तन के तुमने पूरण काम करे॥) and six stanzas of two printed lines, each stanza's first line opening with the cue "हरि ॐ जय श्रीश्याम हरे,". Its order: गल पुष्पों, रत्नसिंहासन, मोदक खीर, झांझ नागारा, जो ध्यावे, श्रीश्याम बिहारीजी की आरती (with the poet's name, दासमनोहर).
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/khatu-shyam/khatu-shyam-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shyam-baba-aarti.html`): another version, the one the recording sings: the text (below).

- **Bharat Discovery** (bharatdiscovery.org, श्यामबाबा जी की आरती, found by web search): Drik Panchang's version, with कहत मनोहर स्वामी for the poet's line and without the stanza तन मन धन; a check of that stanza only.

The rule: an āratī's text follows the version sung in its chosen recording (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3). Spiritual Activity sings Drik Panchang's version and order, so the text is Drik Panchang's, word for word as it spells it; the book (the base until then) is recorded below. The library's writing standard: an āratī's verse has no comma (a comma between the halves of a line becomes a line break, a half per line); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| after every stanza | ॐ जय श्री श्याम हरे॥ | (not shown) | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item (टेक). |
| every printed line | the halves joined by a comma | two lines | The standard. |
| stanza 4, line 1 | घड़ि़यावल (two nuktas on ड़) | घड़ियावल | A typing slip: ड़ takes one nukta. |
| the stanza तन मन धन सब कुछ है तेरा (its 8th) | printed | left out | Neither the book nor the recording has it, and Bharat Discovery's copy of the same version lacks it; its last line (क्या लोग मेरा) is also corrupt. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥७॥ | The standard. |

**The book's version** (the base before the recording was chosen; p. 156): its refrain is ॐ जय श्रीश्याम हरे, प्रभु जय श्रीश्याम हरे। निज भक्तन के तुमने पूरण काम करे (Drik Panchang's last stanza, जय श्री श्याम हरे … पूरण काज करे); its stanzas, in the order गल पुष्पों, रत्नसिंहासन, मोदक खीर, झांझ नागारा, जो ध्यावे, श्रीश्याम बिहारीजी की आरती, read पीत बसन पीताम्बर, कुण्डल कर्ण पड़े (stanza 1), रत्नसिंहासन राजत, सेवक भक्त खड़े, खेवत धूप अगिन पर, दीपक ज्योति जरे (stanza 2), सिर पर चंवर ढुरे (stanza 3), झांझ नागारा, घुरे, गावें (stanza 4), सेवक जब (stanza 5) and गावत दासमनोहर (the poet, Dāsa Manohara; stanza 6), and it has no खाटू धाम विराजत refrain.

The item ids keep the stanzas they name across the two versions (`aarti.pad2` is रतन जड़ित सिंहासन, now first; `aarti.pad1` गल पुष्पों, now second); `aarti.pad7` is Drik Panchang's closing stanza.
