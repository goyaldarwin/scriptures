# Bavan Akhri — reconciliation

The text is Sri Guru Granth Sahib's, taken unchanged (`text.txt` → `[take guru-granth-sahib 250.1 .. 262.1]`). Its
witnesses and every choice are in `tools/works/guru-granth-sahib/reconciliation.md`; a correction is made there and
reaches this work at the next export.

## Where it begins and ends

Checked against the Granth's data (`library/sikhism/gurbani/guru-granth-sahib/work.json`, made from `text.txt`), not
written from memory:

- **First verse** `250.1` (Ang 250), its first lines: ੴ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ ॥ / ਗਉੜੀ ਬਾਵਨ ਅਖਰੀ ਮਹਲਾ ੫ ॥ / ਸਲੋਕੁ ॥
- **Last verse** `262.1` (Ang 262), its last line: ਏਹੁ ਸਲੋਕੁ ਆਦਿ ਅੰਤਿ ਪੜਣਾ ॥
- The verse before it in the Granth ends ਵਰੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਅੰਤਰਜਾਮੀ ਨਾਨਕ ਸੋਹਾਗੁ ਨ ਟਲਿਆ ॥੪॥੪॥੨॥੫॥੧੧॥ and the verse after it begins
  ਗਉੜੀ ਸੁਖਮਨੀ ਮਃ ੫ ॥: neither is part of the bani.
- 57 saloks, 55 pauris; Ang 250–262, as the Granth's Ang view gives the pages of its first and last lines.
