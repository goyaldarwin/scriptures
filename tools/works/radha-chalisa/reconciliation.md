# Reconciliation: श्री राधा चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 221–224; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-220 … i-223), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/radha/radha-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-radha-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text (two opening dohās, 40 chaupāīs, one closing dohā), in the same order.
- **Chalisa Mala** (the indif.com printout, page images i-431 … i-433): a printout of the same web text as Drik Panchang; not a separate witness.
- **For a doubtful reading only:** Shubh Panchang (shubhpanchang.com, Radha Chalisa) and Aaj Tak (aajtak.in, Shri Radha Chalisa). Sites that copy one another count as one witness.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| opening dohā 1 | वृन्दावनविपिन विहारिणि | वृन्दाविपिन विहारिणि | वन and विपिन both mean "forest", and the extra syllables overfill the dohā's half; the book itself has वृन्दाविपिन स्वामिनी राधा in chaupāī 30, as Drik Panchang and Shubh Panchang have here. |
| chaupāī 1 | जय वृषभान कुँवरि | जय वृषभानु कुँवरि | The chaupāī needs 16 mātrās and has 15 without the u (a dropped vowel sign); Drik Panchang and Aaj Tak वृषभानु, as the book itself in chaupāīs 9 and 40. |
| chaupāīs 8–9 | मुरली में नित नाम उचारे, तुव कारण प्रिया वृषभानु दुलारी (one line) | मुरली में नित नाम उचारे । तुव कारण लीला वपु धारें ॥ / प्रेम स्वरूपिणि अति सुकुमारी । श्याम प्रिया वृषभानु दुलारी ॥ | The printer's eye skipped from तुव कारण to the next line's प्रिया, losing "लीला वपु धारें। प्रेम स्वरूपिणि अति सुकुमारी, श्याम": the line does not rhyme (उचारे / दुलारी), makes no sense and has 20 mātrās in its second half, and the book has only 39 chaupāīs. The lost words are Drik Panchang's (Shubh Panchang has the प्रेम स्वरूपिणि chaupāī too). |
| chaupāī 11 | शशि निंदक बढ़ना | शशि निंदक बदना | बढ़ना ("to grow") is a misprint for बदना, the face that shames the moon, rhyming with नयना; Drik Panchang and Shubh Panchang बदना. |
| chaupāī 12 | जावक युग युग पंकज चरना | जावक युत युग पंकज चरना | युग printed twice: the two (युग) lotus feet are "with" (युत) lac-dye; Drik Panchang, Shubh Panchang and Aaj Tak युत. |
| chaupāī 32 | सारद गान करत नित वेदा | सादर गान करत नित वेदा | सारद is सादर with its letters swapped: "the Vedas ever sing it with reverence" (one subject, करत); Drik Panchang, Shubh Panchang and Aaj Tak सादर. |
| chaupāī 33 | कृष्ण को भेजिहैं | कृष्ण को भजिहैं | भेजिहैं ("will send") is a misprint for भजिहैं ("will worship"), which the sense needs and rhymes with तरिहैं; Drik Panchang and Shubh Panchang भजिहैं. |
| chaupāī 40 | शरण तिहारौ | शरण तिहारी | The rhyme with दुलारी needs तिहारी (शरण is feminine here); Drik Panchang, Shubh Panchang and Aaj Tak तिहारी. |
| closing dohā | श्रीराधासर्वेश्वरी | श्रीराधा सर्वेश्वरी | A missing space: two words in apposition, "Śrī Rādhā, the queen of all" (Drik Panchang श्रीराधा सर्वेश्वरी). |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: प्रणवों; नंदिनि; बिहारिनि (chaupāīs 2, 6, 27), विस्तारिनी, उमंगिनि, विहारिनी (17), विहारिन (39); गावें / हरषावें; उचारे; निशदिन; नित्यधाम; पायें (Drik पाँइ); बनवारी; देई; बिहरत नित्य वृन्दाबन माँही (Drik नित वृन्दावन); नन्दा; दुख; भाँति; न गावे; लीला बपु (chaupāī 30); तरिहैं; भव बाधा; मन भावन; कोउ.

## Meanings

## Readings settled
- chalisa.cau40 — वृन्दावन है शरण तिहारी: changed, Vṛndāvana is in your refuge (was "is your refuge"). In Hindi "X आपकी शरण है" says X is in your refuge (मैं आपकी शरण हूँ), and Rādhā is the one who shelters; the book's two printings and Drik Panchang agree on the words.
