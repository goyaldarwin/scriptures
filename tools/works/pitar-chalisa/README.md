# श्री पितर चालीसा (Shri Pitar Chalisa)

The chālīsā of the Pitaras (the ancestors) that opens "हे पितरेश्वर आपको", in Awadhi (`awa`), with Rajasthani words (मनावा, चाया, धोक, जात जडूला); it praises the ancestors' shrine at Jhunjhunu.

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 162–166), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/plus-bhakti/`: Plus Bhakti (YouTube `WpNJv_iLX00`, "पितृपक्ष में नित्य सुनिए पितर चालीसा. श्री पित्र चालीसा Shri Pitar chalisa. पितृदोष मुक्ति."), its track 0:28–4:10 of the video (before 0:28: a spoken introduction; after 4:10: spoken words). Its starts come from the transcript for now; MMS (`tools/audio/METHOD.md`) is still to time it. A draft (it plays only with Audio timing on): its starts are still to be timed by the forced aligner (MMS): the transcript alone leaves verses it sings untimed or guessed.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohās (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohās (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 40 chaupāīs are numbered 1–40 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the five dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha"). The second opening "dohā" is longer than a dohā (its lines end in जी); the book prints it under the दोहा heading, and it is typed so.
- **Ids:** as the Gaṇeśa Chālīsā's (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** what the ancestors give, the shrine at Jhunjhunu, the ancestors of all, the prayer. **Reading plans:** none.
