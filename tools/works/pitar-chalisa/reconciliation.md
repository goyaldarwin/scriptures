# Reconciliation: श्री पितर चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 162–166; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-161 … i-165; the last closing dohā is on i-165, above the Pitar āratī), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/pitar/pitar-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-pitar-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, verse for verse.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept (the first opening dohā's first line ends in a comma in the book: here `।`).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 8 | झंझुनू ने दरबार है साजे, सब देवो संग आप विराजे | झुंझुनू में दरबार है साजे । सब देवों संग आप विराजे | Three misprints: the town is झुंझुनू (Jhunjhunu), as the book itself spells it in the closing dohās; ने makes no sense ("the court is set up *in* Jhunjhunu"); देवो lacks the plural's nasal (देवों). Drik Panchang has all three. |
| chaupāī 22 | गंगा ये मरूप्रदेश की | गंगा ये मरुप्रदेश की | मरु ("desert") has a short u, as Drik Panchang has it. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: संदेश (chaupāī 18; Drik सन्देश). The spellings पित्तर / पितर / पित्तरेश्वर, योणि, जै-जै-जै, नाहिं, गुणगावे, बिराजे / विराजे, अँजुलि, चरणाँ, अवसी, कीजै / दीजै, पित्तरौं and चाहिए are the book's, as Drik's.
