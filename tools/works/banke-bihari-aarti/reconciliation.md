# Reconciliation: श्री बाँकेबिहारी तेरी आरती गाऊँ

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** Shri Banke Bihari Ki Aarti (`hinduism/aarti/krishna/banke-bihari-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-banke-bihari-aarti.html`): the refrain (three lines) and five stanzas of three lines, with the refrain cue "श्री बाँकेबिहारी तेरी आरती गाऊँ" after every stanza.
- **The book:** none. The āratī is not in ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (`hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/`) or the other booklets; Drik Panchang is the only witness.

The rule: keep Drik Panchang; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): each printed line is a line, ending । and the stanza's last ॥N॥; the refrain (टेक) is shown once.

| Place | Base | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| after every stanza | श्री बाँकेबिहारी तेरी आरती गाऊँ॥ (refrain cue) | (not shown) | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item (टेक). |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥५॥ | The standard. |
| refrain, line 2 | कुन्जबिहारी | कुञ्जबिहारी | Drik Panchang's typing of the nasal as न् before ज; the word is कुञ्ज, written with the class nasal. |

The last stanza (आरती गाऊँ प्यारे तुमको रिझाऊँ) is a stanza of its own, not the refrain repeated: only its last line is the refrain's third line.

Kept as the base has them: बाँकेबिहारी, शीश पे, बंशी, मेरो, देखि, दुःख, श्री हरि दास (two words, as printed), बलि-बलि, गिरिधर.
