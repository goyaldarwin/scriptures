# Reconciliation: श्री महालक्ष्मी चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 187–190; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-186 … i-189), read on the page images. The āratī printed after it on p. 190 (आरती श्री लक्ष्मी (महालक्ष्मी) जी की) is its own work.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/lakshmi/mahalakshmi-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/mahalakshmi-mata-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, with its own slips (किजिये, अद्भूत, ध्यावै से, बिमल for बिलम, जानीमोहीं).

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept, and a space follows ॐ where the book sets it close to the syllable after it (ॐह्रीं, ॐक्लीं).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 6 | गल सौहे मुक्तन की माला | गल सोहे मुक्तन की माला | सौहे is a misprint of सोहे ("shines"), as the next chaupāī has it and Drik Panchang has it. |
| chaupāī 7 | विमल नयन अरू अनुपम भेषा | विमल नयन अरु अनुपम भेषा | अरू is a spelling slip for अरु ("and"), as the book itself has it in chaupāī 5 and Drik Panchang here. |
| chaupāī 18 | पल में राजााहि रंक बनाओ | पल में राजाहि रंक बनाओ | A doubled vowel sign (ाा), a misprint; Drik Panchang has राजाहि. |
| chaupāī 36 | पूरण आशा करहु अब मेरी | पूरण आशा करहु अब मोरी | The rhyme with जोरी needs मोरी ("my"), as Drik Panchang has it; मेरी is the printer's modern form. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: कीजिए (Drik किजिये), भण्डारन, अरु श्वेता भूषन / श्वेतहि (Drik अरू … भूषण / श्वेतही), सुन्दर, तवचारी, अद्भुत, तवबानी, नारि, बिलोक, तव यश … पाया (Drik तब … पाये; पाया rhymes with गाया), सोई, बिलम न लाओ (Drik बिमल; बिलम, "delay", is the sense), सो बहु सुख पावै, रहै, निजजन जानिमोहिं, ब्याधिहटाओ, बिमल दृष्टि, जानूँ, पार करौ भवनिध बन खेवा (Drik करो … वन). The book's short half-line "ॐ जयजयति जयजननी" is the book's, Drik Panchang the same.

## Readings settled
- chalisa.cau40 — गोविंद: kept as the words say, Govinda as the one in her refuge night and day ("Govinda is in your refuge"). The closing chaupāī is where a Chālīsā signs its poet, and the line goes on to हमारी अभिलाष; Viṣṇu as one sheltering in Lakṣmī's refuge would be odd. The meaning names no more than the verse.
- chalisa.cau34 — हृदय निवास भक्त बर दीजै: one request: "grant your devotee the boon of your dwelling in his heart". There is one verb (दीजै) and हृदय निवास stands before बर as what the boon is; "dwell in my heart" would need a verb of its own (कीजै).
