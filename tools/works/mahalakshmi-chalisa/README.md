# श्री महालक्ष्मी चालीसा (Shri Mahalakshmi Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Mahālakṣmī that opens "जय जय श्री महालक्ष्मी", in Awadhi (`awa`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 187–190), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/anuradha-paudwal/`: Anuradha Paudwal (YouTube `otWnO-2pwuM`, "Lakshmi Chalisa By Anuradha Paudwal I Sampoorna Mahalakshmi Poojan"), its track 0:00–9:46 of the video (after 9:46: music). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list.

The last chaupāī names the poet: "गोविंद निशदिन शरण तिहारी" (Govinda).

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohā (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohā (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 40 chaupāīs are numbered 1–40 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the two dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Hanumān Chālīsā's in the Sundarakāṇḍa (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** Mahālakṣmī's form, creation and her glory, praise with the sacred syllables, the prayer and the fruit of worship. **Reading plans:** none.
