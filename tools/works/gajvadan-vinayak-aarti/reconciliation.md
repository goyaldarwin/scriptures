# Reconciliation: आरती गजबदन विनायक की

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** `aarti/shree-ganesh/gajavadan-vinayaka-aarti` (`hinduism/aarti/ganesha/gajvadan-vinayak-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/gajavadan-vinayaka-aarti.html`): the only witness. The book (५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित, Randhir Prakashan; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/`) does not print this āratī. The page prints the refrain twice at the start and its first line after every stanza.

The rule: keep Drik Panchang's text; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (the page's commas at line ends and inside lines are dropped); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| the refrain | printed twice at the start, and its first line after every stanza | once, first | The standard (the refrain is shown once). |
| stanza 2 | विद्या-विनय-विभव-दायककी | विद्या-विनय-विभव-दायक की | A missing space: every other stanza and the refrain end "… -दायक की", "… लायक की" with की apart. |
| stanza 3 | पिङ्गलनयन, विशाल शुण्डधर | पिङ्गलनयन विशाल शुण्डधर | The standard: no comma in an āratī's verse. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥३॥ | The standard. |

Kept as the page has them: गजबदन, सुर-मुनि-पूजित, वन्द्यमान-चतुरानन, सुविमल-मति, अबिगत, पिङ्गलनयन, वज्रांकुश-कर, and the page's hyphens.

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
The readings the words alone leave open, each decided (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §2).

- **aarti.pad3** — वज्रांकुश-कर: a dvandva, "with the thunderbolt and the goad in his hands". MW knows वज्राङ्कुश only as a mountain's name, so no compound "adamantine goad" is attested, and a pair joined in one compound with -कर ("in whose hands") is the plain reading. Meaning unchanged.
