# Reconciliation: श्रीहनुमानचालीसा

The text is the Sundarakāṇḍa work's Chālīsā, taken unchanged; its witnesses and every choice are in `tools/works/sundarkand/reconciliation.md`. Drik Panchang's page (scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/hanuman/hanuman-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/`) and the book's (५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित, Randhir Prakashan, images i-021 … i-024) are kept there as further witnesses; a reading they prove better is corrected in the Sundarakāṇḍa work, so it reaches both.

**Where the book's words differ** (images i-021 to i-024, read on the images): its दोहा 2 has देऊ (ours देहु), chaupāī 33 भावै (ours पावै), chaupāī 37 जय जय जय (ours जै जै जै), chaupāī 38 शत … छूटहिं (ours सत … छूटहि), besides spellings of its own (गुरू, बरनऊँ, बज्र, गोसाँई). The text stays the Sundarakāṇḍa's: its sources (Gita Press and the editions in `tools/works/sundarkand/reconciliation.md`) read so, Drik Panchang agrees on देहु and पावै, जै and सत are the Mānas's spellings, and छूटहि keeps the chaupāī's sixteen mātrās; none of the book's words shows ours wrong.

## Meanings
The Sundarakāṇḍa's own (taken with the verses).
