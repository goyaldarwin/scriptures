# Reconciliation: श्रीहनुमानचालीसा

The text is the Sundarakāṇḍa work's Chālīsā, taken unchanged; its witnesses and every choice are in `tools/works/sundarkand/reconciliation.md`. Drik Panchang's page (scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/hanuman/hanuman-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/`) and the book's (५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित, Randhir Prakashan, image i-022 …) are kept there as further witnesses; a reading they prove better is corrected in the Sundarakāṇḍa work, so it reaches both.

## Meanings
The Sundarakāṇḍa's own (taken with the verses).
