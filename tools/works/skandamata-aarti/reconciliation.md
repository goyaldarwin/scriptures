# Reconciliation: जय तेरी हो स्कन्द माता

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** "Skandamata Mata Aarti" (`hinduism/aarti/durga/skandamata-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/devi-skandamata-aarti.html`), headed आरती देवी स्कन्दमाता जी की. Five groups of four printed lines, no refrain marked.
- The book (५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित) and the other booklets have no copy of this āratī; Drik Panchang is the only witness.
- **For a doubtful reading only:** the āratī as DNA Hindi (dnaindia.com/hindi/spiritual/report-5-day-of-chaitra-navratri-worship-goddess-skandamata-know-mantra-aarti-offering-katha-and-puja-vidhi-4156424) and Boldsky (boldsky.com/yoga-spirituality/chaitra-navratri-2024-day-5-maa-skandmata-puja-vidhi-katha-mantra-and-aarti-155047.html) print it, found by web search. Bhakti Bharat prints Drik Panchang's text and is not counted.

The rule: keep the base; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; stanzas numbered (॥१॥). The page marks no refrain, so none is made: the text is five stanzas.

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| stanza 2, line 2 | ध्याता रहूँ मै | ध्याता रहूँ मैं | मै is मैं with its anusvāra dropped: the line rhymes with the one before (जलाता रहूँ मैं); DNA Hindi has मैं. |
| stanza 3, line 1 | कही पहाड़ों पर है डेरा | कहीं पहाड़ों पर है डेरा | The sense is "somewhere on the mountains is your dwelling" (कहीं), beside कई शहरों in the next line; कही ("said") does not construe. Boldsky has कहीं. |
| stanza 5, line 2 | तू ही खण्ड हाथ उठाये | तू ही खण्डा हाथ उठाये | खण्ड ("a piece") gives no sense and leaves the line a mātrā short of 16; खण्डा is the sword she raises against the demons. DNA Hindi and Boldsky both have खंडा (written here as the base writes the nasal, ण्ड). |
| line ends | । and ॥ alternating | । and, at a stanza's end, ॥N॥ | The standard. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥५॥ | The standard. |

Kept as the base has them: स्कन्द माता, पाँचवाँ, जानन हारी, महतारी, जोत, नजारे, गुण गाये, बिगड़ी, करे पुकार, तू ही (DNA Hindi तुम ही), चढ़ कर, आये, उठाये, आयी, पुजाने.

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
- **aarti.pad4** (शक्ति मेरी बिगड़ी बना दो): शक्ति is the vocative, "O Śakti", and मेरी बिगड़ी बना दो the set phrase "set right what has gone wrong for me" (बिगड़ी बनाना); "make good my failing strength" would need बिगड़ी शक्ति. Meaning unchanged.
