# Reconciliation: श्री बालाजी चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 112–115; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-111 … i-114), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/hanuman/balaji-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-balaji-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, the same 40 chaupāīs in the same order, with its own slips (उध्दार, the commas splitting the opening dohās after चितलाय and दानी).

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| opening dohā 2 | …बालाजी भगवान। | …बालाजी भगवान ॥ | The standard: a dohā's second line ends ॥ (the book ends it with a single daṇḍa). |
| chaupāī 40 | कल्याण स्नेही, स्नेह से गावे | कल्याण स्नेही स्नेह से गावे | The comma inside the half is not kept. |

No word of the book is changed. Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: सब भग जाते (chaupāī 6; Drik भाग), मेवा अरू मिष्ठान (chaupāī 30; Drik अरु), and the opening dohās' halves (Drik splits the first after चितलाय, putting के in the second half). Otherwise the two texts agree word for word (Drik's उध्दार is a typing of उद्धार).
