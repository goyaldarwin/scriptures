# Reconciliation: सुन मेरी देवी पर्वतवासिनि

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), p. 182, under the heading "आरती श्री विन्ध्येश्वरी देवी जी की"; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-181), read on the page image. The refrain line (marked ॥ टेक ॥), then the line पान सुपारी (ending ॥), then three couplets (सुवा चोली, ऊँचे ऊँचे पर्वत, धूप दीप), each a line ending । and an indented line ending ॥. The four lines above the heading close the Vindhyācala Chālīsā; they are not part of the āratī.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/vindhyeshwari/vindhyeshwari-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/vindhyeshwari-mata-aarti.html`): the same lines in the same order, grouped as four stanzas (the refrain line and पान सुपारी as one), with a refrain cue "जय विन्ध्येश्वरी माता" after each, which the book does not print.
- **Aarti Mala** (page image i-334, an indif.com printout): the same version, one line per printed line, the first marked टेक and each other line followed by the cue "सुन०"; its own spellings (पर्वतवासिनी, कोई तेरा पार, नंगे पग मां अकबर आया, तेरे गुण गाया).

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (the book's comma between the halves of a line becomes a line break, a half per line); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| refrain | सुन मेरी देवी पर्वतवासिनि, तेरा पार न पाया ॥ टेक ॥ | the line, as `aarti.tek` | The book marks this line as the refrain (Aarti Mala too, whose cue after every line is सुन०); the mark is not written. |
| stanza 1 | पान सुपारी … चढ़ाया ॥ (a line of its own) | stanza 1, two lines | The book ends it with ॥ as its own unit after the refrain, and Aarti Mala gives it its own cue; Drik Panchang prints it with the refrain line as one group. |
| every line | the halves joined by a comma | two lines | The standard; the comma marks the half. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥४॥ | The standard. |
| stanza 4, line 3 | ध्यानू भगत मैया (तेरा) गुण गावैं | ध्यानू भगत मैया गुण गावैं | A word the page offers in brackets is not part of the printed line; the base reading is kept. Drik Panchang prints the same bracket; Aarti Mala has मैया तेरे गुण गाया. |
| refrain cue | (none) | (none) | Drik Panchang's cue "जय विन्ध्येश्वरी माता" is not in the book; the refrain is the first item. |

Kept as the book has them, where Drik Panchang differs: नंगे पांव तेरे अकबर जाकर (Drik without तेरे); every other word is as Drik Panchang has it. Both have सत्युग and कलयुग; neither is clearly wrong in this register, so they stay.

## Readings settled
- **aarti.pad3** — सत्युग त्रेता द्वापर मध्ये कलयुग राज सवाया: both books (Randhir and Aarti Mala) and Drik Panchang print it so. सवाया "one and a quarter, greater by a quarter" (Platts sawāyā = sawaiyā): "your reign stood through the Satya, Tretā and Dvāpara ages, and in the Kali age it is greater by a quarter." Meaning unchanged.
