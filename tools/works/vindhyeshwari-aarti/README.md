# सुन मेरी देवी पर्वतवासिनि (Sun Meri Devi Parvatvasini)

The āratī of Vindhyeśvarī Devī, in Hindi (`hi`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, p. 182), checked against Drik Panchang and Aarti Mala. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/vinod-sain-sanjo-baghel/`: Vinod Sain, Sanjo Baghel (YouTube `-40UYGEc1dY`, "Sun Meri Devi पर्वत वासिनी || बुन्देली देवी माता गीत || Vinod Sain (Bindu), Sanjo Baghel"). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list. A draft (it plays only with Audio timing on): a folk rendering with lines no written witness has.

The closing stanza names its singer: ध्यानू भगत (the devotee Dhyanu).

Verse types: टेक (refrain, `tek`, shown once at the start although it is sung after every stanza) and पद (stanza, `pad`). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (Sun Meri Devi Parvatvasini) › one section (`aarti`) › verses: the refrain (`aarti.tek`, type `tek`) and 4 stanzas.
- **Numbering:** each stanza's `n` is its number (॥१॥ …), and it is cited by it ("Stanza 3"); the refrain has none.
- **Topics:** one, the āratī's title. **Reading plans:** none.
