# The Hindi of the Śiva Purāṇa, from the owner's scans

The scans (Gita Press code 2223; Kalyan's 2018 Śivamahāpurāṇāṅka) have no text layer. The owner's choice for them: the Hindi is read by OCR, and every word the checks flag is read by eye on the page image before it is used (`docs/PLAYBOOK.md` §11 stays the rule for every other book; the scans' `.meta.json` in `scriptures-data` say so too).

- `ocr.py`: the raw passes of one page range → `ocr/<book>/p<NNNN>.json` (the gutter; per Hindi column Tesseract's `hin` and `Devanagari` passes, the second with each em dash told from a hyphen by its ink; a whole-page `hin` pass for the lines that run across both columns). Run on GitHub by the `ocr` workflow (`docs/HOW-TO.md`, "OCR a scanned book"), or here.
- `ocr/shiv-1/`: Gita Press code 2223 (layout `sh`: Sanskrit left, Hindi right). `ocr/kalyan-2018/`: Kalyan 2018 (layout `hh`: Hindi in both columns).
- `merge.py`: the two passes merged word by word and walked adhyāya by adhyāya → `<book>.merged.json` (each verse mark's paragraph, with every doubtful word in ⟦…⟧), `<book>.flags.jsonl` (one line per word to read by eye) and `<book>.chosen.jsonl` (the words taken by the one-real-word rule, for audit). In Kalyan an adhyāya's closing line runs across both columns: `ocr.py` reads the page in bands split at such lines (the column rule, fitted down a leaning page, is broken off there), and `merge.py` takes each closing line's number from the whole-page pass; a closing line names its adhyāya, so the walk follows it (a misread number still closes the adhyāya it stands in).
- `notes.py`: each page's footnotes (from the whole-page pass) and the column lines they take → `<book>.notes.json`; `merge.py` leaves those lines out.
- `review.py`: `prepare` cuts each flagged line from the page onto sheets, a dozen to a sheet, for reading by eye (`redo`: the lines read as cut off, again with wide margins); `apply` gathers the readers' answers by printed place → `<book>.read.json` (with `doubt.json`, `extra.json`); `lines` sets right, from the readers' whole lines, a word both passes misread alike → `<book>.lines.json`.
- `kalyan-2018.proof.json`: Kalyan paragraphs proofread whole against the page — each adhyāya's first three and its last, and every paragraph a heading ran into (Kalyan sets an adhyāya's heading and subtitle inside a column band).
- `<book>.full.json`: every paragraph proofread against its page: the changes (`edits`, each as the page has it), paragraphs given whole (`set`), and paragraphs the adhyāya walk missed (`add`).
- `make_hindi.py`: → `meanings/hi/gitapress.json`: the flagged words as read, the lines' fixes, an adhyāya's first paragraph cut to its speaker's words, heading words taken out, the proofread paragraphs, `corrections.json`, the book's typography (curly quotes, one em dash, the speaker's dash, ½), a paragraph split at a verse mark inside it. Then the full proofreading (`<book>.full.json`: edits, whole paragraphs, added paragraphs, and moves to the mark's last verse). A paragraph with a word still in doubt is left out.
