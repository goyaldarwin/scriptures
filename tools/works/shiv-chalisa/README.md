# श्री शिव चालीसा (Shri Shiv Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Śiva that opens "जय गणेश गिरिजा सुवन", in Awadhi (`awa`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 17–20), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** none yet.
- **Poet and date:** the text names its poet, Ayodhyādāsa (opening dohā, last chaupāī), and its date in the last closing dohā: the sixth of Mārgaśīrṣa (मगसर छठि), in the Hemanta season, saṁvat "चौंसठ" (…64).

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohā (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohās (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 41 chaupāīs are numbered 1–41 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the three dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Hanumān Chālīsā's in the Sundarakāṇḍa (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** Śiva's form, his deeds, the prayer, the fruit of the reading. **Reading plans:** none.
