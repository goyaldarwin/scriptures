# Reconciliation: श्री शिव चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 17–20; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-016 … i-019), read on the page images. The first letter of षडानन (chaupāī 11) is broken in the print; its remains and Drik Panchang read षडानन.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/shiva/shiv-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shiva-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, the same 41 chaupāīs in the same order, with many small differences of wording (below).

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas (and the full stop that stands for one in chaupāī 40) are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 5 | मैना मातु कि हवे दुलारी | मैना मातु की हवे दुलारी | The long ī of the postposition is dropped: "the darling of mother Mainā" needs की, as Drik Panchang has; कि ("that") makes no sense. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: गिरजापति दीनदयाला, छार लगाये (Drik क्षार लगाए), छवि को देख नाग मुनि मोहे (Drik देखि नाग मन मोहे), वाम, सोहैं, तबहीं दुःख (Drik तब ही दुख), लव निमेष, गिरायऊ (Drik गिरायउ), कोई नाहिं (Drik कोउ नाहीं), अस्तुति (Drik स्तुति, twice), वेद नाम महिमा तव गाई (Drik वेद माहि महिमा तुम गाई), प्रगटी, जरे सुरासुर भये विहाला (Drik जरत … भए), कीन्हीं दया तहँ, राखे जोई (Drik राखेउ), चहँ, जै जै जै अनन्त अविनासी (Drik जय जय जय … अविनाशी), सबकी घटवासी (Drik सब के), यहि अवसर, संकट से (Drik ते), मातु पिता भ्राता सब कोई (Drik मात-पिता … सब होई), नहीं, सदाहीं, जाँचे वो फल (Drik जांचे सो), करों तिहारी (Drik करैं तुम्हारी), योगि, नारद शारद (Drik शारद नारद), नमो नमो जय नमो शिवाये / पाए (Drik नमः शिवाय / पाय; the book's rhyme), तापर होत हैं, ऋनिया, पुत्रहीन इच्छा कर कोई (Drik पुत्र होन कर इच्छा जोई), पंडित, करे, तन नहिं ताके रहे कलेशा (Drik ताके तन नहीं रहै), अन्त वास शिवपुर (Drik धाम), कहै अयोध्या आस तुम्हारी (Drik कहैं अयोध्यादास; the book's half is a full sixteen mātrās, Drik's is not), नित्त नेम कर प्रातः ही पाठ करौं चालीस (Drik उठि … करो चालीसा), मगसर, संवत् चौंसठ (Drik संवत चौसठ).

## Recording
The chosen recording is BhaktiDhaam's "सम्पूर्ण चालीसा संग्रह" (YouTube `lCwPg0LRRjk`, the Śiva Chālīsā from 0:00 to 6:31). It sings the book's text: where the book and Drik Panchang differ, it sings the book's reading (नाग मुनि मोहे, वेद नाम महिमा तव गाई, जरे सुरासुर, संकट से, सब कोई, जाँचे वो फल, नारद शारद, नित्त नेम कर … चालीस, मगसर), so nothing is changed. In chaupāī 37 it is heard as ध्यान पूर्वक हवन करावे, a word no witness has; the book's होम is kept. Chaupāī 41 (कहै अयोध्या आस तुम्हारी …) is not sung: it stays, untimed.

## Readings settled
- **chalisa.cau8** (कार्तिक श्याम और गणराऊ): श्याम as the adjective of Kārttikeya, as our meaning has it: it follows his name directly and और joins only two figures; no figure Śyāma stands in Śiva's household.
- **chalisa.cau35** (ऋनिया जो कोई हो अधिकारी । पाठ करे सो पावन हारी): ऋनिया 'debtor' (Platts: riniyā), अधिकारी 'one fit for it', पावन हारी the agent noun of पाना (-हार/-हारा, Platts), 'one who obtains': the debtor gains release from the debt, as our meaning has it, the object taken from ऋनिया, as the next chaupāī gives the sonless man a son.
- **chalisa.do3** (संवत् चौंसठ; कल्याण): 'saṁvat sixty-four', no century added (the words give none), and कल्याण 'for blessing', as our meaning has it: no source names a poet or patron Kalyāṇa for this chālīsā.
