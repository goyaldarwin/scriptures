# ॐ जय परशुधारी (Om Jai Parshudhari)

The āratī of Paraśurāma, in Hindi (`hi`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 150–151), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/supertone-digital/`: Supertone Digital (YouTube `TkZjHxhDtA8`, "परशुराम जयंती स्पेशल 2026 - भगवान परशुराम जी की आरती Bhagwan Parshuram Ji Ki Aarti - ॐ जय परशुधारी"), its track 0:00–5:24 of the video (after 5:24: music only, about 4 s, to the end of the recording). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list.
- **Poet:** the last stanza names पूर्णेन्दु (Purnendu).

Verse types: टेक (refrain, `tek`, shown once at the start although it is sung after every stanza) and पद (stanza, `pad`). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (Om Jai Parshudhari) › one section (`aarti`) › verses: the refrain (`aarti.tek`, type `tek`) and 7 stanzas.
- **Numbering:** each stanza's `n` is its number (॥१॥ …), and it is cited by it ("Stanza 3"); the refrain has none.
- **Topics:** one, the āratī's title. **Reading plans:** none.
