# Reconciliation: ॐ जय परशुधारी

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), pp. 150–151; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-149, i-150), read on the page images. The āratī starts under its heading "आरती श्री परशुराम जी की" on p. 150; the दोहा and श्लोक above it close the Paraśurāma Chālīsā and are not part of the āratī. Its order: the refrain, जमदग्नी सुत, कांधे सूत्र, ताम्र श्याम, मुख रवि, कर शोभित, माता पिता, and the closing stanza with the poet's name (पूर्णेन्दु).
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/parashurama/parashuram-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-parashurama-aarti.html`): the same text, word for word, in the same order.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (the book's comma between the halves of a line becomes a line break, a half per line); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| every stanza | two printed lines, the halves of each joined by a comma | four lines, ending । and ॥N॥ | The standard; the comma marks the half. |
| after every stanza | ॐ जय'' | (not shown) | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item (टेक). |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥७॥ | The standard. |
| stanza 7 | 'पूर्णेन्दु' शिव साखि | पूर्णेन्दु शिव साखि | The quotation marks are the printer's, not part of the verse. |

Kept as the book has them (Drik Panchang agrees throughout): जमदग्नी, नर-सिंह, मां, खड़ाऊँ, ताम्र, बांधी, आंधी, विप्रन, बर परशु, कंध चाप-शर, बिरद संभारो, सम्पति.
