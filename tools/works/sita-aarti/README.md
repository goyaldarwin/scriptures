# आरती श्री जनक दुलारी की (Aarti Shri Janak Dulari Ki)

The āratī of Sītā, Janaka's beloved daughter, in Hindi (`hi`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): Drik Panchang, "Sita Mata Aarti" (no copy in the owner's books). Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/spiritual-activity/`: Spiritual Activity (YouTube `NbYKO1-CjAQ`, "सीता नवमी स्पेशल- Aarti Shri Janak Dulari Ki | श्री सीता आरती | Sita Aarti With Lyrics | Sita Navami"), its track 0:00–4:42 of the video (after 4:42: music). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list.

Verse types: टेक (refrain, `tek`, shown once at the start although it is sung after every stanza) and पद (stanza, `pad`). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (Aarti Shri Janak Dulari Ki) › one section (`aarti`) › verses: the refrain (`aarti.tek`, type `tek`) and 3 stanzas.
- **Numbering:** each stanza's `n` is its number (॥१॥ …), and it is cited by it ("Stanza 3"); the refrain has none.
- **Topics:** one, the āratī's title. **Reading plans:** none.
