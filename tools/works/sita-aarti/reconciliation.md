# Reconciliation: आरती श्री जनक दुलारी की

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base)** (`hinduism/aarti/sita/sita-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/sita-mata-aarti.html`): the refrain couplet (printed twice), then three stanzas of four lines, the refrain marked after each. The owner's books have no copy of this āratī, so the page is the only printed witness.
- **For a doubtful reading only** (stanzas 1 and 2), the text as other sites print it, found by web search: Amar Ujala (amarujala.com, "Sita Navami 2020: Mata Sita aarti lyrics in Hindi") and mantra.tips (Sita Aarti).

The rule: keep the base; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Base | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| the refrain | printed twice at the start, and after each stanza | once (`aarti.tek`) | The standard: the repeat is not written. |
| every stanza | lines ending with a comma, the last ॥ | । and ॥N॥ | The standard. |
| stanza 1, line 3 | दीनोद्वारिणि | दीनोद्धारिणि | द्व for द्ध is a typing slip: दीन + उद्धारिणि, "she who uplifts the lowly" (उद्धार); Amar Ujala has दीनोद्धारिणि (mantra.tips दीनोद्धारिणी). |
| stanza 2, line 1 | सती श्रोमणि | सती शिरोमणि | श्रोमणि is not a word: शिरोमणि, "crest-jewel", which the metre (16 mātrās, as its neighbours) needs; Amar Ujala and mantra.tips have शिरोमणि. |

Kept as the base has them: सीताजी, विस्तारिणि, साकेत-विहारिणि, दयामयी, भक्तन, हित कारिणि, वन-वन चारिणि, स्वीकारिणि, मूरति, लोकन, दुखदाई, भय-हारी.
