# मंगल मूरति जय जय हनुमन्ता (Mangal Murti Jai Jai Hanumanta)

The āratī of Hanumān sung on Tuesdays (Maṅgalavāra), in Hindi (`hi`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): Drik Panchang, "Mangalwar Ki Aarti" (its first āratī, ॥ हनुमानजी की आरती ॥), the only witness; the āratī is not in ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/amal-bhakti/`: Amal Bhakti (YouTube `TP1GQ9veSuU`, "मंगल मूर्ति जय जय हनुमंता | Hanuman Aarti | Full Lyrics | Hanuman Bhajan"). Its starts come from the transcript for now; MMS (`tools/audio/METHOD.md`) is still to time it. A draft (it plays only with Audio timing on): its starts are still to be timed by the forced aligner (MMS): the transcript alone leaves verses it sings untimed or guessed.

Verse types: टेक (refrain, `tek`, shown once at the start although it is sung after every stanza) and पद (stanza, `pad`). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (Mangal Murti Jai Jai Hanumanta) › one section (`aarti`) › verses: the refrain (`aarti.tek`, type `tek`) and 11 stanzas, the last the closing verse (श्री मंगल जी की आरती …).
- **Numbering:** each stanza's `n` is its number (॥१॥ …), and it is cited by it ("Stanza 3"); the refrain has none.
- **Topics:** one, the āratī's title. **Reading plans:** none.
