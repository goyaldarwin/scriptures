# Reconciliation: मंगल मूरति जय जय हनुमन्ता

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** "Mangalwar Ki Aarti" (<https://www.drikpanchang.com/lyrics/aarti/weekdays/mangalwar/mangalwar-aarti.html>), scriptures-data `hinduism/aarti/hanuman/mangal-murti-hanuman-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/weekdays-mangalwar-aarti.html`: the āratī under ॥ हनुमानजी की आरती ॥, from मंगल मूरति जय जय हनुमन्ता to the closing verse श्री मंगल जी की आरती and its refrain cue. It is the only witness.
- **The book:** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan), scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/`: the āratī is not in it.
- **Web search** (for the doubts below): no independent site printing this āratī was found, so nothing was settled by it.

The page's second āratī, under ॥ आरती श्री हनुमानजी ॥ (आरती कीजै हनुमान लला की, printed there with the मंगल मूरति refrain cue after each stanza), is the library's existing work `hanuman-aarti` and is left out of this text.

The rule: keep Drik Panchang; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (each of the page's printed lines, a half-line ending in a comma, is its own line); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| refrain | the page's first group: the two refrain lines and the first stanza together | the refrain (`aarti.tek`), then stanza 1 | The page's refrain cue repeats मंगल मूरति जय जय हनुमन्ता; the next four lines are a stanza like the others. |
| every stanza | half-lines ending in commas; । and ॥ alternating | one line per printed line, each ending ।, the last ॥N॥ | The standard. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥११॥ | The standard. |
| after every stanza | ⟨refrain⟩ मंगल मूरति जय जय हनुमन्ता॥ | not shown | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item. |
| stanza 11 | श्री मंगल जी की आरती / हनुमत सहितासु गाई। / होई मनोरथ सिद्ध जब / अन्त विष्णुपुर जाई॥ | kept, as stanza 11 | It is a verse of the āratī, not a caption: its own group on the page, with the refrain cue after it like every stanza, and it says what singing this āratī brings (wishes fulfilled, Viṣṇu's abode at the end), the āratī's phalaśruti. |

No word is changed. Kept as Drik Panchang has them: मंगल-मंगल, कांधे मूंज, जुद्ध, फारत, उजारत, शत्रुन काट-काट महिं डारे, निवारें, हांक तें कांपै, रोग शोक बहुत विपत्ति घिराने, दारिद्र दहन ऋण त्रासा (a short line; `docs/reviews/mangal-murti-hanuman-aarti-doubts.jsonl`), अङ्गीकार, देऊ, करोरी, सहितासु (the same doubts file).
