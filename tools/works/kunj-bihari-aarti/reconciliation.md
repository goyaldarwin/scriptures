# Reconciliation: आरती कुंजबिहारी की

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** "Kunj Bihariji Ki Aarti" (`hinduism/aarti/krishna/kunj-bihari-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/kunj-bihari-aarti.html`). Four stanzas (गले में बैजंती माला, कनकमय मोर मुकुट, जहां ते प्रकट भई गंगा, चमकती उज्ज्वल तट रेनू), the first opening with the refrain's two lines; after each stanza the cue श्री गिरिधर कृष्णमुरारी की and the refrain (x2); at the end the refrain twice more.
- The book (५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित) and the other booklets have no copy of this āratī; Drik Panchang is the only witness.

The rule: keep the base; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (a line split at a comma becomes two lines); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| stanza 1 | opens with आरती कुंजबिहारी की, श्री गिरिधर कृष्ण मुरारी की। | the refrain (`aarti.tek`) | The refrain, which the page repeats after every stanza; shown once, first. |
| the end of every stanza | श्री गिरिधर कृष्णमुरारी की॥ | (not written) | The cue back to the refrain (its second line), as the ॐ जय जगदीश हरे cues. |
| after every stanza; at the end | the refrain (x2); the refrain twice | (not written) | The refrain is shown once. |
| the short lines | भ्रमर सी अलक, कस्तूरी तिलक, / बजे मुरचंग, मधुर मिरदंग, / बसी सिव सीस, जटा के बीच, / हंसत मृदु मंद,चांदनी चंद, | each half its own line | The standard: a line split at its comma; each half is one rhyming member (अलक / तिलक / झलक). |
| line ends | , ; । ॥ | । and, at a stanza's end, ॥N॥ | The standard; the semicolons are punctuation. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥४॥ | The standard. |

Kept as the base has them: बैजंती, बजावै, झलकाला, नंद के आनंद, लतन में ठाढ़े बनमाली, बिलसै, तरसैं, गगन सों, रासि, मुरचंग, मिरदंग, कलुष कलि हारिणि, स्मरन, सिव सीस, चरन, रेनू, बेनू, चहुं दिसि गोपि, the refrain's कृष्ण मुरारी written apart (the cue writes कृष्णमुरारी), and stanza 1's seven rhyming lines before its short lines (the other stanzas have three).
