# Reconciliation: आरती कीजै नरसिंह कुँवर की

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** "Narsingh Bhagwan Ki Aarti" (`hinduism/aarti/narasimha/narsingh-kunwar-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/aarti-kijai-narsingh-kunwarki.html`), headed आरती श्री नृसिंह भगवान जी की. The opening couplet (आरती कीजै नरसिंह कुँवर की) as its own group, then three groups of four lines (पहली / दूसरी आरती; तीसरी / चौथी आरती; पाँचवीं आरती and the closing couplet with the poet's name).
- The book (५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित) and the other booklets have no copy of this āratī; Drik Panchang is the only witness.

The rule: keep the base; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): the refrain (टेक) is shown once, first; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| the opening couplet | its own group | the refrain (`aarti.tek`) | The āratī's opening words, which carry its title (आरती कीजै …) and stand apart from the counted āratīs; the page marks no repeat of it. |
| stanzas 1–3 | each couplet ending ॥ | । inside the stanza, ॥N॥ at its end | The standard: every line but the last ends । . The page's groups are kept as the stanzas. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥३॥ | The standard. |

Kept as the base has them: कुँवर, गाऊँ, प्रभुजी, हिरणाकुश, ब्रह्म पधारे, सहसबाहु के भुजा उखारे, लंक, पाँचवीं, पछारे, प्रतिपाले (an imperfect rhyme with पछारे, as printed), तुलसी को पत्र, हरषि-निरखि, गावें.
