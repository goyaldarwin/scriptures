# Reconciliation: जय महागौरी जगत की माया

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** "Mahagauri Mata Aarti" (`hinduism/aarti/durga/mahagauri-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/devi-mahagauri-aarti.html`), headed आरती देवी महागौरी जी की. Five groups of four printed lines, no refrain marked.
- The book (५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित) and the other booklets have no copy of this āratī; Drik Panchang is the only witness.
- **For a doubtful reading only:** the āratī as Aaj Tak prints it (aajtak.in/religion/vrat-katha/maa-mahagauri-vrat-katha-2505756), found by web search. Bhakti Bharat and Tring (tring.co.in/aarti/mahagauri-aarti) print Drik Panchang's text, slips included, and are not counted.

The rule: keep the base; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; stanzas numbered (॥१॥); a variant a page offers in brackets is not part of the verse. The page marks no refrain, so none is made: the text is five stanzas.

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| stanza 1, line 4 | महागौरी तेरा वहा निवास | महागौरी तेरा वहाँ निवासा | वहा is वहाँ with its candrabindu dropped; निवास breaks the rhyme with पासा (every other couplet rhymes). Aaj Tak has वहां निवासा. |
| stanza 2, line 4 | जग विख्यता | जग विख्याता | विख्यता is not a word, and it breaks the rhyme with माता; Aaj Tak has विख्याता. |
| stanza 3, line 3 | सती (सत) हवन कुंड में था जलाया | सती हवन कुंड में था जलाया | The bracketed सत is a variant the page offers; the base reading is kept (Aaj Tak prints 'सत' in quotation marks after सती: settled under Readings settled). |
| line ends | । and ॥ alternating | । and, at a stanza's end, ॥N॥ | The standard. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥५॥ | The standard. |

Kept as the base has them: पासा, चन्द्रकली, ममता अम्बे, भीमा देवी, विमला, कौशिक देवी, हिमाचल के घर गौरी रूप तेरा, कुंड, धुएं, बना धर्म सिंह, आनेवाले, शनिवार, बिगड़ा, हरदम.

## Readings settled
- aarti.pad3 — सती (सत) हवन कुंड: kept सती हवन कुंड में था जलाया. सत is set off in every source (Drik Panchang in brackets, Aaj Tak and InstaAstro in quotation marks), none prints it as part of the line, and the stanza's lines are too uneven for the metre to decide; the base reading stands.
