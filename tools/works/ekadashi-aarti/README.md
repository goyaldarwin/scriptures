# ॐ जय एकादशी (Om Jai Ekadashi)

The āratī of Ekādaśī Mātā, the eleventh-day vow of Viṣṇu as a goddess, naming the Ekādaśīs of the year month by month, in Hindi (`hi`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): Drik Panchang, "Ekadashi Aarti", the only witness; the āratī is not in ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/spiritual-activity/`: Spiritual Activity (YouTube `b3xCDF85gek`, "जया एकादशी स्पेशल आरती | Om Jai Ekadashi Mata | ॐ जय एकादशी माता |  Ekadashi Aarti Hindi Lyrics🙏"), its track 0:00–10:02 of the video (after 10:02: music). Its starts come from the transcript for now; MMS (`tools/audio/METHOD.md`) is still to time it. A draft (it plays only with Audio timing on): its starts are still to be timed by the forced aligner (MMS): the transcript alone leaves verses it sings untimed or guessed.

Its last stanza names its poet, Gurditta (जन गुरदिता).

Verse types: टेक (refrain, `tek`, shown once at the start although it is sung after every stanza) and पद (stanza, `pad`). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (Om Jai Ekadashi) › one section (`aarti`) › verses: the refrain (`aarti.tek`, type `tek`) and 14 stanzas: the first in praise, twelve naming the Ekādaśīs from Mārgaśīrṣa (Utpannā, Mokṣadā) to Padminī, and the closing stanza with the poet's name.
- **Numbering:** each stanza's `n` is its number (॥१॥ …), and it is cited by it ("Stanza 3"); the refrain has none.
- **Topics:** one, the āratī's title. **Reading plans:** none.
