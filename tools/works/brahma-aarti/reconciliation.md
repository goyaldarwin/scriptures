# Reconciliation: पितु मातु सहायक स्वामी सखा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), p. 53; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-052), read on the page image, under the heading "आरती श्री ब्रह्मा जी की". It prints ten lines, each ending ।, with no refrain, no stanza breaks and no ॥.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/brahma/brahma-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-brahma-aarti.html`): the same text, word for word, set out in five stanzas of two printed lines each.
- **For a doubtful reading only:** Atul's Song A Day (atulsongaday.me, 'Pitu maatu sahaayak swaami sakha', 2013), a transcription of Mukesh's 1950 recording of this prayer, independent of the book's edition. Other web searches for the lines with निर्गुण, भुलि हैं and बुधवारे found no further independent text.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (the book's comma between the halves of a line becomes a line break, a half per line); stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| the stanzas | ten lines, no breaks | five stanzas of two printed lines (four half-lines) each | The book marks no stanzas; every line ends in the same rhyme (…रे हो), so the division into couplets is Drik Panchang's, which follows the sense (each pair of lines is one sentence). |
| every line | the halves joined by a comma | two lines | The standard; the comma marks the half. |
| stanza ends | । | ॥N॥ | The standard. |
| stanza 5 | पाय 'प्रताप' हरि | पाय प्रताप हरि | The quotation marks are the printer's, not part of the verse. |

Kept as the book has them (Drik Panchang agrees throughout): भाँति, सिगरे, भुलि हैं हम तो तुमको तुम तो (the words as printed; the half-line ends after तुम तो: see Readings settled), नाहिं, छिन ही छिन, तुम्हरी, मुझसे बिरले बुधवारे, प्रेमनिधि, सों.

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
The readings the words alone leave open, each decided (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §2).

- **aarti.pad2** — दुःख निर्गुण नाशन हारे: corrected to दुःख दुर्गुण नाशन हारे. Both witnesses (the book, i-052, and Drik Panchang, a copy of the same collection) print निर्गुण, but "destroyer of sorrow and of the attributeless" is a sense that does not fit (PLAYBOOK §2), and an independent witness, Mukesh's 1950 recording as transcribed on Atul's Song A Day (atulsongaday.me), sings durgun: दुःख and दुर्गुण are the pair destroyed. Meaning changed: "you are the destroyer of sorrow and vice" (hi: दुःख और दुर्गुणोंका नाश करनेवाले). New word: दुर्गुण (hi).
- **aarti.pad3** — भुलि हैं हम तो तुमको, a half-line short of its fellows: no word is missing; the half ends after तुम तो. Each first half has four IIS feet of 16 mātrās (पितु मा | तु सहा | यक स्वा | मि सखा), and भुलि हैं | हम तो | तुमको | तुम तो is exactly that, leaving हमरी सुधि नाहिं बिसारे हो as long as the other second halves; the book's comma follows the sense, not the metre. The words are as printed; only the line division moves. Meaning unchanged.
- **aarti.pad4** — मुझसे बिरले बुधवारे हो: kept as printed (both witnesses agree; समुझे, "only a rare wise one understands", is attested nowhere we could read, and the metre is whole as printed). Read as the words say: "rarely found by the likes of me, you belong to the wise". Meaning unchanged.
