# Reconciliation: श्री गणेश चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 7–10; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-006 … i-009), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/ganesha/ganesha-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/`): a typed copy of the same text, with its own slips (हवै for ह्वै, पदतहिं, मुषक, शुभः, सुहावना, दीना).
- **For a doubtful reading only:** the text as other sites print it, found by web search (Bhāratkosh, bharatdiscovery.org; Prabhat Khabar; Shubh Panchang; Hindu Blog; Bhakti Bharat, which prints Drik Panchang's text). Sites that copy one another count as one witness.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 16 | अस केहि अन्तर्धान रूप ह्वै | अस कहि अन्तर्धान रूप ह्वै | केहि is a misprint: Drik Panchang (कही), Bhāratkosh, Prabhat Khabar, Shubh Panchang and Hindu Blog all have कहि ("having said so"), which the sense needs. |
| closing dohā 2 | सम्बन्ध अपना सहस्र दश | सम्वत् अपना सहस्र दश | The line gives the year of writing (saṁvat); सम्बन्ध ("relation") makes no sense there. Bhāratkosh, Prabhat Khabar and Hindu Blog have सम्वत् (Shubh Panchang संवत्); Drik Panchang and Bhakti Bharat have सम्बन्ध, as the book does. अपना is the book's (Shubh Panchang the same; others अपन). |
| chaupāī 30 | नाम 'गणेश' शम्भु तब कीन्हें | नाम गणेश शम्भु तब कीन्हें | The quotation marks are the printer's, not part of the verse. |
| chaupāī 37 | भजत 'राम सुन्दर' प्रभुदासा | भजत राम सुन्दर प्रभुदासा | The same. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: करि वर बदन (Drik कविवर बदन; करि वर, "the best of elephants", is the elephant-faced one, as the other Gaṇeśa Chālīsā's करिवर वदन), विश्वविनायक, सुहावन (rhymes with भावन), मूषक, कहौं, कीन्हों, ह्वै, पड़तहिं (Bhāratkosh the same), दृगकोण, सिधाये (rhymes with लाये; Drik सिधायो), गजशिर, हर्ष्यो / वर्ष्यो (Drik हरषे / बरसे), तुम्हारी, मति हीन, कीजे / दीजे, सनमान. The spellings गनि (Drik गुनि), कह्यऊ and दुख are the book's.
