# श्री गणेश चालीसा (Shri Ganesh Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Gaṇeśa that opens "जय गणपति सदगुण सदन", in Awadhi (`awa`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 7–10), checked against Drik Panchang; chaupāī 38 and closing dohā 2 as Anuradha Paudwal sings them (Drik Panchang's wording). Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/anuradha-paudwal/`: Anuradha Paudwal (YouTube `oHHniScYJzo`); the starts come from the transcript, to be set by ear; hand-checked starts in `fixes.md`.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohā (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohās (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 38 chaupāīs are numbered 1–38 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the three dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Hanumān Chālīsā's in the Sundarakāṇḍa (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** Gaṇeśa's form, his birth, Śani's gaze and the elephant head, the test of wisdom, the prayer. **Reading plans:** none.
