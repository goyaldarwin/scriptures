# Reconciliation: जय शीतला माता

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), pp. 219–220, under the heading "आरती श्री शीतला माता जी की"; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-218, i-219), read on the page images. The refrain (जय शीतला माता), then ten stanzas of two printed lines each, the first ending with a comma, the second with ॥ and the refrain cue (जय…): रतन सिंहासन, विष्णु सेवत, इन्द्र मृदंग, घंटा शंख, ब्रह्म रूप, जो जन ध्यान, रोगों से, बांझ पुत्र, शीतल करती, and the closing stanza with the poet's name (दास नारायण).
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/shitala/shitala-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shitala-mata-aarti.html`): the same text in the same order, a half-line per line, with the refrain cue ॐ जय शीतला माता after each stanza.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| every stanza | two printed lines, the first ending with a comma, the second with ॥ | two lines, ending । and ॥N॥ (the refrain ॥) | The book's own lines (each rhymes in -आता); the book halves a line with a comma only in the refrain and stanzas 1–2, so the printed line stays one line throughout, the commas inside it dropped (the standard). |
| after every stanza | जय… | not written | The refrain cue; the refrain is shown once. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥१०॥ | The standard. |

Kept as the book has them (Drik Panchang the same unless noted): ऋद्धि सिद्धि मिल चँवर (Drik ऋद्धि-सिद्धि चँवर, without मिल), डोलावें, सेवें, वरणत, मृदंग (Drik मृदङ्ग), बजावै, घंटा शंख (Drik घण्टा शङ्ख), बाजै, करै, तुही, भक्तन, प्रेम शक्ति, दारिद्र, भजै, धुनि, उत्पत्ति बाला बिनाशन ("Readings settled" below), दास नारायण कर जोरी माता (a short line in both), दीजैं (Drik दीजै).

The doubt (बाला) was searched on the web; the sites found ([Bhakti Bharat](https://www.bhaktibharat.com/amp/aarti/sheetla-mata-ki-aarti), which copies Drik Panchang; [InstaAstro](https://instaastro.com/blog/sheetla-mata-ki-aarti); [Bharat Discovery](https://bharatdiscovery.org/india/शीतला_माता_की_आरती), not reachable) did not settle it, so the book's reading stays.

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
- **aarti.pad9** (उत्पत्ति बाला बिनाशन): as printed. The book, Drik Panchang, InstaAstro and Pujasthan (pujasthan.com) have बाला; only Bhakti Bharat, whose text is otherwise Drik's, has उत्पत्ति व्याधि बिनाशन … घाता. बाला is a word (Bālā, the goddess as the maiden), so the plain reading stands: "creation, the maiden Bālā, destruction: you are the mother of all". Meaning unchanged.
