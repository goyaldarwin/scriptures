# श्री शाकम्भरी चालीसा (Shri Shakambhari Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Śākambharī that opens "बन्दउ माँ शाकम्भरी चरणगुरू का धरकर ध्यान", in Awadhi (`awa`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 272–276), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** none yet.
- **Poet:** the chaupāīs name him, Kāntā Prasāda of Jagādharī (chaupāīs 37, 39, 46: कान्ता, कान्त).

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohās (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohās (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 46 chaupāīs are numbered 1–46 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the four dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Hanumān Chālīsā's in the Sundarakāṇḍa (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** the Mother's glory, her sisters and companions, the Mother's deeds, the worship and the Mother's grace, the poet's prayer and the fruit of the reading. **Reading plans:** none.
