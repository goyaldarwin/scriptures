# Reconciliation: श्री शाकम्भरी चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 272–276; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-271 … i-275), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/shakambhari/shakambhari-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shakambhari-mata-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, word for word the book's but for the readings listed below.
- **For a doubtful reading only:** Shubh Panchang (shubhpanchang.com/hi/chalisa/shakambhari-chalisa), found by web search; it agrees with Drik Panchang word for word where checked (कमलेश्वर, ने, the line printed once) and is counted with it.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept; its hyphens are.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| after chaupāī 38 (p. 275) | जो कोई जन चालीसा गावे, मात कृपा अति सुख पावे। printed twice, on two lines in a row | once | A repeated line (the typesetter's slip): the same words twice in a row, and Drik Panchang has it once. With it the book would have 47 chaupāīs. |
| chaupāī 8 | जब-जब भक्तों नें है ध्याई | जब-जब भक्तों ने है ध्याई | नें is a misspelling of the postposition ने; Drik Panchang has ने. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: चरणगुरू (Drik चरणगुरु), जगदम्बिका-अनन्त (Drik a comma there), जोतमन (Drik जोत मन), कम्पलेश्वर (Drik कमलेश्वर; neither reading is clearly wrong), दुख in chaupāī 30 (Drik दुःख). Kept as every witness has them: अनपा, स्वरुपा, सहस्या, शिव शंकर प्रभु भोले भण्डारी (a half that does not rhyme with चेरा), जग की एक भ्रमण की कारण.

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
- **chaupāī 4**, अर्पणा in the second half (Aparṇā again, or 'offered'): 'offered, devoted' stands. Platts gives अरपना *arpnā*, 'to present an offering, to devote', so the printed word reads plainly as it is; the first half's अर्पणा stays Aparṇā (MW *a-parṇā*, a name of Durgā/Pārvatī).
- **chaupāī 10**, भीम (the name Bhīmā or the adjective): the name Bhīmā. The chaupāīs around it name the sisters (Śatākṣī in 9, Bhrāmarī in 11), and Bhīmā, Bhrāmarī and Śatākṣī are the Devī's forms named together with Śākambharī (Durgāsaptaśatī, ch. 11).
- **chaupāī 27**, शोक पात (or शाक पात, 'greens and leaves'): शोक as printed. The page image (i-273) and Drik Panchang both print शोक; no independent source was reachable to show a corruption (sanskritdocuments.org's copy is closed to the fetch tool; Shubh Panchang copies Drik Panchang), so the print stands.
- **chaupāī 28**, कान्त and कम्पलेश्वर: कान्त is the poet's signature, as in chaupāīs 37, 39 and 46 (Kāntā Prasād of Jagādhrī); कम्पलेश्वर as the page image prints it (Drik Panchang कमलेश्वर is not clearly the better reading).
- **chaupāī 43**, शोक मोह शत्रु न संहारे (न against the first half): a rhetorical question, 'does she not destroy…?', which affirms what the first half says; the printed न is kept and read as it stands (every witness has it).
