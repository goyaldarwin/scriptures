# सुनो जी भैरव लाड़िले (Suno Ji Bhairav Ladile)

The āratī of Bhairava (as worshipped at Mehandipur), in Hindi (`hi`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 67–68), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/brijwani/`: Brijwani (YouTube `Zye96yeM2NI`, "सुनो जी भैरव  लाडले । भैरव जी की आरती ।  Sunoji Bhairav Ladle |  Bhairavji Ki Aarti"). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list.

Verse types: टेक (refrain, `tek`, shown once at the start although it is sung after every stanza) and पद (stanza, `pad`). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (Suno Ji Bhairav Ladile) › one section (`aarti`) › verses: the refrain (`aarti.tek`, type `tek`) and 5 stanzas.
- **Numbering:** each stanza's `n` is its number (॥१॥ …), and it is cited by it ("Stanza 3"); the refrain has none.
- **Topics:** one, the āratī's title. **Reading plans:** none.
