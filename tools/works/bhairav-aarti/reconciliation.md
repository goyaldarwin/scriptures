# Reconciliation: सुनो जी भैरव लाड़िले

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), pp. 67–68; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-066, i-067), read on the page images. The āratī starts under its heading "आरती श्री भैरव जी की" on p. 67; the chaupāīs and the दोहा above it close the Bhairava Chālīsā and are not part of the āratī. The book ends a unit with ॥ and the cue "सुनो जी भैरव''" after its 5th printed line and then after every 3rd line (each closing on करूँ / धरूँ).
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/bhairava/bhairav-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-bhairava-aarti.html`): the same words in the same order, but grouped in fours of half-lines with the cue "सुनो जी भैरव लाड़िले॥" after each, so that its groups cut across the sentences and the rhyme (one group ends नृत्य भी करते सदा, another माताजी खुश रहें).

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (the book's comma between the halves of a line becomes a line break, a half per line); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| refrain and stanza 1 | the first five printed lines, one unit (ending …वर्णन करूँ॥ सुनो जी भैरव'') | the refrain (the first two printed lines, ending धरूँ) and stanza 1 (the next three, ending करूँ) | The cue after every stanza repeats the opening words, so the opening lines are the refrain; its two lines close on करूँ / धरूँ as every stanza does, and what follows them is three printed lines like every other stanza of the book. |
| every stanza | printed lines, the halves of each joined by a comma | a half per line, ending । and ॥N॥ | The standard; the comma marks the half. |
| after every stanza | सुनो जी भैरव'' | (not shown) | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item (टेक). |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: छूता (Drik छुता); otherwise the words agree. As both have them: लाड़िले, विनती, अर्जी, स्वान, सांकली, मेंहदीपुर, सरनाम, अरु.
