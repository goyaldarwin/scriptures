# श्री रामदेव चालीसा (Shri Ramdev Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Bābā Rāmdev (Rāmdev Pīr of Ruṇīcā, the son of Ajmal) that opens "श्री गुरु पद नमन करि", in Awadhi (`awa`) with many Rajasthani words (परच्या, थारी, थणा, सेठाणी). Its chaupāīs tell his twenty-four परचे (miracles).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 157–160), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** none yet.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › two sections: the opening dohās (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`) › verses. The work has no closing dohā.
- **Numbering:** the 40 chaupāīs are numbered 1–40 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the two dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Gaṇeśa Chālīsā's (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** Rāmdev's coming, the first to the eleventh miracle, the twelfth to the twenty-fourth, he comes when called, the prayer. **Reading plans:** none.
