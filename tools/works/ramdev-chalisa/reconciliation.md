# Reconciliation: श्री रामदेव चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 157–160; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-156 … i-159), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/ramdev/ramdev-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-ramdev-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, verse for verse, with its own slips (चैबीसवां, रखियों, उक्त उजागर).

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept.

No change from the book.

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: द्वार केश ने आय कर (opening dohā 2; Drik से: Dvārakeśa, the lord of Dvārakā, came and took a human birth), निकलंग नाडी (chaupāī 18; Drik नाड़ी), भक्त उजागर (chaupāī 28; Drik उक्त उजागर), चौबीसवां (chaupāī 32; Drik चैबीसवां), रखियो (chaupāī 40; Drik रखियों). The spellings गनेश, कथूं, बधावणा, अवतंश, परच्या / परचा, सप्तम् / अष्टम्, पीर परी पधारया, बकरया, सुखी नाडी, कीन्हों / दीन्हों, चीहां, दीन्हां, सुमरण, तब (chaupāī 38) and मती are the book's, as Drik's.
