# Reconciliation: आरती प्रेतराज की कीजै

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), p. 112; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-110), read on the page image. Its order: the refrain (आरती प्रेतराज की कीजै, printed once under the title), दीन दुखिन, जिनके चरण, बाबा के तुम, जिन्न आदि, वेश राजसी.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/pretraj/pretraj-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-pretraj-aarti.html`): the same āratī, in the same order and the same words; the refrain is its heading (॥ आरती प्रेतराज की कीजै ॥).
- **For a doubtful reading only:** a web search for the line ढाल कृपाल धनुष अति भाता (and for ढाल कृपाण धनुष) found only Bhakti Bharat, which copies Drik Panchang; it settled nothing.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (the book's comma between the halves of a line becomes a line break, a half per line); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| every stanza | two printed lines, the halves joined by a comma | four lines, ending । and ॥N॥ | The standard; the comma marks the half. |
| refrain | आरती प्रेतराज की कीजै। | आरती प्रेतराज की कीजै ॥ | The refrain's last line ends ॥. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥५॥ | The standard. |

Kept as the book has them: every word; Drik Panchang does not differ from the book in any word. ढाल कृपाल धनुष (stanza 5) is kept as both witnesses print it, although the list of arms suggests कृपाण ("sword"); no independent witness was found (`docs/reviews/pretraj-aarti-doubts.jsonl`).
