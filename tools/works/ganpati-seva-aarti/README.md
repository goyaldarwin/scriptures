# गणपति की सेवा मंगल मेवा (Ganpati Ki Seva Mangal Meva)

An āratī of Gaṇeśa, in Hindi (`hi`); it tells of his birth on Bhādrapada śukla caturthī and his curse on the Moon.

- **Text** (`text.txt`): Drik Panchang (`aarti/shree-ganesh/ganapati-seva/ganapati-seva-aarti`, "आरती श्री गणपति जी"); the book (५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित, Randhir Prakashan) does not print this āratī. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/vikrant-singh/`: Vikrant Singh (YouTube `yl4ec1kq-E0`, "Ganpati Ji Ki Seva मंगल मेवा | गणपति आरती | Ganesh Aarti | New Ganpati Song 2026 |"), its track 0:00–4:18 of the video (after 4:18: music to the end). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list.

Verse types: टेक (refrain, `tek`, shown once at the start although it is sung after every stanza) and पद (stanza, `pad`). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (Ganpati Ki Seva Mangal Meva) › one section (`aarti`) › verses: the refrain (`aarti.tek`, type `tek`) and 9 stanzas.
- **Numbering:** each stanza's `n` is its number (॥१॥ …), and it is cited by it ("Stanza 3"); the refrain has none.
- **Topics:** one, the āratī's title. **Reading plans:** none.
