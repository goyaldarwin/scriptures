# Reconciliation: ॐ जय जगदीश हरे

## Source
The text the owner pasted (no page named). It is the only witness.

The rule: keep the source; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. House style: ।/।। for the dandas, stanzas numbered (।।१।।), and the refrain (टेक, the first stanza) shown once although it is sung after each stanza.

| Place | Source | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| after every stanza | ॥ ॐ जय जगदीश हरे..॥ | (not shown) | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item (टेक). |
| at the end | the first stanza again | (not repeated) | Same reason. |
| stanza 7, line 3 | स्वमी पाप(कष्ट) हरो देवा | स्वामी पाप हरो देवा | स्वमी is a typing slip. "(कष्ट)" is a variant the source offers in brackets; we keep its main reading पाप, as in line 2 ("पाप हरो देवा"). |

Kept as the source has them: the six short lines of each stanza, their commas, गहूं, करूं, मिलूं (anusvāra for candrabindu), सम्पति, सन्तन, the hyphens in दीन-बन्धु, दुःख-हर्ता, विषय-विकार.
