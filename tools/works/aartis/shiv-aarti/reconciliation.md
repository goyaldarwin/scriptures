## Meanings
Our own, one per verse. The āratī praises Śiva as Oṅkāra, the one in whom Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva are one; stanzas 1–5 name the three in turn (face, seat, arms, garland, garment, companions, what they hold, and their work), and the meanings say which belongs to whom. The word धारा ("bore") has that sense in this work only (`senses.json`).
