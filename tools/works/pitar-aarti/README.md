# जय जय पितरजी महाराज (Jai Jai Pitarji Maharaj)

The āratī of the Pitars, the ancestors, in Hindi with Rājasthānī forms (थारी, जाणू) (`hi`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, p. 166), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/divine-devika/`: Divine Devika (YouTube `3QrtNqYV-rE`, "पितृ देव आरती 🙏 | जय जय पितरजी महाराज | पितृ पक्ष विशेष | Female Version"), its track 0:06–1:48 of the video (before 0:06: the title announced, 'श्री पितर आरती'). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list.

Verse types: टेक (refrain, `tek`, shown once at the start although it is sung after every stanza) and पद (stanza, `pad`). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (Jai Jai Pitarji Maharaj) › one section (`aarti`) › verses: the refrain (`aarti.tek`, type `tek`) and 4 stanzas.
- **Numbering:** each stanza's `n` is its number (॥१॥ …), and it is cited by it ("Stanza 3"); the refrain has none.
- **Topics:** one, the āratī's title. **Reading plans:** none.
