# Reconciliation: जय जय पितरजी महाराज

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), p. 166, "आरती श्री पितर जी की"; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-165), read on the page image. Ten printed lines in pairs: the first pair ends ॥ with no cue (the refrain), the four after it each end ॥ with the cue "जय". (The dohā printed above the heading, जीवन सफल जो चाहिए…, closes the Pitar Chālīsā on the page before; it is not part of the āratī.)
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/pitar/pitar-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-pitar-aarti.html`): the same text word for word, with the refrain and the first stanza printed as one group and the cue जय जय पितरजी महाराज after each group.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (the book's comma between the halves of a line becomes a line break, a half per line); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| after every stanza | जय" | (not shown) | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item (टेक). |
| every printed line | the halves joined by a comma | two lines | The standard; the comma marks the half. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥४॥ | The standard. |
| the refrain | the book's first pair (Drik Panchang joins it to the first stanza) | its own item, before stanza 1 | The book closes the pair with ॥ and puts the cue only after the pairs that follow; Drik Panchang's cue (जय जय पितरजी महाराज) is that pair's first words. |

Kept as the book has them (Drik Panchang agrees): पितरजी joined, पड़यो, थारी, खेवनहारे, कछु नहि जाणू, हरदम हर घड़ी, अरज गुजारी, सहाई, बारम्बार.
