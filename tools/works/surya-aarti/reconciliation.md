# Reconciliation: जय कश्यप-नन्दन

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** Aarti Mala, the page "सूर्य देव जी की आरती"; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-332), read on the page image. The page is a printout carrying the indif.com watermark. Its order: the refrain (marked टेक), सप्त-अश्वरथ, सुर-मुनि, सकल-सुकर्म, कमल-समूह, नेत्र-व्याधि, सूर्यदेव करुणाकर.
- **Drik Panchang**, two pages with the same text: `hinduism/aarti/surya/surya-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/surya-deva-aarti.html` and `.../weekdays-raviwar-aarti.html` (Raviwar Ki Aarti). The same āratī in the same order, with its own slip (साहज for सहज).

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (a printed line is split at its half, a half per line); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| every stanza | two printed lines, the halves joined by a comma in some lines and by a space in others | four lines, ending । and ॥N॥ | The standard. Where the book prints no comma, the line is split at the same half as its fellows, where Drik Panchang breaks it (त्रिभुवन-तिमिर निकन्दन / भक्त-हृदय-चन्दन; सप्त-अश्वरथ राजित / एक चक्रधारी, and so on); in stanza 4 the metre puts अति in the first half (सेवत सहज हरत अति / मनसिज-संतापा, twelve mātrās like कमल-समूह-विकासक), where Drik Panchang breaks before it. |
| refrain | …चन्दन ।। टेक ।। | …चन्दन ॥ | टेक is the book's label, not part of the verse. |
| after every stanza | ।। जय ।। | (not shown) | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item (टेक). |
| refrain, line 3 | त्रिभुवन-तिमिन निकन्दन | त्रिभुवन-तिमिर निकन्दन | तिमिन is not a word; तिमिर ("darkness", which निकन्दन "destroyer" needs) is Drik Panchang's. |
| stanza 3, line 1 | सकल-सुर्कम-प्रसविता | सकल-सुकर्म-प्रसविता | सुर्कम is a misprint (the repha on the wrong letter); Drik Panchang has सुकर्म. |
| stanza 6 | करूणाकर, करूणा | करुणाकर, करुणा | A spelling slip (ū for u); Drik Panchang has करुणा. |
| stanzas 1, 5, 6 | सप्त - अश्वरथ, दुखहारी - सुखकारी, परहित - व्रतधारी, अज्ञान - मोह | सप्त-अश्वरथ, दुखहारी-सुखकारी, परहित-व्रतधारी, अज्ञान-मोह | The printer's spaced hyphens are written as the book's other hyphens. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: अदिति-नन्दन (Drik अदिति नन्दन), दुखहारी-सुखकारी (Drik दुःखहारी, सुखकारी), वंदित (Drik वन्दित), भव बंधन (Drik भव-बन्धन), कमल-समूह-विकासक (Drik कमल-समूह विकासक), सहज (Drik साहज), नेत्र-व्याधि-हर (Drik नेत्र-व्याधि हर), वृष्टि-विमोचन (Drik वृष्टि विमोचन), परहित-व्रतधारी (Drik परहित व्रतधारी), संतत, संतापा.
