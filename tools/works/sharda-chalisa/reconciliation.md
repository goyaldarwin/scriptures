# Reconciliation: श्री शारदा चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 266–269; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-265 … i-268), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/sharda/sharda-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/sharda-mata-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, word for word the book's but for the readings listed below.
- **For a doubtful reading only:** Shubh Panchang (shubhpanchang.com/hi/chalisa/sharda-chalisa), found by web search, for chaupāī 7. Bhakti Bharat prints Drik Panchang's text and is not counted.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept; its hyphens are.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 3 | दो सहस्त्र बर्षहि अनुमाना | दो सहस्र बर्षहि अनुमाना | सहस्त्र is a misspelling of सहस्र ("thousand"); Drik Panchang has सहस्र. |
| opening dohā | माला, पुस्तक, धारिणी | माला पुस्तक धारिणी | The commas inside the line are not kept (the standard). |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: जहां (Drik जहाँ), बध (Drik वध), स्वरूपा and रूपा in chaupāī 22 (Drik स्वरुपा, रुपा). Kept as every witness has them: सूर्य in chaupāī 15 (the metre needs its three mātrās), पढ़ीहे in chaupāī 36, and शचि in chaupāī 7 (Shubh Panchang the same; read as शुचि, settled below).

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
- chalisa.cau7 — शचि: the text stays as every witness prints it (book, Drik Panchang, Shubh Panchang; a web search found no other), read as the spelling of शुचि, "pure", as our glossary (awa शचि) has it: Śacī, Indra's queen (MW), has no place beside Sarasvatī's swan. The meaning now gives the word, "the royal swan is your pure vehicle" (before, it was left out). Meaning changed (Hindi and English).
- chalisa.cau15 — सूर्य: Sūradāsa. It stands beside Tulasī among learned men (विद्वाना) who sang Śāradā's fame, which the Sun is not; the metre keeps the printed form. Meaning unchanged.
