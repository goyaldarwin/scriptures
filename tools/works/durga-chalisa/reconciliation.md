# Reconciliation: श्री दुर्गा चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 173–176; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-172 … i-175), read on the page images. The "देवी खण्ड" star beside the title is the book's section mark, not part of the text.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/durga/durga-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/durga-mata-chalisa.html`): the widely printed web text of the same chālīsā, with its own slips (हिरण्याक्ष for Hiraṇyakaśipu, whom Narasiṁha slew).
- **Chalisa Mala** (the indif.com printout in the same scriptures-data folder, images i-404 … i-406): another copy of the web text, opening with a Sanskrit dhyāna (दुर्गा दुर्गतिहारिणी …) that is not part of the chālīsā; used only as a check.
- **For a doubtful reading only:** Green Message (greenmesg.org, Durga Chalisa), found by web search, an independent typed copy.

The book's version differs from the web text in a few places. Where the recording sings the web text's reading, the text has it (below); elsewhere the book's is kept: रिपु मुरख मोहि अति डरपावे (chaupāī 36's second half, as the recording sings it; web मोह मदादिक सब विनशावै), दुर्गा चालीसा जो गावैं (web जो नित गावै), and the closing dohā शरणागत रक्षा करे, which the web text does not have.

## Recording
The text follows the version sung in the chosen recording, BhaktiDhaam's "सम्पूर्ण चालीसा संग्रह" (YouTube `lCwPg0LRRjk`, the Durgā Chālīsā from 24:46 to 29:49), as far as it sings another written witness's reading: in the four places below it sings Drik Panchang's (and Chalisa Mala's) reading, written as they spell it; elsewhere it sings the book's text. It sings chaupāī 40 as दुर्गा चालीसा जो कोई गावै, which no witness has; the book's line is kept. Chaupāī 41 and the closing dohā are not heard in the transcript; they stay, untimed if they are not sung.

## Corrections
The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 20 | नाग कोटि में तुम्हीं विराजत | नगर कोटि में तुम्हीं विराजत | The chaupāīs name the Mother's seats (हिंगलाज, ज्वाला): Nagarkoṭ (Kāṅgṛā) is one, and "नाग कोटि" gives no sense with विराजत. Drik Panchang and Chalisa Mala have नगर कोटि, Green Message नगरकोट. |
| chaupāī 26 | बाला में है ज्योति तुम्हारी | ज्वाला में है ज्योति तुम्हारी | The flame (ज्योति) is that of Jvālāmukhī, the seat named ज्वाला; बाला ("girl") is a misprint. Drik Panchang, Chalisa Mala and Green Message have ज्वाला. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: निरंकार (Drik निराकार), महा विशाला, भृकुटी, अन्नपूरना (Drik अन्नपूर्णा), शिव शंकर, गावैं / ध्यावैं, परगट भई फाड़ कर (Drik प्रगट भईं फाड़कर), रक्षा करि प्रहलाद, हिरणाकुश (Drik हिरण्याक्ष), दया सिंधु, मातंगी धूमावती माता (Drik मातंगी अरु धूमावति), खड़ग विराजे, सोहे, तिहूं लोक, रहे, आवे, ध्यावै, ताको, कीनों / लीनों, निशि दिन, नहीं, हरे, रिपु मुरख मोहि अति डरपावे, इक चित, जियौं (Drik जियउं), पाऊँ / सुनाऊँ, जो गावैं … पावैं, and the closing dohā.

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
- **chalisa.cau5** (संसार शक्ति लय कीना: the world dissolved, or absorbed into her?): the world brought to dissolution by her power, as our meaning has it: लय is 'dissolution' (Monier-Williams, Apte: laya), and the line sets dissolution beside sustenance (पालन हेतु अन्न धन दीना); nothing in the witnesses or the words supports the other reading.
