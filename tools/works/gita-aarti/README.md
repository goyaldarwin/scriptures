# जय भगवद् गीते (Jai Bhagavad Gite)

The āratī of the Bhagavad Gītā, sung to the Gītā as mother, in Hindi (`hi`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): Drik Panchang, "श्रीमद भगवद गीता आरती" (the book has no copy). Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/anup-jalota/`: Anup Jalota (YouTube `-UE9JVIJD7Y`, "Jay Bhagwat Geete जय भगवद् गीते | Aarti Bhagwat Geeta Ki | Aarti Sangrah | Anup Jalota - Best Aarti"). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list.

Verse types: टेक (refrain, `tek`, shown once at the start although it is sung after every stanza) and पद (stanza, `pad`). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (Jai Bhagavad Gite) › one section (`aarti`) › verses: the refrain (`aarti.tek`, type `tek`) and 6 stanzas.
- **Numbering:** each stanza's `n` is its number (॥१॥ …), and it is cited by it ("Stanza 3"); the refrain has none.
- **Topics:** one, the āratī's title. **Reading plans:** none.
