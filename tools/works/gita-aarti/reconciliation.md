# Reconciliation: जय भगवद् गीते

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base)** (`hinduism/aarti/gita/gita-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/bhagavad-gita-aarti.html`), "श्रीमद भगवद गीता आरती": the opening stanza (जय भगवद् गीते), six stanzas of four printed lines, the refrain cue (जय भगवद् गीते, माता जय...) after each, and the opening stanza again at the end. The printed book (Aarti Chalisa Sangrah) has no copy, so Drik Panchang is the only witness.

The rule: keep the base; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Base | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| after every stanza | जय भगवद् गीते, माता जय...॥ | (not shown) | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item (टेक). |
| at the end | the opening stanza again (with कमल-विहारिणि hyphened) | (not repeated) | Same reason; the refrain is kept as the page prints it first. |
| refrain, line 1; stanza 5, lines 1 and 3 | the commas at the line ends | । | The standard: no comma; every line but the last ends ।. |
| stanza 6, line 2 | मातु! कृपा कीजै | मातु कृपा कीजै | The exclamation mark is punctuation, not part of the verse. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥६॥ | The standard. |

Kept as the base has them: भगवद् गीते written apart with the halant, विहारिणि, सुपुनीते, कामासक्तिहरा, विकाशिनि, आसुर-भाव-विनाशिनि, हरि-रसिका, दया-सुधा and the other hyphens, परमानन्दप्रदा, सिखावनि, बरसावनि, अपनो.
