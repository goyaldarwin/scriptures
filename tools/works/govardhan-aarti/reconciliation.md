# Reconciliation: श्री गोवर्धन महाराज

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** Shri Govardhan Aarti (`hinduism/aarti/krishna/govardhan-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-govardhan-aarti.html`): the refrain (two lines) and five stanzas, each printed as two lines followed by the refrain's second line, "तेरे माथे मुकुट विराज रहेओ" (not marked as a refrain on the page, but the same line after every stanza).
- **The book:** none. The āratī is not in ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (`hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/`) or the other booklets; Drik Panchang is the only witness.

The rule: keep Drik Panchang; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (a pause within a line is a space); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Base | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| after every stanza | तेरे माथे मुकुट विराज रहेओ। | (not shown) | The refrain's line, repeated as the cue after each stanza; the refrain is the first item (टेक). Each stanza is its own two lines. |
| every line | the printed commas (also inside the refrain's first line: श्री गोवर्धन महाराज, ओ महाराज,) | (none) | The standard. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥५॥ | The standard. |

No reading of the base is changed. Kept as the base has them: रहेओ, तोपे, परिकम्मा, चकलेश्वर, कण्ठा, ठोड़ी पे, कानन, झाँकी, धरण.
