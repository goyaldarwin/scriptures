# Reconciliation: श्री परशुराम चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 146–150; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-145 … i-149), read on the page images. The āratī that follows it on p. 150 (आरती श्री परशुराम जी की) is a separate work.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/parashurama/parashurama-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-parashurama-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text (two opening dohās, 40 chaupāīs, the closing dohā and the śloka), in the same order, with its own slips (पृर्णेन्दु).

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's and the śloka's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept. The śloka is Sanskrit (`@lang sa`); the standard writes its nasals.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 19 | सहस्त्रबाहु दुर्जन विकराला | सहस्रबाहु दुर्जन विकराला | सहस्त्र is a misspelling of सहस्र ("thousand"); Drik Panchang सहस्रबाहु. |
| closing śloka | सहसबाहुर्मर्दनम् | सहस्रबाहुर्मर्दनम् | सहस lacks the र of सहस्र (a dropped sign); Drik Panchang सहस्रबाहुर्मर्दनम्. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: बरणौं (Drik बरणों), बिकट, ब्यापि, रुदन (chaupāī 6; Drik रूदन), जनेउ (Drik जनेऊ), बिशाला, बेद-संहिता, अरू, शस्त्र विद्या (chaupāī 33; Drik शास्त्र विद्या), पढ़ै, पूर्णेन्दु. The book and Drik Panchang agree on रूद्र, रूदन (chaupāī 25), चारू, the joined परशुंवन्दे and the śloka's other words.
