# श्री परशुराम चालीसा (Shri Parshuram Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Bhagavān Paraśurāma that opens "श्री गुरु चरण सरोज छवि", in Awadhi (`awa`), closing with a Sanskrit śloka (`sa`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 146–150), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/snehvardhan-shukla/`: Snehvardhan Shukla (YouTube `duUrb46o_NU`, "परशुराम जयंती को सुनें परशुराम चालीसा सभी संकट भय का नाश होगा | Parshuram Chalisa |Parshuram Jayanti"). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī, `shloka` śloka).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohās (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohā and śloka (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 40 chaupāīs are numbered 1–40 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the three dohās and the śloka have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha", "Closing · Shloka").
- **Ids:** as the Hanumān Chālīsā's in the Sundarakāṇḍa (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …; the śloka `chalisa.shloka1`).
- **Topics:** praise, his birth and form, the fight with Gaṇapati and the slaying of Sahasrabāhu, in the Tretā and Dvāpara ages, refuge and the fruit of reading, the close. **Reading plans:** none.
- The āratī printed after it on p. 150 (ॐ जय परशुधारी) is its own work.
