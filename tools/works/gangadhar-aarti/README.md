# ॐ जय गङ्गाधर (Om Jai Gangadhar)

The āratī of Śiva as Gaṅgādhara, the bearer of the Gaṅgā, describing his dance on Kailāsa. Its words are mostly Sanskrit, with Hindi forms among them (खेलत, नृत्यत, सेवत, धिनकत थै थै); the work's language is Hindi (`hi`), as for every āratī.

- **Text** (`text.txt`): Drik Panchang, "Lord Gangadhara Aarti", the only witness; the āratī is not in ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** none yet.

Verse types: टेक (refrain, `tek`, shown once at the start) and पद (stanza, `pad`). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (Om Jai Gangadhar) › one section (`aarti`) › verses: the refrain (`aarti.tek`, type `tek`) and 8 stanzas.
- **Numbering:** each stanza's `n` is its number (॥१॥ …), and it is cited by it ("Stanza 3"); the refrain has none.
- **Topics:** one, the āratī's title. **Reading plans:** none.
