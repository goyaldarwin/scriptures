# Reconciliation: ॐ जय गङ्गाधर

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** "Lord Gangadhara Aarti" (<https://www.drikpanchang.com/lyrics/aarti/lord-shiva/lord-gangadhara-aarti.html>), scriptures-data `hinduism/aarti/shiva/gangadhar-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/lord-gangadhara-aarti.html`. It is the only witness.
- **The book:** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan), scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/`: the āratī is not in it.
- **Web search** (for the readings settled below): it found Bhakti Bharat (which copies Drik Panchang) and sanskritdocuments.org ("आरती भगवान गंगाधर"), whose page this session cannot open; nothing was settled by it.

The rule: keep Drik Panchang; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): one line per printed line, every line ending । and the last ॥N॥; the refrain (टेक) shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| every stanza | । and ॥ alternating | each line ending ।, the last ॥N॥ | The standard. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥८॥ | The standard. |
| after every stanza | ⟨refrain⟩ ॐ हर हर हर महादेव॥ | not shown | The cue sung after each stanza; the refrain is the first item (the page's first group, ॐ जय गङ्गाधर …). |
| stanza 2, line 1 | तस्मिन्ल्ललितसुदेशे | तस्मिँल्ललितसुदेशे | A broken sandhi: तस्मिन् before ललित becomes तस्मिँल्, the न् turning into a nasalised ल् (Pāṇini 8.4.60); the page has the doubled ल्ल of that sandhi and also the न्, which cannot stand before it. |
| stanza 2, line 3 | भुषारज्जित | भूषारञ्जित | Two slips in one word: भुषा and रज्जित are not words; भूषारञ्जित, "adorned with ornaments", is the sense the line needs. |
| stanza 5, line 1 | कर्पूरघुतिगौरं | कर्पूरद्युतिगौरं | घुति is not a word; द्युति ("lustre"): "white with the lustre of camphor", Śiva's epithet कर्पूरगौर. A typing slip, घ for द्य. |
| stanza 5, line 3 | सुन्दरजटायकलापं | सुन्दरजटाकलापं | जटाय is not a word, and the extra य breaks the metre (13 mātrās against the 12 of every other first half in the stanza: कर्पूरद्युतिगौरं, त्रिनयनशशिधरमौलिं); जटाकलाप, "the mass of matted hair". |
| stanza 8, line 4 | श्रृणुते | शृणुते | श्रृ is a common mistyping; the verb (root श्रु, "hear") is शृणुते. |

Kept as Drik Panchang has them: गुन्जति, मधुकरपुन्जे, कुन्जवने, पन्चानन, सन्गति (न typed for the class nasal; the dictionaries fold them with the anusvāra), हन्सावन, मुदसहिता, नामयते, बिबुधबधू, नृत्यत, धिनकत थै थै, नूपुरमुज्ज्वलिता, धिक तां, अङ्गुष्ठांगुलिनादं, शङ्खनिनदम् (settled below), वेद-ऋचां, रामस्त्रिजटानाथं, ध्यानं आरति.

## Readings settled
- **aarti.pad7** (शङ्खनिनदम् कृत्वा): निनद is a word (MW ninada, "sound"), so the printed form stands though निनाद would fill the metre: a correction is only for what is clearly wrong. Meaning: "having sounded the conch".
- **aarti.pad1** (हन्सावन ललिता): हन्स is हंस as this page types nasals; हंसावन read as the swans' lovely play (आवन, their coming, their movement), the base's word kept: हंसावलि would be a new word in the text, which no witness has. Meaning as given.
- **aarti.pad1** (रचयति कलाकलापं नृत्यति मुदसहिता): ललिता, the lovely one, is the subject who unfolds her arts and dances, the feminine agreeing with the participles. Meaning as given.
- **aarti.pad4** (the dancer not named): Śiva, whose ḍamaru the line names; नूपुरमुज्ज्वलिता describes the anklets' shining. Meaning as given.
- **aarti.pad8** (रामस्त्रिजटानाथं, सन्गतिमेवं): रामः + त्रिजटानाथम् (त्रिजट, an epithet of Śiva, MW), Rāma bowing to the Lord of the matted locks; सन्गति read as सङ्गीत, this song, which the line goes on to say is recited daily. Meaning as given.
