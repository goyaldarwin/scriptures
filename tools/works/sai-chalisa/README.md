# श्री साई चालीसा (Shri Sai Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Shirdi Sai Baba that opens "पहले साई के चरणों में", in Hindi verse (catalogued `awa`, as the shelf's other chālīsās). It is a long one: 102 rhyming couplets of two long lines each (16 + 14 mātrās, a tāṭaṅka-like metre), no dohā. Its poet names himself in the last verses: निर्भय (Nirbhay, verse 101).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 117–133), checked against Drik Panchang and the Chalisa Mala printout. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/sai-aashirwad/`: Sai Aashirwad (YouTube `mZ2KP0p-NYo`, "साई चालीसा |Sai Chalisa | Sai Baba Song | Shri Sai Chalisa | Bhakti Song | Sai Baba Bhajan |"), its track 0:00–13:32 of the video (after 13:32: music only to the video's end). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`cau` chaupāī), used for the couplets.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › one section, the verses (`chalisa-cau`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 102 couplets are numbered 1–102, as Drik Panchang and the Chalisa Mala number them, and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"). A verse is two printed lines (each line two halves, the book's comma between them), the first ending `।`, the second `॥`: one line per printed line, because each line is already as long as a whole chaupāī.
- **Ids:** as the Gaṇeśa Chālīsā's (`chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** who Sai is and his coming to Shirdi, his grace on his devotees, Kashiram saved, Sai of every faith, the false sadhu, Sai the avatar. **Reading plans:** none.
