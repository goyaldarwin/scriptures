# Reconciliation: आरती कीजै श्रीजनक लली की

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base)** (`hinduism/aarti/sita/janaki-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/janaki-mata-aarti.html`): the refrain couplet, then three stanzas of three lines, the refrain's first line marked after each. The owner's books have no copy of this āratī, so the page is the only witness.

The rule: keep the base; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (a pause within a line is a space); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Base | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| every stanza | lines ending । or a comma, the last ॥ | । and ॥N॥ | The standard. |
| stanza 1, line 1 | रामचन्द्र, मुखचन्द्र चकोरी | रामचन्द्र मुखचन्द्र चकोरी | The standard: no comma. |
| the refrain cue | आरती कीजै श्रीजनक लली की...॥ after each stanza | not written | The refrain is shown once, first. |
| stanza 1, line 3 | सुमन्गल | सुमंगल | The page types every nasal before a consonant as न्; न्ग is not a Hindi spelling. |
| stanza 2, line 1 | जुग-वन्धन डोरी | जुग-बन्धन डोरी | वन्धन is not a word: the cord (डोरी) that binds (बन्धन) the pair of the beloved's deer-like eyes; a व/ब typing slip. |

Kept as the base has them: कीजै, श्रीजनक लली, राममधुपमन, साँवर, दृगमृग, पिय and पीय (both as printed), रस-राशि, रूप-रास, गुननिधि, स्वामिनि, प्रवीन, अभिरामिनि, सरबस, हरिचन्द अली, and the page's hyphens.
