# आरती कीजै श्रीजनक लली की (Aarti Kijai Shri Janak Lali Ki)

The āratī of Sītā, Janaka's daughter, in Hindi (`hi`). Its last stanza names its poet: हरिचन्द (सरबस धन हरिचन्द अली की).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): Drik Panchang, "Janaki Mata Aarti" (no copy in the owner's books). Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/vrat-katha/`: Vrat Katha (YouTube `d7nooBAWoAg`, "जानकी माता की आरती | Janki Mata Ki Aarti | आरती कीजै श्रीजनक लली की | Sita Mata Ki Aarti"), its track 0:09–5:04 of the video (before 0:09: a spoken introduction; after 5:04: a spoken jaikāra after the last refrain: 'बोलो जानकी माता की जय, जय सियाराम'). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list.

Verse types: टेक (refrain, `tek`, shown once at the start although it is sung after every stanza) and पद (stanza, `pad`). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (Aarti Kijai Shri Janak Lali Ki) › one section (`aarti`) › verses: the refrain (`aarti.tek`, type `tek`) and 3 stanzas.
- **Numbering:** each stanza's `n` is its number (॥१॥ …), and it is cited by it ("Stanza 3"); the refrain has none.
- **Topics:** one, the āratī's title. **Reading plans:** none.
