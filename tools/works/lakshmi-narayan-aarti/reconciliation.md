# Reconciliation: जय लक्ष्मी-विष्णो

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** Shri Lakshminarayana Aarti (`hinduism/aarti/vishnu/lakshmi-narayan-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-lakshminarayana-aarti.html`): the refrain and seven stanzas, each half-line printed on its own line, with the refrain cue "जय लक्ष्मी-विष्णो" after every stanza.
- **The book:** none. The āratī is not in ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (`hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/`) or the other booklets; Drik Panchang is the only witness.

The rule: keep Drik Panchang; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (a pause within a line is a space); each printed line is a line, ending । and the stanza's last ॥N॥; the refrain (टेक) is shown once.

| Place | Base | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| after every stanza | जय लक्ष्मी-विष्णो। (refrain cue) | (not shown) | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item (टेक). |
| every line | the printed commas (also inside lines: जय माधव, जय श्रीपति, / जय, जय, जय विष्णो) | (none): जय माधव जय श्रीपति । / जय जय जय विष्णो ॥ | The standard: no commas in an āratī's verse; a pause within a line is a space. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥७॥ | The standard. |
| stanza 1, line 4 | जय अदभुत शान्ते | जय अद्भुत शान्ते | A dropped virāma: the word is अद्भुत ("wondrous"). |
| stanza 5, line 2 | सन्तित जय देनेवाली | सन्तति जय देनेवाली | सन्तित is a typing slip; the list "धन जन सुख सन्तति" (wealth, people, happiness, progeny) needs सन्तति. |
| stanza 7, line 4 | नव-मन्गल दाता | नव-मङ्गल दाता | Drik Panchang's typing of the nasal as न् before ग; the word is मङ्गल, written with the class nasal as Drik Panchang itself writes शङ्खादिकधारिन् in stanza 2. |

Kept as the base has them: the hyphens (लक्ष्मी-विष्णो, सम-वर्णे, स्मित-शोभे, वराभय-हस्ते, दिव्यानन्द-विलासिनि, लोक-त्रय-जननी, लक्ष्मी-नारायण, नव-मङ्गल), the Sanskrit vocatives (शङ्खादिकधारिन्, गरुडासनचारिन्, कमलालयवासिनि, सच्चिन्मयमूर्ते), बिधाता, the line divisions of stanza 5 (तुम धन जन सुख / सन्तति जय देनेवाली) and stanza 6 (चेतन और अचेतन / के अन्तर्यामी), as printed.
