# जय लक्ष्मी-विष्णो (Jai Lakshmi-Vishno)

The āratī of Lakṣmī-Nārāyaṇa (Viṣṇu with Lakṣmī), in Hindi (`hi`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): Drik Panchang, Shri Lakshminarayana Aarti; none of the printed booklets has it. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/bk-videography/`: BK Videography (YouTube `CQ110lVQlrE`, "जय लक्ष्मी-विष्णो 🙏 | Jai Lakshmi Narayan | लक्ष्मी नारायण आरती | भगवान विष्णु और माँ लक्ष्मी भजन"), its track 0:00–2:30 of the video (after 2:30: music). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list.

Verse types: टेक (refrain, `tek`, shown once at the start although it is sung after every stanza) and पद (stanza, `pad`). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (Jai Lakshmi-Vishno) › one section (`aarti`) › verses: the refrain (`aarti.tek`, type `tek`) and 7 stanzas.
- **Numbering:** each stanza's `n` is its number (॥१॥ …), and it is cited by it ("Stanza 3"); the refrain has none.
- **Topics:** one, the āratī's title. **Reading plans:** none.
