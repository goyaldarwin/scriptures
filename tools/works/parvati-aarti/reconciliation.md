# Reconciliation: जय पार्वती माता

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), p. 250, under the heading "आरती श्री पार्वती जी की"; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-249), read on the page image. Eight printed lines, one stanza each (the first the refrain), each line's halves joined by a comma.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/parvati/parvati-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/parvati-mata-aarti.html`): the same refrain and seven stanzas in the same order, each in four lines, with the cue "जय पार्वती माता" after each; a typed copy with its own spellings.
- **Aarti Mala** (page image i-348, an indif.com printout): the same version, one stanza per line, the first marked टेक; it has देव वधू and सारा मदमाता.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (the book's comma between the halves of a line becomes a line break, a half per line); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| every stanza | one printed line, its halves joined by a comma (the refrain has a second comma inside its first half) | two lines; the inner comma is a space | The standard; the comma marks the half. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥७॥ | The standard. |
| stanza 2, line 2 | देवबंधु जस गावत नृत्य करत ता था | देववधू जस गावत नृत्य करत ता था | A misprint (ब for व, ं for the ू mark): those who sing her praise and dance "tā thā" are the wives of the gods (देववधू, the apsarās); देवबंधु ("kin of the gods") does not dance in any witness. Drik Panchang and Aarti Mala have देव वधू, and InstaAstro (<https://instaastro.com/blog/maa-parvati-ji-ki-aarti/>), an independent web copy, renders the line as celestial women singing her praise while dancing. Written as one word, as the book joins it. |
| stanza 4, line 2 | सहस्त्र भुज | सहस्र भुज | सहस्त्र is a common misspelling of सहस्र ("thousand"); Drik Panchang सहस्र. |

Kept as the book has them, where Drik Panchang differs: शुभफल (Drik शुभ फल), अरिकुलपद्म विनासनी (Drik अरिकुल पद्म विनाशिनि), सेवकत्राता, जगजीवन जगदंबा (Drik separate, जगदम्बा), सिंह का बाहन (Drik सिंह को वाहन), ता था (Drik ताथा), रूप शील (Drik रूपशील), भुज (Drik भुजा), धरिके (Drik धरि के), सृष्टिरूप (Drik सृष्टि रूप), बीन लही है हाथन मदमाता (Drik बीन लही सारा जग मदमाता), देवन अरज करत तव चित को लाता (Drik हम), सदा सुखी (Drik सदासुखी).
