# Reconciliation: जगजननी जय जय

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** "Shri Devi Ji Ki Aarti" (`hinduism/aarti/durga/jagjanani-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-jagajanani-aarti.html`), headed श्रीदेवीजी की आरती. The refrain's two lines, then thirteen stanzas of two printed lines, each followed by the refrain cue जगजननी जय जय….
- The book (५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित) and the other booklets have no copy of this āratī; Drik Panchang is the only witness.
- **For a doubtful reading only:** a web search for stanza 8 (विवसन विकट-सरुपा) found no independent printing (the reading is settled below); Bhakti Bharat prints Drik Panchang's text and is not counted.

The rule: keep the base; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; the refrain (टेक) is shown once, first; stanzas numbered (॥१॥). The commas inside the lines separate the names in a list, not the halves of a line, so each printed line stays one line and its commas become spaces.

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| refrain, line 1 | जगजननी जय! जय!! (मा! जगजननी जय! जय!!)। | जगजननी जय जय । | The bracketed words are the sung repeat of the line, not a further line of the verse; the exclamation marks are the printer's punctuation. |
| after every stanza | the refrain cue जगजननी जय जय… | (not written) | The refrain is shown once. |
| refrain, line 2; stanzas 11, 12, 13 | जय! जय!!; विमले!; मा!; जननी!, करुणामयि! | जय जय; विमले; मा; जननी, करुणामयि | Exclamation marks are the printer's punctuation, not part of the verse. |
| refrain, line 2; stanzas 3–10, 12 | the commas inside the lines | (none) | The standard: no commas in an āratī's verse; a pause within a line is a space. |
| line ends | । and ॥ | । and, at a stanza's end, ॥N॥ | The standard. |
| stanza 8 | विकट-सरुपा | विकट-सरूपा | सरुपा is not a word: सरूपा, "having the form", its long ū dropped in typing; she sings सरूपा (settled below). |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥१३॥ | The standard. |

Kept as the base has them: the hyphens (सत-चित-सुखमय, पर-शिव, सुर-भूपा, सुर-मुनि-मोहिनि, विकट-सरूपा, स्नेह-सुधामयि, अस्थि-तना, इह-पर-सिद्धिप्रदे, विपत-जाल, चरण-शरण), आनँदराशी, कर्त्ता, भर्त्ता, सँहारकारी, विधिवधू, व्रजरानी, वांछाकल्पद्रुम, नानाशस्त्रकरा, ताण्डवलासिनि, शोभाऽऽधारा, गरलमना (settled below), मा, कीजै, दीजै.

Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
- **aarti.pad8** (विवसन विकट-सरुपा): सरुपा is not a word: the line means सरूपा, "having the form" (विकट-सरूपा, of fearsome form), the long ū dropped in typing; Anuradha Paudwal sings सरूपा (a fresh Whisper transcript of 1584–1592 s hears "विख़त सरुपा … स्वरुपा"). Corrected to सरूपा (above, Choices).
- **aarti.pad9** (तू अति गरलमना): गरलमना, "poison-minded", set against स्नेह-सुधामयि in the same line: the Goddess full of the nectar of love to the good and deadly as poison to the wicked. Meaning as given.
