# श्री गोपाल चालीसा (Shri Gopal Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Gopāla (Kṛṣṇa) that opens "श्री राधापद कमल रज", in Awadhi (`awa`); it runs through Kṛṣṇa's deeds from his birth to the Mahābhārata, then the avatars, and closes with a prayer.

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 43–47), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/nova-spiritual-india/`: Nova Spiritual India (YouTube `hiZB_bwV0R4`, "गोपाल चालीसा Gopal Chalisa with Lyrics | Santan Gopal Mantra | Garbh Sanskar | Krishna Bhajan"), its track 0:00–10:44 of the video (after 10:44: music only). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list.
- **Poet:** the text names him in its closing chhanda: Jayarāmadeva (जयरामदेव).

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī, `cha` chhanda).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohā (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing chhanda and dohā (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 41 chaupāīs are numbered 1–41 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the dohās and the chhanda have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Chhand", "Closing · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Hanumān Chālīsā's in the Sundarakāṇḍa (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1`, `chalisa.cha1` …).
- **Topics:** birth and childhood play, the deeds in Braj, the deeds in Mathurā and after, the avatars and the devotees saved, the prayer and the fruit of the reading. **Reading plans:** none.
