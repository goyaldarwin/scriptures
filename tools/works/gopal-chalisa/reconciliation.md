# Reconciliation: श्री गोपाल चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 43–47; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-042 … i-046), read on the page images. The closing dohā (प्रणत पाल अशरण शरण) is at the top of p. 47 (i-046), above the āratī आरती श्री गोपाल जी की, which is its own work.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/krishna/gopal-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-gopala-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, the same dohās, 41 chaupāīs and chhanda in the same order, with its own slips (रुप, अम्बरीय).

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the chhanda's four lines as printed, ending `।` `॥` `।` `॥`; the book's commas are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chhanda | 'जयरामदेव' सदैव सो | जयरामदेव सदैव सो | The quotation marks (marking the poet's name) are the printer's, not part of the verse. |

No word of the book is changed. The two texts agree word for word but for Drik Panchang's slips रुप (chaupāī 32, for रूप) and अम्बरीय (chaupāī 33, for अम्बरीष); the book's hyphen in करुणा-सिन्धु (closing dohā) is kept, as Drik Panchang has it.

## Readings settled
- chalisa.cau41 — देहि सकल बाँछित फल शीशा: kept शीशा as "head" ("is given every fruit he desires, upon his head"). The book, Drik Panchang and Shubh Panchang (shubhpanchang.com) all print शीशा; ईशा is in none, so the plain word stands.
- chalisa.cha1 — जो भक्तजन सन महँ चहैं: सन now read as printed, the dialect postposition "from" (Platts san, postpn. = se), not as मन: every witness (the book, Drik Panchang, Shubh Panchang) prints सन. The meaning changes from "devotees desire in their hearts" to "that devotees seek from him"; the work's own word entry सन = मन is removed (the shared dictionary's सन "से / साथ" covers it).
