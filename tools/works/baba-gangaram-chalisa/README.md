# श्री बाबा गंगाराम चालीसा (Shri Baba Gangaram Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Bābā Gaṅgārāma of Jhunjhunu (झुंझनूं), that opens "अलख निरंजन आप हैं", in Awadhi (`awa`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 167–171), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Poet:** chaupāī 40 names महावीर ("महावीर धर ध्यान पुनीता । विरचेउ गंगाराम सुगीता").
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** none yet.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohās (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohā (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 40 chaupāīs are numbered 1–40 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the three dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Hanumān Chālīsā's in the Sundarakāṇḍa (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** his birth and austerity, the message from the world of the gods, his grace on the devotees, the Jhunjhunu shrine and praise. **Reading plans:** none.
