# Reconciliation: श्री बाबा गंगाराम चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), pp. 167–171; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-166 … i-170; the closing dohā is on i-170, above the book's "आरती बाबा गंगाराम जी की", which is a work of its own), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/baba-gangaram/baba-gangaram-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-baba-gangaram-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, word for word but for spellings.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 31 | जहँ जन्में | जहँ जन्मे | जन्में is a printer's form with a stray anusvāra: the past tense of जन्मना is जन्मे, as our meanings give it. |
| chaupāī 40 | 'महावीर' धर ध्यान पुनीता | महावीर धर ध्यान पुनीता | The quotation marks (around the poet's name) are the printer's, not part of the verse. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: संदेश (chaupāī 10; Drik सन्देश), भांति (chaupāīs 15, 26; Drik भाँति), प्रातःकाल (chaupāī 37; Drik प्रातः काल), पूरई (closing dohā; Drik पुरई). Kept as both have them: अरू, रहेऊ / भयेउ, नाहीं / गुसाई, रावरो, निश्छल, मँह, विरचेउ.

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
- chalisa.cau5 — बीतहिं जन्म: kept, as his life went by he had no thought for his body (बीतना, to pass, of time: Platts). The plain words; both witnesses agree.
- chalisa.cau12 — the subject of कीन्हों: changed, Bābā did all the work for dharma (was a passive). दीन्हों and कीन्हों are parallel verbs of one subject, Bābā, the subject of the stanza before (पठाया, दिखावा, बतलावा).
- chalisa.cau23 — सुखसंतोष लहै न मन में: changed, a question, do they not find happiness and contentment in their minds? लहना is to find, get (Platts); "cannot contain" has no dictionary support, and the literal statement contradicts the stanza. Both witnesses print लहै न; a web search found only Bhakti Bharat (a copy of Drik Panchang), so the text stays as printed.
- chalisa.cau40 — महावीर: kept, the poet's name (the signature): the book and Drik Panchang both print it in quotation marks, 'महावीर'.
