# Gurmat Sangeet Darbar (Kirtan)

Kirtan in raag from the YouTube channel Gurmat Sangeet Darbar, one video for each of 13 items Shabad Guru has no kirtan of:
the item its title matches. Timed line by line (`tools/audio/METHOD.md`, "Line by line"). `recordings.json` is made by
`../kirtan_recordings.py` from the channel's table in `scriptures-data`
(`sikhism/gurbani/guru-granth-sahib/printouts/gurmat-sangeet-darbar/videos.csv`); the recordings are in `scriptures-data`
at `sikhism/gurbani/guru-granth-sahib/audio/gurmat-sangeet-darbar/`. How the starts were made and what is listed to check:
`aligned.md`.

The channel's other videos of items Shabad Guru or another source already has are listed, not used
(`printouts/gurmat-sangeet-darbar/downloads.csv` there).
