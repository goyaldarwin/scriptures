# Sehaj Path Kirtan (Bhai Parminder Singh, Bhai Jagjit Singh)

The whole Granth sung through by Bhai Parminder Singh and Bhai Jagjit Singh, in 40 tracks of the series "Sehaj Path Kirtan"
on YouTube: Jap to the Mundavani, one track per raag (Gauri in two), then the banis after the raags. It is the Granth's
default recording (`docs/PLAYBOOK.md` §8: a complete series sung through is a choice of its own). Timed line by line
(`tools/audio/METHOD.md`, "Line by line"); tracks over 40 minutes are aligned in overlapping 8-minute pieces, each looking
± 6% of its track for its verses. `recordings.json` is made by `make_recordings.py` from the series' table in
`scriptures-data` (`sikhism/gurbani/guru-granth-sahib/printouts/sehaj-path-kirtan/videos.csv`): each track sings whole
parts of the Granth (`work.json` "parts"), Gauri cut at Ang 250 where its second track begins. The recordings are in
`scriptures-data` at `sikhism/gurbani/guru-granth-sahib/audio/sehaj-path-kirtan/`. How the starts were made and what is
listed to check: `aligned.md`.

Tracks 34–40 are also in the Granth's Kirtan source (`../shabad-guru/`), where they sing what its other singers lack; their
MMS results are the same here. The Raag Mala (1429.13) is in no track.
