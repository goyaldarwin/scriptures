# Sehaj Path Kirtan (Bhai Parminder Singh, Bhai Jagjit Singh)

The end of the Granth sung through, Ang 1353 to 1429, in seven tracks (34–40 of the series): the saloks, swayyas and
short banis Shabad Guru has no kirtan of. Timed line by line (`tools/audio/METHOD.md`, "Line by line"); the four tracks
over 40 minutes (34, 35, 37, 38) are aligned in overlapping 8-minute pieces. `recordings.json` is made by
`../kirtan_recordings.py` (its `SPK`: the first and last item of each track, at the headings of the banis its title
names); the recordings are in `scriptures-data` at `sikhism/gurbani/guru-granth-sahib/audio/sehaj-path-kirtan/`, the Angs
each covers in its `.meta.json`. How the starts were made and what is listed to check: `aligned.md`.
