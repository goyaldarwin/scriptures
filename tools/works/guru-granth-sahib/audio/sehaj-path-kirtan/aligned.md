# Line starts from Meta's MMS (`aligned.json`, `lines.json`)

**What:** for Sehaj Path Kirtan (Bhai Parminder Singh, Bhai Jagjit Singh)'s 40 recordings (103.6 h; `recordings.json`), every singing of every line of the verse each sings, found in MMS's frame-by-frame letter probabilities (torchaudio `MMS_FA`, the whole track at once) by `tools/audio/line_align.py`: a search through how a shabad is sung (the rahao before the first pada and after each, lines and padas sung again, a stanza on its own) with a filler for what the text does not hold (alaap, music, a dhaarna, lines of another shabad), and the plain in-order alignment beside it (`tools/audio/METHOD.md`, "Line by line"); one run per recording in the public `align` workflow (`mms_align.py`, raw results in `mms/<video id>.json`); `mms_merge.py` writes `aligned.json` (each verse's first singing), `lines.json` (the singings the search scores 0.35 or more, and the in-order alignment's first singings of lines it left out, where the lines around place them: `line_align.merge`; a singing that swallowed the lines before it shared by length among them and its line: `line_align.split_swallowed`) and `check.json`. Each start is MMS's less the lean, 0.6 s until the owner has set starts by ear here.
