# Har Ki Oat (Kirtan)

Kirtan from the YouTube channel Har Ki Oat (Sri Darbar Sahib's hazuri ragis), one video for each of 50 items Shabad Guru
has no kirtan of: the item its title matches, a shabad, or a single salok or pauri sung as a shabad. Timed line by line
(`tools/audio/METHOD.md`, "Line by line"). `recordings.json` is made by `../kirtan_recordings.py` from the channel's table
in `scriptures-data` (`sikhism/gurbani/guru-granth-sahib/printouts/har-ki-oat/videos.csv`); the recordings are in
`scriptures-data` at `sikhism/gurbani/guru-granth-sahib/audio/har-ki-oat/`. How the starts were made and what is listed to
check: `aligned.md`.

The channel's other videos of items Shabad Guru or another source already has are listed, not used
(`printouts/har-ki-oat/downloads.csv` there).
