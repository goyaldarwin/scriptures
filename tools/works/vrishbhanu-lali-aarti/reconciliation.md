# Reconciliation: आरति श्रीवृषभानुलली की

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** "Devi Radhika Aarti" (`hinduism/aarti/radha/vrishbhanu-lali-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/devi-radhika-aarti.html`), headed देवी राधिका आरती. Four stanzas (भयभन्जिनि, अखिल विश्व, नित्यानन्दमयी, नित्य निकुन्जेश्वरि), the first opening with the refrain's two lines, and the refrain after each.
- The book (५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित) and the other booklets have no copy of this āratī; Drik Panchang is the only witness.

The rule: keep the base; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; the refrain (टेक) is shown once, first; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| stanza 1 | opens with आरति श्रीवृषभानुलली की। सत-चित-आनन्द कन्द-कली की॥ | the refrain (`aarti.tek`) | The refrain, which the page repeats after every stanza; shown once, first. |
| after every stanza | the refrain | (not written) | The refrain is shown once. |
| stanza 3, line 3 | रसमयि, रसमय-मन-उन्मादिनि, | रसमयि रसमय-मन-उन्मादिनि । | No commas in an āratī's verse; a pause within a line is a space. |
| line ends | , । ॥ | । and, at a stanza's end, ॥N॥ | The standard. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥४॥ | The standard. |

Kept as the base has them: आरति, the hyphens (सत-चित-आनन्द, कन्द-कली, भव-सागर-तारिणि, पाप-ताप-कलि-कल्मष-हारिणि, गोलोक-विहारिणि, विश्व-आनन्द-विधायिनि, नन्दनन्दन-पदप्रेम, अमिय-राग-रस, रंग-रली, आनन्दघन-आनन्द-प्रसाधिनि, रसमय-मन-उन्मादिनि, कृष्ण-अली, भाव-अवली), भयभन्जिनि, निकुन्जेश्वरि, गोपिगणाश्रयि.
