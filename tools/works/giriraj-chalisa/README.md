# श्री गिरिराज चालीसा (Shri Giriraj Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Girirāja Govardhana, the hill of Braj worshipped as Kṛṣṇa, that opens "बन्दहुँ वीणा वादिनी", in Awadhi (`awa`; its language leans to Braj: कूँ, पामें, तिहारौ).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 135–139), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/ved-puraan/`: Ved Puraan (YouTube `ae43Hp90buA`, "⁠ગિરિરાજ ચાલીસા | Gujarati Meaning"), its track 0:00–6:25 of the video (after 6:25: the closing words 'इति श्री गिरिराज चालीसा सम्पूर्ण'). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list.
- **Poet:** the text names him in its last closing dohā (and as "श्याम" in chaupāī 37): Śyāma Bihārī.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohās (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohās (`chalisa-ant`) › verses. The āratī the book prints after it (ॐ जय जय जय गिरिराज) is its own work.
- **Numbering:** the 40 chaupāīs are numbered 1–40 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the four dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Hanumān Chālīsā's in the Sundarakāṇḍa (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** how Girirāja came to Braj, the Govardhana worship and the lifting of the hill, the fruit of serving Girirāja. **Reading plans:** none.
