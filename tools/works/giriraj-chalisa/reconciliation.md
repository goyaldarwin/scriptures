# Reconciliation: श्री गिरिराज चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 135–139; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-134 … i-138), read on the page images. The āratī printed after it on p. 139 (आरती श्री गिरिराज जी की) is not part of this work.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/krishna/giriraj-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-giriraj-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, the same four dohās and 40 chaupāīs in the same order.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥` (the book ends every dohā line with a single daṇḍa); the book's commas are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 22 | नमो नमो ब्रज के पखवारे | नमो नमो ब्रज के रखवारे | A misprint (प for र): रखवारे, "protector of Braj", as Drik Panchang has; पखवारे ("fortnight") makes no sense. |
| chaupāī 34 | जल फल तुलसी पत्र पढ़ावें | जल फल तुलसी पत्र चढ़ावें | A misprint (प for च): one offers (चढ़ाना) water, fruit and tulasī leaves, as Drik Panchang has and the book itself says in chaupāī 33 (दूध चढ़ा). |
| chaupāī 37 | 'श्याम' शिलामय | श्याम शिलामय | The quotation marks (marking the poet's name) are the printer's, not part of the verse. |
| closing dohā 1 | जो यह चालिसा पढ़ै | जो यह चालीसा पढ़ै | A misspelling: the book's own title and Drik Panchang have चालीसा. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: लखि नर नारी (chaupāī 18; Drik नारि), गिरिवर करैं सहाय (Drik करै). Otherwise the two texts agree word for word.

## Readings settled
- chalisa.cau7 — मुनिवर संघ … लखि ब्रजभूमि यहाँ ठहराये: read as printed, संघ "company" (Platts saṅgh) and ठहराये transitive, "set (you) down" (Platts ṭhahrānā, v.t. "to cause to stay"): "When the company of great sages came into Braja, they saw the land of Braja and set you down here", as Pulastya set Govardhana down in Braja. Both witnesses print संघ; the meaning no longer reads it as संग.
