# Reconciliation: श्री अन्नपूर्णा चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 241–244; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-240 … i-243), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/annapurna/annapurna-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/annapurna-mata-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, sharing most of the book's slips (कीह्न, इज्ञ, पदिदेवता, गाय, मनभाय).
- **For a doubtful reading only:** the text as other sites print it, found by web search (Shubh Panchang, shubhpanchang.com/hi/chalisa/annapurna-chalisa; InstaAstro, instaastro.com/blog/shri-annapurna-mata-chalisa). Bhakti Bharat prints Drik Panchang's text and is not counted.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas and exclamation marks are not kept; its hyphens are.

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: आनंद, सिंधु, संतन, अनंत, सिंधुजा with the anusvāra (Drik आनन्द, सिन्धु, सन्तन, अनन्त), बृषभारूढ़ (Drik बृषभारुढ़), गिरि-नंदिनी (Drik नंदिनि), पति-विछोह, हिमगिरी-गेहू (Drik हिमगिरि), ब्रह्मा-वरुण-कुबेर-गनाये, संत-बचन, अनुरूपा, सरूपा (Drik अनुरुपा, सरुपा), स्वर्ग-महालछमी, लछमी (Drik स्वर्ग-महालक्ष्मी, लक्ष्मी), सोहै / मोहे, अधिकायो (Drik अघिकायो). The readings सुतीत, निज कौ तव, करमँह and अन्नपूर्णे सदपूर्णे (a short half) are in every witness and stay.

## Readings settled
- chalisa.cau20 — करत वेद विद ब्रह्मा: "Brahmā, who brings forth the Vedas and knows them" stays; करत is "making, doing" (Platts), never a verb of speaking, and वेद is its object; Shubh Panchang and Drik Panchang print करत too.
- chalisa.cau30 — अपूर्णे: "O Aparṇā" (Pārvatī) stays; the line is a row of praises (अज, अनवद्य, अनंत) where "O incomplete one" contradicts सदपूर्णे beside it, and Aparṇā, the name Pārvatī won by her penance (Kumārasambhava 5.28), is the name of the penance this chālīsā has just told (cau15–27), its spelling bent to rhyme with पूर्णे.
- chalisa.cau31 — भव-विभूति: "the world's riches" stays; the Mother praised as Annapūrṇā, giver of plenty, and the half pairs विभूति with आनंद, plenty and bliss; the glossary keeps Śiva's glory as its second sense.
- chalisa.cau39 — the subject of राज विमुखको राज दिवावै: the reading (पाठ) stays; cau36–40 are its fruits, cau40 names it (पाठ महा मुद मंगल दाता), and the Mother herself is addressed (तेरो) in the second half, not spoken of.
