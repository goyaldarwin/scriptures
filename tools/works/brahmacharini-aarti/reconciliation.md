# Reconciliation: जय अम्बे ब्रह्मचारिणी माता

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** "Brahmacharini Mata Aarti", under the heading "आरती देवी ब्रह्मचारिणी जी की" (`hinduism/aarti/durga/brahmacharini-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/devi-brahmacharini-aarti.html`): five groups of four lines in chaupāī couplets (each line ending । or ॥), no refrain and no refrain cue.
- **The book:** the booklets in scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` do not print this āratī, so Drik Panchang is the only written witness.
- **For a doubtful reading only (web search):** रद्रक्षा (stanza 4); Navbharat Live (navbharatlive.com/religion/navratri-day-2-maa-brahmacharini-aarti-mantra-rrs05) and Patrika (patrika.com/religion-news/maa-brahmacharini-mantra-shardiya-navratri-day-2-invoke-maa-with-mantras-and-aarti-every-wish-fulfilled-19965169) both print रुद्राक्ष.

The rule: keep the base; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Base | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| each group | four lines, the second and fourth ending ॥ | one stanza: lines ending ।, the last ॥N॥ | The standard; each group of the page is a stanza. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥५॥ | The standard. |
| stanza 4, line 1 | रद्रक्षा की माला ले कर | रुद्राक्ष की माला ले कर | रद्रक्षा is not a word: a typing slip for रुद्राक्ष ("rudrākṣa beads", the rosary the next line tells with), as the two sites above print it. |

Kept as the base has them: चतुरानन प्रिय सुख दाता, ब्रह्मा जी, सिखलाती, ब्रह्म मन्त्र, जिसको जपे सरल संसारा, तुम्हें ध्याता, ना पाये and न पाये, विरति, ले कर and दे कर (two words each), गुणगाना, पहुँचाना, तेरो नाम, महतारी.
