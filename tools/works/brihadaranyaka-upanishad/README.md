# Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad (बृहदारण्यकोपनिषद्)

Shelf: हिन्दू धर्म › वेद › यजुर्वेद › शुक्ल यजुर्वेद › उपनिषद् › बृहदारण्यकोपनिषद् (`library/hinduism/veda/yajurveda/shukla-yajurveda/upanishad/brihadaranyaka-upanishad/`, work id `brihadaranyaka-upanishad`), beside the Īśāvāsya. The Upaniṣad of the Śukla Yajurveda in its Kāṇva recension (काण्व पाठः, as the printout titles it): six adhyāyas of 47 brāhmaṇas, 435 mantras (80, 66, 92, 92, 30, 75), with the śānti mantra पूर्णमदः पूर्णमिदम् before them; the work ends with 6.5.4.

## Files
The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.
- `source/extract.py <the PDF>` → `source/pdf_raw.json`: each mantra's printed rows (page, row, text), the śānti and the headings, read from the content stream.
- `source/settle.py` → `text.txt`: the settled text, one verse per mantra, one sentence per line, with the parts, the sections and the topics; the export writes it by the library's standard and makes the transliteration (PLAYBOOK §3). Its `FIX` and `NOTES` hold every correction (`reconciliation.md`).
- `source/witness_upanishads_org.py <folder of the saved pages>` (the archive in scriptures-data, unpacked) → `source/witness/upanishads-org-in.txt`.
- `source/witness_iitk.py <folder of the batch JSON>` (the archive in scriptures-data, unpacked) → `source/witness/iitk.txt`.
- `source/check.py`: every mantra of each witness must spell the same letters as `text.txt` once the witness's word breaks are joined with the printout's sandhi; every difference is listed with its decision in `source/witness/<witness>.decisions.json`. Run it after any change to the text.
- `meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`: our meanings, with footnotes (`notes`).
- `glossary/sa.json`: the senses of words only this work uses.
- `audio/himalaya-swamyji/`: the recording source.

Rebuild: `python3 tools/works/brihadaranyaka-upanishad/source/settle.py && python3 tools/works/brihadaranyaka-upanishad/source/check.py && python3 tools/works/export.py brihadaranyaka-upanishad && python3 tools/site/build.py`. Run `extract.py` again only if the PDF changes.

## Structure
- **Parts:** the six adhyāyas, प्रथम … षष्ठ अध्याय. The printout groups them in three kāṇḍas (मधु काण्ड 1–2, मुनि (याज्ञवल्क्य) काण्ड 3–4, खिल काण्ड 5–6); the library's format is part › section › verse, and a mantra's citation takes its part's key, the number its section ends with and its own number (`tools/text/cite.py`), so a kāṇḍa level above the adhyāya would be a fourth level the format does not have. The kāṇḍas are not parts; the adhyāya keeps its own number (Adhyay 1 … 6), as the book cites it.
- **Sections (pages):** the 47 brāhmaṇas (`b1.1` … `b6.5`), plus `shanti` (opening).
- **Hierarchy:** six adhyāyas (parts) › brāhmaṇas (sections) › mantras.
- **Items:** `shanti.1`; `1.1.1` … `6.5.4` (436 items).
- **Numbering:** a mantra's `n` is its number within its brāhmaṇa, as the printout heads it (मन्त्र N [I.ii.N]) and ends it (॥१॥); it is cited adhyāya.brāhmaṇa.mantra, like the Kaṭha's adhyāya.vallī.mantra (`work.json` → `cite`: Mantra 4.3.21). 3.9.28 is one mantra holding seven ślokas, numbered ॥१॥ … ॥७॥ inside it as the printout prints them, and it ends ॥२८॥. Ids are permanent.
- **Types:** `shanti` (शा॰ / Shan.) and `mantra` (मं॰ / Man.), as in the Kaṭha and the Īśāvāsya.
- **Topics:** one per brāhmaṇa with its theme, in our words, plus the śānti pāṭha.
- **Lines:** the printout runs each mantra as one paragraph that the browser wrapped; the text sets one sentence per line, a line ending at each daṇḍa.
- **Loading:** whole, not `"load": "parts"` (PLAYBOOK §10): the 436 verses with their transliteration are 0.48 MB (`work.json`), and meanings in two languages of the Kaṭha's length per mantra keep the work well under the few MB at which a phone should fetch a work by part.
- **Reading plans:** none yet.
