# Kirtan Sohila — reconciliation

The text is Sri Guru Granth Sahib's, taken unchanged (`text.txt` → `[take guru-granth-sahib 12.3 .. 13.3]`). Its
witnesses and every choice are in `tools/works/guru-granth-sahib/reconciliation.md`; a correction is made there and

## Where it begins and ends

Checked against the Granth's data (`library/sikhism/gurbani/guru-granth-sahib/work.json`, made from `text.txt`), not
written from memory:

- **First verse** `12.3` (Ang 12), its first lines: ਸੋਹਿਲਾ ਰਾਗੁ ਗਉੜੀ ਦੀਪਕੀ ਮਹਲਾ ੧ ॥ / ੴ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ ॥ / ਜੈ ਘਰਿ ਕੀਰਤਿ ਆਖੀਐ ਕਰਤੇ ਕਾ ਹੋਇ ਬੀਚਾਰੋ ॥
- **Last verse** `13.3` (Ang 13), its last line: ਨਾਨਕ ਦਾਸੁ ਇਹੈ ਸੁਖੁ ਮਾਗੈ ਮੋ ਕਉ ਕਰਿ ਸੰਤਨ ਕੀ ਧੂਰੇ ॥੪॥੫॥
- The verse before it in the Granth ends ਸਰਣਿ ਪਰੇ ਕੀ ਰਾਖਹੁ ਸਰਮਾ ॥੨॥੪॥ and the verse after it begins
  ੴ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ ॥: neither is part of the bani.
- 5 shabads; Ang 12–13, as the Granth's Ang view gives the pages of its first and last lines.
