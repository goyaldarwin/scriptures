# Reconciliation: बजरंग बाण

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 28–31; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-027 … i-030), read on the page images. The work begins under its heading बजरंग बाण on p. 28; the dohā above it on that page (लाल देह लाली लसे) closes the Saṅkaṭamocana Hanumānāṣṭaka and is not part of this work.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/hanuman/bajrang-baan/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-bajarang-baan.html`): a typed copy of the same text, the same 35 chaupāīs in the same order, with its own readings (ॐ for the book's ओं, and an ॐ before the हनु हनु chaupāī where the book has श्री).

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 19 | वन उपवन मग, गिरी गृह माँही | वन उपवन मग गिरि गृह माँही | गिरि, "mountain", as the list (forest, grove, road, mountain, house) needs and Drik Panchang has; गिरी ("fallen") gives the half a seventeenth mātrā. The comma inside the half is not kept. |
| chaupāī 20 | पाँय परौ कर जोरि | पाँय परौं कर जोरि | The anusvāra is missing on the page; परौं ("I fall"), first person like मनावौं, as the book itself prints it in chaupāī 28 and Drik Panchang has. |
| chaupāī 29 | ओं हनु हुन हुन हनु हनुमन्ता | ओं हनु हनु हनु हनु हनुमन्ता | A misprint: हुन is not a word; the bīja is हनु, as in chaupāī 12 and Drik Panchang. |

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
- **chaupāī 3**, सिन्धु महि पारा … सुरसा बदन पैठि विस्तारा: महि as the book prints it ('to the land beyond'; Drik Panchang वहि is not clearly better), and विस्तारा with Surasā's mouth, the noun beside it: 'entered Surasā's mouth when she spread it wide'.
- **chaupāī 6**, सिंधु मँह बोरा … यम कातर तोरा: the garden, the only object in the half, is what was sunk; यम कातर as Yama's dagger (कातर for कटार). 'Like Yama you broke the frightened ones' would add a comparison and an object the verse does not print. Plain reading.
- **chaupāī 25**, जनक सुता हरिदास कहावो । ताकी शपथ: 'the servant of Janaka's daughter and of Hari; by her oath', as the book prints it (हरिदास joined); Rāma's oath was invoked in chaupāī 24, and ताकी follows जनक सुता. Plain reading kept.
