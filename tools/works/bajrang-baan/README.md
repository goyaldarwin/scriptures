# बजरंग बाण (Bajrang Baan)

The Bajrang Bāṇ, the "arrow of Bajrang", a prayer to Hanumān in chālīsā form that opens "निश्चय प्रेम प्रतीति ते", in Awadhi (`awa`); it calls on him, by the oath of Rāma and Sītā and with bīja syllables (ओं ह्रीं, हुँ, चं, हं, सं), to come at once and destroy the devotee's foes.

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 28–31), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/aditya-bhakti-sagar/`: Aditya Bhakti Sagar (YouTube `aH8PEBlOCSg`, "Bajrang Baan with lyrics in Hindi by Anup Jalota hariharan Gulshan Kumar बजरंग बाण अनूप जलोटा 2020"), its track 0:00–6:28 of the video (after 6:28: a closing dohā not in the text). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list. Not sung, so untimed: `chalisa.do2`.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohā (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohā (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 35 chaupāīs are numbered 1–35 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the two dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Hanumān Chālīsā's in the Sundarakāṇḍa (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** Hanumān's deeds in Laṅkā, delay no more, the oath of Rāma, the fruit of the Bajrang Bāṇ. **Reading plans:** none.
