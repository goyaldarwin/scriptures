# श्री शीतला चालीसा (Shri Shitala Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Śītalā that opens "जय-जय माता शीतला तुमहिं धरै जो ध्यान", in Awadhi (`awa`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 215–218), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/mantra-duniya/`: Mantra Duniya (YouTube `kyTMnUnElYo`, "Shree Sheetala Mata Chalisa"), its track 0:00–11:52 of the video (after 11:52: the singer's refrain 'पापनाशिनी दुखहरणी माँ शीतला'). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list. A draft (it plays only with Audio timing on): it sings the dohā घट-घट वासी last, as Drik Panchang prints it, and the text follows that order.
- **Poet:** chaupāīs 36–37 name him, Rāmasundara Prabhudāsa, of Purī Tiwārī east of Prayāga, by Kakarā on the Gaṅgā (the same signature as the Gaṇeśa Chālīsā's chaupāī 37).

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohā (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`) and the closing dohā (`chalisa-ant`) › verses. The book prints the dohā घट-घट वासी second at the opening; Drik Panchang prints it at the end, where the recording sings it, and the text follows that order (`chalisa.do2`, its id unchanged).
- **Numbering:** the 39 chaupāīs are numbered 1–39 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"), as the book has them; the two dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Hanumān Chālīsā's in the Sundarakāṇḍa (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** the Mother's form, the subduing of the demon Visphoṭaka, praise of the Mother, the fruit of remembering her, the poet's signature and prayer. **Reading plans:** none.
