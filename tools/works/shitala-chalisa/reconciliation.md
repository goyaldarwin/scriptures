# Reconciliation: श्री शीतला चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 215–218; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-214 … i-217), read on the page images. The āratī printed after it (p. 219, i-218) is its own work.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/shitala/shitala-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shitala-mata-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same 39 chaupāīs, with the dohā घट-घट वासी शीतला placed after them as a closing dohā, and its own slips (कन्दिनी, मे).

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas and quotation marks are not kept; its hyphens are.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 24 | नमो नमो त्रलोक्य वन्दिनी | नमो नमो त्रैलोक्य वन्दिनी | त्रलोक्य is a dropped ै: त्रैलोक्य ("the three worlds") is the word, and the half needs its 16 mātrās; Drik Panchang has त्रैलोक्य. |
| chaupāī 37 | पुरी तिवारी मोर मोर निवासा | पुरी तिवारी मोर निवासा | मोर is printed twice (a typesetter's repeat): the half then has 19 mātrās for 16, and Drik Panchang has it once. |
| chaupāī 36 | भनत 'रामसुन्दर' प्रभुदासा | भनत रामसुन्दर प्रभुदासा | The quotation marks are the printer's, not part of the verse. |
| the dohā घट-घट वासी शीतला | second opening dohā (before the chaupāīs) | the same (`chalisa.do2`) | The book's order is kept; Drik Panchang has it as the closing dohā. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: शरदचंद्र (Drik शरदचन्द्र), बजावैं (Drik बजावै), पार न पावैं (Drik ना), हरिहैं in chaupāī 16 (Drik हरिहौं; neither is clearly wrong), निकन्दनी (Drik कन्दिनी, a syllable short), जग में (Drik मे). Kept as both have them: सक्यो न जब संकट टारी (a half one mātrā short), रुणलीह्युणनी, पड़ा क्षर.

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
The open questions of `docs/reviews/shitala-chalisa-doubts.jsonl`, each decided (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §2).

- **chalisa.cau16** — who speaks जहँ अपवित्र सकल दुःख हरिहैं, and the book's हरिहैं against Drik Panchang's हरिहौं: the book's हरिहैं kept and read as the demon's speech going on (from chaupāī 15): "where there is impurity, all its sorrow will be taken away". हरिहैं is a third-person future with no subject named, so the Hindi and English give it in the passive; हरिहौं is Drik Panchang's typing of the same edition, not a correction of it, and a search found no independent witness for either; chaupāī 17 goes on in the same third-person future, and chaupāī 18 closes the episode. Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.cau25** — रुणलीह्युणनी, an unclear folk name: kept and transliterated as printed (Ruṇalīhyuṇanī). The page image (i-216) prints the word so, Drik Panchang the same; a split into another pair of names (रुणकी-झुणकी) is not attested by any witness. Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.cau37** — पुरी तिवारी, a place or the poet's caste: read as the place-name the words give ("Purī Tivārī is my home"), with Kakarā on the Gaṅgā by Durvāsā's hermitage; "I am a Tivārī" needs words the line does not have. Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.cau38** — अब विलम्ब with no verb: the plain reading "now it grows late" (विलम्ब, "delay", the glossary; Monier-Williams vilamba) kept; "(make no) delay" needs a negative the line does not have. Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.cau39** — पड़ा क्षर: kept as printed (the page image i-217 prints क्षर clearly, Drik Panchang the same) and read "this perishable one lies here" (क्षर, "perishable", Monier-Williams, Apte). द्वार ("at your door") is attested by no witness (Shubh Panchang has क्षति), so the print is not shown to be a corruption. Meaning unchanged.
