# Reconciliation: श्री गोरख चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 95–99; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-094 … i-098), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/gorakhnath/gorakhnath-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-gorakha-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, verse for verse, with its own slips (निराकर, करुण, ब्रह्मा for ब्रह्म, होइ).
- **For a doubtful reading only:** the text as InstaAstro prints it ("Shri Goraksh Ji Ki Chalisa", instaastro.com), found by web search; Bhakti Bharat's Goraksh Chalisa page was also found.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| closing dohā 2 | अगर अगोचर नाथ तुम | अगम अगोचर नाथ तुम | अगर ("if") makes no sense; अगम अगोचर ("beyond reach, beyond the senses") is the pair of chaupāī 30 (अगम अगोचर निर्भय नाथा), and Drik Panchang and InstaAstro have अगम. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: विनती (opening dohā; Drik बिनती), रूप (chaupāīs 2, 6, 13, 14, 16; Drik रुप), पिशाच (chaupāī 5; Drik पिसाच), निराकार (chaupāī 7; Drik निराकर), अङ्ग (chaupāī 9; Drik अंग), मंगल करण (chaupāī 11; Drik करुण), डमरू (chaupāī 14), सुर नर मुनि जन पावें न पारा (chaupāī 16; Drik सुर नर मुनि पावै न पारा), हरो पाप हर शरण तुम्हारी (chaupāī 17; Drik and InstaAstro हम शरण: हर, "O Hara", is a valid reading of a chālīsā that calls Gorakhnāth Śiva; `docs/reviews/gorakhnath-chalisa-doubts.jsonl`), सन्तन (chaupāī 18; Drik संतन), बढ़े (chaupāī 19; Drik बढ़ै), अविनाशी (chaupāī 22; Drik अविनासी, as the book has it in chaupāīs 1 and 37), अजर अमर है तुम्हरी देहा (chaupāī 25; Drik अजर-अमर … तुम्हारी), पार ब्रह्म (chaupāī 27; Drik ब्रह्मा), गोपीचन्द (chaupāī 31; Drik गोपीचन्द्र), करें (chaupāī 37; Drik करै), पढ़े … होई (chaupāī 40; Drik पढ़ै … होइ), करूं (closing dohā 3; Drik करुँ). The spellings सुमिरूँ, लख्या, जोरहिं, पढ़हि and योगेश्वरो are the book's, as Drik's.
