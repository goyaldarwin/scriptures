# Reconciliation: जय जानकीनाथा जय श्रीरघुनाथा

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** `aarti/shree-rama/shri-janakinatha-aarti` (`hinduism/aarti/rama/janakinath-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-janakinatha-aarti.html`): the only witness. The book (५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित, Randhir Prakashan; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/`) does not print this āratī. The page prints the refrain (four lines) at the start and again at the end, and its first line ("जय जानकीनाथा, जय श्रीरघुनाथा") after every stanza.

The rule: keep Drik Panchang's text; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (the page's commas are dropped; a pause within a line is a space); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| the refrain | at the start, after every stanza (its first line) and again at the end | once, first | The standard (the refrain is shown once; the closing repeat is not written). |
| the refrain | प्रभु! सुनिये बाता | प्रभु सुनिये बाता | The exclamation mark is the page's punctuation, not part of the verse. |
| stanza 1 | प्रान, पिता माता | प्रान पिता माता | The standard: no comma in an āratī's verse. |
| stanza 5 | सुभग मुकुट सिर, धनु सर | सुभग मुकुट सिर धनु सर | The same. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥५॥ | The standard. |

Kept as the page has them: बिनवौं, प्रान, सज्जन-सङ्गी, निसिदिन, लछिमन, सँग शत्रुहन, लहिया (rhymes with भैया), नेवर झमकाता, स्वर्णथाल, सोभा, मनीराम.
