# Reconciliation: आरती श्री वृषभानुसुता की

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), p. 225, under the heading "आरती श्री राधा जी की"; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-224), read on the page image. Nine printed lines: the refrain, then eight lines without stanza breaks.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/radha/vrishbhanu-suta-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-radha-aarti.html`): word for word the same text, grouped in four stanzas (the refrain's line and त्रिविध, पावन; मुनि मन, अविरलप्रेम; संतत, आकर्षिणी; कृष्णात्मिका, जगज्जननि), with the refrain's first line after each.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (the book's comma between the halves of a line becomes a line break, a half per line); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| line 1 | the first printed line | the refrain (`aarti.tek`) | It is the refrain: its two halves end in -ता की, the rhyme every stanza returns to; Drik Panchang repeats it after each stanza. |
| lines 2–9 | eight printed lines, no breaks | four stanzas of two printed lines (four half-lines) | Each stanza is three half-lines rhyming in -इनि / -नकी / -इणि and a fourth ending -ता की that rhymes with the refrain (सुन्दरता की, ललिता की, समता की, विभुता की); Drik Panchang groups them the same way. |
| every line | halves joined by a comma | a half per line, ending । and ॥N॥ | The standard. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥४॥ | The standard. |

Kept as the book has them: every word; Drik Panchang has no reading that differs from the book (मूर्ति, विवेकविराग, अविरलप्रेम, जनकी, दिव्यगुन गनकी, आकर्षिणी, चिन्मयवृन्दा, दुःखनिवारिणि are the same in both).
