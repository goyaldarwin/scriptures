# आरती श्री वृषभानुसुता की (Aarti Shri Vrishbhanu Suta Ki)

The āratī of Śrī Rādhā, the daughter of Vṛṣabhānu, in Hindi (`hi`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, p. 225, "आरती श्री राधा जी की"), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/ambey-bhakti/`: Ambey Bhakti (YouTube `EvXXThG-J6k`, "राधा अष्टमी स्पेशल - आरती श्री वृषभानु सुता की | Aarti Shri Vrishbhanu Suta Ki | Radha Rani ji Aarti"), its track 0:00–4:26 of the video (after 4:26: music). Its starts come from the transcript for now; MMS (`tools/audio/METHOD.md`) is still to time it. A draft (it plays only with Audio timing on): its starts are still to be timed by the forced aligner (MMS): the transcript alone leaves verses it sings untimed or guessed.

Verse types: टेक (refrain, `tek`, shown once at the start although it is sung after every stanza) and पद (stanza, `pad`). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (Aarti Shri Vrishbhanu Suta Ki) › one section (`aarti`) › verses: the refrain (`aarti.tek`, type `tek`) and 4 stanzas of four lines.
- **Numbering:** each stanza's `n` is its number (॥१॥ …), and it is cited by it ("Stanza 3"); the refrain has none.
- **Topics:** one, the āratī's title. **Reading plans:** none.
