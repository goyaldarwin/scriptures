# Reconciliation: जय श्री राणी सती मैया

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), pp. 287–288, after the Rāṇī Satī Chālīsā, the last pages of the book; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-286, i-287), read on the page images. Its order: the refrain, अपनि अनन्तर ज्योति, मरकत मणि, घण्टा घनन, सप्त मातृका, संकट विकट, अमल कमल, and the closing stanza (श्री राणीसती मैयाजी की आरती). The dohā above the āratī on p. 287 (दुख आपद विपदा हरण) is the Chālīsā's closing dohā, not part of this work. (Page image i-284 is a page of the Chālīsā.)
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/rani-sati/rani-sati-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-rani-sati-aarti.html`): the same āratī, in the same order and the same words, each printed line broken in two.
- **For a doubtful reading only:** a web search for the line अपनि अनन्तर ज्योति अखण्डित found bharatdiscovery.org (whose page could not be read) and Bhakti Bharat (which copies Drik Panchang); it settled nothing.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (the book's comma between the halves of a line becomes a line break, a half per line); a line the book prints without a comma stays one line; the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| every stanza | two printed lines, most of them halved by a comma | a line per half, ending । and ॥N॥ | The standard; the comma marks the half. The refrain's second line and stanza 1's first line have no comma and stay whole (Drik Panchang breaks them after की and अखण्डित). |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥७॥ | The standard. |
| after every stanza | जय. | (not shown) | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item (टेक). |
| refrain, line 3 | क़रो (a dot under क) | करो | A speck of ink: क़रो is not a word; Drik Panchang has करो. |
| stanza 4, line 1 | संप्त मातृका | सप्त मातृका | A misprint: संप्त is not a word; the seven Mothers are सप्त मातृका, as Drik Panchang has it. |

Kept as the book has them (Drik Panchang the same): अपनि अनन्तर, चहुँककूंभा (`docs/reviews/rani-sati-aarti-doubts.jsonl`), खडग, विद्युतसम, शोभा लखि न बड़े, घड़ावल, घुरे, सुरगम, हृदय पटले, करन, आयो, सदनसिद्धि, विपती.
