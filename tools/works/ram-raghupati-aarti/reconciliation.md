# Reconciliation: बन्दौं रघुपति करुना निधान

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base)** (`hinduism/aarti/rama/ram-raghupati-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-rama-raghupati-aarti.html`): nine couplets, no line marked as a refrain. The owner's books have no copy of this hymn, so the page is the only witness.

The rule: keep the base; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; stanzas numbered (॥१॥). The page marks no refrain, so none is made: the nine couplets are the nine stanzas, in the page's order.

| Place | Base | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| every couplet | ending । and ॥ | । and ॥N॥ | The standard. |
| stanzas 5, 6, 9 | सुररन्जन, भन्जन · मृगपति, मुरारि · त्रैलोक-तिलक, गुनगहन | no comma | The standard. |
| stanza 2 | रघुबन्स · पद-पन्कज | रघुबंस · पद-पंकज | The page types every nasal before a consonant as न्; न् before a non-dental (न्स, न्क, न्ग, न्ज) is not a Hindi spelling, and is written ं. |
| stanza 3 | भृन्ग · अनन्ग | भृंग · अनंग | The same. |
| stanza 5 | सुररन्जन भन्जन | सुररंजन भंजन | The same. |
| stanza 4 | अग्यान-गहन- पावक-प्रचण्ड | अग्यान-गहन-पावक-प्रचण्ड | A stray space inside the compound. |
| stanza 6 | रागादि- सर्पगन | रागादि-सर्पगन | The same. |

Kept as the base has them: बन्दौं, करुना, ग्यान, निसेस, लावन्यबपुष, मारतण्ड, कुम्भज, कन्दर्प, चरनारबिन्द, रवन, बापी, कामधुक, गुनगहन, तुलसिदास, बिश्राम, and the page's hyphens.
