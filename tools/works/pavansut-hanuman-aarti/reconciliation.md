# Reconciliation: जयति मंगलागार

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** "Shri Pavanasuta Hanuman Aarti" (<https://www.drikpanchang.com/lyrics/aarti/lord-hanuman/shri-pavanasuta-hanuman-aarti.html>), scriptures-data `hinduism/aarti/hanuman/pavansut-hanuman-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-pavanasuta-hanuman-aarti.html`. It is the only witness.
- **The book:** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan), scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/`: the āratī is not in it.

The rule: keep Drik Panchang; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (each of the page's printed lines is its own line; a comma inside a line becomes a space); stanzas numbered (॥१॥). The page marks no refrain, so there is no टेक: the five stanzas are the text.

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| every stanza | lines ending in commas, commas inside lines; । and ॥ alternating | one line per printed line, each ending ।, the last ॥N॥; inner commas as spaces | The standard. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥५॥ | The standard. |
| stanza 4, line 4 | शीतलकरणकल्पशाषी | शीतलकरणकल्पशाखी | शाषी is not a word; कल्पशाखी, the wish-granting tree (शाखी, "tree"), is what the line says: Hanumān is the kalpa-tree that cools Bharata and the others burning in the sun of separation from Rāma. ष for ख is a typing slip. |

Kept as Drik Panchang has them: मंगलागार, भारापहर, मिषध्वान्तचर-सलभ, मरुदन्जनामोद (न्ज for ञ्ज, the page's way of typing a nasal; the dictionaries fold it with the anusvāra), नतग्रीवसुग्रीव, यातुधानोद्धत, सामगाताग्रणी, कौशला-कुशल-कल्याणभाषी, संभ्राज, तुलसि.
