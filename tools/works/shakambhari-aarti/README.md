# हरि ॐ श्री शाकम्भर अम्बा जी की आरती कीजो (Hari Om Shri Shakambhar Amba Ji Ki Aarti Kijo)

The āratī of Mother Śākambharī, in Hindi (`hi`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, p. 277), checked against Drik Panchang and Aarti Mala. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/vrat-katha/`: Vrat Katha (YouTube `DZ-7mUlZwMs`, "शाकम्भरी माता की आरती | Maa Shakambhari Ki Aarti | शाकम्भरी अम्बे देवी की आरती #matarani #matabhajan"). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list. A draft (it plays only with Audio timing on): MMS could not time every verse it sings (some it scored too low, others it placed nowhere: `aligned.md`); they are left untimed rather than guessed.

Verse types: टेक (refrain, `tek`, shown once at the start although it is sung after every stanza) and पद (stanza, `pad`). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (Shri Shakambhar Amba Ji Ki Aarti) › one section (`aarti`) › verses: the refrain (`aarti.tek`, type `tek`) and 4 stanzas.
- **Numbering:** each stanza's `n` is its number (॥१॥ …), and it is cited by it ("Stanza 3"); the refrain has none.
- **Topics:** one, the āratī's title. **Reading plans:** none.
