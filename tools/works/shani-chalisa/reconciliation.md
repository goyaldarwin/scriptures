# Reconciliation: श्री शनि चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 59–62, headed "श्री शनि चालीसा (२)"; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-058 … i-061), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/shani/shani-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shani-dev-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text (two opening dohās, 40 chaupāīs, one closing dohā), in the same order, with its own slips (चाँजी, दिग्ज).
- **Chalisa Mala** (the indif.com printout, page images i-398 … i-400): a printout of the same web text as Drik Panchang; not a separate witness.
- **For a doubtful reading only:** Aaj Tak (aajtak.in, Shri Shani Chalisa), which prints the book's text (जग दिग्गज, हवैं, 'भक्त'), and AstroVed (astroved.com, Shani Chalisa in Hindi). Sites that copy one another count as one witness.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas and quotation marks are not kept.

| chaupāī 8 | जापर प्रभु प्रसन्न हवैं जाहीं | जापर प्रभु प्रसन्न ह्वैं जाहीं | हवैं has lost the virāma of ह्वैं ("having become"), the book's own form in chaupāī 18 (प्रभु ह्वै सुख दीन्हयों); Drik Panchang है. |
| chaupāī 32 | जब आवहिं प्रभु स्वान सवार | जब आवहिं प्रभु स्वान सवारी | The rhyme with भारी needs सवारी ("mount"), as the chaupāī's sense; Drik Panchang सवारी. |
| chaupāī 35 | स्वर्ण सर्व सर्वसुख मंगल भारी | स्वर्ण सर्वसुख मंगल भारी | सर्व printed twice: the half has 16 mātrās without it, 19 with it; Drik Panchang स्वर्ण सर्वसुख. |
| closing dohā | कीहों 'भक्त' तैयार | कीन्हों भक्त तैयार | कीहों has lost the न् of कीन्हों ("made"); Drik Panchang कीन्हों. The quotation marks round the preparer's name are the printer's. Drik Panchang has 'विमल' here, the name the book prints in the same dohā of the other Śani Chālīsā (`shanishchar-chalisa`); the book's भक्त is kept. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: दमकै; कृष्णो; दुःखभंजन; शनी; जापर; रंकहुँ; होइ; बन, दीन्हयो / लीन्हयो, कैकेइहुँ, बनहूँ; गई (chaupāīs 11, 20); लषणहिं (Drik लखनहिं); नौलखा; तेलहिं; कीन्हयों / दीन्हयों, ह्वै (chaupāī 18; Drik है); आपहुं; भूंजी; शंकरहिं गह्यो; पारवती; कहँ, महँ; उपजावैं, करावैं; मंगल भारी (Drik मंगल कारी); अद्भुत; शांति; शनीश्चर. Chaupāī 27 keeps the book's जग दिग्गज गर्दभ मृग स्वाना (Aaj Tak the same): जग ("world") is not one of the seven mounts, but the witnesses differ on the word it stands for (Drik Panchang हय "horse", AstroVed गज "elephant"), so the doubt is in `docs/reviews/shani-chalisa-doubts.jsonl`.
