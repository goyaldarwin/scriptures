# Reconciliation: श्री शनि चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 59–62, headed "श्री शनि चालीसा (२)"; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-058 … i-061), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/shani/shani-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shani-dev-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text (two opening dohās, 40 chaupāīs, one closing dohā), in the same order, with its own slips (चाँजी, दिग्ज).
- **Chalisa Mala** (the indif.com printout, page images i-398 … i-400): a printout of the same web text as Drik Panchang; not a separate witness.
- **For a doubtful reading only:** Aaj Tak (aajtak.in, Shri Shani Chalisa), which prints the book's text (जग दिग्गज, हवैं, 'भक्त'), and AstroVed (astroved.com, Shani Chalisa in Hindi). Sites that copy one another count as one witness.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas and quotation marks are not kept.

Chaupāī 27 keeps the book's जग दिग्गज गर्दभ मृग स्वाना (Aaj Tak the same): जग ("world") is not one of the seven mounts, but the witnesses differ on the word it stands for (Drik Panchang हय "horse", AstroVed गज "elephant"), the reading is settled below (Readings settled).

## Recording
The text follows the version sung in the chosen recording, BhaktiDhaam's "सम्पूर्ण चालीसा संग्रह" (YouTube `lCwPg0LRRjk`, this Śani Chālīsā, the one that opens जय गणेश गिरिजा सुवन मंगल करण कृपाल, from 35:11 to 44:56): it sings the book's text (in the closing dohā the book's भक्त, not Drik Panchang's विमल; in chaupāī 27 the book's जग दिग्गज), except in one place, where it sings Drik Panchang's reading, written as Drik Panchang spells it. In chaupāī 2 it is heard as मुकुट छवि साजे, which no witness has; the book's छाजै is kept.

## Meanings

## Readings settled
- **chalisa.cau27** (जग दिग्गज गर्दभ मृग स्वाना: जग is not a mount): जग stays, read 'in the world', as our meaning has it. The book and Aaj Tak print it and the recording sings it (the text follows the recording, above); the witnesses that differ do not agree with each other (Drik Panchang हय, AstroVed गज), so no independent pair shows a corruption.
- **chalisa.cau14** (दियो कीट करि कंचन लंका: कीट 'insect' strained): कीट here is the Hindi कीट 'dregs, dross' (Platts: kīṭ, from S. किट्ट; Monier-Williams: kiṭṭa 'dirt, rust of metal'), the right sense for burnt gold; the meaning now reads 'You turned golden Laṅkā into dross' (सोनेकी लंकाको मैल बना दिया), and the word sheet gives the sense.
- **chalisa.cau21** (पारवती को सती कराई): 'made Pārvatī give up her body as Satī', as our meaning has it: सती होना / कराना is the self-immolation, the story of Satī at Dakṣa's sacrifice, Pārvatī being the same goddess; 'made her a faithful wife' would not be a misfortune brought by Śani.
- **chalisa.cau26** (शेष देव-लखि विनती लाई: 'the rest of the gods' or Śeṣa?): 'the rest of the gods', as our meaning has it: शेष before a noun is the plain Hindi 'remaining', and nothing in the witnesses or a source settles the other reading; the plain reading stands.
