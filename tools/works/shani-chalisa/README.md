# श्री शनि चालीसा (Shri Shani Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Śanideva that opens "जय गणेश गिरिजा सुवन", in Awadhi (`awa`). The book prints it as its second Śani Chālīsā, "श्री शनि चालीसा (२)"; the first is `shanishchar-chalisa`.

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 59–62), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** none yet.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

The poet names himself in chaupāī 40 (कहत राम सुन्दर प्रभु दासा: Rāma Sundara), and the closing dohā, in the book, names its preparer 'भक्त' (Bhakta).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohās (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohā (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 40 chaupāīs are numbered 1–40 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the three dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Hanumān Chālīsā's in the Sundarakāṇḍa (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** Śani's form and names, the Rāmāyaṇa story, his course over kings and gods, his mounts and his feet, the fruit of reading and worship. **Reading plans:** none.
