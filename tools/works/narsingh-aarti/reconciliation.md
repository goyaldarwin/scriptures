# Reconciliation: ॐ जय नरसिंह हरे

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** "Narsingh Bhagwan Ki Aarti" (`hinduism/aarti/narasimha/narsingh-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/om-jai-narsingh-hare.html`), headed श्री नृसिंह भगवान की आरती. The refrain (ॐ जय नरसिंह हरे … जन का ताप हरे) and three stanzas (तुम हो दीन दयाला, सबके हृदय विदारण, ब्रह्मा करत आरती), the cue ॐ जय नरसिंह हरे॥ after each.
- The book (५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित) and the other booklets have no copy of this āratī; Drik Panchang is the only witness.
- **For a doubtful reading only:** a web search for stanza 2 found only Bhakti Bharat (bhaktibharat.com, "Narasimha Bhagwan Aarti"), which prints Drik Panchang's text; it counts as the same witness.

The rule: keep the base; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (a line split at a comma becomes two lines); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| after every stanza | ॐ जय नरसिंह हरे॥ | (not written) | The cue back to the refrain. |
| stanzas 1–3, line 1 | तुम हो दीन दयाला, भक्तन हितकारी, (and the same in 2, 3) | two lines | The standard: a line split at its comma. |
| stanza 2, lines 2–3 | दुस्यु जियो मारी / प्रभु दुस्यु जियो मारी | दस्यु जियो मारी / प्रभु दस्यु जियो मारी | दुस्यु is not a word; दस्यु (the demon, Hiraṇyakaśipu) is the word the sense needs ("tearing open the heart, slew the demon"). No other witness prints the line; see "Readings settled". |
| line ends | , । ॥ | । and, at a stanza's end, ॥N॥ | The standard. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥३॥ | The standard. |

Kept as the base has them: the repeated lines (स्तम्भ फाड़ प्रभु प्रकटे, अद्भुत रूप बनाकर, दास जान अपनायो, each printed twice), and stanza 3's शिवजी जय जय कहकर printed once (the other stanzas print their fourth line twice; the page does not, and nothing shows a line dropped), सबके, जियो, पहिनावे, पुष्पन.

## Readings settled
- aarti.pad2 — दुस्यु जियो मारी: the correction दस्यु stands. दुस्यु is not a word; दस्यु (MW, enemy of the gods) is the word the sense needs, one vowel sign away; जियो, the life (जी). A web search found only Bhakti Bharat, which copies Drik Panchang.
- aarti.pad2 — सबके हृदय विदारण: kept, you who tear open the hearts of all (MW vidāraṇa, tearing asunder, piercing). The plain words; no witness reads otherwise.
