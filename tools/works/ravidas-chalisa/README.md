# श्री रविदास चालीसा (Shri Ravidas Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Guru Ravidāsa, the saint of Kāśī, that opens "बन्दौं वीणा पाणि को", in Awadhi (`awa`); it tells his birth, Gaṅgā's bracelet ("मन चंगा तो कठौती में गंगा"), the floating Śālagrāma, his debates and Sikandar Lodī.

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 90–93), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** none yet.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohās (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohās (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 40 chaupāīs are numbered 1–40 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the four dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Hanumān Chālīsā's in the Sundarakāṇḍa (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** his birth and initiation, Mother Gaṅgā's bracelet, the test of the Śālagrāma, debates with the saints, Sikandar Lodī and Mīrā. **Reading plans:** none.
