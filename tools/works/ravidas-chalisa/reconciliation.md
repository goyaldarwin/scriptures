# Reconciliation: श्री रविदास चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), pp. 90–93; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-089 … i-092), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/ravidas/ravidas-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-ravidas-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, with its own slips (रल for रत्न, पडित, हार्योो).

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 38 | दै प्रभुता अरुमान बड़ाई | दै प्रभुता अरु मान बड़ाई | A missing space: अरु मान, "and honour" (as प्रभुता … बड़ाई around it); अरुमान is not a word. Drik Panchang has the same slip. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: बन्दौं (Drik बंदौं), राहू (Drik राहु), पर्यो (chaupāī 21, the book's पर्‍यो; Drik परयो), गोरखहिं (Drik गोरखहि), हार्यो, नहिं (chaupāī 35; Drik नाहिं), भईं (chaupāī 36; Drik भई). Kept as both have them: आयों, लै, दै, चौगानी, पुरइ, कीन्हाँ / दीन्हाँ, सालिग राम, उनहुँ, तकि, लोधि सिकन्दर, बंदीगृह काटी है रानी, माम सिकन्दर, योगावति, तहूं.

## Readings settled
The open questions of `docs/reviews/ravidas-chalisa-doubts.jsonl`, each decided (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §2).

- **chalisa.cau10** — चरित भव भय नाशी के, whose deed: Gaṅgā's, the act just told (chaupāī 9: she stretched out her hand and took the cowrie), which is what the paṇḍitas of Kāśī saw. नाशी is used of goddesses as of gods in this Awadhi (our glossary takes it so here), so the grammar does not turn it to Ravidāsa. Meaning unchanged.
