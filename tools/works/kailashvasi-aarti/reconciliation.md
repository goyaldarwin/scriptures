# Reconciliation: शीश गंग अर्धन्ग पार्वती

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** "Lord Kailasavasi Aarti" (<https://www.drikpanchang.com/lyrics/aarti/lord-shiva/lord-kailasavasi-aarti.html>), scriptures-data `hinduism/aarti/shiva/kailashvasi-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/lord-kailasavasi-aarti.html`. It is the only witness.
- **The book:** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan), scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/`: the āratī is not in it.

The rule: keep Drik Panchang; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; one line per printed line; stanzas numbered (॥१॥). The page marks no refrain, so there is no टेक: its ten groups are the ten stanzas.

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| every stanza | some lines ending in commas, some in nothing; । and ॥ alternating | each line ending ।, the last ॥N॥ | The standard. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥१०॥ | The standard. |

No word is changed. Kept as Drik Panchang has them: अर्धन्ग, भृन्गी, गुन्जा-सी (न typed for the class nasal; the dictionaries fold them with the anusvāra), सुखरासी, मधुरासी, जहँ, वनके, लक्षासी, हमकूँ फरमासी, सिद्धिके, आनँदराशी, काल-यमकी, त्रिशूलधरजीका, गासी, पासी, चरनन, ढकियो, किंकरकी.
