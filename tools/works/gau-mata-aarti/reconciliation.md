# Reconciliation: आरती श्री गैया मैया की

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** Aarti Mala (booklet `aarti-mala`), one printed page, "आरती श्री गोमाताजी की"; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-342), read on the page image. The page is a printout of indif.com (its watermark). Nine printed lines, no stanza breaks: the refrain line, then eight lines whose every second line ends with the rhyme …या की and ।।.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/gau-mata/gau-mata-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/gau-mata-aarti.html`): the same refrain and four stanzas in the same order, each stanza two printed lines (four half-lines), the refrain repeated at the end; a typed copy with its own spellings.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (the book's comma between the halves of a line becomes a line break, a half per line); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| the stanzas | eight lines after the refrain, no breaks | four stanzas of two printed lines (four half-lines) each | Every second line ends with the refrain's rhyme (…या की) and ।।; Drik Panchang divides the same way. |
| every line | the halves joined by a comma (the refrain by ।) | two lines | The standard; the comma marks the half. |
| stanza numbers | none (।।) | ॥१॥ … ॥४॥ | The standard. |
| stanza 3, line 2 | दारिद्रय | दारिद्र्य | A typing slip (a dropped halant): दारिद्रय is not a word; दारिद्र्य is "poverty", beside दुख and दैन्य. Drik Panchang has the same slip. |

Kept as the book has them, where Drik Panchang differs: गैया मैया (Drik गैय्या मैंय्या), विश्वधैया (Drik विश्व धैय्या), प्रदायिनि, मुक्तिपददायिनि (Drik मुक्तिपद्दायिनी), सौभाग्यविधायिनि (Drik सौभाग्या विधायिनी), नंद छैया (Drik नन्द छैय्या), प्रतिपालिनि, दृढ़ नैया (Drik दृढ नैय्या), आरोग्यविकाशिनि (Drik आरोग्य विकाशिनी), दुख (Drik दुःख), दैया, जैया (Drik दैय्या, जैय्या). Both have सुष्मा (for सुषमा) and विकाशिनि; neither is clearly wrong in this register, so they stay.

## Readings settled
- aarti.tek — हरनि and धैया: "her who takes away sorrow, the nurse of the world" stays; हरनि is "she who takes away" (हरना, "to take away", with the feminine agent -नि, as in कलिमलहरनि), its object the sorrow and ills the āratī itself says she removes (रोग शोक संकट परित्राता, दुख दैन्य दारिद्र्य विनाशिनि); धैया is धाय, a wet-nurse (Platts), the cow that feeds all with her milk (stanza 2).
- aarti.pad4 — जैया: "the mother of the world" stays; -ैया makes a doer, as in this āratī's own दैया ("giver", stanza 3), from जाना "to bear, give birth" (Platts जाया, "born"): she who bears the world.
