# Reconciliation: जय जय अम्बे जय कात्यायनी

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** "Katyayani Mata Aarti" (`hinduism/aarti/durga/katyayani-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/devi-katyayani-aarti.html`), headed आरती देवी कात्यायनी जी की. Five groups of four printed lines, no refrain marked.
- The book (५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित) and the other booklets have no copy of this āratī; Drik Panchang is the only witness.
- **For a doubtful reading only:** the āratī as DNA Hindi prints it (dnaindia.com/hindi/spiritual/report-chaitra-navratri-day-6-maa-katyayani-devi-mantra-prasad-auspicious-time-puja-vidhi-maa-maa-katyayani-aarti-4156731), found by web search. Bhakti Bharat prints Drik Panchang's text and is not counted.

The rule: keep the base; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; stanzas numbered (॥१॥). The page marks no refrain, so none is made: the text is five stanzas.

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| stanza 4, line 1 | छुडाने | छुड़ाने | A dropped nukta: छुडाने is not a word ("who frees from false attachment" is छुड़ाने); DNA Hindi has छुड़ाने. |
| line ends | । and ॥ alternating | । and, at a stanza's end, ॥N॥ | The standard. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥५॥ | The standard. |

Kept as the base has them: बैजनाथ, वरदाती, ज्योत, कही योगेश्वरी (DNA Hindi कहीं; कही, "called", also reads; settled below, Readings settled), भगत है कहते (DNA Hindi भक्त), कत्यानी and कात्यानी (DNA Hindi कात्यायनी; the short forms keep the line's sixteen mātrās), ग्रन्थि, जपाने, बृहस्पतिवार, करिये, धरिये, भण्डारे.

## Meanings

## Readings settled
- **pad 2**, कही योगेश्वरी महिमा न्यारी (कही or कहीं): कही, as the base (Drik Panchang) prints it: 'you were called Yogeśvarī'. DNA Hindi's कहीं is not clearly better, and nothing shows the base's reading is a slip.
