# Reconciliation: जय सरस्वती माता

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** "Maa Saraswati Aarti" (<https://www.drikpanchang.com/lyrics/aarti/saraswati-mata/saraswati-mata-aarti.html>), scriptures-data `hinduism/aarti/saraswati/saraswati-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/saraswati-mata-aarti.html`. It is the only witness: the refrain, six stanzas, each followed by the cue "जय सरस्वती माता", and the refrain again at the end.
- **The book:** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan), scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/`: the āratī is not in it.

The rule: keep Drik Panchang; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; one line per printed line; the refrain (टेक) shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| every stanza | lines ending in commas (or nothing), । and ॥ | each line ending ।, the last ॥N॥ | The standard. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥६॥ | The standard. |
| after every stanza | ⟨refrain⟩ जय सरस्वती माता॥ | not shown | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item. |
| at the end | जय सरस्वती माता, जय जय सरस्वती माता। सदगुण वैभव शालिनी, त्रिभुवन विख्याता॥ | not shown | The closing repeat of the refrain (with जय जय for मैया जय); it is not a stanza of its own. |

Kept as Drik Panchang has them: सदगुण, चन्द्रवदनि पद्मासिनि, सोहे, बाएं, दाएं, गल मोतियन माला, आए, पैठी मंथरा दासी, प्रदायिनि, मोह अज्ञान और तिमिर का, ज्ञानचक्षु, हितकारी सुखकारी.
