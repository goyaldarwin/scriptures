# श्री कुबेर चालीसा (Shri Kuber Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Kubera, the lord of wealth, that opens "जैसे अटल हिमालय", in Awadhi (`awa`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): Drik Panchang (Shri Kubera Chalisa); our book (५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित, Randhir Prakashan) has no copy. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** none yet.

The last chaupāī names its makers: "मानस परिवार" (the Mānas Parivāra), a modern devotional group; the text is modern (it speaks of court cases and elections).

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohās (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohās (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 44 chaupāīs are numbered 1–44 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the four dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Gaṇeśa Chālīsā's (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** Kubera the guardian and warrior, his birth and penance, his splendour, what his grace does, the fruit of the recitation. **Reading plans:** none.
