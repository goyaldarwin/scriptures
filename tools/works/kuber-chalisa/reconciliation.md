# Reconciliation: श्री कुबेर चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** Shri Kubera Chalisa (`hinduism/chalisa/kubera/kuber-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-kubera-chalisa.html`). Our book (५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित, Randhir Prakashan; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/`) has no Kubera Chālīsā, so the web text is the base.
- **For a doubtful reading only:** InstaAstro (instaastro.com, Shri Kuber Ji Ki Chalisa), found by web search: it has Drik Panchang's text with the same slips (सम सम, चढ़ादे), so it is a copy and settles nothing. Bhakti Bharat prints Drik Panchang's text and counts with it as one witness.

The rule: keep Drik Panchang's text; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the page's commas are not kept.

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| opening dohā 2 | धन माया के ढ़ेर | धन माया के ढेर | ढ़ेर (with the dot) is not a word: the word is ढेर, "heap", and it rhymes with टेर. |
| chaupāī 31 | कुबेर चढ़े को और चढ़ादे | कुबेर चढ़े को और चढ़ा दे | A missing space: every other half of this run ends in a verb and दे written apart (उठा दे, जगा दे, बता दे …). |

Kept as Drik Panchang has them: पवन वेग सम सम तनु (the doubled सम may be a slip, but no independent witness was found to settle it), पहरे दारी, पुलिस्ता, इडविडा, देवत्य, योगनी, ढूलावैं, देवन्ह, क्युं, and the signature मानस परिवार.

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
- **chalisa.cau2** — पवन वेग सम सम तनु: kept as Drik Panchang prints it, the base; its only other copy (InstaAstro) is a copy, and the metre of this Chālīsā is too loose to prove a slip (the first half, तप तेज पुंज निर्भय भय हारी, is as long). Read as printed: "swift as the wind (पवन वेग सम), of even, well-proportioned body (सम तनु)". Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.cau39** — कला हो सदा सवाई: सवाई "one and a quarter" (Platts sawāyā = sawaiyā), the idiom of a thing that keeps growing; कला "power, skill". "His powers ever grow by a quarter more." Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.cau40** — कुबेर की माई: the plain words, "Kubera's mother" (Iḍaviḍā, named in cau8); no witness names Lakṣmī here. Meaning unchanged.
