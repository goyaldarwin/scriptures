# Reconciliation: जयति जयति जग-निवास

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** "Lord Shankara Aarti" (<https://www.drikpanchang.com/lyrics/aarti/lord-shiva/lord-shankara-aarti.html>), scriptures-data `hinduism/aarti/shiva/shankar-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/lord-shankara-aarti.html`. It is the only witness.
- **The book:** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan), scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/`: the āratī is not in it.

The rule: keep Drik Panchang; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; one line per printed line; the refrain (टेक) shown once, its repeats not written; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| refrain | its two lines printed twice (the sung repeat) | once | The standard: repeats are not written. |
| every stanza | lines ending in commas or nothing; । and ॥ | each line ending ।, the last ॥N॥ | The standard. |
| stanza 1, line 4 | भव! भव-भय-हारी | भव भव-भय-हारी | Punctuation is not part of the verse. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥७॥ | The standard. |
| after every stanza | ⟨refrain⟩ जयति जयति जग-निवास...॥ | not shown | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item. |

No word is changed. Kept as Drik Panchang has them: जग-निवास, सत चित, बिधुबाल, सुरसरिमय, हियकी, हूल, शान्तिकारी, माया-भ्रम-टारी, कैलास-देश, ब्याल, सिंह-चर्म, आर्तिहरण, बिधि.
