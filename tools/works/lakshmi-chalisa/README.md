# श्री लक्ष्मी चालीसा (Shri Lakshmi Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Lakṣmī that opens "मातु लक्ष्मी करि कृपा", in Awadhi (`awa`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 183–186), checked against Drik Panchang and Chalisa Mala. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** none yet.

The poet names himself in the last dohā: "रामदास धरि ध्यान नित" (Rāmadāsa).

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `so` sorathā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohā and sorathā (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohās (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 39 chaupāīs are numbered 1–39 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the dohās and the sorathā have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Opening · Soratha", "Closing · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Hanumān Chālīsā's in the Sundarakāṇḍa (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.so1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** the plea, Lakṣmī's glory, the fruit of the recitation, the prayer. **Reading plans:** none.
