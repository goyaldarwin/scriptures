# Reconciliation: श्री लक्ष्मी चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 183–186; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-182 … i-185), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/lakshmi/lakshmi-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/lakshmi-mata-chalisa.html`): the same chālīsā in a version of its own wording in many lines (जब जब जन्म जहां प्रभु लीन्हा, जो चालीसा पढ़ै पढ़ावै, करो शत्रु को नाश …), with its own slips (परुवहु, ताको कोई कष्ट नोई, दजै). It has the same 39 chaupāīs, but "केहि प्रकार मैं करौं बड़ाई" comes last, where the book has it after "भूल चूक करि क्षमा हमारी". The book's order is kept.
- **Chalisa Mala** (a printout of indif.com; the same book folder, page images i-428 … i-430): another web copy, close to Drik Panchang's wording.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's and a sorathā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 21 | ताप भव बंधन हारिणी | त्रिविध ताप भव बंधन हारिणी | The book drops a word: the half-line is short of its 16 mātrās, and Drik Panchang and Chalisa Mala both have त्रिविध ताप ("the threefold affliction"). |
| chaupāī 38 | नहिं मोहि ज्ञान बुद्धि है मन में | = | Kept: "I have no knowledge or wisdom in my mind" is sound and keeps the metre; only its rhyme repeats the second half's मन में. Drik Panchang and Chalisa Mala (one typed text) have तन में. |

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
- chalisa.do3, रामदास: the poet's name, Rāmadāsa, as the meaning has it; a chālīsā's closing dohā names its poet, and the next line's दास पै ("on [your] servant") is he; the awa glossary's रामदास already lists the poet's name beside "Rāma's servant".
