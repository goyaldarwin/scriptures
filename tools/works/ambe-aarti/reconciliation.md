# Reconciliation: जय अम्बे गौरी

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), pp. 177–178; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-176, i-177), read on the page images, under the heading "आरती श्री दुर्गा जी की". The refrain and twelve stanzas, each in two printed lines (the halves of a line divided by a comma in the refrain's first line and in stanzas 6, 7 and 9, by nothing elsewhere), with the cue "जय." after every stanza but the first.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/durga/ambe-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/ambe-mata-aarti.html`, "Ambe Ji Ki Aarti"): the same twelve stanzas in the same order, each in four printed lines, with the cue "जय अम्बे गौरी" after each.
- **Aarti Mala** (page image i-336, "श्री अम्बे जी की आरती", a printout carrying the indif.com watermark): the same āratī without the stanza तुम ही जग की माता, with some other wordings (मृदमद, गलमाला, कमलारानी, ज्योती, जो कोई नित गावे).

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and follow the version Anuradha Paudwal sings in the chosen recording (the "Om Jai Jagdish Hare Aarti Sangrah" jukebox, YouTube `sd31yGqZFlA`, 15:12–22:28; docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3) where she sings another witness's line, each word as that witness spells it. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (a printed line is split at its half, one half per line); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: निशि दिन (Drik निशिदिन), मांग सिंदूर (Drik माँग सिन्दूर), उज्ज्वल (Drik उज्जवल), दोऊ (Drik दोउ), कंठन, खड्ग खप्पर धारी (Drik खप्परधारी), सुर-नर-मुनिजन (Drik सुर-नर-मुनि-जन), शुम्भ निशुम्भ विदारे (Drik शुम्भ-निशुम्भ बिदारे), निशदिन, ब्रह्माणी (Drik ब्रहमाणी), आगम निगम बखानी (Drik आगम-निगम-बखानी), चौंसठ योगिनी गावत (Drik चौंसठ योगिनी मंगल गावत; she sings the book's words), भैरू (Drik भैरूँ), अरु, डमरू (Drik डमरु), भक्तन की दुःख हरता (as Drik), वरमुद्रा (Drik वर-मुद्रा), मनवांछित (Drik मनवान्छित), नर नारी (Drik नर-नारी), कंचन (Drik कन्चन), श्रीमालकेतु, गावे, पावे (Drik गावै, पावै), सुख-सम्पत्ति (Drik सुख सम्पत्ति).

## Meanings

## Readings settled
- **aarti.pad5** (धूम्र विलोचन नैना निशदिन मदमाती: a demon slain, or 'your smoke-dark eyes'?): the demon Dhūmravilocana (Dhūmralocana) among the slain, then 'your eyes are ever flushed', as our meaning has it. The stanza and the next are a list of the demons she slew in the Devī Māhātmya (Śumbha, Niśumbha, Mahiṣa; Caṇḍa, Muṇḍa, Raktabīja, Madhu, Kaiṭabha), and Dhūmralocana is the one of them the list would otherwise lack; नैना निशदिन मदमाती is a clause of its own.
- **aarti.pad11** (श्रीमालकेतु: a place, or 'Śrī's banner'?): kept as one name, as the book and Drik Panchang both print it as one word; neither dictionary has it and a web search found no source that explains it, so the plain reading of the printed word stands (a name, untranslated).
