# Reconciliation: जय संतोषी माता

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), p. 240, under the heading "आरती श्री संतोषी माँ जी की"; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-239), read on the page image. Eight printed lines, each two halves joined by a daṇḍa and followed by the refrain cue (जय.): the refrain (जय संतोषी माता), then सुन्दर चीर, गेरू लाल, स्वर्ण सिंहासन, गुड़ अरु चना, शुक्रवार, मन्दिर जगमग, भक्ति भावमय; the page ends there with its ornament.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/santoshi/santoshi-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/santoshi-mata-aarti.html`) and its weekday page, **Shukrawar Ki Aarti** (`hinduism/aarti/santoshi/santoshi-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/weekdays-shukrawar-aarti.html`), the same text word for word: a longer version. After the book's seven stanzas it has four more: दुखी दरिद्री रोग, ध्यान धर्यो जिस जन ने, शरण गहे की लज्जा, and the closing सन्तोषी माता की आरती जो कोई जन गावे. Its readings that differ: अपने सेवक जन को, सुन्दर चीर, तन श्रृंगार कीन्हों, धूप दीप मधुमेवा (no नैवेद्य), शुक्रवार प्रिय, भक्त मण्डली.
- **Aarti Mala** (scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/`, page image i-358, a printout of indif.com): the longer version, with slips of its own; it has अपने सेवक जन, चीर, लीन्हों, नैवेध, प्रिय, भक्त मण्डली.

The recording: Anuradha Paudwal, "Jai Santoshi Mata Aarti" (YouTube `LCYAyR6EmDI`), the owner's choice. She sings the longer version: the refrain, the book's seven stanzas, then Drik Panchang's दुखी दरिद्री, ध्यान धर्यो and शरण गहे stanzas, the शुक्रवार stanza once more (a repeat, not a new verse), and the closing stanza, each followed by the refrain. The text follows her (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): the four stanzas the book lacks are added (stanzas 8–11), and where she sings another witness's wording, the text takes it, each word as a witness writes it.

The rule: keep the book, except where the recording sings another witness's wording or a stanza the book lacks; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| stanza 1, line 1 | सुन्दर वीर सुनहरी | सुन्दर चीर सुनहरी | वीर ("hero") makes no sense with माँ धारण कीन्हों ("she wears"); चीर ("garment") is Drik Panchang's and Aarti Mala's reading: one letter misprinted. |

Not changed: stanza 6, line 2 (विनय करें हम बालक चरनन सिर नाई), where both transcripts hear सेवक for बालक, which no witness writes: for the owner's ear.

Kept as the book has them: संतोषी (Drik सन्तोषी), तन श्रृंगार लीन्हों (Drik कीन्हों), हँसत (Drik हंसत), चँवर ढुरे (Drik चंवर ढुरें), धरे (Drik धरें), परम प्रिय (Drik परमप्रिय), तामें (Drik तामे), चरनन (faint on the page, read with Drik Panchang), बसै.
