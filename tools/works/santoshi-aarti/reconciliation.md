# Reconciliation: जय संतोषी माता

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), p. 240, under the heading "आरती श्री संतोषी माँ जी की"; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-239), read on the page image. Eight printed lines, each two halves joined by a daṇḍa and followed by the refrain cue (जय.): the refrain (जय संतोषी माता), then सुन्दर चीर, गेरू लाल, स्वर्ण सिंहासन, गुड़ अरु चना, शुक्रवार, मन्दिर जगमग, भक्ति भावमय; the page ends there with its ornament.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/santoshi/santoshi-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/santoshi-mata-aarti.html`) and its weekday page, **Shukrawar Ki Aarti** (`hinduism/aarti/santoshi/santoshi-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/weekdays-shukrawar-aarti.html`), the same text word for word: a longer version. After the book's seven stanzas it has four more: दुखी दरिद्री रोग, ध्यान धर्यो जिस जन ने, शरण गहे की लज्जा, and the closing सन्तोषी माता की आरती जो कोई जन गावे. Its readings that differ: अपने सेवक जन को, सुन्दर चीर, तन श्रृंगार कीन्हों, धूप दीप मधुमेवा (no नैवेद्य), शुक्रवार प्रिय, भक्त मण्डली.
- **Aarti Mala** (scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/`, page image i-358, a printout of indif.com): the longer version, with slips of its own; it has अपने सेवक जन, चीर, लीन्हों, नैवेध, प्रिय, भक्त मण्डली.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The book prints its own, shorter version of the āratī; that version is the text. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| every stanza | one printed line, its halves joined by a daṇḍa | two lines, ending । and ॥N॥ | The standard; the book's daṇḍa marks the half. |
| refrain, line 1 | …मैया जय संतौषी माता | …मैया जय संतोषी माता | संतौषी is a misprint: the same line's first संतोषी, the heading and both other witnesses have ो. |
| refrain, line 2 | अपने जन को सुख सम्पत्ति दाता | अपने सेवक जन को सुख सम्पत्ति दाता | A dropped word: the half lacks four mātrās of the twelve every other first half has (अपने सेवक जन को, हीरा पन्ना दमके); Drik Panchang and Aarti Mala have सेवक. |
| stanza 1, line 1 | सुन्दर वीर सुनहरी | सुन्दर चीर सुनहरी | वीर ("hero") makes no sense with माँ धारण कीन्हों ("she wears"); चीर ("garment") is Drik Panchang's and Aarti Mala's reading: one letter misprinted. |
| stanza 3, line 2 | धूप, दीप, नैवेद्य, मधुमेवा | धूप दीप नैवेद्य मधुमेवा | The standard (no comma). नैवेद्य is kept: Aarti Mala has it too. |
| stanza 5, line 1 | शुक्रवार प्रिया मानत | शुक्रवार प्रिय मानत | प्रिया (a feminine noun, "beloved") does not fit: शुक्रवार प्रिय मानत, "holds Friday dear", as Drik Panchang and Aarti Mala have it. |
| after every stanza | जय. | not written | The refrain cue; the refrain is shown once. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥७॥ | The standard. |

Kept as the book has them: संतोषी (Drik सन्तोषी), तन श्रृंगार लीन्हों (Drik कीन्हों), हँसत (Drik हंसत), चँवर ढुरे (Drik चंवर ढुरें), नैवेद्य (Drik none), धरे (Drik धरें), परम प्रिय (Drik परमप्रिय), तामें (Drik तामे), भक्ति मण्डली (Drik भक्त मण्डली; "a devotional gathering", a valid reading), चरनन (faint on the page, read with Drik Panchang), बसै.
