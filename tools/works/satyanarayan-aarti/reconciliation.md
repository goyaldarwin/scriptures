# Reconciliation: जय लक्ष्मी रमणा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** Aarti Mala (booklet `aarti-mala`), one printed page, "श्री सत्यनारायण जी की आरती"; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-329), read on the page image. The page is a printout of indif.com (its watermark). The refrain and eight stanzas, each stanza two printed lines, with the cue "जय लक्ष्मी रमणा...." after the refrain and every stanza. (The kit's second image, i-331, is the Śiva āratī "ओं जय शिव ओंकारा", another work.)
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/vishnu/satyanarayan-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shree-satyanarayan-aarti.html`): the same refrain and eight stanzas in the same order, a typed copy with its own spellings.
- **For the one doubtful reading only:** the text as other sites print it, found by web search (Bhāratkosh, bharatdiscovery.org; Boldsky; Bhakti Bharat, which prints Drik Panchang's text). Sites that copy one another count as one witness.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (the book's comma inside a line becomes a space; each printed line is one line of the couplet); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: जय लक्ष्मी रमणा स्वामी जय लक्ष्मी रमणा (Drik जय लक्ष्मीरमणा श्री जय लक्ष्मीरमणा), जन पातक हरना (Drik जनपातक हरणा), रतन जड़त (Drik रत्नजड़ित), छबी (Drik छवि), घंटा धुन (Drik घंटा ध्वनि), भए (Drik भये), धरयो / सरयो (Drik धर्यो / सर्यो), दीन्हा (Drik दीनो; she sings the book's ending, "दीना" in the transcript at 1865 s), श्रीसत्यनारायण जी (Drik श्री सत्यनारायणजी).

Anuradha Paudwal's recording (the "Om Jai Jagdish Hare Aarti Sangrah" jukebox, YouTube `sd31yGqZFlA`, 27:15–33:12; docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3) sings this version, the refrain and the eight stanzas in this order; where she sings another witness's words, the text takes them (above: सवाया, नर). Where she sings words no witness has, the text keeps the book (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3: each word as a written witness has it): stanza 8 opens लक्ष्मीरमण जी की आरती (the 5-s windows at 1911–1912 s: "लग्श्मीर मनजी की आरे ती"; scored −27.6 against −72.2 for श्रीसत्यनारायण जी) and its second line is another (the transcript: "ब्रे थि से ती सुख्य संपती · जी बर्छे पावे", for कहत शिवानन्द स्वामी मनवांछित फल पावे); stanza 3 has जिन पर कृपा करी (the transcript at 1763 s; both witnesses: इन पर). Drik Panchang's spellings of words sung alike (रत्न जड़ित, छवि, ध्वनि) are not taken.

## Readings settled
- **aarti.pad3** (दुर्बल भील कठारो इन पर: कठारो not in Platts, इन plural): the poor Bhīl woodcutters, as our meaning has it. कठारो is काठ 'wood' + the agent suffix -हारा, 'one who gathers wood' (Platts has no entry for the word itself), and the Satyanārāyaṇa story's woodcutter is the one this stanza means, beside King Candracūḍa in the next half; इन पर is the woodcutter and his fellows (or the plural of respect). A list of three (the poor one, the Bhīl, the woodcutter) has no figure in the story.
