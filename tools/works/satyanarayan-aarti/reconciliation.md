# Reconciliation: जय लक्ष्मी रमणा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** Aarti Mala (booklet `aarti-mala`), one printed page, "श्री सत्यनारायण जी की आरती"; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-329), read on the page image. The page is a printout of indif.com (its watermark). The refrain and eight stanzas, each stanza two printed lines, with the cue "जय लक्ष्मी रमणा...." after the refrain and every stanza. (The kit's second image, i-331, is the Śiva āratī "ओं जय शिव ओंकारा", another work.)
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/vishnu/satyanarayan-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shree-satyanarayan-aarti.html`): the same refrain and eight stanzas in the same order, a typed copy with its own spellings.
- **For the one doubtful reading only:** the text as other sites print it, found by web search (Bhāratkosh, bharatdiscovery.org; Boldsky; Bhakti Bharat, which prints Drik Panchang's text). Sites that copy one another count as one witness.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (the book's comma inside a line becomes a space; each printed line is one line of the couplet); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| after the refrain and every stanza | जय लक्ष्मी रमणा.... | (not shown) | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item (टेक). |
| refrain, stanza 1 | the commas inside the lines | a space | The standard. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥८॥ | The standard. |
| stanza 1, line 2 | नारद कहत निरंजन | नारद करत निराजन | The line pictures the āratī itself: Nārada waves the lamps (nīrājana) while the bell rings. "कहत निरंजन" (says "the stainless one") does not fit the bell and is a corruption of it: Drik Panchang has करत निराजन; a web search for "करत नीराजन घंटा" finds the line on Bhāratkosh (bharatdiscovery.org) and Boldsky (whose page has Nārada performing the waving of lamps as the bells ring), and one for "कहत निरंजन घंटा" finds no site. We take Drik Panchang's spelling, the other witness. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: जय लक्ष्मी रमणा स्वामी जय लक्ष्मी रमणा (Drik जय लक्ष्मीरमणा श्री जय लक्ष्मीरमणा), जन पातक हरना (Drik जनपातक हरणा), रतन जड़त (Drik रत्नजड़ित), छबी (Drik छवि), घंटा धुन (Drik घंटा ध्वनि), भए (Drik भये), धरयो / सरयो (Drik धर्यो / सर्यो), दीन्हा (Drik दीनो), सवायो (Drik सवाया), श्रीसत्यनारायण जी की आरती जो कोई गावे (Drik श्री सत्यनारायणजी की आरती जो कोई नर गावे).
