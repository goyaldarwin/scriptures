# श्री ब्रह्मा चालीसा (Shri Brahma Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Bhagavān Brahmā that opens "जय ब्रह्मा जय स्वयम्भू", in Awadhi (`awa`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 48–52), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** none yet.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › two sections: the opening dohās (`chalisa-do`) and the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`) › verses. The book prints no closing dohā: the chālīsā ends with chaupāī 53.
- **Numbering:** the 53 chaupāīs (the book prints 53, not 40) are numbered 1–53 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the two dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Hanumān Chālīsā's in the Sundarakāṇḍa (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** Brahmā's form and family, delusion on the lotus, the voice from the sky, the vision of Viṣṇu and his teaching, Pitāmaha and Puṣkara. **Reading plans:** none.
