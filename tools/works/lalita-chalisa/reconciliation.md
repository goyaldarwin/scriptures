# Reconciliation: श्री ललिता चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 278–281; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-277 … i-280), read on the page images. The book prints the chaupāīs under the title with no heading and no opening dohā.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/lalita/lalita-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/lalita-mata-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, with its own slips (छिन्न्मस्ता, ललितेशक्ति, सिद्दि, नैमिश्वासिनी, अधनाशिनी, भण्डासुर कि).

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 1 | तब गुण महिमा है विख्याता | तव गुण महिमा है विख्याता | The sense is "your (तव) virtues and glory are renowned", as Drik Panchang has it; तब ("then") is a slip. |
| chaupāī 7 | श्री चन्द्रेश्वरि! नैमिष प्यारा | श्री चन्द्रेश्वरि नैमिष प्यारा | The exclamation mark is the printer's punctuation, not part of the verse. |
| chaupāī 9 | ललिते! शक्ति तुम्हारी संगी | ललिते शक्ति तुम्हारी संगी | The same. |
| chaupāī 28 | महिमा तब सब जग विख्याता | महिमा तव सब जग विख्याता | As chaupāī 1: "your glory", as Drik Panchang has it. |
| chaupāī 33 | सहस्त्रार गामिनी माँ जय जय | सहस्रार गामिनी माँ जय जय | सहस्त्र is a common misspelling of सहस्र ("thousand"); the cakra is the sahasrāra, as Drik Panchang has it. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: सुन्दरि (Drik सुन्दरी), हृदय निवासिनी भक्त तारिणी (Drik निवासिनी-भक्त), रूप, चन्द्रेश्वरि (Drik चन्द्रेश्वरी), ललिते शक्ति (Drik ललितेशक्ति), बचाये, पाई / आई (Drik पायी / आयी), सिद्धि (Drik सिद्दि), महामूढ़, त्रिपुर-प्यारी, भयहारी, महानन्दे, नामा-कर्षिणी चित्ता-कर्षिणी सर्व-मोहिनी (Drik चिन्ता कर्षिणी), जगमाता, सौभाग्य-दायिनी, मनवांछित, भण्डासुर का (Drik कि), चन्द्र-धारणी नैमिषवासिनी (Drik धारिणी नैमिश्वासिनी), अघनाशिनी (Drik अधनाशिनी), सन्तति, नाय कर (Drik करे). The book's होता तब सेवा अनुरागी is the book's, Drik Panchang the same.

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
- **chalisa.cau19** — त्रिपुर-प्यारी: "beloved of the three worlds" (त्रिपुर, the three worlds, as in the Goddess's names Tripurasundarī and Tripureśvarī); Śiva is Tripurāri, not त्रिपुर, so "beloved of Śiva" would need a word the line does not have. Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.cau32** — चक्र-कपाली: the plain words, "bearer of the discus and the skull", in a line naming her as Lakṣmī, Durgā, Kālī and Śāradā. Meaning unchanged.
