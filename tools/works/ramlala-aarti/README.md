# आरती कीजे श्री रामलला की (Āratī Kīje Śrī Rāmalalā Kī)

An āratī of Śrī Rāma in Hindi (`hi`), in seven stanzas: words by Suresh Yash, sung by Sohini Mishra (Vianet Media, 2024, for the consecration of Rāmalalā at the Rāma temple in Ayodhya).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): the words shown with the song, from the owner's screenshot (`scriptures-data`: `hinduism/aarti/rama/ramlala-aarti/screenshots/sohini-mishra/lyrics.jpg`). Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recording:** Sohini Mishra (`audio/sohini-mishra`, YouTube `3_QR2HxO3N4`; the audio in `scriptures-data` at `hinduism/aarti/rama/ramlala-aarti/audio/sohini-mishra/`), timed from its transcript.

Verse types: टेक (refrain, `tek`, shown once at the start although it is sung after every stanza) and पद (stanza, `pad`). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (Aarti Kije Shri Ramlala Ki) › one section (`aarti`) › verses: the refrain (`aarti.tek`, type `tek`) and seven stanzas (`aarti.pad1`–`pad7`, type `pad`).
- **Numbering:** each stanza's `n` is its number (॥१॥ …), and it is cited by it ("Stanza 3"); the refrain has none.
- **Topics:** one, the āratī's title. **Reading plans:** none.
