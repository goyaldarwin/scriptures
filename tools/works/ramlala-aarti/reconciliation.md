# Reconciliation: आरती कीजे श्री रामलला की

## Source
The owner's screenshot of the words shown with Sohini Mishra's song (two columns), shared with the recording (YouTube `3_QR2HxO3N4`); in `scriptures-data` at `hinduism/aarti/rama/ramlala-aarti/screenshots/sohini-mishra/lyrics.jpg`. It is the text's only witness; the recording's transcript confirms the order of every line.

The rule (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §2): keep the source; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (§3): ।/॥ for the daṇḍas, stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

## Choices
| Place | Screenshot | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| refrain | each line twice, then the first again | once | The refrain is shown once (docs/PLAYBOOK.md: āratīs); it is sung after every stanza. |
| every stanza | its first couplet twice | once | A sung repeat of the same words, as the refrain. |
| the end | the refrain again | (not repeated) | The closing repeat of the refrain. |
| 1 | दशरथसूत हरी | दशरथसुत हरि | सूत (charioteer) and हरी (green) are slips: the line names Daśaratha's son (सुत, as in कौशल्यासुत before it) and Hari. |
| 2 | दिव्यायुद्ध | दिव्यायुध | आयुध (weapon) goes with धनुष बाण; युद्ध (war) gives no sense. |
| 6 | शिव आज इंद्र | शिव अज इंद्र | अज (Brahmā, the unborn) stands with Śiva and Indra; आज (today) gives no sense. |
