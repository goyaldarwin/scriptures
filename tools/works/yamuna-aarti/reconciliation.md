# Reconciliation: ॐ जय यमुना माता

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base)** (`hinduism/aarti/yamuna/yamuna-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/yamuna-mata-aarti.html`), "यमुना माता आरती": the opening stanza (ॐ जय यमुना माता), five stanzas of four printed lines, the refrain cue (ॐ जय यमुना माता...) after each, and the opening stanza again at the end. The printed book (Aarti Chalisa Sangrah) has no copy, so Drik Panchang is the only witness.

The rule: keep the base; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Base | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| after every stanza | ॐ जय यमुना माता...॥ | (not shown) | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item (टेक). |
| at the end | the opening stanza again | (not repeated) | Same reason. |
| refrain, line 1 | ॐ जय यमुना माता, | ॐ जय यमुना माता । | The standard: no comma; every line but the last ends ।. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥५॥ | The standard. |
| stanza 5, line 2 | शुभ मन्गल करणी | शुभ मंगल करणी | The page types the nasal before a consonant as न्; न् before ग is not a Hindi spelling, and is written ं. |

Kept as the base has them: सुख दुःख की दाता, श्रीयमुना joined, बहै, निस्तारा, प्रातः ही, महातम, चारों वेद कही, आन तुम्हारे माता, लियो, दियो, हरणी, करणी, भया, वैतरणी.

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
- aarti.tek — सुख दुःख की दाता: "the giver of joy and sorrow", what the words say (सुख दुःख a pair under one की). Drik Panchang is the only witness; "joy in place of sorrow" would need a word the line does not have.
- aarti.pad5 — तुम बिन वैतरणी, no verb: the least is supplied: "without you, the Vaitaraṇī lies before me" (the restless mind facing the river of death); "who will take me across" adds a question and a crossing the line does not say.
