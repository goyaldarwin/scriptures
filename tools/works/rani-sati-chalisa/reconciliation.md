# Reconciliation: श्री राणी सती चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), pp. 283–287; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-282 … i-286; the closing dohā is on i-286, above the book's "आरती श्री राणीसती जी की", which is a work of its own), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/rani-sati/rani-sati-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-rani-sati-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, sharing the book's slips (भवान, सुन्दरर), with its own (करूणामयी, उछाङ, श्रृंगार).
- **For a doubtful reading only:** the text as other sites print it, found by web search: InstaAstro (instaastro.com, "shri rani sati dadi ji chalisa"), Festivals of India (festivalsofindia.in, "ranisati-mela/sati_dadi_ki_puja") and Bhakti Bharat ("shri-rani-sati-dadi-ji", whose text here differs from Drik Panchang's). Sites that copy one another count as one witness.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥` (the book ends both lines of every dohā with `।`); the book's commas are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 1 | नमो नमो श्री सती भवान | नमो नमो श्री सती भवानी | भवान is not a word here and breaks the rhyme with मानी (and the half's 16 mātrās); InstaAstro, Festivals of India and Bhakti Bharat all have भवानी. Drik Panchang has भवान, as the book does. |
| chaupāī 6 | गज मुक्ता नथ सुन्दरर नीकी | गज मुक्ता नथ सुन्दर नीकी | सुन्दरर is a doubled letter: not a word, and a seventeenth mātrā. InstaAstro, Festivals of India and Bhakti Bharat have सुन्दर / सुंदर; Drik Panchang has सुन्दरर, as the book does. |
| chaupāī 19 | वेद ध्वनि मु से उच्चरते | वेद ध्वनि मुख से उच्चरते | A dropped letter: मु is not a word, and the half is a mātrā short. Drik Panchang, InstaAstro, Festivals of India and Bhakti Bharat have मुख से ("from the mouth"). |
| chaupāī 30 | कष्ट निवारिणी, दुःख नाशिनी, करुणामयी | कष्ट निवारिणी दुःख नाशिनी । करुणामयी | The comma inside the first half is the printer's; the standard keeps no commas. |
| chaupāī 40 | 'गोपीराम' (मैं) शरण ली थारी | गोपीराम शरण ली थारी | The quotation marks around the poet's name are the printer's. The bracketed (मैं) is an editor's gloss, not part of the verse: with it the half has 18 mātrās, without it 16; Festivals of India prints the line without it. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: करुणामयी (dohā 2 and chaupāī 30; Drik करूणामयी), सती को (chaupāī 13; Drik सती का), उछाड़-उछाड़ते (Drik उछाङ), शृंगार (Drik श्रृंगार). Kept as both have them: बरणौं, रह्यो, संकटकूँ, चूँदर, सोलहुँ, गुरसामलजी, महम डोकवा, कुल दोके, झुझारे, सद् गति, हुवा, बावनकूँ / मंगलकूँ, नौमी, आवे, जडूला, बाँसल गोती, न्यूत, सकड़ भीड़ / छीड़, भव भौति, नामां, इसि धामा, टँकारे, छत्तिसों, तेरहुँ मण्ड, पढ़े यह चालीसा जो शतबारा (a long half, as printed).

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
- **dohā 2**, शरण गहि करुणामयी सुख सम्पत्ति संचार (a statement or a prayer): a statement, as printed; संचार is a noun, with no verb of asking: taking the Mother's refuge, happiness and wealth flow in.
- **chaupāī 28**, देश देश में भव भौति है: 'its glory is great'. Platts: भव, 'prosperity, welfare, excellence'; भौत (dialect) = बहुत, 'much'.
- **chaupāī 34**, तेरहुँ मण्ड: 'all thirteen pavilions' (मण्ड for मण्डप), as the words say; that they are the shrines of the thirteen satīs of chaupāī 31 is not printed and is not added.
