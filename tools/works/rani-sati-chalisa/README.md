# श्री राणी सती चालीसा (Shri Rani Sati Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Rāṇī Satī (Nārāyaṇī) of Jhunjhunu (झुँझुनू), that opens "श्री गुरु पद पंकज नमन", in Awadhi (`awa`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 283–287), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Poet:** chaupāī 40 names गोपीराम ("गोपीराम शरण ली थारी"). Chaupāī 14 gives the year of Satī's manifestation, Vikrama 1352 ("विक्रम तेरा सौ बावनकूँ").
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/babiita-ki-pratibha/`: Babiita Ki Pratibha (YouTube `hHH1PS_8NDI`, "Dadi chalisa | Bhadi Mawas Special | Shree Rani Sati Chalisa | Best Hindi Devotional Song"), its track 0:00–4:00 of the video (after 4:00: a jaikāra 'जय दादी की'). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohās (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohā (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 40 chaupāīs are numbered 1–40 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the three dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Hanumān Chālīsā's in the Sundarakāṇḍa (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** salutation and form, her birth and her satī, worship at the Jhunjhunu shrine, the glory of Satī, the prayer. **Reading plans:** none.
