# Reconciliation: आरती कीजै श्री रघुवर जी की

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), p. 16; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-015), read on the page image. It prints the āratī as nine lines, each two halves joined by a comma, with no stanza breaks or numbers.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/rama/raghuvar-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-raghuvar-aarti.html`): the same text, a half-line per line, in four groups with the refrain (आरती कीजै श्री रघुवर जी की) marked after each.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (the book's comma between the halves of a line becomes a line break, a half per line); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| the stanzas | nine printed lines, no breaks | the refrain (line 1) and four stanzas of two printed lines each | Drik Panchang's groups and the rhyme: each stanza's last half ends in की, as the refrain does. |
| every line | one printed line, its halves joined by a comma | two lines, ending । (and ॥ / ॥N॥ at a stanza's end) | The standard; the comma marks the half. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥४॥ | The standard. |
| stanza 1, line 4 | पुरूषोतम | पुरुषोतम | पुरूष (long ū) is a misspelling of पुरुष; Drik Panchang has पुरुषोतम. The single त of both witnesses is kept. |

Kept as the book has them (Drik Panchang the same unless noted): कीजै, सत् चित्, शोक-भय, विभिन्न, नर वर, वन्द्य, अवन्ह, व्रतधारी, दुख.
