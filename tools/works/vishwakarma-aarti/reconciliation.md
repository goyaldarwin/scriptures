# Reconciliation: प्रभु श्री विश्वकर्मा घर आवो

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), p. 89; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-088), read on the page image, under the heading "आरती श्री विश्वकर्मा जी की". It prints the refrain as one line, then four stanzas of two printed lines each (one half divided by a comma: सुदामा की विनय सुनी, और कंचन महल बनाये), with the cue "वि." after stanzas 2–4. The two lines above the heading (भव फंद विघ्नों से उसे … कष्ट विपदा चूर कर॥) are the closing dohā of the Viśvakarmā Chālīsā on the page before, not part of the āratī; the āratī ends on this page (p. 90 begins the Ravidās Chālīsā).
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/vishwakarma/vishwakarma-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-vishwakarma-aarti.html`): the same text, word for word, each printed line of the book set out as two lines; the refrain and the सुदामा stanza in one group, with the cue "प्रभु श्री विश्वकर्मा घर आवो...॥" after every stanza.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (a printed line is split at its half, one half per line); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| every line | one printed line, the half marked by a comma only once (सुदामा की विनय सुनी, …) | two lines, a half per line | The standard. Where the book prints no comma, the line is split where Drik Panchang splits it, as its fellow is split at the comma. |
| the refrain | the first printed line, no cue | `aarti.tek`, two lines ending । and ॥ | It is the line the "वि." cue (Drik Panchang: प्रभु श्री विश्वकर्मा घर आवो...) repeats after each stanza; the सुदामा lines are the first stanza. |
| after stanzas 2–4 | वि.॥ | (not shown) | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item (टेक). |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥४॥ | The standard. |

Kept as the book has them (Drik Panchang agrees throughout): आवो, बनाये, पदारथ, प्रभु जी, टारे (which does not rhyme with बनाये), करी, द्वारिकापुरी बनाओ, ग्वाल बालों, बचायो, बांध रचि डारो, करावो, आके दर्श दिखावो, बनावो.

## Meanings

## Readings settled
- **aarti.pad3** — प्रभु लंका विजय करावो: read as a past deed, like its stanza (पूजन की, रचि डारो, पार किया): करावो is the Braj past करायो written with व, as आवो/आयो, beside बचायो in stanza 2. Meaning unchanged.
- **aarti.pad4** — श्री कृष्ण की विजय सुनो: kept as printed (the book and Drik Panchang agree on विजय; विनय is a guess no witness prints). "Hear of Śrī Kṛṣṇa's victory." Meaning unchanged.
