# Reconciliation: जय हो गंगाराम बाबा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), pp. 171–172, after the Bābā Gaṅgārām Chālīsā; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-170, i-171), read on the page images. Its order: the refrain, सच्चे मन से, प्रातःकाल, रोग शोक, देवलोक में, श्रद्धा भाव, म्हें हां बालक, सुख सम्पत्ति. The dohā above the āratī on p. 171 (सुने सुनावे प्रेम से) is the Chālīsā's closing dohā, not part of this work.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/baba-gangaram/baba-gangaram-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/baba-gangaram-aarti.html`): the same āratī, in the same order and the same words, each printed line broken in two.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; each line as the book prints it; the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥७॥ | The standard. |
| after the refrain and every stanza | बाबा'' | (not shown) | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item (टेक). |

Kept as the book has them: every word; Drik Panchang does not differ from the book in any word (it ends stanzas 3 and 5 with । where the book has ॥).
