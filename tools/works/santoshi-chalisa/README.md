# श्री संतोषी माँ चालीसा (Shri Santoshi Maa Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Santoṣī Mā that opens "श्री गणपति पद नाय सिर धरि हिय शारदा ध्यान", in Awadhi (`awa`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 236–239), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/bhaktidhaam/`: BhaktiDhaam (भक्तिधाम; the singers are not named), "सम्पूर्ण चालीसा संग्रह", a jukebox (YouTube `lCwPg0LRRjk`), this chālīsā at 29:50–35:09. The starts come from the transcript (`transcripts/`, with hand-checked starts in `fixes.md`), to be set by ear. It sings the book's text (chaupāī 17 may be sung "हंस वाहिनी" for the witnesses' "हंस मोहिनी": to be heard); chaupāīs 5–8, not in the transcript, are placed by length and listed to check, as is the opening dohā (the 12 s before chaupāī 1 are not in the transcript); the closing dohā is not sung and stays untimed.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohā (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohā (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 40 chaupāīs are numbered 1–40 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the two dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Hanumān Chālīsā's in the Sundarakāṇḍa (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** the Mother's form, the Mother's forms, the fruit of meditation and the vow, the prayer and the fruit of the reading. **Reading plans:** none.
