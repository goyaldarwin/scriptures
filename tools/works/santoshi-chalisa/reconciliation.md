# Reconciliation: श्री संतोषी माँ चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 236–239; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-235 … i-238), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/santoshi/santoshi-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/santoshi-mata-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, with its own slips (पुरनान, मनोकमना).

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept; its hyphens are.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 19 | पृथ्वी सूर्य चन्द्र अरू तारे | पृथ्वी सूर्य चन्द्र अरु तारे | अरू is a slip for अरु ("and"), as Drik Panchang has it. |
| opening dohā | its second line ends । | ends ॥ | The standard (a dohā's second line ends ॥); the book's single daṇḍa is a slip, its closing dohā ends ॥. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: करूँ (Drik करुँ), कहूं (Drik कहुं), पुरानन (Drik पुरनान), मनोकामना (Drik मनोकमना), कोई in chaupāī 26 (Drik कोइ), सम्पति in chaupāī 28 (Drik संपत्ति), आनन्द (Drik आनंद), ब्रत in chaupāī 34 (Drik व्रत). Kept as both have them: कहलावहं, गिरजा, निरंकार साकार दाहिनी, व्याकुलयारी.
