# जय जय श्री बगलामुखी माता (Jai Jai Shri Bagalamukhi Mata)

The āratī of Mother Bagalāmukhī, in Hindi (`hi`). Its closing dohā names its poet, Kulapati Miśra (कुलपति मिश्र).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, p. 255), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** none yet.

Verse types: टेक (refrain, `tek`, shown once at the start although it is sung after every stanza), पद (stanza, `pad`) and दोहा (`do`, the closing dohā). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (Jai Jai Shri Bagalamukhi Mata) › one section (`aarti`) › verses: the refrain (`aarti.tek`, type `tek`), 10 stanzas and the closing dohā (`aarti.do1`, type `do`).
- **Numbering:** each stanza's `n` is its number (॥१॥ …), and it is cited by it ("Stanza 3"); the refrain and the dohā have none.
- **Topics:** one, the āratī's title. **Reading plans:** none.
