# Reconciliation: जय जय श्री बगलामुखी माता

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), p. 255, under the heading "आरती श्री बगलामुखी जी की"; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-254), read on the page image. The refrain (marked टेक), ten printed lines each followed by the refrain cue (जय-जय…): पीत वसन, कर-कमलों, चम्पक माल, त्रिविध ताप, पालत हरत, मोह निशा, तिमिर नशावहु, सन्तन को, तव चरणन, प्रेम सहित; then a दोहा with the poet's name (कुलपति मिश्र). Page image i-250, also listed in the kit, is the Bagalāmukhī Chālīsā (its opening), not part of the āratī.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/bagalamukhi/bagalamukhi-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/bagalamukhi-mata-aarti.html`): the same text, word for word, in the same order, a half-line per line, grouped two printed lines to a stanza (the first group with the refrain), and the same closing दोहा.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (the book's comma between the halves of a line becomes a line break, a half per line); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| the stanzas | ten printed lines, each followed by the cue जय-जय | ten stanzas, one printed line each | The book's own unit: it sings the refrain after every line. Drik Panchang's grouping of two lines to a stanza is not the book's. |
| every line | one printed line, its halves joined by a comma | two lines, ending । and ॥N॥ (the refrain ॥) | The standard; the comma marks the half. |
| stanza 6, line 2 | करहु हृदय महँ, तुम उजियारी | करहु हृदय महँ तुम उजियारी | The standard (no comma). |
| the refrain | … तुम्हारी॥ टेक॥ | … तुम्हारी ॥ | The टेक mark names the refrain; it is not part of the verse. |
| after every stanza | जय-जय… | not written | The refrain cue; the refrain is shown once. |
| the दोहा | दोहा—बगलामुखी की आरती, पढ़ै सुनै जो कोय। … | a verse of type `do` (`aarti.do1`), its two lines ending । and ॥, no commas | Part of the āratī (its closing, with the poet's name), printed as a दोहा in both witnesses; the label दोहा is not part of the verse. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥१०॥ | The standard. |

Kept as the book has them (Drik Panchang the same): आरति करहुँ, सोहै, छबि, धारै, अस्तुति करहिं, लहरावे, मिटि जात, महँ, नशावहु, तुमही, पियारी, लगावै, भव-भयहारी, मोक्षधाम, पढ़ै सुनै, सुख-सम्पति.
