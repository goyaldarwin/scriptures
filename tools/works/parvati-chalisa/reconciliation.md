# Reconciliation: श्री पार्वती चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 246–249; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-245 … i-248), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/parvati/parvati-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/parvati-mata-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, differing only in spellings.
- **For a doubtful reading only** (chaupāī 22): Shubh Panchang (shubhpanchang.com) and tring.co.in, found by web search; both print पहआयी as the book does.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| opening dohā | अम्बे! शक्ति! भवानि | अम्बे शक्ति भवानि | The exclamation marks are the printer's punctuation, not part of the verse. |
| chaupāī 13 | हैं महेश प्राणेश! तुम्हारे | हैं महेश प्राणेश तुम्हारे | The same. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: शंभु (Drik शम्भु), कटि (Drik कटी), कंठ मदार (Drik कण्ठ), आभूषण है भुजंग भयंकर (Drik हैं), विदारिणि, अनपायिनि, सप्तरिषी, चंद्रिका. The book's सौत समान शम्भु पहआयी is kept as printed: Drik Panchang, Shubh Panchang and tring.co.in print the same; it reads as पहँ आयी ("came to Śambhu"), but no witness prints that form (settled below, Readings settled).

## Readings settled
- **chalisa.cau3** — स्थित रक्षा लय हित सजाता: **meaning changed.** It had read स्थित as स्थिति ("continuance") and made the Goddess the subject; but the printed word is स्थित ("stationed, standing"), and सजाता is masculine, agreeing with तेऊ ("they": Brahmā, Śiva, Kārttikeya and Śeṣa of cau1–2), not with the Goddess. Now the plain words: "Even they, who stand in their places ordering protection and dissolution, do not reach your end." Text unchanged; the word senses for स्थित and सजाता added to fit (settle-4 output).
- **chalisa.cau9** — तापर राजति हरि चतुरानन: the plain words, "on it (the throne) shine Hari and the four-faced (Brahmā)"; the Goddess is not named in the line. Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.cau22** — शम्भु पहआयी: kept as printed (the book, Drik Panchang, Shubh Panchang and tring.co.in all print पहआयी; no independent text prints पहँ आयी, so no correction under §2). The sense is पहँ ("to, near") आयी, "came to Śambhu", which the meaning already gives. Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.do2** — कूट चंद्रिका सुभग शिर: कूट "top, summit, crest" (Monier-Williams, Apte): moonlight on the crown of her fair head. Meaning unchanged.
