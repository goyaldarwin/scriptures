# Reconciliation: श्री जाहरवीर चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 101–105; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-100 … i-104), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/jaharveer/jaharveer-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-jaharveer-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, verse for verse.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 2 | गुरु गोरख का हे वरदानी | गुरु गोरख का है वरदानी | हे (a call, "O") cannot stand inside the phrase "Guru Gorakh's boon-given one"; the verb है ("is") is needed, as Drik Panchang has it. A dropped stroke of the ै sign. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: विसाला (chaupāī 3; Drik विशाला), महादुखी (chaupāī 12; Drik महादुःखी), मन्सा (chaupāī 51; Drik मनसा, as the book itself has in chaupāī 30). The spellings रनधीर, वन्स, मुकट, लासानी, ईसवी सन हजार दरमियाना, नौलक्खा / दुक्खा, कुआ, नाई, इकादसी, कपटिन, परसादी, पण्डतानी, भज्जु कुतवाल जना रणधीरा (which does not rhyme with घोड़ा), मेहमाना, जगदीसा and the last half's गोरखनाथ (against विधाता) are the book's, as Drik's.
