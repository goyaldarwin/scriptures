# श्री जाहरवीर चालीसा (Shri Jaharveer Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Jāharvīr Gogājī (Gūgā Vīr, the Chauhān hero of Bāgaṛ, son of Jevar and Bāchhal) that opens "सुवन केहरी जेवर सुत", in Awadhi (`awa`) with Rajasthani and Haryanvi words. Its chaupāīs tell his story: Bāchhal's penance, Gorakhnāth's boon, his birth, marriage and battles.

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 101–105), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** none yet.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › two sections: the opening dohās (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`) › verses. The work has no closing dohā.
- **Numbering:** the 53 chaupāīs (more than forty, as printed) are numbered 1–53 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the two dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Gaṇeśa Chālīsā's (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** Jāharvīr's form, the queen's penance for a son, Gorakhnāth's boon, birth, marriage and battle, the fruit of service and reading. **Reading plans:** none.
