# श्री विन्ध्येश्वरी चालीसा (Shri Vindhyeshwari Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Vindhyeśvarī, the Mother of the Vindhya hills, that opens "नमो नमो विन्ध्येश्वरी", in Awadhi (`awa`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 179–182), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** none yet.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › two sections: the opening dohā (`chalisa-do`) and the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`) › verses. The book prints no closing dohā; the chālīsā ends with its last chaupāī.
- **Numbering:** the 38 chaupāīs are numbered 1–38 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the dohā has no number of its own and is cited by its section ("Opening · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Gaṇeśa Chālīsā's (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** hail to the Mother and her glory, her many names, what her worship brings, the closing prayer. **Reading plans:** none.
