# Reconciliation: श्री विन्ध्येश्वरी चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 179–182; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-178 … i-181), read on the page images. The āratī printed after the last chaupāī (आरती श्री विन्ध्येश्वरी देवी जी की) is its own work.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/vindhyeshwari/vindhyeshwari-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/vindhyeshwari-mata-chalisa.html`): the web text of the same chālīsā, with its own slips (लक्श्मी, रक्शिणी).
- **Chalisa Mala** (the indif.com printout in the same scriptures-data folder, images i-425 … i-427): another copy of the web text, opening with a Sanskrit śloka (देवि त्वं भक्तसुलभे …) that the book does not print; used only as a check.

The book's version differs from the web text in many small readings, and the book's is kept: सन्त जनों के काज में करती (web माँ करती), जय जय सन्त असुर सुर सेवी (web जय जय जय जय असुरासुर सेवी), अष्ट भुजी वाराहिनी देवा … सेवा (web देवी … सेवी), मात संकटी (web मातु सुगन्धा), सिद्ध करिए अब यह मम बानी (web सिद्धि करिय अम्बे), जा कहँ पुत्र (web जो नर पुत्र), कृपा दृष्टि जापर हुई जाई (web तापर होई जाई).

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 15 | चौसट्टी देवी कल्यानी | चौसट्ठी देवी कल्यानी | A wrong letter: the word is "sixty-four" (चौंसठ), with ठ; Chalisa Mala has चौसट्ठी, Drik Panchang चोंसट्ठी. |
| chaupāī 25 | जो नर पाठ करे मन माई | जो नर पाठ करे मन लाई | मन माई gives no sense; the phrase is मन लाई ("with the mind fixed on it"), as the book itself has in chaupāī 35 (पाठ करे मन लाई). Drik Panchang and Chalisa Mala have मन लाई. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: सन्त जनों, सिंहवाहिनी जय जगमाता (Drik जै जग माता), निवारिणी, दुख, कोउ (Drik कोई), तुरतहिं, रुद्रानी, श्यामा (Drik शामा), मातु / मात as the book has each, सुख दाता, जाह्नवी (Drik जान्हवी), उत्राणी, हेमावती अम्ब निरवाणी (Drik अम्बे निर्वानी), अष्ट भुजी, भद्रकालि, वज्र धारिणी, बरनै, जापर, मोपर, विपति, जो देवी का जाप (Drik कर), करे शतबारा, अति सुख (Drik बहु), सतावे, दूर, महँ, वचन, देविहिं सुमिरे, पाँच, करावे / जिमावे, नौरातन (Drik नौरातर; नौरातन, "in the Navarātras", is the -न plural of नौरात), होहिं, देहिं ताकहँ गुणखानी, आन चढ़ावे, करवावे, नित्य प्रति, मानहुँ, जग मातु, मोहिं पर.

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
- **chalisa.cau13** (उत्राणी): kept as a name of the Goddess, as all three witnesses print it (the book, Drik Panchang उत्रानी, Chalisa Mala); not a slip for रुद्राणी, which the chālīsā already names (chaupāī 8, रुद्रानी). Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.cau16** (दन्त कुमारी): kept as one name, Danta Kumārī, as the line's words give it; splitting it (Danteśvarī, Kumārī) would supply names the text does not print. Meaning unchanged.
