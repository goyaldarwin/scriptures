# Reconciliation: मैया शारदा तोरे दरबार आरती नित गाऊँ

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), pp. 270–271, under the heading "आरती श्री शारदा जी की"; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-269, i-270), read on the page images. A dohā (भुवन विराजी शारदा), the refrain (मैया शारदा तोरे दरबार आरती नित गाऊँ, with नित गाऊँ मैया नित गाऊँ), then six stanzas, each printed as its line, the line again with दर्श तोरे पाऊँ, and the refrain cue (-३): श्रद्धा को दीया, मन की माला, बल को भोग, तप को हार, माँ के भजन, सुमर-सुमर; it ends with the refrain repeated.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/sharda/sharda-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/sharda-mata-aarti.html`): the same text in the same order, a half-line per line, with x2 / x3 repeat marks and a closing return of the refrain.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| stanza 5 | सुनाऊ (the second printing; the first has सुनाऊँ) | सुनाऊँ | The rhyme of every stanza is -ऊँ (चढ़ाऊँ, नवाऊँ, पाऊँ); the book's first printing has सुनाऊँ. Drik Panchang's सुनाऊ is the same dropped sign. |
| the closing | मैया शारदा तोरे दरबार आरती नित गाऊँ, twice and -३ | not written | The closing repeat of the refrain. |

Kept as the book has them (Drik Panchang the same unless noted): विराजी, अपरम्पार, तोरे, असुअन, स्वांस, कंधे से, कर्ण, सन्तन, सुमर-सुमर, गावे (the second printing and Drik Panchang; the book's first printing गावें), जस, चरनन, दर्श.

## Readings settled
The readings the words alone leave open, each decided (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §2).

- **aarti.do1** — धरो मात अवतार: past, the Braj perfective (धर्यो/धरो), after the description भुवन विराजी … महिमा अपरम्पार; the dohā tells who she is and the prayer begins only with the refrain. Meaning unchanged.
- **aarti.pad3** — कंधे से विनय सुनाऊँ: kept as printed (the book and Drik Panchang agree; कंठ से is attested by no source a web search found). स्वांस दिन राती stays one of the stanza's offerings. The meaning now marks "bowed" as implied: "with (bowed) shoulders" / "(झुके) कंधोंसे", since the words say only "with the shoulders".
- **aarti.pad4** — कर्ण को टीका: kept as printed (both witnesses; कर्म को is attested nowhere), read in the stanza's pattern of inner offerings as the ṭīkā of the ear, of listening. Meaning unchanged.
