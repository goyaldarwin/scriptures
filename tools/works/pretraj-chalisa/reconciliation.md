# Reconciliation: श्री प्रेतराज चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), pp. 107–110; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-106 … i-109), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/pretraj/pretraj-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-pretraj-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, with its own slips (गजारुढ़, रुप, भुपति).
- **For a doubtful reading only:** a web search for chaupāī 22 found no other copy of this chālīsā; the reading is settled under "Readings settled" below.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 25 | रोगी, पागल सन्तति हीना | रोगी पागल सन्तति हीना | The comma inside the half is the printer's; the standard keeps no commas. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: गुरू (Drik गुरु), अरू (chaupāīs 5, 31; Drik अरु), गजारूढ़, मगन ही मन में (Drik मगन हो), रूप, सुत अरु धन हीना (Drik धन छीना), भूपति. Kept as both have them: पड़ा हूँ चरण सहारे (chaupāī 22, short by about four mātrās; nothing shows the missing words: "Readings settled" below), लिखूं, अंजिन सुवन, चालीसा / लवलेशा.

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
- **chalisa.cau22** (पड़ा हूँ चरण सहारे, about four mātrās short): as printed; the book (i-108) and Drik Panchang agree and a web search found no other copy, so no word is supplied. Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.cau3** (सुन for सुर?): सुन, as the book (page image) and Drik Panchang print it, and a web search found no other copy; सुन, "hearing", reads plainly beside देखत: "on seeing it and hearing of it, men and sages are enchanted". Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.cau30** (whose red body?): the messengers of cau29, the subject the couplet runs on from; nothing in the line names another. Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.do4** (अंजिन सुवन and प्रेतराज: one or two?): two, addressed together: अंजिन सुवन is Hanumān's own epithet (Añjanī's son), and at Mehandipur, the shrine this chālīsā belongs to, Pretarāja is a deity of his own beside Hanumān (Bālājī), never Añjanī's son. Meaning unchanged.
