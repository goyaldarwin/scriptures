# Reconciliation: श्री प्रेतराज चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), pp. 107–110; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-106 … i-109), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/pretraj/pretraj-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-pretraj-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, with its own slips (गजारुढ़, रुप, भुपति).
- **For a doubtful reading only:** a web search for chaupāī 22 found no other copy of this chālīsā; the open reading is in `docs/reviews/pretraj-chalisa-doubts.jsonl`.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 25 | रोगी, पागल सन्तति हीना | रोगी पागल सन्तति हीना | The comma inside the half is the printer's; the standard keeps no commas. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: गुरू (Drik गुरु), अरू (chaupāīs 5, 31; Drik अरु), गजारूढ़, मगन ही मन में (Drik मगन हो), रूप, सुत अरु धन हीना (Drik धन छीना), भूपति. Kept as both have them: पड़ा हूँ चरण सहारे (chaupāī 22, short by about four mātrās; nothing shows the missing words: `docs/reviews/pretraj-chalisa-doubts.jsonl`), लिखूं, अंजिन सुवन, चालीसा / लवलेशा.
