# श्री प्रेतराज चालीसा (Shri Pretraj Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Pretarāja Sarakāra, the deity of the Mehandipur Balaji shrine (घाटा मेंहदीपुर), that opens "गणपति की कर वंदना", in Awadhi (`awa`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 107–110), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Poet:** the last dohā names भक्त सुखराम ("कहत भक्त सुखराम").
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** none yet.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohās (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohās (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 40 chaupāīs are numbered 1–40 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the four dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Hanumān Chālīsā's in the Sundarakāṇḍa (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** Pretarāja's form, his coming to Mehandipur, the prayer, the glory of the Mehandipur shrine, the fruit of recitation and worship. **Reading plans:** none.
