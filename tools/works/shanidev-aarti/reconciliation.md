# Reconciliation: जय जय श्री शनि देव

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), p. 58; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-057), read on the page image, under the heading "आरती श्री शनि देव जी की". Each stanza is two printed lines, the first ending in a comma. (Page image i-058, also listed in the kit, is the second Śani Chālīsā, not part of the āratī.)
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/shani/shanidev-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-shanidev-aarti.html`): the same āratī in the same order, each printed line broken into two, with other wordings in several lines (below).

The recording: Chand Kumar, "Shani Aarti" (YouTube `JtJ4P0302FY`), the owner's choice. It sings this āratī (not जय शनि देवा, `jai-shani-deva-aarti`): the refrain, stanzas 1–3, and the refrain over and over to the end; **it does not sing stanza 4** (देव दनुज ऋषि मुनी…), which stays in the text, untimed (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3). Where it sings another written wording, the text takes it (the refrain's two lines); its other lines are the book's: नीलाम्बर, शीश राजित, मोदक मिष्ठान पान चढ़त हैं सुपारी, महिषी अति प्यारी.

The rule: keep the book, except where the recording sings another witness's wording; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

|---|---|---|---|
| every stanza | two printed lines, the first ending in a comma | two lines, ending । and ॥N॥ | The standard. |

| refrain, line 1 | जय जय जय श्री शनि देव | जय जय श्री शनि देव | As Chand Kumar sings it: two जय, every time he sings the line (the transcript: "जेजे श्रिशनी देव"; scored on the audio, two जय against three: −24.1 against −34.0 at 19–29 s, −22.7 against −32.6 at 53–63 s), as Drik Panchang writes it (जय जय श्री शनिदेव), with शनि देव as the book spaces it. |
| refrain, line 2 | सूर्य पुत्र प्रभुछाया महतारी | सूरज के पुत्र प्रभु छाया महतारी | As Chand Kumar sings it (the recording, below): Drik Panchang's wording (सूरज के पुत्र प्रभु / छाया महतारी), the same sense. प्रभु and छाया apart, as Drik Panchang prints them: प्रभु ends the address "Lord, son of the Sun", and छाया महतारी is "whose mother is Chhāyā". |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: नीलाम्बर (Drik निलाम्बर), शीश राजित (Drik शीश सहज), मोदक मिष्ठान पान चढ़त हैं सुपारी (Drik मोदक और मिष्ठान चढ़े, चढ़ती पान सुपारी), लोहा तिल तेल उड़द महिषी अति प्यारी (Drik … महिषी है अति प्यारी), मुनी (Drik मुनि), विश्वनाथ धरत ध्यान शरण हैं तुम्हारी (Drik … ध्यान हम हैं शरण तुम्हारी), क्रीट, मिष्ठान, लिलारी (both).

## Readings settled
- **aarti.pad4** (विश्वनाथ धरत ध्यान शरण हैं तुम्हारी): विश्वनाथ is kept as the name in the line (Śiva, Lord of the universe, or the poet's signature: both are the name Viśvanātha), who meditates on Śani and is in his refuge; Drik Panchang's हम हैं is another line. Meaning as given.
