# Reconciliation: जय जय जय श्री शनि देव

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), p. 58; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-057), read on the page image, under the heading "आरती श्री शनि देव जी की". Each stanza is two printed lines, the first ending in a comma. (Page image i-058, also listed in the kit, is the second Śani Chālīsā, not part of the āratī.)
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/shani/shanidev-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-shanidev-aarti.html`): the same āratī in the same order, each printed line broken into two, with other wordings in several lines (below).

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| every stanza | two printed lines, the first ending in a comma | two lines, ending । and ॥N॥ | The standard. |
| after every stanza | ॥ जय.॥ (after the refrain and the last stanza ॥ जय जय जय (श्री) शनि देव.॥) | (not shown) | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item (टेक). |
| refrain, line 2 | सूर्य पुत्र प्रभुछाया महतारी | सूर्य पुत्र प्रभु छाया महतारी | A missing space: प्रभु ends the address "Lord, son of the Sun", and छाया महतारी is "whose mother is Chhāyā"; Drik Panchang prints प्रभु and छाया apart. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: जय जय जय श्री शनि देव (Drik जय जय श्री शनिदेव), सूर्य पुत्र (Drik सूरज के पुत्र), नीलाम्बर (Drik निलाम्बर), शीश राजित (Drik शीश सहज), मोदक मिष्ठान पान चढ़त हैं सुपारी (Drik मोदक और मिष्ठान चढ़े, चढ़ती पान सुपारी), लोहा तिल तेल उड़द महिषी अति प्यारी (Drik … महिषी है अति प्यारी), मुनी (Drik मुनि), विश्वनाथ धरत ध्यान शरण हैं तुम्हारी (Drik … ध्यान हम हैं शरण तुम्हारी), क्रीट, मिष्ठान, लिलारी (both).
