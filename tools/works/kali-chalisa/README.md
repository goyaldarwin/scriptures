# श्री काली चालीसा (Shri Kali Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Kālī that opens "जय काली जगदम्ब जय", in Awadhi (`awa`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 202–206), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** none yet.

The closing dohā's "शरणागत 'भक्त' है" prints भक्त in quotation marks, as a poet's pen-name may be; no poet is named otherwise.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohās (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohā (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 54 chaupāīs are numbered 1–54 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the three dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Hanumān Chālīsā's in the Sundarakāṇḍa (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** the Mother's names, Kālī's form and her battles, the Śakti Pīṭhas and the cosmic form, the prayer. **Reading plans:** none.
