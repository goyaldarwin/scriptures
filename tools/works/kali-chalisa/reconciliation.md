# Reconciliation: श्री काली चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 202–206; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-201 … i-205), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/kali/kali-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/kali-mata-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, with its own slips (अग्निज्वाला, भुजामल, मनमोहन for the rhyme's मनमोहत).

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 14 | रूद्राणी तुम कृष्ण पिंगला | रुद्राणी तुम कृष्ण पिंगला | The name is Rudrāṇī (Rudra's consort); रूद्र- with a long ū is a spelling slip (Drik Panchang has the same slip). |
| chaupāī 14 | अग्रिज्वाल तुम सर्वमंगला | अग्निज्वाल तुम सर्वमंगला | अग्रि- is not a word: a misprint of अग्नि ("fire-flame"), as Drik Panchang has it. ज्वाल is the book's (Drik ज्वाला; ज्वाल keeps the half-line at 16 mātrās). |
| chaupāī 15 | सहस्त्राक्षि तुम अगजग भोगिनी | सहस्राक्षि तुम अगजग भोगिनी | सहस्त्र is a common misspelling of सहस्र ("thousand"); Drik Panchang has सहस्राक्षि. |
| chaupāī 24 | शंख चक्र अरू गदा सुहावन | शंख चक्र अरु गदा सुहावन | अरू is a spelling slip for अरु ("and"); Drik Panchang has the same slip. |
| closing dohā | शरणागत 'भक्त' है | शरणागत भक्त है | The quotation marks are the printer's, not part of the verse. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: हृदय-निकुंज (Drik हृदय निकुंज), कार्तिकी (Drik कार्तिका), उठाये / गिराये (Drik उठाए / गिराए), भुजामाल किंकण मनमोहत (Drik भुजामल किंकण मनमोहन; मनमोहत rhymes with सोहत), कीजिये (Drik कीजिए), होइ (Drik होई). The book's दाक्षाणायिनी, महातमा, कीन्हैउ, जिव्हा, मशान, विन्धवासिनी and the mixed तव / तब (तनु छवि … तव, अष्टादश तब) are the book's, Drik Panchang the same.

## Readings settled
The readings the words alone leave open, each decided (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §2).

- **chalisa.cau3** — जगजाया, "mother of the world" or "wife of the world's Lord": "she who gave birth to the world" kept. The line is a list of the Mother's names; जाया is "bringing forth" by its derivation (Monier-Williams, after Manu 9.8) and "born" in Hindi (Platts), and Śiva is not named in the line. Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.cau27** — मद्यपान कीन्हैउ रण गंगा: read as the words give it, रण गंगा "the Gaṅgā of battle", the stream of blood on the field, where she drank; no witness makes रण गंगा a place. Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.cau36** — काटहु: an imperative (the -हु form of the second person; the glossary's आज्ञार्थक), so the half is a request, "cut at once the net of worldly existence", as the poem's other requests in -हु (करहु, chaupāī 45). Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.cau42** — लेहु: an imperative ("take"), so the half is a request, "take incarnation to crush the hosts of the enemy"; a request inside praise is the chālīsā's habit. Meaning unchanged.
