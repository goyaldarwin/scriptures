# श्रीरामरक्षास्तोत्रम् (Śrī Rāma Rakṣā Stotram)

The Rāma Rakṣā of Budha Kauśika, in Sanskrit (`sa`), as the Vande Guru Paramparaam video shows it: the invocation, the viniyoga, the dhyāna, ślokas 1–38 and the colophon.

- **Text** (`text.txt`): made by `source/make.py` from `source/video_text.tsv`, the video's on-screen lines typed from the owner's screenshots; every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`), shown first; open questions on them: `docs/reviews/ramraksha-doubts.jsonl`. Copied: the video's English (`meanings/en/vande-guru-paramparaam.json`, made by `source/make.py`) and the स्तुति चालीसा संग्रह Hindi (`meanings/hi/stuti-chalisa-sangrah.json`, typed from the owner's printout of the page).
- **Recordings:** `audio/vande-guru-paramparaam/` (YouTube `ZIDdE4SZAuw`): live; hand-checked starts in `fixes.md`.

Verse types: मन्त्र (`mantra`), विनियोग (`viniyoga`), ध्यान (`dhyana`), श्लोक (`shloka`) and the closing words (`iti`). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.
