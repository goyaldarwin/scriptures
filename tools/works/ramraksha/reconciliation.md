**Meanings.** The Hindi of स्तुति चालीसा संग्रह is typed from the owner's printout of the page (kept in scriptures-data); text-layer breaks (कं ठ, कु ल, मुकु ट …) and plain typos are mended, the wording is the page's. Where the Sanskrit allows two readings, ours take: कण्ठे धारयेत् (13) literally, with “keeps by heart” in brackets; नः (21) as “us”, गच्छन् as “as he goes”; पुरुषः पूर्णः (22) as two names, as the word sheet lists them; मनोरमे (38) as the locative of रामे, with Śiva named as the speaker to Pārvatī; विश्वामित्रप्रिय (5) and विप्रप्रिय (26) as “dear to”; शतकोटिप्रविस्तरम् (1) as a hundred crore verses, the Śatakoṭi Rāmāyaṇa; मधुरं (34) adverbial; सर्वत्र (14) with लभते. Each is the plain construction of the words and the common traditional reading.
