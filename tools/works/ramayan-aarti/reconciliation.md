# Reconciliation: आरति श्रीरामायनजी की

## Source
The āratī printed at the end of the owner's Gita Press Śrī Rāmacaritamānasa (`scriptures-data`: `hinduism/granth/tulsidas/ramcharitmanas/books/gitapress/hindi.pdf`, under the heading श्रीरामायणजीकी आरती), as the Mānas's generator reads it from the PDF's glyphs (`tools/works/manas/source/make_appdata.py` → `book.json`, `vidhi.aarti`). It is the text's only witness; read again on the page image, every word agrees. The recording sings the same words in the same order.

The rule (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §2): keep the source; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. Nothing here is.

## Choices
| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| line ends | `।` and `।।` | `।` and `॥` | `।।` is one character, ॥ (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3). |
| verses | 18 lines, unnumbered | the refrain (lines 1–2) and four stanzas of four lines (`pad1`–`pad4`) | Each stanza's last line rhymes with the refrain (…की) and the refrain is sung after it in the recording; the stanzas are numbered for citing, as every āratī here, but no number is printed. |
| the refrain | printed once | once | It is sung after every stanza and twice at the end; shown once (docs/PLAYBOOK.md: āratīs). |

Kept as printed: the Awadhi spellings of Tulasīdāsa's house style (श्रीरामायनजी, बालमीक, बिग्यान, सेष, सास्त्र, संमत, कबिबर्ज, बिषय, जुबती), and the joined words (घटसंभव, कलिमल, पवनसुत).

## Meanings
Our own Hindi and English (tools/meanings/style.md), one piece per verse; no meaning of the āratī is printed in the book. The readings taken:
- **tek, कीरति कलित ललित सिय पी की:** the Rāmāyaṇa, adorned (कलित) with the glory of Sītā's beloved (सिय पी, Śrī Rāma) and lovely (ललित).
- **pad1, बरनि पवनसुत कीरति नीकी:** Hanumān has told of its fair glory; the subject of the stanza's गावत, as in the stanzas after it, is the Rāmāyaṇa.
- **pad2, छओ सास्त्र सब:** "all six śāstras" (the six darśanas) go with गावत, as the Vedas and Purāṇas before them; ग्रंथन को रस, the essence of the scriptures. सार अंस संमत सबही की: the essential part, approved by all (संमत, MW sammata "approved by").
- **pad3, घटसंभव:** Agastya, the sage born of a pot; कागभुसुंडि गरुड के ही की: of the heart (ही, हिय) of Kākabhuśuṇḍi and Garuḍa, the Mānas's own teller and listener: it dwells in their hearts.
- **pad4, सुभग सिंगार मुक्ति जुबती की:** the lovely adornment of Liberation pictured as a young woman; मूरि अमी की, a herb of nectar; दलन रोग भव, that crushes the disease of worldly existence.
