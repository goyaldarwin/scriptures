# आरती श्री रामायण जी की (Aarti Shri Ramayan Ji Ki)

The āratī of the Rāmāyaṇa (the Rāmacaritamānasa), sung at the close of its reading, in Hindi (`hi`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): Aarti Mala (page image i-364), checked against Drik Panchang, its stanzas in the order Kumar Vishu sings them (the book's stanzas 2 and 3 change places). Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/kumar-vishu/`: Kumar Vishu (कुमार विशु), the "Aarti Collection" jukebox (YouTube `rI-DXgiHALU`), the track 19:46–25:41 (1186–1541 s); every verse sung, stanzas in the text's order. The starts come from the transcript (`transcripts/`, with hand-checked starts in `fixes.md`), to be set by ear.

Verse types: टेक (refrain, `tek`, shown once at the start although it is sung after every stanza) and पद (stanza, `pad`). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (Aarti Shri Ramayan Ji Ki) › one section (`aarti`) › verses: the refrain (`aarti.tek`, type `tek`) and 4 stanzas, in the recording's order (`aarti.pad1`, `aarti.pad3`, `aarti.pad2`, `aarti.pad4`).
- **Numbering:** each stanza's `n` is its place in the recording's order (॥१॥ …), and it is cited by it ("Stanza 3"); the refrain has none. The book prints `aarti.pad3` as ३ and `aarti.pad2` as २.
- **Topics:** one, the āratī's title. **Reading plans:** none.
