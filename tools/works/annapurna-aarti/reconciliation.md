# Reconciliation: बारम्बार प्रणाम मैया बारम्बार प्रणाम

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), p. 245, under the heading "आरती श्री अन्नपूर्णा देवी जी की"; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-244), read on the page image. The refrain (बारम्बार प्रणाम), then ten printed lines, two to a stanza (the first ending ।, the second ॥): जो नहीं ध्यावै, प्रलय युगान्तर, चूमहि चरण, देवी देव, श्रीं ह्रीं.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/annapurna/annapurna-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/annapurna-mata-aarti.html`): the same text in the same order and grouping, each printed line broken at its half.
- **Aarti Mala** (scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/`, page image i-337, a printout of indif.com): the same text, with many slips of its own (जो ध्यावे तुम्ही, तिहारो, लेत, लखहि सलाम, तव जामा, कमला, सयोवर); not used for any reading.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| every stanza | two printed lines, the first ending ।, the second ॥ | two lines, ending । and ॥N॥ | The book's own lines (each rhymes in -आम); the book breaks no line at its half except by a comma in stanza 2, so the printed line stays one line throughout. |
| stanza 2 | युगान्तर और जन्मान्तर, कालान्तर … रचना करती, कहाँ | no commas | The standard (a pause within a line is a space). |
| stanza 5, line 1 | श्रीं, ह्रीं, श्रद्धा, श्रीं ऐं विद्या | श्रीं ह्रीं श्रद्धा श्रीं ऐं विद्या | The standard. |
| the refrain | one printed line ending । | one line ending ॥ | The standard (the refrain's last line ends ॥). |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥५॥ | The standard. |

Kept as the book has them (Drik Panchang the same unless noted): ध्यावै (Drik ध्यावे), कहां … कहाँ, तिहारे, सुर सुरों, चारु चक्रधरश्याम, चन्द्र चूड़, लखहि ललाम, त्राहि-त्राहि, शरण रूप, कान्तिभ्रांतिमयी कांति शांतिमयी, देतु.

## Readings settled
- aarti.pad4 — देवी देव दयनीय दशा में: "to goddesses and gods in a pitiable plight, your name is mercy, mercy" stays; the half has no verb of crying out or addressing, and देवी देव is a pair as in the stanza before (चतुरानन, चक्रधर, चन्द्रचूड़); the plain words give it.
