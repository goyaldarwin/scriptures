# Śrī Viṣṇu Sahasranāma Stotram — how the text is settled

**Base:** the owner's PDF of vishnusahasranamam.in, *विष्णु सहस्रनाम (सहस्त्रनाम) संपूर्ण पाठ* (pages 3–23, the stotra),
read in full. Its text layer splits conjuncts and words with spaces the printed page does not have (कृ ष्ण, के शव, सं सार,
यस्मिं श्च, स्वयं भूः, दुः ख, stotra 90 वं शवर्धनः); every such line was read against the printed page (rendered from the PDF) and written as
printed. Only the stotra's own text is taken; the page's headings (पूर्वन्यासः, अथ न्यासः, अथ करन्यासः, अथ षडङ्गन्यासः,
अथ ध्यानम्, स्तोत्रम्, उत्तरन्यासः, उपसंहारश्लोकाः, पर्यायोपसंहारश्लोकाः) are the work's sections and topics instead.

**Second witness:** the owner's roman transliteration of the stotra (no diacritics; `vsn-en`, private). It has the same
verses and order, without the second opening śloka, the nyāsas, the saṅkalpa, the seventh dhyāna śloka and the closing
ślokas, and it is loosely typed; it is used to confirm a reading, never alone to change one.

**Trusted sources used to settle a doubt:**
- sanskritdocuments.org, *Shrivishnu Sahasranama Stotram* (`doc_vishhnu/vsahasranew.pdf`, read through the fetch tool;
  its text layer is a legacy font, so only readings it shows clearly are counted). The base is a copy of this edition
  (the same headings पूर्वन्यासः and उत्तरन्यासः, the same refrains after 107 and 108), so where the two agree they count once.
- The Mahābhārata's critical text, Anuśāsana-parva 13.135 (as enjoylearningsanskrit.com prints it, verses 56 and 101).
- Monier-Williams for word forms.
The Gita Press Mahābhārata (Anuśāsana 149) could not be reached from this session.

प्राग्वंशो, as the printed page has them. लोके किं (purva 8) and इह लोके परे (paryāya 9) are two words on the page.

**Writing standard** (PLAYBOOK §3), applied to the base's spelling: a nasal before a stop inside a word is the class
nasal (सम्भवो, स्वयम्भूः, सङ्ग्रहः, सङ्कल्पः, सङ्कर्षणः, सत्यसन्धो, धनञ्जयः, सात्वताम्पतिः, किञ्चित्, निष्कण्टकः, गोविन्दाय,
मनसेन्द्रियैर्वा, अलङ्कृतम्, सङ्कीर्त्य …); a word-final ं before a daṇḍa or a vowel is म्; ॥ is one character and the verse
number is written ॥१॥.

**Kept as the base prints them** (valid readings): सुखिनो भवन्तु (uttara 32; sanskritdocuments.org, the second witness and
Sanskrit.Today, *Shloka – Vishnu 23*, read भवन्ति, but both recordings sing भवन्तु, and the optative is sound Sanskrit),
योगिभिर्ध्यानगम्यं (dhyāna 3; the second witness has
yogihṛddhyānagamyaṃ), the hyphen ending dhyāna 1's third line (…वर्षै-, the word runs on into रानन्दी), the ॥ inside the
viniyoga after …महामन्त्रस्य and …दिग्बन्धः, रोगास्सत्यं and पुनस्सत्यम् (visarga as स् before स), श्रीमतांवरः, क्षमिणांवरः,
अपांनिधिः (written as one word, as the base does), सन्तो (99), समात्माऽसम्मितः (12), भगहाऽऽनन्दी (60).

**Left out:** the lines closing each nyāsa (इति ऋष्यादिन्यासः ॥, इति करन्यासः ॥, इति षडङ्गन्यासः ॥), which the base prints as
headings' closes, as the Āditya Hṛdayam leaves them out.


Stotra 41, second line (करणं कारणं कर्ता विकर्ता गहनो गुहः): hidden on the base's printed page (PDF page 11) by the site's header bar; taken from the PDF's text layer, and the second witness has the same line ("Karanam karanam karta vikarta gahano guhah").

## Readings settled

**How a name is given in the meanings (the stotra, and the names in the pūrva-pīṭhikā, viniyoga and nyāsas).** Each
name's sense comes first and the name follows in round brackets, in IAST in English and in Devanagari in Hindi:
"He is the all-pervading (Viṣṇu), …" / "वे सर्वव्यापक (विष्णु), … हैं"; English names each name as a noun or adjective
phrase after "He is" ("the one who …", "he who …"), never as a bare verb. A one-word name is written without its
case ending (Viśva, Bhartā, Śrīmān; विश्व, भर्ता, श्रीमान्), but अहः / Ahaḥ, whose stem would not be recognised; a name of
two or more words is written as the verse has it, ending kept, its words apart in English (Nidhir Avyayaḥ, Satāṃ
Gatiḥ, Bījam Avyayam, Padam Anuttamam; निधिरव्ययः, सतां गतिः, बीजमव्ययम्). Where the Hindi sense would only repeat the
name, a plain gloss is written instead (परम ईश्वर (परमेश्वर), not परमेश्वर (परमेश्वर)), unless the word is itself plain
Hindi (साक्षी, सिंह, गुरु).

**Stotra 1–36 (॥ हरिः ॐ ॥ and ślokas 1–36), the names' senses.** Each name is given its sense as Śaṅkara's commentary
reads it where Monier-Williams or Apte bear that sense out, and its plain dictionary sense otherwise. Where Śaṅkara
counts two words as one name, the meaning does too: मुक्तानां परमा गतिः (2), योगविदां नेता (3), निधिरव्ययः (4),
धातुरुत्तमः (5), स्थविरो ध्रुवः (6), मङ्गलं परम् (7), सतां गतिः and गोविदां पतिः (20), अहः संवर्तकः (25), जगतः सेतुः (31).
Names whose sense was open, and what the meaning gives:
- त्वष्टा (6): "the fashioner of forms" (MW tvaṣṭṛ, the fashioner); Śaṅkara's "who makes all things small at the dissolution" is an etymology the dictionaries do not give.
- व्यालः (10): "who like a serpent cannot be seized" (MW vyāla, snake; Śaṅkara's reading of the same word).
- वृषाकपिः (11): "dharma and the boar" (vṛṣa = dharma, kapi = the boar, Śaṅkara); MW gives only "N. of Viṣṇu" for this name.
- सर्वयोगविनिःसृतः (11): "who stands apart from every bond" (yoga = connection, viniḥsṛta = gone out from: the plain sense of the parts).
- बभ्रुः (13): "the bearer of the worlds" (MW: from √bhṛ, Uṇ. i, 23; Śaṅkara the same), not the colour "tawny".
- वरारोहः (13): "the highest ascent, reaching whom none returns" (Śaṅkara; MW gives the word as a name of Viṣṇu).
- जनार्दनः (14): "who chastises the wicked" (MW "agitating men"; Śaṅkara the same).
- वैद्यः (18): "the knower of all learning" (vidyā → vaidya, Śaṅkara), not "physician".
- माधवः (8 and 18): the consort of Mā (Lakṣmī) in 8, the lord of knowledge (mā = vidyā) in 18, as Śaṅkara distinguishes the repeated name.
- गोविन्दः (20): "who recovered the lost earth" (go = the earth, Śaṅkara's verse from the Mahābhārata); the word's entry keeps "protector of cows" as its second sense.
- सन्धिमान् (22): "he in whom that union rests", after सन्धाता "who joins beings to the fruits of their deeds" (MW sandhimat, possessing union).
- जह्नुः (26): "who draws all beings into himself at the dissolution" (Śaṅkara; MW: N. of Viṣṇu).
- वृषाही, वृषपर्वा, वृषोदरः (28): from vṛṣa = dharma with ahan (day: the sacrifice), parvan (step), udara (womb), as Śaṅkara takes vṛṣa throughout the śloka.
- शिपिविष्टः (29): "who pervades the rays" (MW, after Sāyaṇa).
- नहुषः (34): "who binds all by māyā" (√nah, to bind; Śaṅkara), not the king Nahuṣa.
- नारायणः (26): "the refuge of all beings and of the waters" (nara, nāra; Śaṅkara), the same in Hindi and English.
- स्कन्दः, स्कन्दधरः (36): "who flows forth as nectar" and "the upholder of the path of dharma" (Śaṅkara, who reads skanda in both as one family of senses); the word स्कन्दः keeps "Skanda (Kārttikeya)" as its first sense for the other works.

**Opening prayers, pūrva-pīṭhikā, viniyoga, nyāsas, saṅkalpa and dhyāna, the readings taken.** The names in these
verses are given their sense as Śaṅkara reads them where Monier-Williams or Apte bear it out, else the dictionary sense:
- वसिष्ठनप्तारं (opening 3): "the great-grandson of Vasiṣṭha"; MW gives naptṛ as "descendant" (grandson in later use), and the verse itself names Vyāsa the grandson of Śakti, Vasiṣṭha's son.
- विष्वक्सेनं (opening 2): Viṣvaksena, the chief of Viṣṇu's attendants (MW: "an attendant of Viṣṇu", Pur.), the deity of the obstacle-removing prayer; द्विरदवक्त्राद्याः is "the elephant-faced one and the rest", his attendants.
- ब्रह्मण्यं (pūrva 13; kara- and ṣaḍaṅga-nyāsa): "the friend of the brāhmaṇas and the Veda" (MW, Apte: friendly to Brāhmaṇas, devoted to sacred knowledge; Śaṅkara: good to brahman, the Vedas, the brāhmaṇas).
- गौणानि (pūrva 19): "drawn from his qualities" (MW: relating to a quality, attributive), not "secondary".
- अमृतांशूद्भवः (pūrva 21; viniyoga, nyāsas): "the source of the moon, from whom the moon arose" (amṛtāṃśu = the moon, MW; udbhava = origin), as Śaṅkara reads it, not "born of the moon".
- त्रिसामा (pūrva 21; viniyoga): "praised in three Sāmans" (Śaṅkara); MW's "singing three Sāmans" is the same compound read actively, and सामगः beside it in the viniyoga already says "the singer of the Sāman".
- अनेकरूपदैत्यान्तं (pūrva 22): "who takes many forms and ends the daityas" (anekarūpa as his epithet, as अनेकरूपरूपाय in dhyāna 5), not "the end of daityas of many forms".
- क्षोभणः (viniyoga): "he who stirs nature into creation" (MW kṣobhaṇa, agitating; Śaṅkara: who stirs prakṛti and puruṣa at creation).
- सुवर्णबिन्दुः (kara- and ṣaḍaṅga-nyāsa): "golden of limb" (bindu = limb, Śaṅkara); MW gives only "N. of Viṣṇu".
- निमिषः, अनिमिषः (nyāsas): "whose eyes are closed (in yogic sleep)", "the unwinking, ever awake" (MW nimiṣa, shutting the eye; animiṣa, not winking, vigilant; Śaṅkara the same).
- अरि (dhyāna 1, अरिनलिनगदाशङ्खपाणिः): "the discus" (MW arin, ari v.l.: a wheel, discus), not "enemy".
- वास्तेयम् (dhyāna 2): "the abdomen" (Apte vāsteya, abdominal; MW, of the bladder), the ocean being the Lord's belly.
- रंरम्यते (dhyāna 2): the intensive of √ram, "sports on, delights again and again"; चित्रं with it, "in its wonderful variety".

**Stotra 37–72, the names' senses.** As for 1–36: Śaṅkara's sense where Monier-Williams or Apte bear it out or give
the word only as "N. of Viṣṇu", the plain dictionary sense where the dictionary gives the name a clear sense of its own.
Where Śaṅkara counts two words as one name, the meaning does too: शक्तिमतां श्रेष्ठः (43), स्थावरस्थाणुः and बीजमव्ययम्
(46), सतां गतिः and ज्ञानमुत्तमम् (48), देवभृद्गुरुः (52, "the teacher of Indra, who sustains the gods"; devabhṛt = Indra),
महर्षिः कपिलाचार्यः (57). सदसत्क्षरमक्षरम् (51) is four names, सत्, असत्, क्षरम्, अक्षरम्. Names whose sense was open, and what
the meaning gives:
- शतावर्तः (37): "who comes in hundreds of incarnations" (āvarta, a turning, coming round: MW); MW's "with a hundred curls" is said of Śiva.
- शरभः (39): "who shines in perishable bodies as their inner self" (Śaṅkara: śara, the bodies, from √śṝ, to waste away); the mythical beast śarabha of MW is no form of Viṣṇu. The word's entry keeps the beast as its second sense.
- विक्षरः (40): "imperishable" (vi- with kṣara, perishable: free from decay, Śaṅkara); MW's "pouring out" does not fit a name of praise, and MW also gives the word as "N. of Viṣṇu-Kṛṣṇa".
- दामोदरः (40): "who was bound with a cord round his waist" (MW dāmodara, "having a rope round the waist", N. of Kṛṣṇa; Śaṅkara gives Yaśodā's cord among his readings); the name is given its sense, not repeated as itself.
- मार्गः (40 and 43): "the path to the highest state" in 40, "he whom seekers search for" (√mṛg) in 43, as Śaṅkara distinguishes the repeated name.
- अनयः (43): "who has no leader" (a-naya, after नयः "the leader"), not MW's anaya "misfortune".
- क्षामः (47): "who remains when all has wasted away at dissolution" (kṣāma, wasted: MW, Apte; Apte: "an epithet of Viṣṇu"; Śaṅkara the same).
- नक्षत्रनेमिः, नक्षत्री (47): "who turns the wheel of the stars", "the lord of the stars, as the moon" (MW: the pole-star, the moon, N. of Viṣṇu).
- वत्सरः (50): "the year" (MW: the Year personified, applied to Viṣṇu); Śaṅkara's "in whom all dwell" (√vas) stays the entry's second sense. वत्सी: "the keeper of the calves" (MW vatsin, having calves).
- अविज्ञाता (51): "free from the limited knowing of the individual soul" (Śaṅkara; MW gives it as "N. of Viṣṇu"), not "ignorant".
- कृतलक्षणः (51): "marked with auspicious signs" (MW: marked; noted for good qualities).
- गभस्तिनेमिः (52): "who stands amid the wheel of rays, as the sun" (Śaṅkara; MW's own gloss is queried).
- कपीन्द्रः (53): "the lord of the monkeys" (Śrī Rāma; MW, and Śaṅkara's first reading), not the boar.
- महाशृङ्गः (57): "of the great horn, as the Fish" to whose horn the boat was tied (Śaṅkara); MW gives only a stag.
- कृतान्तकृत् (57): "who brings to an end the world he made" (kṛta, the made world; Śaṅkara), not "the slayer of Death".
- सुषेणः (58): "with a splendid army" (su + senā, Śaṅkara: his attendants); MW's "having a good missile" is the same word read from another sense of sena.
- स्वाङ्गः (59): "who is himself his own means in all he does" (Śaṅkara), kept apart from स्वङ्गः (66) "of beautiful limbs".
- वारुणः (59): "the son of Varuṇa" (Vasiṣṭha or Agastya; MW vāruṇa, belonging to Varuṇa).
- भगहा (60): "who takes away all fortune at dissolution" (bhaga, fortune; Śaṅkara); MW's "slayer of Bhaga" is Śiva's story.
- कुमुदः, कुवलेशयः, गोहितः, वृषभाक्षः (63): "who gladdens the earth" (ku, the earth), "who lies in the waters that encircle the earth" (as महोदधिशयः in 55), "who does good to the cows", "whose glance showers every wish" (√vṛṣ, to rain), all Śaṅkara's; MW gives the water-lily, a resting on the water-lily, two plants and "bull-eyed". कुमुदः keeps the water-lily as its second sense.
- गोपतिः: "the lord of the cows" in 53, "the lord of the earth" (go, the earth) in 63, as Śaṅkara distinguishes the repeated name.
- कुम्भः (68): "the vessel in which all is contained" (Śaṅkara).
- सर्वदृग्व्यासः, वाचस्पतिरयोनिजः (61): each part given its sense in order ("all-seeing; Vyāsa, who divided the Veda"; "the lord of speech; not born of a womb"), whether one counts them as one name or two.

**Stotra 73–108, the names' senses.** As for 1–72: Śaṅkara's sense where Monier-Williams or Apte bear it out or give
the word only as "N. of Viṣṇu", the plain dictionary sense where the dictionary gives the name a clear sense of its own.
Where Śaṅkara counts two words as one name, the meaning does too: पदमनुत्तमम् (78), सर्वशस्त्रभृतां वरः (81), सर्वविज्जयी
(85, "the all-knowing conqueror"), क्षमिणां वरः (98), भुवो लक्ष्मीः (101). सनात्सनातनतमः (96) is two names, सनात् and
सनातनतमः. Verse 108 (वनमाली गदी …) is the closing prayer, not counted among the thousand: the meaning gives it as a
prayer for protection, with the weapon-epithets' senses. Names whose sense was open, and what the meaning gives:
- वसुप्रदः, वसुप्रदः (74): "the giver of wealth", then "the giver of the highest wealth, liberation", as Śaṅkara distinguishes the repeated name.
- वासुदेवः: "the son of Vasudeva" in 74, "the god who dwells in all and covers all" in 76, as Śaṅkara distinguishes the repeated name.
- सुयामुनः (75): "whose people by the Yamunā are noble" (Śaṅkara: the cowherds and kin of the Yamunā's banks); MW gives only "N. of Viṣṇu".
- कः, किम्, यत्, तत् (78): pronouns read as names of Brahman, as Śaṅkara reads them: "bliss" (ka = sukha), "the one to be inquired into", "the Absolute from which all arises", "That"; the words' entries keep their pronoun senses first and add these.
- सवः (78): "the soma sacrifice" (MW sava, the pressing of soma; Śaṅkara the same).
- माधवः (78): "born in the line of Madhu", the third sense Śaṅkara gives the repeated name (8: the consort of Mā; 18: the lord of knowledge).
- घृताशीः (79): "free of all wishes" (ghṛta, melted away, + āśis, wish: Śaṅkara); not "eater of ghee", which would be घृताशी from aśin.
- वीरहा: "the slayer of the (demon) heroes" in 79; in 99 "who ends the many courses of rebirth" (vi-ira, the various ways of the wandering soul: Śaṅkara), as he distinguishes the repeated name.
- प्रग्रहः (81): "who accepts what his devotees offer" (MW pragraha, taking hold of, friendly reception; Śaṅkara the same); व्यग्रः: "ever intent on granting his devotees' wishes" (MW vyagra, intent on, engrossed in; Śaṅkara's second reading), not Śaṅkara's first, "endless", which MW does not give.
- नैकशृङ्गः (81): "many-horned" (the Veda's four horns, चत्वारि शृङ्गा, Śaṅkara).
- सुन्दः (85): "the tender-hearted" (√und, to wet: moist with compassion, Śaṅkara); MW gives only "N. of Viṣṇu". अर्कः: "the sun" (MW arka), not Śaṅkara's "worthy of worship". शृङ्गी: "the horned one, as the Fish", as महाशृङ्गः in 57.
- सुवर्णबिन्दुः (86): "golden of limb", as in the nyāsas.
- महागर्तः (86): "the great abyss" (MW garta, a hole, chasm; Śaṅkara: his māyā, hard to cross).
- कुन्दरः, कुन्दः (87): "who gives fruits pure as the kunda flower", "fair as the kunda jasmine" (MW kunda, the jasmine; kundara only "N. of Viṣṇu"); Śaṅkara's other readings (who split the earth; who gave the earth to Kaśyapa) are not taken.
- सप्तैधाः (89): "of seven flames" (edhas, fuel, flame: fire with its seven flames, Śaṅkara).
- स्वास्यः (90): "of lovely face" (su + āsya, Śaṅkara: from whose face the Veda came). प्राग्वंशः: "whose line is the first of all" (MW prāgvaṃśa, a former generation, N. of Viṣṇu).
- श्रमणः (91): "the ascetic" (MW śramaṇa), not Śaṅkara's "who wearies the undiscerning". क्षामः: "who remains when all has wasted away at the dissolution", as in 47. सुपर्णः: "the tree of the world with its fair leaves" (MW suparṇa, having beautiful leaves; Śaṅkara: the tree whose leaves are the Vedas). वायुवाहनः: "who sets the wind moving" (Śaṅkara; MW only "N. of Viṣṇu").
- दमः (92): "the restraint" that the rod (दण्डः) and the subduer (दमयिता) bring about (Śaṅkara). अनियमः, अयमः: "controlled by none", "beyond the reach of death (Yama)" (Śaṅkara), not MW's aniyama, irregularity.
- अभिप्रायः (93): "the one sought by all who seek the aims of life" (MW abhiprāya, aim, wish; Śaṅkara the same).
- रविः (94): "the sun that draws up the waters" (Śaṅkara), beside सूर्यः "the sun"; सविता (94, 104): "the begetter of all" (MW savitṛ, the vivifier, from √sū; Śaṅkara the same).
- सनात् (96): "the everlasting, time itself" (MW sanāt, from of old, always; Śaṅkara: time). कपिलः: "the sage Kapila" (MW, and the Gītā's सिद्धानां कपिलो मुनिः), not the colour. कपिः: "the sun" (MW kapi, the sun), not the boar or the monkey.
- शब्दसहः (97): "declared by all the words of the Veda" (Śaṅkara); शर्वरीकरः: "the maker of night" (the plain sense of the parts).
- दक्षिणः (98): "kindly and upright" (MW dakṣiṇa, upright, sincere, amiable), beside दक्षः "skilful".
- सन्तः (99): "the good, present as the saints" (Śaṅkara), a plural name, as the base prints सन्तो.
- चतुरश्रः (100): "the just, who gives each his due" (MW caturaśra, regular, even; Śaṅkara: who gives the fit fruit). विदिशः, व्यादिशः, दिशः: "who grants in many ways", "who gives his various commands", "who, as the Veda, points out the fruits of acts" (all from √diś, to point out, grant, as Śaṅkara reads them; MW gives vyādiśa as "N. of Viṣṇu"), not the quarters of the sky.
- अनादिर्भूर्भुवो लक्ष्मीः (101): three names, "without beginning" (अनादिः), "the ground of all" (भूः), "the splendour of the earth" (भुवो लक्ष्मीः, Śaṅkara), not Lakṣmī the goddess; the word लक्ष्मीः keeps "Lakṣmī" as its first sense.
- अधाता (102): "who has no upholder" (a-dhātṛ); पुष्पहासः: "who blossoms like a flower", unfolding as the world (MW puṣpahāsa, N. of Viṣṇu; Śaṅkara); पणः: "the dealer who gives the fruit in return for acts" (√paṇ, to deal; MW paṇa, a compact, wages), not "a coin".
- प्रमाणम् (103): "consciousness, the measure of all knowing" (MW pramāṇa, the means of right knowledge; Śaṅkara: pure consciousness).
- भूर्भुवःस्वस्तरुः (104): "the tree of Bhūḥ, Bhuvaḥ and Svaḥ, the three worlds" (Śaṅkara). तारः: "the one who carries across" (MW tāra, saviour, said of Viṣṇu).
- यज्ञसाधनः (105): "the one reached through sacrifice" (Śaṅkara: to whom sacrifice is the means). यज्ञगुह्यम्: "the secret of sacrifice" (MW yajñaguhya, N. of Kṛṣṇa). यज्ञी: "the one to whom sacrifice belongs as its principal" (MW yajñin, abounding in sacrifices; Śaṅkara: the aṅgin of the sacrifice), kept apart from यज्ञपतिः (104) "the lord of sacrifices".
- वैखानः (106): "who dug the earth as the Boar" (vi + √khan, Śaṅkara); MW gives only "N. of Viṣṇu".
- सर्वप्रहरणायुध ॐ नम इति (after 107) and श्रीवासुदेवोऽभिरक्षतु ॐ नम इति (after 108): the refrain's इति is read as the close: "thus the thousand names are complete" after 107, the last name being सर्वप्रहरणायुधः, and "thus the prayer is complete" after 108, written in the sentence, without brackets.
- The names in brackets (stotra 1–108) are given as in ślokas 1–54: the nominative without its visarga (वेधा, महामना, हवि, सहस्रार्चि; Vedhā, Mahāmanā, Havi, Sahasrārci), except घृताशीः (79), kept apart from घृताशी (aśin), and the plural सन्तः (99); a two-word name is written as two words (श्रीमतां वर, क्षमिणां वर).

**Uttara-pīṭhikā, closing words and closing ślokas (upasaṃhāra, paryāya), the readings taken.** The plain reading of
the words, from Monier-Williams and Apte, wherever a sentence left a choice:
- वासनाद्वासुदेवस्य वासितं भुवनत्रयम् (uttara 25): "by Vāsudeva's dwelling in them the three worlds are pervaded" (MW vāsana, causing to abide or dwell; vāsita, caused to dwell, peopled, infused), the verse playing on vāsa, Vāsudeva and nivāsa; MW's other vāsana, covering, is not taken.
- सोऽहमेकेन श्लोकेन स्तुत एव (uttara 24): "by him I am praised indeed with a single śloka"; सः takes up यः of the first half (the one who wishes to praise me), and the agent of स्तुतः is supplied from it.
- धातवः (uttara 18): "the dhātus, the constituent elements" (MW dhātu, constituent part, element), beside the महाभूतानि it follows; not "metals".
- गोविन्दरहितागमम् (paryāya 4): "a teaching (āgama, scripture, doctrine) without Govinda", the object of विजानीयात् with कुपथं as its predicate.
- मम सन्निधौ (paryāya 9): the Lord speaks ("in my presence"), as paryāya 12 calls these words चक्रपाणेर्वचः; the meaning glosses "my" once as the Lord's.
- शनैर्दहन्ति पापानि कल्पकोटिशतानि च (paryāya 10): दहन्ति is active and its subject unnamed; it is those who recite, of whom paryāya 9 speaks, so the meaning writes them in ("those who recite it burn away, little by little, sins …"). The line reads so in Sanskrit.Today's text as well; शनैः is the adverb "slowly, gradually", placed in the meaning so that it goes with "burn away", not with "recite".
- पठते (paryāya 6): the epic middle form of √paṭh, "recites", as पठति.
