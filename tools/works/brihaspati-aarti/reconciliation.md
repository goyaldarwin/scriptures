# Reconciliation: ॐ जय बृहस्पति देवा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** Aarti Mala, the page "अथ बृहस्पतिवार की आरती"; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-357), read on the page image. The page is a printout carrying the indif.com watermark. Each stanza is one printed line, its halves divided by ।, each half by a comma (some halves have none); every line ends ।।ॐ।।; the page closes with the cry "सब बोलो विष्णु भगवान की जय । सब बोलो बृहस्पति भगवान की जय ।।".
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/brihaspati/brihaspati-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/weekdays-guruwar-aarti.html`, Guruwar Ki Aarti): the same āratī in the same order, each stanza in four printed lines.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (a printed line is split at its quarters, one per line); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| every stanza | one printed line, halves divided by ।, quarters by a comma | four lines, ending । and ॥N॥ | The standard. Where a half has no comma (छिन छिन भोग लगाऊं फल मेवा; तन मन धन अर्पणकर जो जन शरण पड़े; विषय विकार मिटाओ सन्तन सुखकारी; both halves of the last stanza) it is split where its fellows and Drik Panchang split it. |
| after every stanza | ।।ॐ।। | (not shown) | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item (टेक). |
| refrain, line 4 | फल मेवा | कदली फल मेवा | The quarter is two syllables short of every other closing quarter (तुम सबके स्वामी, कृपा करो भर्ता, आकर द्वार खड़े); Drik Panchang gives the missing word, कदली ("plantain"). |
| stanza 4, line 4 | भव बन्धन हार | भव बन्धन हारी | हार drops the ī that the rhyme with हितकारी needs ("remover of the bonds of the world"); Drik Panchang has हारी. |
| after the āratī | सब बोलो विष्णु भगवान की जय । सब बोलो बृहस्पति भगवान की जय ।। | (not written) | The closing cry (jayakāra), not a stanza of the āratī. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: लगाऊं (Drik लगाऊँ), तन मन धन अर्पणकर (Drik तन, मन, धन अर्पण कर), दीन दयाल (Drik दीनदयाल), and, as both have them, सब संशय तारो (which does not rhyme with सुखकारी) and जेष्टानन्द बन्द (settled below, Readings settled).

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
- **aarti.pad5** — सब संशय तारो, which does not rhyme with सुखकारी: kept as printed (the book and Drik Panchang agree; Shubh Panchang's हारो does not rhyme either, and no rhyming text is attested). Read as printed, तारो "carry (us) across". Meaning unchanged.
- **aarti.pad6** — जेष्टानन्द बन्द सो सो निश्चय पावे: kept as printed (both witnesses agree; Shubh Panchang's जेठानन्द आनन्दकर is another wording that settles nothing, and the other pages a web search found could not be read). Read as the poet's signature, जेष्टानन्द, with बन्द = वन्दना ("bows"), सो सो "each such one". Meaning unchanged.
