# ॐ जय बृहस्पति देवा (Om Jai Brihaspati Deva)

The āratī of Bṛhaspati, sung on Thursdays, in Hindi (`hi`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): Aarti Mala (page image i-357), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/tara-devi/`: Tara Devi (YouTube `C6HqjnuArlE`, "श्री बृहस्पतिवार की आरती | ॐ जय बृहस्पति देवा | Best Morning Aarti | Tara Devi | Ambey bhakti"), its track 0:00–4:56 of the video (after 4:56: music only to the video's end). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list.
- **Poet:** the last stanza names जेष्टानन्द (Jeshtanand).

Verse types: टेक (refrain, `tek`, shown once at the start although it is sung after every stanza) and पद (stanza, `pad`). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (Om Jai Brihaspati Deva) › one section (`aarti`) › verses: the refrain (`aarti.tek`, type `tek`) and 6 stanzas.
- **Numbering:** each stanza's `n` is its number (॥१॥ …), and it is cited by it ("Stanza 3"); the refrain has none.
- **Topics:** one, the āratī's title. **Reading plans:** none.
