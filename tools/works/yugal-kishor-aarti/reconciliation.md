# Reconciliation: आरती जुगल किशोर की कीजै

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), p. 47, under the heading "आरती श्री गोपाल जी की"; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-046), read on the page image. The refrain (marked टेक), eight lines (रवि शशि, गौर श्याम, कंचन थार, फूलन की सेज, मोर मुकुट, आधा नील, श्री पुरुषोत्तम, नन्द लाला), and the refrain again. The dohā printed above the heading (प्रणत पाल अशरण शरण) closes the Gopāla Chālīsā on the pages before; it is not part of the āratī.
- **Drik Panchang, Shri Gopala Ki Aarti** (`hinduism/aarti/krishna/yugal-kishor-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-gopala-aarti.html`): the same text in the same order, grouped two lines to a stanza, the refrain repeated after each.
- **Drik Panchang, Budhwar Ki Aarti** (`hinduism/aarti/krishna/yugal-kishor-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/weekdays-budhwar-aarti.html`) and **Aarti Mala** (scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/`, page image i-356, "अथ बुधवार की आरती", a printout of indif.com): another recension, in another order (गौरश्याम before रवि शशि; ओढ़े नील after रवि शशि; कंचन after फूलन; no मोर मुकुट line), with तन मन (धन) न्यौछावर, निरखन लीजै, हरि का स्वरूप नयन भरि पीजै, ओढ़े नील पीत पट सारी, नन्दनन्दन बृजभान (ब्रजभान) किशोरी.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (the book's comma between the halves of a line becomes a line break, a half per line); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| every stanza | one printed line, its halves joined by a comma | two lines, ending । and ॥N॥ | The standard; each printed line is a rhyming couplet (शोभा / लोभा), the book's own unit, as in ganesh-aarti. Drik Panchang's grouping of two couplets to a stanza is not the book's. |
| refrain | ॥ टेक॥ after it | (not written) | The mark names the refrain; it is the first item (`aarti.tek`). |
| at the end | the refrain again | (not repeated) | The closing repeat of the refrain; shown once. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥८॥ | The standard. |

Kept as the book has them, where Drik Panchang differs: स्वरूप (Drik Panchang स्वरुप, a slip). Where the Budhwar page and Aarti Mala differ, the book's readings stand (neither is clearly wrong): राधे धन न्यौछावर, मेरा, निरखत रीझै, प्रभु को स्वरूप नयन भर पीजै, थार, आये, रतन, आधा नील पीत पटसारी, कुञ्ज बिहारी, गिरवरधारी (stanza 7) beside गिरिवरधारी (stanza 6), करें, नन्द लाला वृषभानु किशोरी.

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
- aarti.tek, राधे धन न्यौछावर कीजै: राधे as the invocation "Rādhe!", as the meaning has it; राधे is the vocative of राधा, and "for Rādhā" would need a postposition (पर, पै); the Budhwar recension's तन मन (धन) is another recension's reading, not a correction of the book's.
- aarti.pad6, आधा नील पीत पटसारी: the pair's one form, half in a blue sari, half in a yellow robe, as the meaning has it; आधा ("half") is the book's and Drik Panchang's word, and it gives that sense; ओढ़े नील पीत पट सारी is the other recension.
- aarti.pad8, परमानन्द स्वामी: the poet's signature, "the lords of Paramānanda", as the meaning has it; the name-plus-स्वामी signature closes the āratī as in Braj padas (कृष्णदास-प्रभु in yashoda-lala-aarti), and the line supplies no verb for "says".
