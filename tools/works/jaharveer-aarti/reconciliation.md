# Reconciliation: जय जय जाहरवीर हरे

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), pp. 105–106, after the Jāharvīr Chālīsā; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-104, i-105), read on the page images. Its order: the refrain, जो कोई भक्ति करे, जेवर राव के पुत्र, धर्म की बेल, सत्य अहिंसा, माड़ी में, भवन पधारो, तन मन धन. (Page image i-107 is a page of the Pretrāj Chālīsā, not of this āratī.)
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/jaharveer/jaharveer-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-jaharveer-aarti.html`): the same āratī, in the same order, each printed line broken in two; its refrain cue is जय जय जाहरवीर हरे.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (the book's comma between the halves of a line becomes a line break, a half per line); each line as the book prints it; the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| line ends | the first line of each couplet without a daṇḍa | । and ॥N॥ (the refrain ॥) | The standard. |
| stanza 1, line 2 | भागे दुख परे विघन हरे, मंगल के दाता तन का कष्ट हरे। | two lines, ending । and ॥१॥ | The book's comma marks the half. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥७॥ | The standard. |
| after the refrain and every stanza but the first | जय-जय॥ | (not shown) | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item (टेक). |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: विघन (Drik विघ्न); the comma after the refrain's first half (Drik जय जय जाहरवीर हरे,) is not kept, as the standard has it.
