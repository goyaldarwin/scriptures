# जय जय जाहरवीर हरे (Jai Jai Jaharveer Hare)

The āratī of Jāharvīr Gogājī (Gūgā Vīr), in Hindi (`hi`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 105–106), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/ramawtar-sharma/`: Ramawtar Sharma (YouTube `cKYi3i6-jPM`, "जाहर बीर गोगा जी की आरती | जय जाहरवीर हरे | Jai Jahar Veer Hare | Ramawtar Sharma"), its track 0:17–5:34 of the video (before 0:17: a spoken introduction with jaikāras, 3-18 s; after 5:34: a closing spoken jaikāra 'जय गोगा जी महाराज'). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list. A draft (it plays only with Audio timing on): it sings stanza 5 before stanza 4: the text is to follow its order first.

Verse types: टेक (refrain, `tek`, shown once at the start although it is sung after every stanza) and पद (stanza, `pad`). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (Jai Jai Jaharveer Hare) › one section (`aarti`) › verses: the refrain (`aarti.tek`, type `tek`) and 7 stanzas.
- **Numbering:** each stanza's `n` is its number (॥१॥ …), and it is cited by it ("Stanza 3"); the refrain has none.
- **Topics:** one, the āratī's title. **Reading plans:** none.
