# गाओ गाओ री प्रियाप्रीतम की आरती गाओ (Gao Gao Ri Priyapritam Ki Aarti Gao)

The āratī of Sītā and Rāma, the beloved pair (प्रियाप्रीतम), in Hindi (`hi`). Its last stanza names रामेस्वर, which may be the poet's signature; that is not settled (`docs/reviews/sitaram-aarti-doubts.jsonl`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): Drik Panchang, "Shri Sitarama Aarti" (no copy in the owner's books). Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** none yet.

Verse types: टेक (refrain, `tek`, shown once at the start although it is sung after every stanza) and पद (stanza, `pad`). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (Priyapritam Ki Aarti) › one section (`aarti`) › verses: the refrain (`aarti.tek`, type `tek`) and 4 stanzas.
- **Numbering:** each stanza's `n` is its number (॥१॥ …), and it is cited by it ("Stanza 3"); the refrain has none.
- **Topics:** one, the āratī's refrain. **Reading plans:** none.
