# Reconciliation: गाओ गाओ री प्रियाप्रीतम की आरती गाओ

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base)** (`hinduism/aarti/rama/sitaram-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-sitarama-aarti.html`): four stanzas of four lines, the refrain (गाओ गाओ री, प्रियाप्रीतम की आरती गाओ) marked after each. The owner's books have no copy of this āratī, so the page is the only witness.

The rule: keep the base; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (a pause within a line is a space); the refrain (टेक) is shown once, first; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Base | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| the refrain | after each stanza, the page beginning with stanza 1 | once, first (`aarti.tek`) | The standard. |
| the refrain | गाओ गाओ री, प्रियाप्रीतम की आरती गाओ। | गाओ गाओ री प्रियाप्रीतम की आरती गाओ ॥ | The standard: no comma; a refrain ends ॥. It is one printed line, kept as one. |
| every stanza | lines ending with a comma or nothing, the last ॥ | । and ॥N॥ | The standard. |
| stanza 1, line 2 | सिन्गार | सिंगार | The page types every nasal before a consonant as न्; न्ग is not a Hindi spelling. |
| stanza 2, line 1 | दन्पति | दंपति | The same (न् before प; दंपति, "the couple"). |

Kept as the base has them: सखियाँ, दैनी, सुनैनी, लिएँ, पिकबैनी, गाइ, छबि, भूषन, चितवनमें, अधरनमें, मुसकनमें (joined as printed), नयनाँ, अरुझाओ, सँवारि, सुभ, ज्योतिकर, चवँर, रामेस्वर.
