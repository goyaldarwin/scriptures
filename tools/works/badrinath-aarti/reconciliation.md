# Reconciliation: जय जय श्री बदरीनाथ

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** "Lord Badarinatha Aarti" (<https://www.drikpanchang.com/lyrics/aarti/lord-narayan/lord-badarinatha-aarti.html>), scriptures-data `hinduism/aarti/vishnu/badrinath-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/lord-badarinatha-aarti.html`. It is the only witness.
- **The book:** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan), scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/`: the āratī is not in it.

The rule: keep Drik Panchang; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; one line per printed line; the refrain (टेक) shown once, its repeats not written; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| refrain | its two lines printed twice (the sung repeat) | once | The standard: repeats are not written. |
| every stanza | lines ending in commas or nothing; । and ॥ | each line ending ।, the last ॥N॥; inner commas as spaces | The standard. |
| stanza 1, line 2 | मेधवर्ण अति अनूप | मेघवर्ण अति अनूप | मेघवर्ण, "the colour of a rain-cloud", Viṣṇu's dark hue; मेध ("sacrifice") makes no sense with वर्ण here. A typing slip, ध for घ. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥५॥ | The standard. |
| after every stanza | ⟨refrain⟩ जय जय श्री बदरीनाथ...॥ | not shown | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item. |

Kept as Drik Panchang has them: योग ध्यानी, सुरभूप, बरनत, बरबानी, मुक्त-माल, प्रनतपाल, दयाल, सुखमा की धाम, सुमिरत हों सिद्धि काम, हमेश, जोरी जुगल पानी.
