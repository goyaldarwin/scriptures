# Reconciliation: जय शरणं वरणं नमो नमः

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), p. 282, under the heading "आरती श्री ललिता जी की"; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-281), read on the page image. A centred line जय शरणं वरणं नमो नमः, eleven printed lines (ending । and ॥ in turn), and the centred line again (॥). Lines 3 and 6 are the same: जय शरणं वरणं नमो नमः श्री मातेश्वरि जय त्रिपुरेश्वरि; line 11 repeats line 1. The kit's other pages (i-277, i-278) are the Lalitā Chālīsā, not part of the āratī.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/lalita/lalita-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/lalita-mata-aarti.html`): the same lines in the same order, each printed line split in two, without the book's centred lines and without its line 10 (जय राजेश्वरी जय नमो नमः जय ललिते माता नमो नमः); it ends by repeating the first line, as the book does.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; one line per printed line; the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

Kept as the book has them, where Drik Panchang differs: मातेश्वरि, त्रिपुरेश्वरि, राजेश्वरि, हारिणि (Drik with ी), खलदल नाशिनि (Drik खल-दल नाशिनी), शरणागति (Drik शरण गति), the book's line 10 (not in Drik). The book's own mixed spellings (हारिणि beside हारिणी, राजेश्वरि beside राजेश्वरी) stay: both are valid forms.

## Readings settled
- **aarti.tek** (शरणं वरणं, no verb): "I choose your refuge": वरण is "the act of choosing, choice" (Monier-Williams 2. varaṇa); its other senses ("rampart", "surrounding, keeping off", 1. varaṇa) give no "protection" or "chosen one". Meaning unchanged.
