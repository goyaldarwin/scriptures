# Reconciliation: मंगल की सेवा सुन मेरी देवा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), pp. 212–214, under the heading "आरती श्री महाकाली जी की"; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-211, i-212, i-213), read on the page images. Twenty-six printed lines with no stanza breaks; ॥ ends the fourth line and then every third line (lines 4, 7, 10 … 25), and the last line (संतन प्रतिपाली सदा खुशहाली, मैया जय काली कल्याण करे ॥) repeats the fourth. The Mahākālī Chālīsā's last lines and closing dohā above the heading on p. 212 are not part of the āratī.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/kali/mahakali-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/mahakali-mata-aarti.html`): the same twenty-six lines in the same order, grouped as thirteen stanzas of two printed lines, each followed by the cue "मंगल की सेवा सुन मेरी देवा"; its last group ends with the repeated संतन प्रतिपाली line.
- **Aarti Mala** (page images i-345, i-344; i-346 is a second copy of i-344; an indif.com printout): the same āratī in groups of three lines, each followed by the line "संतन प्रतिपाली सदा खुशहाली, जय काली कल्याण करे" — the division the book's ॥ marks show.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (the book's comma between the halves of a line becomes a line break, a half per line); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| the refrain | lines 1–4, ending ॥ | `aarti.tek`, eight half-lines | The opening group as the book's ॥ closes it; its last line (संतन-प्रतिपाली …) is the line Aarti Mala repeats after every stanza. |
| the stanzas | lines 5–25, ॥ after every third | seven stanzas of three printed lines (six half-lines) | The book's ॥ marks; Aarti Mala divides the same way. Drik Panchang's two-line groups are not followed. |
| the last line | संतन प्रतिपाली सदा खुशहाली, मैया जय काली कल्याण करे ॥ | (not written) | The closing repeat of the refrain's last line; the refrain is shown once. |
| every line | the halves joined by a comma, with more commas inside the halves | two lines; the other commas are spaces | The standard; the comma at the caesura marks the half (as Drik Panchang divides). |
| stanza 5, line 3 | सौम्य स्वभाव धरयो मेरी माता जन की अर्ज कबूल करे ॥ (no comma) | two lines, split after माता | A dropped comma: every other line has it at the caesura; Drik Panchang splits here. |
| refrain, line 1; stanza 4, line 3 | 'मंगल', 'आदित' | मंगल, आदित | Quotation marks are not part of the verse. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥७॥ | The standard. |

Kept as the book has them, where Drik Panchang differs: सिंहासन (Drik सिहांसन), त्रिशूल (Drik त्रिशुल), कुपित होय के दानव मारे (Drik without के), अचल भवन (stanza 7; Drik अटल भवन). Both have मैया जै काली in the refrain, लुँकड़, बरणो, धरयो and रक्त बीज; neither is clearly wrong, so they stay.

## Readings settled
- aarti.tek, मंगल: Tuesday ("this Tuesday's worship"), as the meaning has it; the book and Drik Panchang both set 'मंगल' in quotation marks, as they do 'आदित' (Sunday, stanza 4), and the āratī runs through the weekdays (वार शनिश्चर, stanza 3; सात बार की महिमा बरनी, stanza 6).
- aarti.pad3, सहसफण लिए: "with the thousand-hooded Śeṣa", as printed; the book and Drik Panchang have सहसफण, a valid word (Śeṣa beside Viṣṇu); Aarti Mala's फल लिए is another recension's reading, not evidence that the base is wrong.
- aarti.pad3, कुंकुम बरणो … लुँकड़: बरणो as वर्ण ("your hue is of kumkum"), confirmed by Aarti Mala's कुंकुमवरणी ("kumkum-coloured"); लुँकड़ as लौंकड़ा, the Goddess's boy attendant (Platts), as the meaning has it.
- aarti.pad4, आदित वारी: "on Sunday", as the meaning has it; 'आदित' is quoted as a day name in the book and Drik Panchang, in the āratī's run of weekdays.
