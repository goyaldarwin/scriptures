  HI: यद्यपि आप स्नेह [वश उन्हें] नहीं छोड़ सकते, तो भी अब दशरथजीको आज्ञा दीजिये। ‘हे नाथ! बहुत अच्छा’ कहकर जनकजीने मन्त्रियोंको बुलवाया। वे आये और ‘जय जीव’ कहकर उन्होंने मस्तक नवाया ।।४।।
SEG 0: King Daśaratha extolled in everyway Janaka's affection, amiability, affluence and doings.
SEG 1: Every morning the King of Ayodhyā asked leave to return home;
SEG 2: but each time Janaka would lovingly detain him.
SEG 3: The royal guest received greater and enhanced attentions from day to day and was entertained in a thousand ways each day.
SEG 4: The city witnessed a new rejoicing and festivity everyday;
SEG 5: no one liked Daśaratha's departure.
SEG 6: In this way a number of days passed, as though members of the bridegrooms' party were tied by cords of love.
SEG 7: The sages Kauśika and Śatānanda then called on King Videha and advised him saying, "Now you must let Deśaratha go, even though you may not be able to part with him out of love." “Very well, my lord", replied the king, and sent for his ministers, who came and bowed their heads saying, "May you be victorious, may you live long!"
