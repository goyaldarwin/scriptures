  HI: सिरपर जटा है। कमरमें मुनियोंका (वल्कल) वस्त्र बाँधे हैं और उसीमें तरकस कसे हैं। हाथमें बाण तथा कंधेपर धनुष है। वेदीपर मुनि तथा साधुओंका समुदाय बैठा है और सीताजीसहित श्रीरघुनाथजी विराजमान हैं ।।३।।
ITEM 3 [ayodhya.239.cau4]
  TR: balakala basana jaṭila tanu syāmā, janu muni beṣa kīnha rati kāmā. / kara kamalani dhanu sāyaku pherata, jiya kī jarani harata ha̐si herata ॥4॥
  HI: श्रीरामजीके वल्कल वस्त्र हैं, जटा धारण किये हैं, श्याम शरीर है [सीतारामजी ऐसे लगते हैं] मानो रति और कामदेवने मुनिका वेष धारण किया हो। श्रीरामजी अपने करकमलोंसे धनुष-बाण फेर रहे हैं, और हँसकर देखते ही जीकी जलन हर लेते हैं (अर्थात् जिसकी ओर भी एक बार हँसकर देख लेते हैं, उसीको परम आनन्द और शान्ति मिल जाती है।) ।।४।।
SEG 0: The two charming brothers and their friend (Guha) could not be seen by Lakṣmaṇa, screened as they were by a dense thicket.
SEG 1: Bharata, however, saw the holy and lovely hermitage of his lord, which was an abode of all fair blessings.
SEG 2: Even as he entered it his woe and affliction disappeared;
SEG 3: it seemed as though a Yogī (mystic) had realized the supreme truth.
SEG 4: Bharata saw Lakṣmaṇa standing before the Lord and affectionately answering His queries.
SEG 5: He wore matted hair on his head and had a hermit’s robe girt about his loins.
SEG 6: Besides there was a quiver fastened to his waist and he bore an arrow in his hand and a bow slung across his shoulder.
SEG 7: On the altar in the midst of an assembly of hermits and holy men shone Sītā and the Lord of Raghus, who was clad in the bark of trees and had matted hair on His head and a swarthy complexion;
SEG 8: it seemed as though Rati and the god of love had appeared there in hermit’s garb.
SEG 9: He was revolving His bow and arrow between His lotus hands and would dispel by one smiling glance the anguish of one’s soul.
