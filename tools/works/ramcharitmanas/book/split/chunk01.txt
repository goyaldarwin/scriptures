  HI: शिवजी वर देनेवाले, शरणागतोंके दुःखोंका नाश करनेवाले, कृपाके समुद्र और सेवकोंके मनको प्रसन्न करनेवाले हैं। शिवजीकी आराधना किये बिना करोड़ों योग और जप करनेपर भी वाञ्छित फल नहीं मिलता ।।४।।
SEG 0: “Holy men would never drink wine even if they came to know that it had been made of water from the Gaṅgā;
SEG 1: but the same wine becomes pure when it is poured into the Gaṅgā.
SEG 2: The difference between an individual soul and God should be similarly explained.
SEG 3: Śambhu is all-powerful by nature;
SEG 4: for He is no other than God Himself.
SEG 5: Hence matrimony with Him will prove auspicious in everyway.
SEG 6: The great Lord Śiva is certainly difficult to propitiate;
SEG 7: but He is quickly pleased when penance is undergone.
SEG 8: If your daughter practises austerity, the Slayer of the demon Tripura, Śiva, can even erase the lines of Fate.
SEG 9: Even though there may be many a suitor in the world, there is no match for her except Śiva.
SEG 10: He is the bestower of boons, the dispeller of the agony of the suppliant, an ocean of benevolence and the delight of His devotee.
SEG 11: Without propitiating Śiva the object of one’s desire cannot be attained through millions of Yogic practices and Japa (repetitions of a mystic formula).”
