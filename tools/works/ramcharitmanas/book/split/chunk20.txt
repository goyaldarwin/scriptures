SEG 0: When he thus inveighed against Śrī Rāma, the monkey chief grew furious with rage.
SEG 1: For he who opens his ears to vituperation against Hari (Bhagavān Viṣṇu) or Hara (Lord Śiva) incurs as great a sin as he who kills a cow.
SEG 2: Aṅgada (the great monkey) gave a loud yell and furiously struck both his mighty arms against the ground.
SEG 3: The earth shook and members of the assembly were thrown off their seats and took to flight, possessed as they were by the hobgoblin of fear.
SEG 4: The ten-headed monster (Rāvaṇa) too was about to topple down but recovered himself and stood up.
SEG 5: Yet his most beautiful crowns fell to the ground;
SEG 6: some of them he took and set on his heads, while Aṅgada sent the rest flying to the Lord.
SEG 7: The monkeys fled when they saw the crowns coming.
SEG 8: “Good heavens, how is it that meteors have begun to fall even during the daytime ?
SEG 9: Or is it that Rāvaṇa in his fury has hurled four thunderbolts, which are coming with great speed ?” The Lord smiled and said, “Be not afraid at heart.
SEG 10: They are neither meteors nor thunderbolts, nor even the planets Rāhu and Ketu.
SEG 11: They are the crowns of the ten-headed Rāvaṇa, despatched by Vāli’s son (Aṅgada), that are coming this side.”
