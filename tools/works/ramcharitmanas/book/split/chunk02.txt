SEG 10: Joy and grief, knowledge and ignorance, egoism and pride—these are the characteristics of a Jīva (finite being).
SEG 11: Śrī Rāma is the all-pervading Brahma;
SEG 12: He is supreme bliss personified, the highest Lord and the most ancient Being.
SEG 13: The whole world knows it.
