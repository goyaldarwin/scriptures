  HI: दसों दिशाओंमें अत्यन्त दाह होने लगा (आग लगने लगी)। बिना ही पर्व (योग) के सूर्यग्रहण होने लगा। मन्दोदरीका हृदय बहुत काँपने लगा। मूर्तियाँ नेत्र-मार्गसे जल बहाने लगीं ।।५।।
SEG 0: No sooner were Rāvaṇa’s heads cut off than a fresh crop grew like covetousness, which increases with every new gain.
SEG 1: The enemy could not be killed in spite of the prolonged struggle;
SEG 2: Śrī Rāma then looked at Vibhīṣaṇa.
SEG 3: Umā, (continues Lord Śiva,) the Lord whose will causes the death of Death himself thereby tested the devotion of His servant.
SEG 4: “Listen, all-wise Ruler of the animate and inanimate creation, Protector of the suppliant, delight of the gods and sages :
SEG 5: nectar abides in the depth of his navel;
SEG 6: by virtue of it, my lord, Rāvaṇa survives.” The All-merciful rejoiced to hear the words of Vibhīṣaṇa and took terrible shafts in His hands.
SEG 7: Many ill-omens manifested themselves at that time.
SEG 8: Donkeys, jackals and dogs howled in large numbers.
SEG 9: Birds too screamed, and thereby portended a world calamity:
SEG 10: and comets appeared in every quarter of the heavens.
SEG 11: There was a preternatural and unusual glow in the horizon on all sides and a solar eclipse occurred even without the day of the new moon (when the sun and the moon are in conjunction).
SEG 12: Mandodarī’s heart beat wildly and idols shed tears from their eyes.
