SEG 0: Perceiving him to be a devotee of Śrī Hari She developed an intense affection for him.
SEG 1: Her eyes filled with tears and a thrill ran through Her body.
SEG 2: “To me who was being drowned in the ocean of desolation, dear Hanumān, you have come as a veritable bark.
SEG 3: Now tell me, I adjure you, the welfare of all-blissful Śrī Rāma (the Slayer of Khara) and His younger brother (Lakṣmaṇa).
SEG 4: Wherefore has the tender-hearted and compassionate Lord of the Raghus become so hard-hearted?
SEG 5: Does the Chief of the Raghus ever remember me—He who is by natural disposition a source of delight to His servants ?
SEG 6: Will my eyes, dear Hanumān, be ever gladdened by the sight of His swarthy and delicate limbs?” Words failed Her and Her eyes swam with tears.
SEG 7: “Ah, my lord !
SEG 8: You have entirely forgotten me.” Seeing Sītā sore distressed due to Her separation from Her lord, Hanumān addressed Her in soft and polite accents:
SEG 9: “The Lord and His younger brother (Lakṣmaṇa) are both doing well, mother, except for the fact that the all-merciful is sorrowful because of Your sorrow.
SEG 10: Do not feel vexed at heart, mother;
SEG 11: Śrī Rāma loves You twice as much as You love Him.
