  HI: सभी तारागणोंके साथ सोलह कलाओंसे पूर्ण चन्द्रमा उदय हो और जितने पर्वत हैं उन सबमें दावाग्नि लगा दी जाय, तो भी सूर्यके उदय हुए बिना रात्रि नहीं जा सकती ।।७८ (ख)।।
SEG 0: The man who seeks to attain the state of eternal bliss without adoring Śrī Rāmacandra is a beast without tail and horns, however wise he may be.
SEG 1: Even though the moon rose complete in all her sixteen digits with the entire starry host, and even if all the mountains were set on fire, night would not yield except to the sun.
