  HI: इन्द्रके वज्र, मेरे विशाल त्रिशूल, कालके दण्ड और श्रीहरिके विकराल चक्रके मारे भी जो नहीं मरता, वह भी विप्रदोहरूपी अग्निसे भस्म हो जाता है ।।७।।
ITEM 7 [uttar.109.cau8]
  TR: asa bibeka rākhehu mana māhī̐, tumha kaha̐ jaga durlabha kachu nāhī̐ / aurau eka āsiṣā morī, apratihata gati hoihi torī ॥8॥
  HI: ऐसा विवेक मनमें रखना। फिर तुम्हारे लिये जगत्‌में कुछ भी दुर्लभ न होगा। मेरा एक और भी आशीर्वाद है कि तुम्हारी सर्वत्र अबाध गति होगी (अर्थात् तुम जहाँ जाना चाहोगे, वहीं बिना रोक-टोकके जा सकोगे) ।।८।।
SEG 0: “Now do that which may bring him supreme blessedness, O fountain of mercy !” On hearing the Brāhmaṇa’s words, steeped as they were in charity, the heavenly voice replied :
SEG 1: “So be it !
SEG 2: Although he has committed a grievous sin and I in My wrath have pronounced a curse on him, yet, realizing your goodness, I shall do him a special favour.
SEG 3: O holy Brāhmaṇa, they who are of a forgiving disposition and beneficent are as dear to Me as Śrī Rāma (the Slayer of the demon Khara) Himself.
SEG 4: Nonetheless, O Brāhmaṇa, My curse shall not go in vain :
SEG 5: this fellow shall surely pass through a thousand incarnations.
SEG 6: But the terrible agony involved in each successive birth and death shall not affect him in the least.
SEG 7: (Turning to me, the voice continued :) Hear, O Śūdra, my authentic word :
SEG 8: in none of your births shall your awareness (of previous existences) leave you.
SEG 9: (In the first place) You were born in the capital of Śrī Rāma (the Lord of the Raghus), and besides that you set your heart on My worship.
SEG 10: Due to the miraculous power of the holy city and by My grace, devotion to Śrī Rāma shall spring up in your bosom.
SEG 11: Now, brother, hear My solemn declaration:
SEG 12: a vow to serve the Brāhmaṇas is the surest means of propitiating Śrī Hari.
SEG 13: Insult the Brāhmaṇas no more and reckon a saint to be on a par with the infinite Lord Himself.
SEG 14: Even he who does not succumb to the stroke of Indra’s thunderbolt, My own mighty trident, the rod of Death and the terrible discus of Śrī Hari, is consumed by the fire of hostility with the Brāhmaṇas.
SEG 15: Treasure up this counsel in your heart, and there will be nothing in this world which may be too difficult for you to attain.
SEG 16: I bestow one more blessing on you:
SEG 17: you shall have unobstructed access everywhere.”
