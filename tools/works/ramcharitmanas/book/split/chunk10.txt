  HI: उन्होंने अपने श्रीमुखसे सीताजी, लक्ष्मणजी और सखा गुहको तीर्थराजकी महिमा कहकर सुनायी। तदनन्तर प्रणाम करके, वन और बगीचोंको देखते हुए और बड़े प्रेमसे माहात्म्य कहते हुए— ।।२।।
ITEM 2 [ayodhya.106.cau3]
  TR: ehi bidhi āi bilokī benī, sumirata sakala sumaṁgala denī. / mudita nahāi kīnhi siva sevā, pūji jathābidhi tīratha devā ॥3॥
  HI: इस प्रकार श्रीरामने आकर त्रिवेणीका दर्शन किया, जो स्मरण करनेसे ही सब सुन्दर मङ्गलोंको देनेवाली है। फिर आनन्दपूर्वक [त्रिवेणीमें] स्नान करके शिवजीकी सेवा (पूजा) की और विधिपूर्वक तीर्थदेवताओंका पूजन किया ।।३।।
ITEM 3 [ayodhya.106.cau4]
  TR: taba prabhu bharadvāja pahiṁ āe, karata daṁḍavata muni ura lāe. / muni mana moda na kachu kahi jāī, brahmānaṁda rāsi janu pāī ॥4॥
  HI: [स्नान, पूजन आदि सब करके] तब प्रभु श्रीरामजी भरद्वाजजीके पास आये। उन्हें दण्डवत् करते हुए ही मुनिने हृदयसे लगा लिया। मुनिके मनका आनन्द कुछ कहा नहीं जाता। मानो उन्हें ब्रह्मानन्दकी राशि मिल गयी हो ।।४।।
SEG 0: Who can describe the glory of Prayāga, a lion as it were for the herd of elephants in the shape of sins?
SEG 1: The Chief of Raghu’s race, who is an ocean of bliss, was filled with delight to see this glorious king of holy places.
SEG 2: With His own gracious lips He told Sītā, Lakṣmaṇa and His friend (Guha) the greatness of Prayāga.
SEG 3: Making obeisance to this holy place He cast a look round the groves and gardens and expatiated on its glory with the utmost devotion.
SEG 4: In this way he arrived at and saw the confluence of the Gaṅgā and Yamunā, the very thought of which bestows all choice blessings.
SEG 5: After bathing in the confluence He gladly adored Lord Śiva and worshipped the deities presiding over the holy Prayāga according to the prescribed ritual.
SEG 6: The Lord then called on Bharadvāja;
SEG 7: and the sage clasped Him to his bosom as He fell prostrate before him.
SEG 8: The joy that the sage felt within his heart cannot be described in words;
SEG 9: it looked as if he had found the bliss of oneness with Brahma incarnate.
