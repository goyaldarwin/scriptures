# Reconciliation: जय जय भगीरथनन्दिनि

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base)** (`hinduism/aarti/ganga/bhagirath-nandini-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/ganga-bhagiratha-nandini-aarti.html`), "गंगा माता आरती": the opening stanza, marked as the refrain (जय जय भगीरथनन्दिनि...), and five stanzas of four printed lines, the refrain cue after each. The printed book (Aarti Chalisa Sangrah) has no copy, so Drik Panchang is the only witness.
- **For the one doubtful line division only:** a web search for the lines of stanza 3 found only Drik Panchang and sites that print its text (Bhakti Bharat; brah.ma; hindimejane.net), so nothing was settled by it; the rhyme settles it (below).

The rule: keep the base; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (a comma inside a line becomes a space); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Base | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| after every stanza | जय जय भगीरथनन्दिनि...। | (not shown) | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item (टेक). |
| stanza 1, line 3; stanza 4, line 3 | त्रिपथगासि, पुन्यरासि · कीट, जटिल तापस | त्रिपथगासि पुन्यरासि · कीट जटिल तापस | The standard: the comma inside a line becomes a space. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥५॥ | The standard. |
| stanza 3, lines 1–2 | पुरजन पूजोपहार सोभित / ससि धवल धार | पुरजन पूजोपहार / सोभित ससि धवल धार | A misplaced line break. In every stanza the first three lines rhyme (नन्दिनि · चन्दिनि · बन्दिनि; जासि · भासि · रासि; बारि · हारि; बिहंग · पतंग; तीर · बीर), here पूजोपहार · धार · भार, so सोभित begins the second line, and the sense agrees: the stream "shining white as the moon" (सोभित ससि धवल धार). |
| stanza 2, lines 3–4; stanza 4, lines 1–2; stanza 5, line 2 | बिभन्गतर · तरन्ग · बिहन्ग · पतन्ग · रघुवन्स | बिभंगतर · तरंग · बिहंग · पतंग · रघुवंस | The page types the nasal before a consonant as न्; न् before ग or स is not a Hindi spelling, and is written ं. |

Kept as the base has them: the hyphens (मुनि-चय, चकोर-चन्दिनि, नर-नाग-बिबुध-बन्दिनि, विष्णु-पद-सरोजजासि, ईस-सीस, पाप-छालिका, त्रयताप-हारि, तरंग-मालिका, भव-भार, भक्ति-कल्प, जल-थल-चर पसु-पतंग, रघुवंस-बीर, मोह-महिष-कालिका), the Avadhī spellings (बिबुध, ईस, सीस, बिभासि, पुन्यरासि, बिमल, बिपुल, बारि, सीतल, सोभित, ससि, बासी, पसु, बिचरत), जह्नुबालिका, भक्ति-कल्प थालिका written apart, विष्णु and रघुवंस with व beside the ब of the rest.
