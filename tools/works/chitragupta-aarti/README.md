# ॐ जय चित्रगुप्त हरे (Om Jai Chitragupt Hare)

The āratī of Citragupta, the keeper of the record of every soul's deeds, in Hindi (`hi`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): Drik Panchang, "Lord Chitragupta Aarti" (drikpanchang.com/lyrics/aarti/lord-chitragupta/lord-chitragupta-aarti.html); the āratī is not in the book. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/sapt-swar-sargam-bhakti/`: Sapt Swar Sargam Bhakti (YouTube `RRLj-2TkGj0`, "चित्रगुप्त महाराज की आरती |ॐ जय चित्रगुप्त हरे | Chitragupta Ji Aarti | Kayastha Puja | Hindi Bhajan"), its track 0:00–2:54 of the video (after 2:54: music only to the video's end). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list. A draft (it plays only with Audio timing on): not every verse it sings is timed yet: the transcript heard too little of it for the forced aligner.

Verse types: टेक (refrain, `tek`, shown once at the start although it is sung after every stanza) and पद (stanza, `pad`). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (Om Jai Chitragupt Hare) › one section (`aarti`) › verses: the refrain (`aarti.tek`, type `tek`) and 10 stanzas.
- **Numbering:** each stanza's `n` is its number (॥१॥ …), and it is cited by it ("Stanza 3"); the refrain has none.
- **Topics:** one, the āratī's title. **Reading plans:** none.
