# Reconciliation: ॐ जय चित्रगुप्त हरे

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** "Lord Chitragupta Aarti", under the heading "श्री चित्रगुप्त जी की आरती" (`hinduism/aarti/chitragupta/chitragupta-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/lord-chitragupta-aarti.html`): the refrain and ten stanzas, each in four printed lines, with the cue "ॐ जय चित्रगुप्त हरे...॥" after the refrain and after every stanza.
- **The book:** the booklets in scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` do not print this āratī, so Drik Panchang is the only written witness.
- **For doubtful readings only (web search):** Astroyogi (hindi.astroyogi.com/spirituality/aarti/chitragupt-aarti.aspx), Sri Mandir (srimandir.com/articles/aarti/sri-chitragupt-ji-ki-aarti) and Kayastha Samaj (kayasthasamaj.in/home/shriChitragupt/5) were checked for स्वास्थ and for where the lines of stanzas 2 and 4 break. All three print स्वास्थ; all three end the first printed line of stanza 2 at राजै and Astroyogi ends those of stanzas 2 and 4 at साजै and सोहै (each printed line holding two half-lines), which puts the half after मूरति and after पत्रिका.

The rule: keep the base; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (a pause within a line is a space; a half-line per line); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Base | Ours | Why |

The words are unchanged; the break follows the metre and the other sites (above). |

Kept as the base has them: इच्छित, सुखदायी, छायी, राजै, अङ्ग, शङ्ख, हुये, सोहै, हर्षाये, तैंतीस, कीन्हा, वेगि विलम्ब न लायो, दारा सुत भगिनी / सब अपने स्वास्थ के कर्ता (every witness has स्वास्थ, Bhakti Bharat too; read as "their own well-being", which gives the stanza's sense, as स्वारथ would), तुम तज मैं भर्ता (settled below, under Readings settled), गहूँ, करूँ, गावैं, छूटैं, बैकुण्ठ, तिहारी; the order with the stanza न्यायाधीश बैकुण्ठ निवासी after the stanza that names the singer.

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
The readings the words alone leave open, each decided (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §2).

- **aarti.pad7** — तुम तज मैं भर्ता: kept as printed (every witness found prints it: Drik Panchang, Bhakti Bharat its copy) and read with the half before it, जाऊँ कहाँ शरण में किसकी: "where shall I go, whose refuge shall I seek? Leaving you, O sustainer (भर्ता, "cherisher, supporter, lord", Platts), where could I go?" No other reading is attested. Meaning unchanged.
