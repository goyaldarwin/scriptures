# Reconciliation: जय माँ चन्द्रघण्टा सुख धाम

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** "Chandraghanta Mata Aarti", under the heading "आरती देवी चन्द्रघण्टा जी की" (`hinduism/aarti/durga/chandraghanta-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/devi-chandraghanta-aarti.html`): four groups of four lines and a last group of two, in chaupāī couplets (each line ending । or ॥), no refrain and no refrain cue.
- **The book:** the booklets in scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` do not print this āratī, so Drik Panchang is the only written witness.
- **For a doubtful reading only (web search):** चन्द्र समाज (stanza 1); Patrika (patrika.com/religion-and-spirituality/maa-chandraghanta-aarti-katha-navratri-day-3-pooja-vidhi-benefits-20439793) prints समान; India TV (indiatvnews.com/lifestyle/books-culture-navratri-2020-day-3-here-s-the-vidhi-mantra-and-aarti-for-maa-chandraghanta-658020) prints समाज as Drik Panchang does. Both print मालक (stanza 2).

The rule: keep the base; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Base | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| each group | four lines (the last two), the second and fourth ending ॥ | one stanza: lines ending ।, the last ॥N॥ | The standard; each group of the page is a stanza. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥५॥ | The standard. |
| stanza 1, line 3 | चन्द्र समाज तू शीतल दाती | चन्द्र समान तू शीतल दाती | समाज ("society") makes no sense here; the line says she gives coolness like the moon (चन्द्र समान), as the next line (चन्द्र तेज किरणों में समाती) carries on. A one-letter typing slip; Patrika prints समान. |

Kept as the base has them: सुख धाम, कीजो, दाती, मन की मालक, वर दाती, ध्याये, सन्मुख, ज्योत, बाता, जगत दाता, कांचीपुर, कर्नाटिका, रटू, and the two-line last stanza.
