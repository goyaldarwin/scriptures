# Reconciliation: श्री बटुक भैरव चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 69–72; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-068 … i-071), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/bhairava/batuk-bhairav-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-batuka-bhairava-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, with its own slips: chaupāī 23's first half is lost and chaupāī 22's first half (शंकर के अवतार कृपाला) printed in its place; भेरा, पढे.
- **For a doubtful reading only:** Shubh Panchang (shubhpanchang.com, Baṭuka Bhairava Chālīsā), found by web search. Bhakti Bharat prints Drik Panchang's text and counts with it as one witness.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| opening dohā 2 | बटुकनाथ भैरव भजं | बटुकनाथ भैरव भजूं | भजं is not a word: the ū sign is lost. The verb is the first person, as रचूं in the dohā before; Shubh Panchang has भजूं, Drik Panchang भजू. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: त्रैल तेश (Drik त्रैलतेश; Shubh Panchang बैलकतेश; settled below), कृत्यायू (Drik कृत्यायु), chaupāī 23 काशी के कुतवाल कहाओ (Drik repeats chaupāī 22's first half), ब्याह (Drik व्याह), भात भरा (Drik भेरा), पढ़े (Drik पढे). The spellings भैरों, दसपाण, आडम्बर, कहैं, करमें, रूदन, हिवारे मैं, सिहावें and पिलावें are the book's, and Drik Panchang has them too.

## Readings settled
- chalisa.cau6 — the name त्रैल तेश (Drik त्रैलतेश, Shubh Panchang बैलकतेश; त्रैलोकेश?): kept as the book prints it and read as one name of Bhairava, unexplained ("त्रैल तेश आपका नाम है" / "Trailateśa is your name"). The page image (i-068) shows the same gap the book sets inside other names (प्रेत नाथ, चक्र तुण्ड), so the spacing proves nothing; no dictionary has either form, and a web search found no source with त्रैलोकेश or any other reading, so nothing shows the print is a corruption.
- chalisa.cau10 — who says गायत्री कहैं: the goddess Gāyatrī, as the words say ("Gāyatrī calls you Nimiṣa and Digambara"). The verse names a speaker before कहैं as cau12 does (कहैं त्र्यम्बक सब नर नारी), and no Bhairava Gāyatrī mantra uses these names; reading it as "in the Gāyatrī" would add a postposition the line does not have.
- chalisa.cau32 — आपहि वारे छीजन धाये: the plain reading stands ("you yourself hastened to Chījana", छीजन as a place). Platts has only छीजना "to waste away" and no place; no other witness or web source tells the Ailādī story; the book, Drik Panchang and Shubh Panchang print the same words.
- chalisa.cau34 — गये हिवारे मैं तुम जाकर, to whom: the words say only that he went into Hivārā (मैं = में); nothing says whose village it is or whether he went to Ailādī or away from her. The Hindi was already neutral ("हिवारेमें जा पहुँचे"); the English "went off to Hivārā" leaned to "away" and is now "went to Hivārā".
- chalisa.cau36 — which प्रभु sent him: left unnamed, "the Lord", as the verse has it. cau35 has Śiva's throne shaking and cau37 Viṣṇu speaking, so either could be meant; no witness names him, and the meaning does not choose.
