# Reconciliation: कूष्माण्डा जय जग सुखदानी

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** "Kushmanda Mata Aarti" (`hinduism/aarti/durga/kushmanda-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/devi-kushmanda-aarti.html`), headed आरती देवी कूष्माण्डा जी की. Five groups of four printed lines, no refrain marked.
- The book (५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित) and the other booklets have no copy of this āratī; Drik Panchang is the only witness.
- **For a doubtful reading only:** the āratī as Aaj Tak (aajtak.in/religion/vrat-katha/maa-kushmanda-vrat-katha-2501867) and InstaAstro (instaastro.com/blog/kushmanda-mata-ki-aarti/) print it, found by web search. Bhakti Bharat prints Drik Panchang's text and is not counted.

The rule: keep the base; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; stanzas numbered (॥१॥). The page marks no refrain, so none is made: the text is five stanzas.

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| line ends | । and ॥ alternating | । and, at a stanza's end, ॥N॥ | The standard. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥५॥ | The standard. |

Kept as the base has them: सुखदानी, पिङ्गला, शाकम्बरी, भोली भाली, भीमा पर्वत, स्वीकारो प्रणाम ये मेरा, सुख पहुँचती हो (Aaj Tak and InstaAstro have it too; see Readings settled), पूर्ण कर दो मेरी आशा, क्यों ना, अरज, कारज, भण्डारे, ध्याये, झुकाये.

## Readings settled
The readings the words alone leave open, each decided (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §2).

- **aarti.pad3** — सुख पहुँचती हो: kept as printed. पहुँचाती would mend the sense and the 16 mātrās, but every witness prints पहुँचती (Drik Panchang, Aaj Tak, InstaAstro, and NavBharat Live, navbharatlive.com, found by a web search), so nothing shows the print is a corruption. The meaning gives the sense the line plainly carries, "you bring happiness", as our word entry for पहुँचती already does. Meaning unchanged.
