# Reconciliation: हर हर हर महादेव

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** "Aarti Shri Shiva Shankarji Ki" (<https://www.drikpanchang.com/lyrics/aarti/lord-shiva/mahadev-aarti.html>), scriptures-data `hinduism/aarti/shiva/har-har-mahadev-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/mahadev-aarti.html`. It is the only witness.
- **The book:** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan), scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/`: the āratī is not in it.

The rule: keep Drik Panchang; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (a comma inside a line becomes a space); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| refrain and stanza 1 | one group: हर हर हर महादेव! / सत्य, सनातन, … स्वामी। / अविकारी … अन्तर्यामी॥ | the refrain हर हर हर महादेव (`aarti.tek`), then सत्य सनातन … as stanza 1 | The page's refrain cue after every stanza is हर हर हर महादेव alone; the couplet after it is a stanza of the same form as the other ten. |
| every line | commas between the epithets | spaces | The standard. |
| refrain; stanza 9, line 2; stanza 11, line 1 | हर हर हर महादेव!; केवल हर!; दयामय! | without ! | Punctuation is not part of the verse; a pause is a space. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥११॥ | The standard. |
| after every stanza | ⟨refrain⟩ हर हर हर महादेव! | not shown | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item. |

No word is changed. Kept as Drik Panchang has them: औढरदानी, मणिमय-भवन, छाल-कपाल गरल-गल, भस्मतन, अयनमहाकाली (a doubt: `docs/reviews/har-har-mahadev-aarti-doubts.jsonl`), शुभ्र-सौम्य, शिवमुनि मन-हारी, निरञ्जन, सत् चित् आनन्द, प्रेम-सुधा-निधि, चरण-शरण.
