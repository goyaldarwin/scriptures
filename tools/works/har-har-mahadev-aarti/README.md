# हर हर हर महादेव (Har Har Har Mahadev)

The āratī of Śiva (Śrī Śivaśaṅkarajī kī Āratī), in Hindi (`hi`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): Drik Panchang, "Aarti Shri Shiva Shankarji Ki", the only witness; the āratī is not in ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** none yet.

Verse types: टेक (refrain, `tek`, the one line हर हर हर महादेव, shown once at the start although it is sung after every stanza) and पद (stanza, `pad`). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (Har Har Har Mahadev) › one section (`aarti`) › verses: the refrain (`aarti.tek`, type `tek`) and 11 stanzas of two lines.
- **Numbering:** each stanza's `n` is its number (॥१॥ …), and it is cited by it ("Stanza 3"); the refrain has none.
- **Topics:** one, the āratī's title. **Reading plans:** none.
