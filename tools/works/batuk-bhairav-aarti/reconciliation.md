# Reconciliation: जय भैरव देवा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), p. 73; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-072), read on the page image, under the heading "आरती श्री बटुक भैरव जी की". Each stanza is one printed line, its halves joined by a comma (the last stanza is printed on two lines). It prints no refrain cues.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/bhairava/batuk-bhairav-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/batuka-bhairava-aarti.html`): the same text, word for word, each stanza in four printed lines, with the cue "ॐ जय भैरव देवा...॥" after each.
- **Aarti Mala** (page image i-321, "श्री भैरवजी की आरती", a printout marked Budhiraja.com): another copy of the same āratī with other wordings: refrain जय भैरव देवा प्रभु जय भैरव देवा । जय काली और गौरा देवी कृत सेवा; तुम्ही पाप उद्धारक; सुख कारक; तुम बिन देवा सेवा; चौमुख दीपक; पांव घुंघरू बाजत अरू डमरू डमकावत; बटुकनाथ जी की आरती जो कोई नर गावे । कहे धरणी धर नर मनवांछित फल पावे. (Page image i-319, also listed in the kit, is the Viśvakarmā āratī, not this one.)
- **For a doubtful reading only:** a web search for चतुर्वतिका; Bhakti Bharat prints the Aarti Mala wording (चौमुख), InstaAstro prints चतुर्वतिका as Drik Panchang does; it settled nothing.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (the book's comma between the halves of a line becomes a line break, a half per line); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| every stanza | one printed line, its halves joined by a comma | two lines, ending । and ॥N॥ | The standard; the comma marks the half. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥६॥ | The standard. |
| stanza 6 | कहे 'धरणीधर' वह नर | कहे धरणीधर वह नर | The quotation marks are the printer's, not part of the verse. |

Kept as the book has them (Drik Panchang agrees throughout): तुम्हरी, उद्धारक, चतुर्वतिका (open doubt: वर्तिका "wick" would give चतुर्वर्तिका, "four-wicked"; `docs/reviews/batuk-bhairav-aarti-doubts.jsonl`), चटकि, भाषावलि, कीजिये, नहिं, पाँवों घूंघरू, हरषावत, वांछित. Against Aarti Mala, the book's readings are kept everywhere (its wordings are listed above).
