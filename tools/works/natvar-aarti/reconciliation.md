# Reconciliation: आरती कीजै श्रीनटवर जी की

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** "Lord Natavara Aarti" (`hinduism/aarti/krishna/natvar-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/lord-natavara-aarti.html`), headed भगवान नटवर आरती. Five stanzas (नन्द-सुवन, जय वसुदेव, अकल कलाधर, पाण्डव-पूत, नित्य सत्य), the refrain आरती कीजै श्रीनटवर जी की। गोवर्धन-धर बन्शीधर की॥ after each, and the refrain twice at the end.
- The book (५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित) and the other booklets have no copy of this āratī; Drik Panchang is the only witness.
- **For a doubtful reading only:** a web search for stanza 4 found only Bhakti Bharat (bhaktibharat.com, "Bhagwan Natwar Aarti"), which prints Drik Panchang's text; it counts as the same witness.

The rule: keep the base; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; the refrain (टेक) is shown once, first; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| the order | stanza 1, then the refrain | the refrain first (`aarti.tek`), then the stanzas | The refrain is shown once, first; the page prints it after every stanza. |
| after every stanza; at the end | the refrain; the refrain twice | (not written) | The refrain is shown once. |
| stanza 3, line 3 | अजर, अमर, मायिक, मायाहर, | अजर अमर मायिक मायाहर । | No commas in an āratī's verse; a pause within a line is a space. |
| line ends | , । ॥ | । and, at a stanza's end, ॥N॥ | The standard. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥५॥ | The standard. |

Kept as the base has them: the hyphens (नन्द-सुवन, वसुदेव-देवकी-नन्दन, कालयवन-कन्सादि-निकन्दन, पाण्डव-पूत, मूषक-भक्षक, गोवर्धन-धर), जसुमतिके and असुरनके written joined, कन्सादि, बन्शीधर, निर्गुन, अजाव्यक्त, अतुलित अहि अघ मूषक-भक्षक (settled below, Readings settled).

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
- **aarti.pad4** — अतुलित अहि अघ मूषक-भक्षक: kept as Drik Panchang, the base, prints it (a web search found only Bhakti Bharat, its copy). Every epithet of the stanza in -क is Kṛṣṇa's (रक्षक, भक्षक, निरीक्षक), so भक्षक is he: he made an end of Agha the serpent as if he were a mouse, भक्षक in its figurative sense "devourer, destroyer". Meaning unchanged.
