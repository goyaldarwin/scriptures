# Reconciliation: ॐ जय जगदानन्दी

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), p. 265, "आरती श्री नर्मदा जी की"; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page image i-264), read on the page image. Fourteen printed lines in pairs, each pair ending ॥ with the cue "ॐ जय": the opening pair (the refrain) and six stanzas.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/narmada/narmada-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/narmada-mata-aarti.html`): the same refrain and six stanzas in the same order, word for word but one letter, each printed line broken at its half.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (the book's comma between the halves of a line becomes a line break, a half per line); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| after every stanza | ॐ जय" | (not shown) | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item (टेक). |
| every printed line | the halves joined by a comma, or (refrain line 2, and most lines of stanzas 1–6) by a space | two lines | The standard; the comma marks the half. Where the book prints the half without a comma, the line is broken at the same place, the place Drik Panchang breaks it, so that every stanza has its four half-lines. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥६॥ | The standard. |
| refrain, line 4 | रूद्री | रुद्री | A misprint: the name is from रुद्र (short u); Drik Panchang has रुद्री. |

Kept as the book has them: रेवा शंकर (stanza 4; Drik Panchang joins रेवाशंकर; the book's spacing gives the meaning 'Revā, Śaṅkara serves you'). Kept as the book has them (Drik Panchang agrees): आनंद कन्दी, पालन्ती, पदचण्डी, पदवन्ती, धूमक वाहन, वादयन्ती, झूमकत, झननन, तोड़ीतान, तुरड़ड़, सुरवन्ती, मेटन्ती, अमरकंठ, कोटी, जोती, पढ़ि, जुग जुग, शिवानंद beside आनन्दी (both spellings of the nasal, as printed).

## Readings settled
- aarti.tek — the bare list of names and रुद्री: kept, Revā is named as one with Brahmā, Hari, Hara, Śaṅkara, Śiva and Rudrāṇī, she who sustains. The line is a list with no verb; nothing in the book or Drik Panchang (word for word the same) supports another construction; a web search found no independent text or gloss.
- aarti.pad1 — नारद शारद तुम वरदायक, पदचण्डी, पदवन्ती: kept, she grants boons to Nārada and Śāradā; पद + चण्डी, holding the rank of Caṇḍī (MW caṇḍī, N. of Durgā); पदवन्ती, worshipping her feet, as the line before (सुर नर मुनि जन सेवत) has the same subjects serving her. The plain reading of the printed words; both witnesses agree.
- aarti.pad2 — धूमक: kept, in splendour (Platts dhūm, s.f., display, parade, pomp). The other senses (MW dhūmaka, smoke; a pot-herb) do not fit a mount.
- aarti.pad3 — the musical words: kept, सुरमण्डल the svaramaṇḍala (MW svara-maṇḍala, a kind of vīṇā), तोड़ीतान tāns in Toḍī (Platts toṛī, ṭoṛī, a rāginī), तुरड़ड़ the instruments' sound, सुरवन्ती melodious. The stanza is about music (ताल, मृदंग).
- aarti.pad6 — who repeats Hari and भजत शिवानंद स्वामी: kept, those who read and sing the āratī, repeating Hari's name, gain the fruit desired; Svāmī Śivānanda sings it in worship. भजत is printed in both witnesses (not कहत/भणत, the signature verb of other āratīs); the plain words stand.
