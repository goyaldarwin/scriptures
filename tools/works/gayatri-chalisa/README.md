# श्री गायत्री चालीसा (Shri Gayatri Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Gāyatrī that opens "ह्रीं श्रीं क्लीं मेधा प्रभा", in Awadhi (`awa`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 197–200), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/ranjanba/`: Ranjanba (YouTube `0Bs8o9P4oo0`, "Re:GAYATRI CHALISA,श्री गायत्री चालीसा"), its track 0:00–5:46 of the video (after 5:46: music to the video's end). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list. A draft (it plays only with Audio timing on): not every verse it sings is timed yet: the transcript heard too little of it for the forced aligner.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohās (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohā (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 40 chaupāīs are numbered 1–40 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the three dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Hanumān Chālīsā's in the Sundarakāṇḍa (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** Gāyatrī's form, her glory, the fruit of worship. **Reading plans:** none.
