# श्री नवग्रह चालीसा (Shri Navagraha Chalisa)

The chālīsā of the nine planets (navagraha) that opens "श्री गणपति गुरुपद कमल", in Awadhi (`awa`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 79–83), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** none yet.

The poet names himself in the closing chaupāīs and dohā: Sundaradāsa, a devotee of Rāma, of Kakarā village (Pure-Tiwari) near Prayāga.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohās (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohās (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 40 chaupāīs are numbered 1–40 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the four dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Gaṇeśa Chālīsā's (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** the book's own headings, two planets to a topic: the Sun and the Moon, Mars and Mercury, Jupiter and Venus, Saturn, Rāhu and Ketu, and the fruit of the prayer. **Reading plans:** none.
