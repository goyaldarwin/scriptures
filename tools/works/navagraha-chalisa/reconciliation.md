# Reconciliation: श्री नवग्रह चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 79–83; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-078 … i-082), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/navagraha/navagraha-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/navagraha-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, with its own slips (दीजैबुद्धि, निरन्तन, बलवान against the rhyme ठिकाना).
- **Chalisa Mala** (the indif.com printout in the same scriptures-data folder, images i-401 … i-403): another typed copy of the web text, used only as a check.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept. The book's headings over each planet's four chaupāīs (श्री सूर्य स्तुति … नवग्रह शांति फल) are not verses; they give the topics.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| opening dohā 1 | नवग्रह चालीसाा कहत | नवग्रह चालीसा कहत | A doubled ā sign, a misprint; Drik Panchang and Chalisa Mala have चालीसा. |
| chaupāī 17 | जय श्री गुरूदेवा | जय श्री गुरुदेवा | गुरू is a spelling slip; the book itself has गुरु in the next chaupāī (देव गुरु ज्ञानी), and Drik Panchang and Chalisa Mala have गुरुदेवा. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: नावों (Drik नावौं), करहु and हरहु (Drik करहुं, हरहुं), दीजै बुद्धिबल (Drik दीजैबुद्धि बल), कल्याना (Drik कल्याणा), कहुँ (Drik कहु), निरन्तर (Drik निरन्तन), द्विजवर (Drik द्विजबर), बलवाना (Drik बलवान; बलवाना rhymes with ठिकाना), प्रेम युत (Drik प्रेम सुत). The spellings कलेषा / कलेशा, नमामि / नमामी, गुरुपद, पुरे-तिवारी and नव-ग्रह (with the book's hyphens) and नवोग्रह are the book's, and Drik Panchang has them too.
