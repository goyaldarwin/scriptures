# शैलपुत्री माँ बैल असवार (Shailputri Maa Bail Asvar)

The āratī of Devī Śailaputrī, one of the nine forms of Durgā (Navadurgā), in Hindi (`hi`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): Drik Panchang, "Shailputri Mata Aarti" (the book has no copy of it). Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Poet:** the last stanza names चमन (Chaman).
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/divine-sanatan/`: Divine Sanatan (YouTube `H42Jg1fgTKY`, "Maa Shailputri Bhajan: शैलपुत्री मां बैल असवार | Navratri Special 2026"), its track 0:00–2:19 of the video (after 2:19: the āratī sung twice more). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list.

Verse types: पद (stanza, `pad`); the source prints no refrain, so there is no टेक (`tek` is declared, as in every āratī, but unused). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (Shailputri Maa Bail Asvar) › one section (`aarti`) › verses: 5 stanzas of four lines.
- **Numbering:** each stanza's `n` is its number (॥१॥ …), and it is cited by it ("Stanza 3").
- **Topics:** one, the āratī's opening words. **Reading plans:** none.
