# Reconciliation: शैलपुत्री माँ बैल असवार

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** "Shailputri Mata Aarti" (`hinduism/aarti/durga/shailputri-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/devi-shailputri-aarti.html`), headed आरती देवी शैलपुत्री जी की. Five groups of four printed lines, no refrain marked; the last names the poet (चमन).
- The book (५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित) and the other booklets have no copy of this āratī; Drik Panchang is the only witness.

The rule: keep the base; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; stanzas numbered (॥१॥). The page marks no refrain, so none is made: the text is five stanzas.

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| line ends | । and ॥ alternating | । and, at a stanza's end, ॥N॥ | The standard. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥५॥ | The standard. |

Kept as the base has them: the page's line breaks and words throughout, असवार, शिव-शंकर, कहलावे, सुमिरे, रिद्धि सिद्धि परवान, सगरी, सगरे, पुजा दो, गोला गरी, जपायें, झुकायें, गिरराज, the short line मनोकामना पूर्ण कर दो, चमन, सम्पत्ति.
