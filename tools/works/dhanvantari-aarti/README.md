# जय धन्वन्तरि देवा (Jai Dhanvantari Deva)

The āratī of Dhanvantari, the physician of the gods who rose from the churned ocean with the pot of amṛta, in Hindi (`hi`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): Drik Panchang, "Lord Dhanvantari Aarti" (drikpanchang.com/lyrics/aarti/lord-dhanvantari/lord-dhanvantari-aarti.html); the āratī is not in the book. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/bhakti-bhajan-sagar/`: Bhakti Bhajan Sagar (YouTube `ohGqcV4JRso`, "धनतेरस दिवाली Special 2026: जय धन्वन्तरि देवा | धन्वंतरि भगवान की आरती | Dhanvantari Aarti"), its track 2:37–4:14 of the video (before 2:37: another āratī of Dhanvantari, not in the text; after 4:14: music). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list.

Verse types: टेक (refrain, `tek`, shown once at the start although it is sung after every stanza) and पद (stanza, `pad`). The folder's format: `tools/works/README.md`.

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (Jai Dhanvantari Deva) › one section (`aarti`) › verses: the refrain (`aarti.tek`, type `tek`) and 6 stanzas.
- **Numbering:** each stanza's `n` is its number (॥१॥ …), and it is cited by it ("Stanza 3"); the refrain has none.
- **Topics:** one, the āratī's title. **Reading plans:** none.
