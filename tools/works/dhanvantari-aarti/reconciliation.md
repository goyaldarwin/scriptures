# Reconciliation: जय धन्वन्तरि देवा

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** "Lord Dhanvantari Aarti", under the heading "आरती श्री धन्वन्तरि जी की" (`hinduism/aarti/dhanvantari/dhanvantari-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/lord-dhanvantari-aarti.html`): the refrain and six stanzas, each in four printed lines, with the cue "जय धन्वन्तरि देवा...॥" after the refrain and after every stanza.
- **The book:** the booklets in scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` do not print this āratī, so Drik Panchang is the only written witness.
- **For a doubtful reading only (web search):** the half-line शोभा भारी (stanza 3) is shorter than its fellows; Patrika (patrika.com/festivals/dhanteras-dhanvantari-ji-ki-aarti-5267379/) and Hindu Blog (hindu-blog.com/2019/10/dhanvantari-stuti-prayer-for-dhanteras.html) print it the same, with no word before शोभा, so it stays.

The rule: keep the base; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Base | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| every line | ends in a comma, । or nothing | ends । (the last of a stanza ॥N॥) | The standard. Stanza 5, line 2 (दास खड़ा तेरा) has no mark in the base, a dropped daṇḍa. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥६॥ | The standard. |
| after the refrain and every stanza | जय धन्वन्तरि देवा...॥ | (not shown) | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item (टेक). |

Kept as the base has them: जय धन्वन्तरि जी देवा, the hyphens of जरा-रोग, जन-जन, वैद्य-समाज, रोग-शोक, सुख-समृद्धि, लिए, किए, शंख सुधा धारी, शोभा भारी, तुम को, प्रभुजी, धन्वन्तरिजी, आए.
