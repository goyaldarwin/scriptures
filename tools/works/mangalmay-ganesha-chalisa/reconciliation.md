# Reconciliation: श्री गणेश चालीसा (मंगलमय मंगल करन)

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** `chalisa/lord-ganesha/others/shree-ganesha-chalisa` (`hinduism/chalisa/ganesha/mangalmay-ganesha-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shree-ganesha-chalisa.html`): the only witness. The book (५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित, Randhir Prakashan; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/`) does not print this chālīsā.
- **For a doubtful reading:** web searches for the doubtful lines (Shubh Panchang, Bhāratkosh, Prabhat Khabar, Bhakti Bharat) found only the other Gaṇeśa Chālīsā ("जय गणपति सदगुण सदन") or Drik Panchang's own text (Bhakti Bharat copies it); no independent copy of this one was found. The doubtful readings are settled below (Readings settled).

The rule: keep Drik Panchang's text; correct it only where it is clearly wrong (a typing slip, a non-word, a dropped sign, a broken rhyme). The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the page's commas are not kept.

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
- **chalisa.cau26** (गए तुम गिरिजा के पास: who goes; each half two mātrās short): the text stays as printed; the meaning keeps Śiva as the one who goes and speaks (he is the subject of chaupāī 25, बोले is his honorific plural, and in chaupāī 27 he says to Girijā तुमने जायो), so तुम cannot be its subject. No independent copy of this chālīsā exists to show a lost word (web searches, as above), so nothing is added.
- **chalisa.cau33** (साई दिवस): read as सोई, "that very" (day): the only sense that fits after the story of chaupāīs 30–32 (साईं "lord" and Sāī Bābā do not); the print stays, there being no second witness. Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.cau39** (डासना पी): the printed words stand: Ḍāsnā, the town near Ghaziabad, with पी as पै, "on". दासन पर ("on your servants") would be a correction no witness shows. Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.cau21** (सो; सफल): सो is "she", the mother of chaupāī 20, who will come (अइहै) and appear (प्रकट); सफल, "fruitful", is a word and fits ("to good effect"), so it is not changed to सकल without a witness. Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.cau34** (पृथ्वी प्रदक्षिणा दोउ दीन्ही: given the task, or made the circuit): प्रदक्षिणा देना is the idiom "to make a circumambulation" (Platts, pradakshiṇā), with दोउ its subject and दीन्ही agreeing with प्रदक्षिणा; both made the circuit, Gaṇeśa by going round his parents (chaupāī 37). Meaning changed: "The two of you made the circuit of the earth; there the six-faced Kārttikeya made his plan."
- **chalisa.cau40** (गौरीसा): kept as the vocative the words give, "O Gaurīśa", addressed to Gaṇeśa in the chālīsā's last line (the poet's rhyme with चालीसा); reading Śiva in as a second companion would add a subject the line lacks. Meaning unchanged.
