# Reconciliation: श्री गणेश चालीसा (मंगलमय मंगल करन)

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** `chalisa/lord-ganesha/others/shree-ganesha-chalisa` (`hinduism/chalisa/ganesha/mangalmay-ganesha-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shree-ganesha-chalisa.html`): the only witness. The book (५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित, Randhir Prakashan; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/`) does not print this chālīsā.
- **For a doubtful reading:** web searches for the doubtful lines (Shubh Panchang, Bhāratkosh, Prabhat Khabar, Bhakti Bharat) found only the other Gaṇeśa Chālīsā ("जय गणपति सदगुण सदन") or Drik Panchang's own text (Bhakti Bharat copies it); no independent copy of this one was found. The open readings are in `docs/reviews/mangalmay-ganesha-chalisa-doubts.jsonl`.

The rule: keep Drik Panchang's text; correct it only where it is clearly wrong (a typing slip, a non-word, a dropped sign, a broken rhyme). The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the page's commas are not kept.

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| opening dohā | रिपु रूज दलन | रिपु रुज दलन | रुज, "disease"; रूज is not a word and gives the half a fourteenth mātrā (13 needed). |
| chaupāī 2 | शम्भ्पूत | शम्भुपूत | A typing slip (a halant for the u-sign). |
| chaupāī 9 | पल मे हरते | पल में हरते | The dropped anusvāra (the page has में elsewhere). |
| chaupāī 16 | हुआ नही कोई | हुआ नहीं कोई | The same. |
| chaupāī 23, 25 | बढाये, बढाया | बढ़ाये, बढ़ाया | The dropped nukta of बढ़ना. |
| chaupāī 24 | रार बढाओं | रार बढ़ाओ | A stray anusvāra (the rhyme is उठाओ) and the dropped nukta. |
| chaupāī 27 | सुत कसे यह | सुत कैसे यह | A typing slip: कैसे, "how". |
| chaupāī 32 | भव भयरूज दहना | भव भयरुज दहना | As the opening dohā (रुज). |
| chaupāī 35 | चढि मयूर | चढ़ि मयूर | The dropped nukta of चढ़ना. |
| chaupāī 40 | जो श्रृद्धा से पढ़े ये चालीस | जो श्रद्धा से पढ़े ये चालीसा | श्रृद्धा is not a word (श्रद्धा); चालीसा rhymes with गौरीसा. |
| closing dohā | शंबू तनय | शम्भु तनय | A misspelling of शम्भु (as the page writes it in chaupāīs 28–29); शंबू gives the half a fourteenth mātrā. |

Kept as the page has them: गिरजा लाल, क्रीट, रिद्धि, भावे मूषक की असवारी (short of a full half, but nothing shows the missing word), तै, दिन्ही, ड्योडी, शम्भू (chaupāī 13), तुम्हे, पूछैउं, या मैं (chaupāī 19), गए तुम गिरिजा के पास / बोले कहां नारी विश्वास (chaupāī 26, short by two mātrās; Śiva is the one who goes), किनी, साई दिवस (chaupāī 33), डासना पी (chaupāī 39; Ḍāsnā is a town near Ghaziabad), प्रभू, संका, गौरीसा. The open ones: `docs/reviews/mangalmay-ganesha-chalisa-doubts.jsonl`.
