# श्री श्याम चालीसा (Shri Shyam Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Khāṭū Śyāma (Barbarīka, worshipped as Kṛṣṇa at Khatu) that opens "श्री गुरु चरण ध्यान धर", in Awadhi (`awa`). The book's heading is श्री श्याम (खाटू) चालीसा; its running head, and Drik Panchang, श्री श्याम चालीसा.

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 152–155), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/lakhbir-singh-lakkha/`: Lakhbir Singh Lakkha (YouTube `9GLVkIigVbw`, "श्याम चालीसा Shyam Chalisa with Lyrics | Lakhbir Singh Lakha | Shyam Bhajan | Khatu Shyam Chalisa"), its track 0:00–9:34 of the video (after 9:34: music only). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohā (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohā (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 40 chaupāīs are numbered 1–40 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the two dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Gaṇeśa Chālīsā's (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** who Śyāma is, his names, the power of the name, his form and his worship, Śyāma at Khatu. **Reading plans:** none.
