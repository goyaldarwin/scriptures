# Reconciliation: श्री श्याम चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 152–155; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-151 … i-154), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/khatu-shyam/khatu-shyam-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-shyam-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, verse for verse, with its own slips (चैपाई for चौपाई, रच for रज).

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 14 | पुरुषोत्तम केशव जगदीश | पुरुषोत्तम केशव जगदीशा | The half must rhyme with ईसा, and every other half ends long as the metre needs; Drik Panchang has जगदीशा. |
| closing dohā | श्याय सलोने साँवरे | श्याम सलोने साँवरे | श्याय is a misprint (not a word); Drik Panchang has श्याम. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: चौपाई छंद (opening dohā; Drik चैपाई छन्द), श्याम चरण रज नित लाई (chaupāī 23; Drik रच: the dust, रज, of the feet), जहां (chaupāī 39; Drik जहाँ). The spellings भणत, रुप, घनश्याम (chaupāī 13, against अभिरामा), अरू (chaupāīs 24, 28), भीलनी, वैजयन्तिमाल, नाशै and चहुं are the book's, as Drik's.

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
- chalisa.cau18 — करि कोविद करि: kept as printed, "the learned tried (करि, having done) but could not count them". Book and Drik Panchang agree; the one web copy with कवि कोविद (tring.co.in) shares Drik's own slip रच for रज (chaupāī 23), so it is a copy of Drik, not an independent witness, and the print stands. Meaning unchanged.
- chalisa.cau23 — नित: kept as printed (every witness), "receiving the dust of Śyāma's feet for ever"; नित is "always", and nothing shows निज. Meaning unchanged.
