# श्री विश्वकर्मा चालीसा (Shri Vishwakarma Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Viśvakarmā, the divine craftsman, that opens "विनय करौं कर जोड़कर", in Awadhi (`awa`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 85–89), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** none yet.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī, `cha` chhanda).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohā (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohās and chhanda (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 40 chaupāīs are numbered 1–40 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the three dohās and the chhanda have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha", "Closing · Chhand").
- **Ids:** as the Gaṇeśa Chālīsā's (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …; the chhanda `chalisa.cha1`).
- **Topics:** the glory of his name, the first craftsman and teacher of the world, homage, what he made for the gods, the line of craftsmen. **Reading plans:** none.
