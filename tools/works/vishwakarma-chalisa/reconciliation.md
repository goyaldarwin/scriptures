# Reconciliation: श्री विश्वकर्मा चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 85–89; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-084 … i-087, and the top of i-088, where the chhanda's last two lines are printed above the āratī), read on the page images. The āratī that follows (आरती श्री विश्वकर्मा जी की) is its own work.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/vishwakarma/vishwakarma-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-vishwakarma-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, with its own slips (विधा for विद्या, विधुत, ध्वापर, रूद्र, दॄश्य / दॄष्टि with the long ṝ, चोपाई), and the second closing dohā's and the chhanda's lines broken at other places.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the chhanda's four lines as printed, `।` and `॥` in turn; the book's commas are not kept (also those inside a half: लोक काष्ठ, शिल; परशुराम, नल, नील, सुचेता, रावण, राम).

No change from the book.

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: सुन्दर, गुणवन्त, धुरन्धर, भण्डारा, चन्द्र, हरिश्चन्द्र (Drik सुंदर, गुणवंत, धुरंधर, भंडारा, चंद्र, हरिश्चंद्र), चौदह विद्या (Drik विधा), रुद्र, विद्युत, द्वापर, दृश्य, दृष्टि, चौपाई. Both have लोक काष्ठ in chaupāī 34, where लोह (iron, one of the five crafts of chaupāī 12) may be meant: an open doubt, `docs/reviews/vishwakarma-chalisa-doubts.jsonl`. The spellings अनरूपा, भय धारा, पालेऊ, पायेऊ / ध्यायेऊ and the hyphens of धन्य-धन्य and तम-अज्ञान-समूह are the book's, and Drik Panchang has them too.
