# Reconciliation: श्री विश्वकर्मा चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 85–89; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-084 … i-087, and the top of i-088, where the chhanda's last two lines are printed above the āratī), read on the page images. The āratī that follows (आरती श्री विश्वकर्मा जी की) is its own work.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/vishwakarma/vishwakarma-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-vishwakarma-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, with its own slips (विधा for विद्या, विधुत, ध्वापर, रूद्र, दॄश्य / दॄष्टि with the long ṝ, चोपाई), and the second closing dohā's and the chhanda's lines broken at other places.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the chhanda's four lines as printed, `।` and `॥` in turn; the book's commas are not kept (also those inside a half: लोक (our लोह) काष्ठ, शिल; परशुराम, नल, नील, सुचेता, रावण, राम).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 34 | लोक काष्ठ शिल ताम्र सुकर्मा | लोह काष्ठ शिल ताम्र सुकर्मा | लोक ("world") is no craft: the half lists the crafts of the पंचक (the five) with the goldsmith's, and the chālīsā's own chaupāī 12 lists the same five as लोह काष्ठ अरु ताम्र सुवर्णा, शिला शिल्प (book and Drik Panchang alike), the five crafts of Viśvakarmā's line (the ironsmith, carpenter, coppersmith, stone-carver and goldsmith). Without लोह the five are four. Drik Panchang, typed from the same text, carries the slip over. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: सुन्दर, गुणवन्त, धुरन्धर, भण्डारा, चन्द्र, हरिश्चन्द्र (Drik सुंदर, गुणवंत, धुरंधर, भंडारा, चंद्र, हरिश्चंद्र), चौदह विद्या (Drik विधा), रुद्र, विद्युत, द्वापर, दृश्य, दृष्टि, चौपाई. The spellings अनरूपा, भय धारा, पालेऊ, पायेऊ / ध्यायेऊ and the hyphens of धन्य-धन्य and तम-अज्ञान-समूह are the book's, and Drik Panchang has them too.

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
- chalisa.cau34 — लोक or लोह: लोह, a text correction (the table above: chaupāī 12's list of the same five crafts, and the count of the पंचक); the meaning already read iron.
- chalisa.cau17 — ब्रह्मा विश्वकर्मा भय धारा: "your name became Brahmā Viśvakarmā, and you took it on" stays; नाम तुम्हारा in the first half is the subject, so the half names one name (भय for भये, "became"; धारा, "bore"), Viśvakarmā as the creator Brahmā (the Ṛgveda's Viśvakarman, 10.81–82).
- chalisa.cau22 — भवोभव: read as भव + उद्भव (भवोद्भव, "the origin of the world") with कारी, "bringing the world into being", as the glossary has it; भवाभव would need the long ā the witnesses do not print, and भव-भव ("birth after birth") gives no sense with कारी.
- chalisa.do3 — विनय करो: the poet's own prayer stays (करो for करौं, as in dohā 1 विनय करौं कर जोड़कर); तुम्हार ("your") addresses Viśvakarmā, which an order to the reader would not.
