# श्री नर्मदा चालीसा (Shri Narmada Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Mother Narmadā that opens "देवि पूजिता नर्मदा", in Awadhi (`awa`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 261–264), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** `audio/ras-mantra/`: Ras Mantra (YouTube `fYDQig0lk90`, "Shri Narmada Chalisa | श्री नर्मदा चालीसा | Powerful Narmada Mata Bhakti Path"), its track 0:00–4:52 of the video (after 4:52: music to the video's end). Starts from MMS (`aligned.md`); the verses the checks flag are on the to-check list. A draft (it plays only with Audio timing on): not every verse it sings is timed yet: the transcript heard too little of it for the forced aligner.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohās (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohā (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 40 chaupāīs are numbered 1–40 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the three dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Gaṇeśa Chālīsā's (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** Narmadā's birth and form, her flowing waters, greater than every holy place, the prayer and the fruit of reading. **Reading plans:** none.
