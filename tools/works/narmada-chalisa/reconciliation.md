# Reconciliation: श्री नर्मदा चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 261–264; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-260 … i-263), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/narmada/narmada-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/narmada-mata-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, verse for verse.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 17 | कुरूक्षेत्र में सरसुति महिमा | कुरुक्षेत्र में सरसुति महिमा | कुरूक्षेत्र is a misprint for the place name Kurukṣetra (कुरु, short u), as Drik Panchang has it. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: सरसुति (chaupāī 17; Drik सरस्वती, which breaks the metre; the book has सरसुति again in chaupāī 28, as Drik does), तरत पीढ़ी है नर नाना (chaupāī 19; Drik नर नारा; नाना rhymes with असनाना), रुद्र लोक में (chaupāī 31; Drik मे). The spellings अश्वनि, साजैं / विराजैं, ध्यावै / जावैं, तुम्हरी भजन, राजें / साजें and पढ़ै are the book's, as Drik's.

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
- **chalisa.cau21** (जटा शंकरी): one name, Jaṭāśaṅkarī: the line says नाम तुम्हारा, "your name", in the singular. Meaning unchanged.
- **chalisa.cau22** (समोद्भवा): read as सोमोद्भवा, "born of the moon", the Narmadā's name (MW somodbhavā, Raghuvaṃśa); समोद्भवा is not a word and its half-line has 15 mātrās against 16. Both witnesses print समोद्भवा, so the text stays as printed; the meaning and the word entry give सोमोद्भवा.
