# Reconciliation: जय शनि देवा

## Witnesses
- **Drik Panchang (base):** Shaniwar Ki Aarti, `hinduism/aarti/shani/jai-shani-deva-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/weekdays-shaniwar-aarti.html`. The page prints two āratīs, each under the heading "॥ शनि देव की आरती ॥": this one (जय शनि देवा: the refrain and three stanzas), and after it another (चार भुजा तहि छाजै, with the refrain cue "जय शनिदेव जी", whose first group has only two lines). The second is another āratī and is not part of this work.
- **The book:** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित and Aarti Mala do not print this āratī; Drik Panchang is the only witness.
- **For a doubtful reading only:** a web search for the line करू आरती भक्ति भाव से found only Bhakti Bharat (which copies Drik Panchang) and Drik Panchang itself; it settled nothing.

The rule: keep Drik Panchang; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma; each line as the page prints it; the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Drik Panchang | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| line ends | । (and , ) | । and ॥N॥ (the refrain ॥) | The standard. |
| after every stanza | जय शनि देवा...॥ | (not shown) | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item (टेक). |
| refrain, line 1; stanza 2, line 5 | जय शनि देवा, जय शनि देवा; ताँबा, तेल और तिल से जो | जय शनि देवा जय शनि देवा; ताँबा तेल और तिल से जो | An āratī's verse has no comma: a pause within a line is a space. |
| stanza 3, line 5 | करू आरती भक्ति भाव से | करूं आरती भक्ति भाव से | करू is not a Hindi verb form; "I perform" is करूं, the nasal dropped by a slip (the same line's partner has चढ़ाऊं, written with the anusvāra, as we write it). |

Kept as Drik Panchang has them: होउ, मान-कीर्ति, पलभर, राजपाट हर लेवा, तुम्हारी कृपा रहे तो, ताँबा, जय-जय कार, महात्तम, दुःख दरिद्रता, चढ़ाऊं.
