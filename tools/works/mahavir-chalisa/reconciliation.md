# Reconciliation: श्री महावीर (तीर्थंकर) चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), pp. 141–144; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-140 … i-143), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/mahavir/mahavir-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-mahavir-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, with the same 36 chaupāīs; it ends both sorathās with `।`.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's or sorathā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 36 | चालीसे को 'चन्द्र' बनावे | चालीसे को चन्द्र बनावे | The quotation marks around the poet's name are the printer's, not part of the verse. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: सब भाग जावें (chaupāī 9; Drik भग जावें). Kept as both have them: करूँ, नवा, द्वेश, वीर रण राग तू हितोपदेश, व्याध, सगरी, सिद्धारथ, बगाया, खिवैया, कछु, बीर. In chaupāī 7 the faint stroke between और and द्वेश on the page is show-through from the next leaf, not a hyphen.

**Chaupāī 7, वीर रण राग, kept.** The Jaina triad of an Arihanta's qualities (वीतराग, सर्वज्ञ, हितोपदेशी) suggests वीतराग, but no witness prints it: the book, Drik Panchang and the web copies (Bhakti Bharat, bhaktibharat.com/chalisa/mahavir-chalisa; Chalisa Lyrics, chalisalyrics.com; All Bhajan Lyrics, allbhajanlyrics.com) all read वीर रण राग तू हितोपदेश, and the emendation does not fit the line: वीतराग तू हितोपदेश has 15 mātrās against the chaupāī's 16, and वीतराग तू हितोपदेशी loses the rhyme with द्वेश. The line is not clearly wrong, so the book's words stay; the meaning reads them as printed ("the hero in the battle with attachment").
