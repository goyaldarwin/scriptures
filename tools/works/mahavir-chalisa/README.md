# श्री महावीर (तीर्थंकर) चालीसा (Shri Mahavir (Tirthankar) Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Mahāvīra, the twenty-fourth Jaina Tīrthaṅkara (not Hanumān), that opens "शीश नवा अरिहन्त को", in Awadhi (`awa`); it tells of the miracle of Chāṁdanapura (Shri Mahavirji, Rajasthan).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 141–144), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Poet:** chaupāī 36 names चन्द्र ("चालीसे को चन्द्र बनावे").
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** none yet.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `so` sorathā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohās (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing sorathās (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 36 chaupāīs (the book and Drik Panchang print 36, not 40) are numbered 1–36 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the dohās and sorathās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Soratha").
- **Ids:** as the Hanumān Chālīsā's in the Sundarakāṇḍa (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1`, `chalisa.so1` …).
- **Topics:** Mahāvīra's form, protection from fear, his birth and renunciation, the miracle of Chāṁdanapura, the prayer. **Reading plans:** none.
