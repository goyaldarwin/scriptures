# श्री वैष्णो देवी चालीसा (Shri Vaishno Devi Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Vaiṣṇo Devī, the Mother of the Trikūṭa hill, that opens "गरुड़ वाहिनी वैष्णवी", in Awadhi (`awa`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 231–234), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** none yet.

The poet names himself in the last chaupāī: "सेवक शर्मा" (Śarmā), a modern author.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › three sections: the opening dohā (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`), the closing dohā (`chalisa-ant`) › verses.
- **Numbering:** the 40 chaupāīs are numbered 1–40 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the two dohās have no number of their own and are cited by their section ("Opening · Doha", "Closing · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Gaṇeśa Chālīsā's (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** her birth and Rāma's promise, the shrine on Trikūṭa, the story of Bhairoṁ, the piṇḍī and her devotees. **Reading plans:** none.
