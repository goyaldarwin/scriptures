# Reconciliation: श्री वैष्णो देवी चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 231–234; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-230 … i-233), read on the page images. The āratī printed after the closing dohā (आरती वैष्णो देवी जी की) is its own work.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/vaishno-devi/vaishno-devi-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/vaishno-mata-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, with its own slips (मे for में, पूजत, रूचिकर, भैंरो).

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas (काली, लक्ष्मी, सरस्वती; ब्रह्मा, विष्णु, शंकर; हनुमत, भैरों; रिद्धि, सिद्धि; मांस, मदिरा) are not kept; its hyphens are.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 1 | नमोः नमोः वैष्णो वरदानी | नमो नमो वैष्णो वरदानी | नमोः, नमो with a visarga, is a broken form, not a word (Drik Panchang has the same). The word is नमो, as the Durgā Chālīsā's "नमो नमो दुर्गे" in the same book. |
| chaupāī 40 | सेवक 'शर्मा' शरण तिहारी | सेवक शर्मा शरण तिहारी | The quotation marks are the printer's, not part of the verse. |
| chaupāī 8 | करेंगी शोषण-पार्वती माँ | करेंगी पोषण-पार्वती माँ | शोषण ('drying up') is a one-stroke corruption of पोषण ('nourishing'): Rāma is telling her (chaupāīs 5–8) that in the dark cave Kālī, Lakṣmī, Sarasvatī and Pārvatī will sustain her. Drik Panchang copies the book's slip; two independent web texts, Patrika (patrika.com, Vaishno Devi aarti-chalisa) and Shubh Panchang (shubhpanchang.com, Vaishno Devi Chalisa), print पोषण-पार्वती. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: त्रिकूटा in the opening dohā (Drik त्रिकुटा; the book has त्रिकुटा in chaupāī 7), कलि काल में, पूजन आवें (Drik पूजत), रुचिकर, पहुंची, भैरों. The spellings कौल-कंदौली, नगरोटा, आद कुंवारी, बरवन and ध्यानूं are the book's, and Drik Panchang has them too.

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
- chalisa.cau8, शोषण or पोषण: पोषण, a text correction (above): independent sources (Patrika, Shubh Panchang) print it, and only it gives the sense of Rāma's promise; the meaning now reads "will sustain you".
- chalisa.cau12, who gives the fruitful boon: the line names no giver, so the meaning keeps it passive ("a boon that would bear fruit was given"); माँ is the subject of मुस्काई and आई, which the meaning keeps.
- chalisa.cau27, who strikes "for protection": the Mother, as the meaning has it; in the story (and in chaupāī 28, where she goes to the fallen head, forgives him and grants the boon) she is the one who beheads Bhairava, and the chālīsā's subject throughout the episode is she.
- chalisa.cau37, जम्बू द्वीप महाराज: the great king of Jambūdvīpa, as printed; the book, Drik Panchang and Shubh Panchang all print जम्बू द्वीप, and द्वीप makes it the continent, not the city of Jammu.
- chalisa.do2, प्रगट हो अवतार: "may the incarnation appear", the plain grammar (अवतार is the subject of प्रगट हो); the dohā echoes the Gītā's promise (4.7) that the incarnation comes when dharma declines.
