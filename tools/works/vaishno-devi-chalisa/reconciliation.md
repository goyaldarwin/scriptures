# Reconciliation: श्री वैष्णो देवी चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 231–234; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-230 … i-233), read on the page images. The āratī printed after the closing dohā (आरती वैष्णो देवी जी की) is its own work.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/vaishno-devi/vaishno-devi-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/vaishno-mata-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, with its own slips (मे for में, पूजत, रूचिकर, भैंरो).

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas (काली, लक्ष्मी, सरस्वती; ब्रह्मा, विष्णु, शंकर; हनुमत, भैरों; रिद्धि, सिद्धि; मांस, मदिरा) are not kept; its hyphens are.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 1 | नमोः नमोः वैष्णो वरदानी | नमो नमो वैष्णो वरदानी | नमोः, नमो with a visarga, is a broken form, not a word (Drik Panchang has the same). The word is नमो, as the Durgā Chālīsā's "नमो नमो दुर्गे" in the same book. |
| chaupāī 40 | सेवक 'शर्मा' शरण तिहारी | सेवक शर्मा शरण तिहारी | The quotation marks are the printer's, not part of the verse. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: त्रिकूटा in the opening dohā (Drik त्रिकुटा; the book has त्रिकुटा in chaupāī 7), कलि काल में, पूजन आवें (Drik पूजत), रुचिकर, पहुंची, भैरों. The spellings करेंगी शोषण-पार्वती, कौल-कंदौली, नगरोटा, आद कुंवारी, बरवन and ध्यानूं are the book's, and Drik Panchang has them too.
