# Reconciliation: श्री विष्णु चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 38–41; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-037 … i-040), read on the page images. The āratī printed after it on p. 41 (आरती श्री विष्णु जी की, ॐ जय जगदीश हरे) is not part of this work.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/vishnu/vishnu-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-vishnu-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same text, the same opening dohā and 40 chaupāīs in the same order; it too has no closing dohā.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 14 | धर मतस्य तन सिन्धु बनाया | धर मत्स्य तन सिन्धु बनाया | A misspelling: मतस्य is not a word; the fish avatar is मत्स्य, as Drik Panchang has. |

Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: भगवान् (Drik भगवान), कौन भांति मैं करहु समर्पण (Drik भाँति … करहुँ). Otherwise the two texts agree word for word, including the readings that look odd (अमिलख, मन्द्राचल, मझाया, हिरणाकुश, धुरू, शैतानी), which both print.

## Meanings
Our own, one per verse (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).

## Readings settled
- **chalisa.cau6** (सत्य धर्म मद लोभ न गाजे): 'before truth and dharma, pride and greed cannot roar', as our meaning has it: the plain reading of the words, the second half then saying the same of him.
- **chalisa.cau14** (धर मत्स्य तन सिन्धु बनाया): बनाया 'set in order' (Platts: बनाना 'to arrange, put in order, repair'), as our meaning has it; both witnesses print बनाया, and no source gives मथाया.
- **chalisa.cau15** (अमिलख): अमिय लखि, 'seeing the nectar', as our meaning has it: both witnesses print अमिलख, and seeing the nectar is what starts the demons' quarrel that Mohinī ends in the next half.
- **chalisa.cau22** (हार पार शिव सकल बनाई): 'Śiva tried every means and still was defeated', as our meaning has it: हार 'defeat' with सकल बनाई 'made every effort'; the story (Śiva Purāṇa) is Śiva's failure to overcome Jalandhara while Vṛndā's chastity guarded him.
- **chalisa.cau25** (देखत तीन दनुज): तीन as printed, 'three', as our meaning has it: both witnesses print it, and no source shows तिन.
- **chalisa.cau30** (देखहुँ मैं निज दरश तुम्हारे): निज with मैं, 'may I myself behold you', as our meaning has it; दरश तुम्हारे already says 'your sight'.
- **chalisa.cau40** (निगम: the Veda or a poet?): the Veda, as our meaning has it: no source names a poet Nigama for this chālīsā, and निगम is the common word for the Veda.
