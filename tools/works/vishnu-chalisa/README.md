# श्री विष्णु चालीसा (Shri Vishnu Chalisa)

The chālīsā of Viṣṇu that opens "विष्णु सुनिए विनय सेवक की चितलाय", in Awadhi (`awa`).

- **Text** (`text.txt`): ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, pp. 38–41), checked against Drik Panchang. Every choice: `reconciliation.md`.
- **Meanings:** Ours (`meanings/hi/scriptures.json`, `meanings/en/scriptures.json`).
- **Recordings:** none yet.

Verse types: the Mānas's own (`do` dohā, `cau` chaupāī).

## Structure
- **Hierarchy:** one part (`chalisa`) › two sections: the opening dohā (`chalisa-do`), the chaupāīs (`chalisa-cau`) › verses. The book prints no closing dohā; the āratī it prints after the chālīsā (ॐ जय जगदीश हरे) is its own work.
- **Numbering:** the 40 chaupāīs are numbered 1–40 and cited by it ("Chaupai 12"); the dohā has no number of its own and is cited by its section ("Opening · Doha").
- **Ids:** as the Hanumān Chālīsā's in the Sundarakāṇḍa (`chalisa.do1`, `chalisa.cau1` …).
- **Topics:** Viṣṇu's form, the avatars and their deeds, the prayer. **Reading plans:** none.
