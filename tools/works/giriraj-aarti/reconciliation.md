# Reconciliation: ॐ जय जय जय गिरिराज

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), pp. 139–140, "आरती श्री गिरिराज जी की", printed after the Śrī Girirāja Chālīsā (its own work); scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-138, i-139), read on the page images. The refrain and seven stanzas, each two printed lines, every stanza ending with the cue "ॐ जय॥".
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/krishna/giriraj-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/shri-giriraj-aarti.html`): the same refrain and seven stanzas in the same order and the same words, each printed line split in two at its caesura.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (the book's comma inside a line becomes a space; each printed line is one line of the couplet, as in the Śiva āratī); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| after every stanza | ॐ जय॥ | (not shown) | The refrain cue; the refrain is the first item (टेक). |
| refrain, stanzas 1, 3, 6 | the commas inside the lines | a space | The standard. |
| stanza numbers | none | ॥१॥ … ॥७॥ | The standard. |

No reading of the book is changed, and Drik Panchang has the same words throughout. Kept as the book has them: राखौ, तुम्हरौं, धरैं / तरैं, तुम्हारौ, सिला, सोहें / मोहें, गङ्गा, तापै, वैतरनी, सुहावन-पावन, राधा-कुण्ड, अन्तरयामी, गिरिवर गिरधारी (two words), देवकीनंदन, परिकम्मा.
