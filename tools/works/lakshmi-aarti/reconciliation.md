# Reconciliation: ॐ जय लक्ष्मी माता

## Witnesses
- **The book:** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, 2000), pp. 190–191, under the heading "आरती श्री लक्ष्मी (महालक्ष्मी) जी की"; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-189 for the heading and the refrain, i-190 for the seven stanzas), read on the page images. Eight couplets, each a line ending । and an indented line ending ॥ and the cue "जय…". Its own wording of the āratī (below). The Mahālakṣmī Chālīsā's last lines and closing dohā above the heading on p. 190 are not part of the āratī.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/aarti/lakshmi/lakshmi-aarti/printouts/drikpanchang/lakshmi-mata-aarti.html`): the widely sung "ॐ जय लक्ष्मी माता": the same refrain and seven stanzas in the same order and on the same rhyme as the book, with other wording in most lines.
- **Aarti Mala** (page image i-347, an indif.com printout, read on the image): Drik Panchang's version, with the sung repeats (ओ मैया …) printed and some words of its own (तुम को निस दिन सेवत, हर विष्णु विधाता, जिस घर तुम रहती तहँ, भव निधि की दाता, जो कोई जन गाता).

## The text: the version she sings
The owner chose Anuradha Paudwal's recording (the "Om Jai Jagdish Hare Aarti Sangrah" jukebox, YouTube `sd31yGqZFlA`, about 33:18–38:48), and an āratī's text follows the version its recording sings (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3). She sings Drik Panchang's and Aarti Mala's version, the refrain and the seven stanzas in the book's order, so the text is that version: each line as she sings it, each word as a witness spells it. Where a word is the same in the book, the book's spelling is kept; where she sings a word only one of Drik Panchang and Aarti Mala has, that one's. The verse ids stay: each stanza is the same stanza of the āratī, in other words.

The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): an āratī's verse has no comma (a line is split at its half, one half per line, as the witnesses' commas divide it); the refrain (टेक) is shown once; stanzas numbered (॥१॥).
