# Reconciliation: श्री राम चालीसा

## Witnesses
- **The book (base):** ५१ चालीसा संग्रह आरतियों सहित (Randhir Prakashan, Haridwar, first edition 2000), pp. 12–15; scriptures-data `hinduism/chalisa/books/aarti-chalisa-sangrah/` (page images i-011 … i-014), read on the page images.
- **Drik Panchang** (`hinduism/chalisa/rama/ram-chalisa/printouts/drikpanchang/shree-rama-chalisa.html`): a typed copy of the same chālīsā, with the chaupāīs in another order in two places (दूत तुम्हार before जय जय जय रघुनाथ; the four सुनहु राम … दशरथ chaupāīs before the four जय जय जय प्रभु ज्योति … गौरिपति ones) and its own slips (नर्गुण ब्रहृ).
- The Chalisa Mala printout (indif.com; the same book, pages i-372 … i-376) is a further copy of the web text and was not needed.

The rule: keep the book; correct it only where it is clearly wrong, and only on the witnesses. The library's writing standard (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §3): a chaupāī's two halves on one line, `।` between them and `॥` at its end; a dohā's two lines, each ending `।` and `॥`; the book's commas are not kept.

| Place | Book | Ours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 17 | नाम शत्रुहन हदय प्रकाशा | नाम शत्रुहन हृदय प्रकाशा | A misprint: the ऋ-sign is dropped (हदय is not a word); Drik Panchang has हृदय. |
| chaupāī 36 | ज्ञान हदय दो ज्ञान स्वरूपा | ज्ञान हृदय दो ज्ञान स्वरूपा | The same. |
| chaupāī 38 | बजी दुन्दुभी शंरव्र बजाया | बजी दुन्दुभी शंख बजाया | A broken word (ख set as र-व with a stray sign); Drik Panchang and the sense ("drums and conches sounded") have शंख. |

The book's order of the chaupāīs is kept (above). Kept as the book has them, against Drik Panchang: रघुवीर (Drik रघुबीर), नहिं (Drik नहीं), ध्यान धरे … माहीं (Drik धरें … मांही), सन्तन, तिहूँ, तव भुज दण्ड (Drik तुव भुजदण्ड), चारिउ वेद (Drik चारिउ भेद), पाहीं (Drik पाहिं), चारिउ वेदन (Drik चारिहु), लीन्हौ / कीन्हौ, पाव न कोउ तुम्हारो पारा (Drik पावत कोऊ न तुम्हरो पारा), कबहु, लषन (Drik लखन), सीता नाम पुनीता गायो (Drik सीता राम), सो तुमरे नित पाँव पलोटत (Drik जो तुम्हरे … पांव), नवों, वारा (Drik बारा), स्वरूपा, निर्गुण ब्रह्म (Drik नर्गुण ब्रहृ, slips), सत्य सत्य सत्य ब्रत स्वामी (Drik सत्य सत्य जय सत्यव्रत), गौरिपति, सिद्धि (Drik सिधि), तुम ही राजा (Drik तुमहिं), दशरथ दुलारे (Drik दशरथ के प्यारे), तीनहूं, ल्यावैं / चढ़ावैं / लगावैं / पावैं (Drik -वै), रघुवर, धाम को जावै (Drik पावै).

## Recording
A chālīsā's text is the base's, corrected only where the base is clearly wrong (docs/PLAYBOOK.md §2–3); the recording is evidence for a reading, never the reason for a change alone. The chosen recording, BhaktiDhaam's "सम्पूर्ण चालीसा संग्रह" (YouTube `lCwPg0LRRjk`, the Rāma Chālīsā from 45:10 to the video's end at 50:28), sings the book's text and the book's order of the chaupāīs (जय जय जय रघुनाथ कृपाला before दूत तुम्हार; ज्योति स्वरूपा after चित लावै; सीता नाम पुनीता, सो तुमरे नित पाँव, दशरथ दुलारे), except that it sings Drik Panchang's हमरे in chaupāī 39, which the text has because the metre needs it (below). The video ends after chaupāī 40: chaupāīs 41–46 and the two closing dohās stay, untimed.

| Place | Book | Drik Panchang | Ours |
|---|---|---|---|
| chaupāī 39 | तुम ही हो हमारे तन मन धन | तुम ही हो हमरे तन-मन धन | हमारे makes the half 17 mātrās, हमरे the caupāī's 16; Drik Panchang has हमरे, and the recording sings it. |

## Readings settled
- **chalisa.cau32** (तुमहिं भरत कुल पूज्य प्रचारे): 'Bharata proclaimed you alone worthy of worship in the family', as our meaning has it: Bharata is the subject of प्रचारे (he set Rāma's sandals on the throne and ruled in his name), and भरत कुल for 'the Indian people' is a modern sense that the chālīsā's idiom does not use.
- **chalisa.cau41** (सत्य वचन माने शिव मेरा): 'Śiva holds this word of mine true', as our meaning has it: शिव is the subject of माने and वचन its object; an oath 'by Śiva' would need a word the line does not have. It answers chaupāī 31, where Śiva swears the same truth.
- **chalisa.cau46** (श्री हरिदास कहै अरु गावै): Haridāsa as the poet's name, as our meaning has it: कहै with a name is the chālīsā signature, and the closing dohā signs with the same name; सो is the reciter of chaupāīs 40–45.
- **chalisa.do1** (हरिदास हरि कृपा से): the poet's signature ('says Haridāsa'), for the same reason; the subject of पाय is the reciter of the first half (जो … पाठ करे).
